# Famotidine

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** H2-receptor antagonist (H2RA)
**Verification Status:** AI-compiled from primary sources, unverified — not yet reviewed by a pharmacist/physician
**Evidence Grade:** High for acid suppression/heartburn relief as an established drug class; generally considered less potent than PPIs for healing erosive esophageal disease (class-level clinical knowledge, not independently re-derived from a head-to-head trial in this pass)
**Last updated:** 2026-08-25
**Sources:** raw/primary-sources/famotidine/statpearls-famotidine.md, raw/primary-sources/famotidine/dailymed-pepcid-ac-max.md

## Indication
Prescription: duodenal/gastric ulcers, GERD (adults and children), pathological hypersecretory conditions. OTC: prevention and relief of heartburn from acid indigestion. Off-label: refractory urticaria, anaphylaxis/allergy adjunct, stress ulcer prophylaxis in critically ill patients, gastritis symptoms, NSAID-GI-risk mitigation.

## Mechanism
Competitive H2-receptor antagonist — selectively binds H2 receptors on the basolateral membrane of gastric parietal cells, blocking histamine and suppressing gastric acid secretion (both volume and acidity). Inhibits basal, nocturnal, and food-stimulated acid secretion.

## Dosing

| Indication | Adult dose |
|---|---|
| Active duodenal ulcer | 40mg PO at bedtime ≤8 weeks, or 20mg PO/IV q12h |
| Maintenance (duodenal ulcer) | 20mg at bedtime |
| Active gastric ulcer | 40mg once daily at bedtime ≤8 weeks |
| Nonerosive GERD | 20mg PO twice daily ≤6 weeks |
| Erosive esophagitis | 20–40mg PO twice daily ≤12 weeks |
| Hypersecretory conditions | 20–60mg PO q6h |
| OTC heartburn (Pepcid AC Max) | 20mg tablet, max 2/24h; take 10–60min before trigger foods for prevention |

**Pediatric:** 1 mg/kg/day divided into 2 doses of 0.5mg/kg each (prescription); OTC strengths 10 or 20mg, not to exceed 2 weeks of use without provider guidance.

## Pharmacokinetics
Oral bioavailability 40–45%; onset within 1h, peak 1–3h. Antisecretory effect lasts ~10–12h (supports twice-daily dosing). Plasma protein binding 15–20%; Vd 1.0–1.3 L/kg. Minimal first-pass metabolism via CYP1A2. Half-life 2.5–3.5h normally, but **>20h in severe renal impairment (CrCl <10 mL/min)** — a clinically important PK shift requiring dose adjustment.

## Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to famotidine or formulation components. Due to potential H2RA cross-sensitivity, avoid in patients with a cimetidine hypersensitivity history. OTC label specifically: do not use with difficulty/pain swallowing, vomiting blood, bloody/black stools, or when combining with other acid reducers.

## Boxed Warning
IV formulations containing benzyl alcohol are contraindicated in neonates and pregnant women — risk of "gasping syndrome"; benzyl alcohol readily crosses the placental and blood-brain barriers.

## Key Safety Data
- Agitation: 14% in infants vs. <1% in adults — a notable pediatric-specific signal
- Headache 5%, dizziness 1%, diarrhea 2%, constipation 1%
- Pediatric use associated with increased risk of community-acquired pneumonia and acute gastroenteritis (consistent with acid-suppression removing a natural GI/respiratory-pathogen barrier)
- **Thrombocytopenia:** documented famotidine-induced cases, sometimes causing prolonged hospital stays and higher injury severity scores; mechanism is bone marrow suppression or platelet autoantibodies — regular CBC monitoring recommended with long-term use, discontinue and switch to a PPI if it develops
- Oral doses up to 640mg/day (far above FDA-approved limits) have been used for hypersecretory conditions without severe adverse outcomes in some reported cases — not a recommendation, just documented tolerance data

## Drug Interactions
Minimal inhibitory effect on most drug metabolism overall, but is a **mild CYP1A2 inhibitor** — significantly raises **tizanidine** levels; avoid combination or monitor closely for hypotension/bradycardia/drowsiness if unavoidable. **Reduces absorption of:** cefuroxime, dasatinib, delavirdine, neratinib, pazopanib, risedronate — avoid coadministration with these.

## Special Population Flags
- **Renal impairment:** CrCl <50 mL/min — reduce dose 50% or extend dosing interval to 36–48h; anuria extends half-life to ~24h, raising CNS adverse-effect risk
- **Hepatic impairment:** no dosage adjustment per manufacturer labeling
- **Pregnancy:** crosses placenta; former FDA Category B; ASA supports use in obstetric anesthesia for aspiration prophylaxis; lifestyle changes/antacids remain first-line for GERD in pregnancy, famotidine considered after risk-benefit assessment
- **Breastfeeding:** appears in breast milk at LOWER concentrations than other H2RAs, making it a potentially preferable H2RA choice during lactation; available evidence suggests unlikely to harm the breastfed infant
- **Elderly:** per 2023 Beers Criteria, reduce dose if CrCl <50 mL/min; avoid in elderly patients experiencing delirium

## Overdose / Toxicity
Risk increases with renal impairment. Management: eliminate unabsorbed drug from the GI tract, close monitoring, supportive care — no specific antidote described.

## Pharmacy Times Context (recommendation share — NOT efficacy)
Pepcid recommendation shares (Pharmacy Times 2026 OTC Guide, ~1,700 pharmacists surveyed): H2-Receptor Antagonists category 83%, Acid Reducers (general) category 35%. These are pharmacist recommendation shares, not response/cure rates.
