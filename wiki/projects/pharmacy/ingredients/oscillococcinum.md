# Oscillococcinum

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Homeopathic preparation, ultra-high dilution (200CK — a different evidence category from pharmacologically active herbal extracts like elderberry or Pelargonium, which retain real drug molecules)
**Verification Status:** AI-compiled from primary sources, unverified — not yet reviewed by a pharmacist/physician
**Evidence Grade:** Low/uncertain — the most recent Cochrane review found insufficient good evidence and explicitly called findings "not compelling," while not ruling out a possible effect

## Indication
Marketed (as a homeopathic product, not an FDA-evaluated drug) to reduce the duration/severity of flu-like symptoms: body aches, headache, fever, chills, fatigue.

## Mechanism
None established by conventional pharmacology. Made from a 1% solution of wild duck (Anas barbariae) heart and liver extract, serially diluted 200 times with water/alcohol using the Korsakovian method (labeled "200CK HPUS"). This dilution factor is extreme enough that, per standard chemistry (Avogadro's number), no molecule of the original duck-extract substance is expected to remain in the final product — a fact stated here plainly rather than glossed over, since it directly bears on evaluating any proposed mechanism.

## Dosing (per FDA/HPUS label)
Adults and children ≥2 years: dissolve entire contents of 1 tube in the mouth every 6 hours, up to 3 times daily, starting at symptom onset. Children <2y: consult a physician.

## Efficacy Evidence — Cochrane, Mathie/Frye/Fisher 2015 (PMID 25629583, update of a review series back to Vickers & Smith 2000, PMID 10796675)
6 studies: 2 prophylaxis trials (327 adults, Russia), 4 treatment trials (1196 teenagers/adults, France/Germany). Overall trial-reporting quality was poor with unclear risk of bias in many domains.
- **Prevention:** no statistically significant difference vs. placebo (RR 0.48, 95% CI 0.17–1.34, p=0.16)
- **Treatment at 48h** (2 "low quality" trials): absolute risk reduction 7.7% in symptom relief (RD 0.077, 95% CI 0.03–0.12); RR 1.86 (95% CI 1.27–2.73, p=0.001)
- **Treatment at 3 days:** smaller but still significant — RR 1.27 (95% CI 1.03–1.56, p=0.03)
- **Treatment at 4–5 days:** not significant (RR 1.11 and 1.06 respectively, both p>0.10)
- **Adverse events:** one headache reported across all 6 studies; no other harms

**Authors' conclusion:** "Insufficient good evidence" to draw robust conclusions about Oscillococcinum for influenza prevention or treatment. The review does not rule out a possible clinically useful effect, but given the low quality of the eligible studies, calls the evidence "not compelling." No evidence of clinically important harm.

**Why this evidence should be weighted cautiously in favor of, not against, this conclusion:** all three review authors are research-active in homeopathy and had current or past ties to homeopathy research organizations (disclosed in full in the raw source file), meaning any bias in this review, if present, would plausibly run toward finding a positive effect — yet the reviewers themselves still concluded the evidence is not compelling.

## Pharmacokinetics
Not applicable / not established — see Mechanism above regarding dilution.

## Contraindications
Per label: do not use if allergic to Oscillococcinum. No other contraindications listed.

## Key Safety Data
Cochrane review across 6 studies found only one reported adverse event (headache) attributable to Oscillococcinum, with no other harms identified — consistent with an inert or near-inert preparation from a safety standpoint, independent of any efficacy question.

## Drug Interactions
None identified — consistent with the preparation containing no detectable amount of the original active substance.

## Special Population Flags
- **Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** label says ask a health professional before use
- **Pediatric:** labeled for ages ≥2y; consult a physician if <2y

## Overdose / Toxicity
Label directs contacting Poison Control in case of overdose as a standard OTC-product precaution; no specific toxicity data exists or would be expected given the extreme dilution.

## Regulatory Note
This product's own FDA label states directly: **"This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy. FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective."** This disclaimer is quoted verbatim here because it is directly relevant to how this page's evidence grade should be read.
