# Miconazole Nitrate (Topical)

**Type:** Active Ingredient
**Drug Class:** Topical azole antifungal (imidazole) — same class as [[clotrimazole]], mechanistically distinct from [[terbinafine]] (allylamine) and [[butenafine]] (benzylamine)
**Verification Status:** AI-compiled from primary sources, unverified — not yet reviewed by a pharmacist/physician
**Evidence Grade:** High — a large Cochrane review (129 trials, 18,086 participants) found all topical antifungal classes reviewed to be effective for tinea cruris/corporis, though direct comparisons suggest allylamines (terbinafine, naftifine) are generally somewhat more efficacious than azoles (miconazole, clotrimazole) — a real, if not perfectly quantified, class-level efficacy gradient
**Last updated:** 2026-08-27
**Sources:** WebSearch (El-Gohary et al. 2014 Cochrane review CD009992.pub2 and secondary summaries; PubMed miconazole-vs-tolnaftate and miconazole-vs-placebo tinea trials), DailyMed (Zeasorb AF, Gold Bond antifungal labels)

---

## Indication
FDA OTC monograph: cures most jock itch (tinea cruris) and athlete's foot (tinea pedis); relieves itching, burning, and scaling. Same general dermatophyte-infection scope as [[clotrimazole]].

## Mechanism
Imidazole antifungal — inhibits ergosterol biosynthesis (same enzyme target as clotrimazole, lanosterol 14-alpha-demethylase), disrupting fungal cell membrane integrity. Fungistatic, like clotrimazole — mechanistically distinct from the fungicidal squalene-epoxidase-inhibiting mechanism shared by [[terbinafine]] and [[butenafine]].

## Efficacy Evidence
- **El-Gohary et al. 2014 (Cochrane Database Syst Rev, CD009992.pub2):** 129 trials, 18,086 participants, tinea cruris/corporis. All topical antifungal treatments examined were found effective, but substantial heterogeneity precluded pooling mycological/clinical cure data across the azole-vs-allylamine comparison specifically. Direct head-to-head comparisons cited in secondary summaries of this review found **allylamines (terbinafine, naftifine) generally more efficacious than azoles (including miconazole)**, with terbinafine/naftifine achieving effective results with few adverse effects — though most individual comparisons rested on single studies, a real evidence-quality limitation.
- **Miconazole vs. tolnaftate, tinea pedis:** 68% therapeutic cure at 28 days for miconazole vs. 50% for tolnaftate, with miconazole showing faster symptomatic relief and mycological clearing — a direct efficacy edge over [[tolnaftate]].
- **Miconazole 2% cream vs. placebo:** 75.5% clinical clearing for tinea cruris/pedis/corporis/cutaneous candidiasis, twice daily for 2 weeks.
- **Miconazole vs. ketoconazole, tinea pedis:** comparable clinical improvement overall, though ketoconazole showed somewhat faster symptom resolution and higher sustained improvement at follow-up.

**Net finding, consistent with this database's other antifungal-comparison research this pass ([[butenafine]] vs. [[clotrimazole]]/[[terbinafine]]):** miconazole outperforms the older tolnaftate but sits behind the newer allylamine/benzylamine agents (terbinafine, butenafine) in most direct comparisons located — a real, evidence-based efficacy hierarchy across this category's brands, not merely a marketing distinction.

## Key Safety Data
Standard topical-azole safety profile per [[clotrimazole]]: generally mild local irritation, rash, burning given minimal systemic absorption through intact skin.

## Contraindications
External use only; not effective for onychomycosis (nail fungus) or scalp infections, the same limitation as [[clotrimazole]]. Azole hypersensitivity.

## Special Population Flags
Not for children under 2 without doctor guidance, per Zeasorb AF's own label — consistent with the general OTC antifungal convention across this category.

## Formulation Caveat
Miconazole nitrate topical products in this category frequently come paired, under the same brand name, with a plain (non-antifungal) moisture-absorbing powder line with no active ingredient at all — see the [[Zeasorb]] and [[Gold Bond]] brand pages for two confirmed examples of this exact pattern.
