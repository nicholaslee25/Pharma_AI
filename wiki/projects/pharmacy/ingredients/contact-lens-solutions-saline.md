## What This Page Is
Contact lens care products are FDA-regulated **Class II devices** (21 CFR 886.5928 for soft/hydrophilic lens products, 886.5918 for rigid gas-permeable lens products), not drugs — evaluated on disinfection efficacy (via the ISO 14729 stand-alone/regimen antimicrobial test against a standard 5-organism panel) and on real-world infection-risk epidemiology, not on a drug-style efficacy grade. Two fundamentally different product types exist in this category and are not interchangeable: **multipurpose solutions (MPS)** (clean, disinfect, rinse, and store lenses in one product — e.g. [[OPTI-FREE Puremoist]]) and **saline solutions** (rinsing/storage only, explicitly **not** a disinfectant — e.g. [[Sensitive Eyes Saline Solution]]). Using a saline-only product as if it were a disinfecting MPS is a real, documented user-error risk factor for infection, not an equivalent substitution.

## Efficacy Evidence

**Epidemiology (foundational study, PMID 9071523, and subsequent literature):** annual microbial keratitis incidence is estimated at ~1.1 per 10,000 daily-wear rigid gas-permeable lens wearers, ~3.5–4.1 per 10,000 daily-wear soft lens wearers, ~13.3–20.9 per 10,000 extended-wear soft lens wearers, and ~52 per 10,000 for therapeutic/bandage lenses — a rate that has remained relatively stable over 25+ years of tracking. ~90% of cases are bacterial (predominantly Pseudomonas aeruginosa, then Staphylococcus aureus); the remaining ~10% are amoebic (Acanthamoeba) or fungal. Key risk factors: overnight/extended wear, smoking, male sex, socioeconomic status, and poor lens/solution hygiene compliance — solution choice is one contributing factor among several, not the sole determinant of risk.

**Acanthamoeba-specific gap (PMID 34064555):** more than 90% of Acanthamoeba keratitis (AK) cases occur in contact lens wearers, with global AK infection rates rising from an estimated 17 to 70 per million among wearers. Critically, the cited authors state — citing their own prior research — that **standard multipurpose contact lens solutions are "not effective against Acanthamoeba"**, even when ISO 14729-validated against the standard bacterial/fungal panel. This is a real, documented limitation of the entire MPS product class, not a defect specific to any one brand tracked in this database.

**Bottom line:** ISO 14729 certification (which every mainstream MPS brand, including those tracked here, is expected to carry) demonstrates efficacy against the standard bacterial/fungal test panel, but does **not** demonstrate meaningful protection against Acanthamoeba — a distinct, less common but more severe pathogen. No brand-specific claim of "kills 99.9% of germs" (a real, accurate ISO 14729-panel claim used in this category's marketing) should be read as covering Acanthamoeba protection.

## FDA Classification
Soft contact lens care products (cleaning, rinsing, disinfecting, lubricating/rewetting, storing): 21 CFR 886.5928, **Class II**, requiring 510(k) premarket notification and special controls per FDA's Contact Lens Care Products guidance. Rigid gas-permeable lens care products: 21 CFR 886.5918, also Class II.

## Special Notes
- Saline-only products (no disinfectant system) are explicitly labeled "not to be used as a disinfectant" — a genuine, safety-relevant product-type distinction within this single Pharmacy Times category, not a marketing nuance.
- No dedicated brand-specific clinical trial exists for either brand tracked in this category beyond general ISO 14729 disinfection-panel data and the class-level epidemiology above — expected for this device category, not a research gap.

## Brands Using This Category (in this database)
**OPTI-FREE Puremoist** (45%, Alcon) — a true multipurpose disinfecting solution using a dual POLYQUAD/ALDOX preservative-disinfectant system plus HydraGlyde Moisture Matrix wetting agent, ISO 14729-tested against the standard 5-organism panel. **Biotrue** (30%, Bausch + Lomb) — see [[Biotrue]] for full detail; a multipurpose solution line formulated with hyaluronan and a pH matched to natural tears. **Sensitive Eyes Saline Solution** (25%, Bausch + Lomb) — a rinsing/storage-only saline product using polyaminopropyl biguanide and edetate disodium as preservatives (not a full disinfectant system), explicitly labeled not for use as a disinfectant — a materially different product type from the other two brands in this category despite being tracked in the same Pharmacy Times list.
