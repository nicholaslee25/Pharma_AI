# AREDS2 Eye Vitamins (Antioxidant/Zinc Formula for Macular Degeneration)

**Type:** Active Ingredient (product class — fixed multi-nutrient combination)
**Drug Class:** Ocular nutritional supplement (dietary supplement, not FDA-regulated as a drug)
**Verification Status:** AI-compiled from primary sources, unverified — not yet reviewed by a pharmacist/physician
**Evidence Grade:** High for slowing progression to advanced age-related macular degeneration (AMD) in a specific high-risk population (intermediate AMD, or advanced AMD in one eye) — two large NEI-funded randomized trials (AREDS, AREDS2) directly support this; **no evidence of benefit for AMD prevention in the general population or for early-stage AMD**, and no evidence this formula treats any other eye condition (e.g., dry eye, general "eye health")
**Last updated:** 2026-08-28
**Sources:** NEI AREDS Research Group 2001 (Archives of Ophthalmology), NEI/JAMA AREDS2 Research Group 2013 (PMID 23644932), AREDS2 Report 28 (PMID 35653117, 10-year follow-up)

**Sourcing note:** built specifically for this database's Ocular Nutritional Supplements category (PreserVision, Ocuvite, TheraTears), which previously had no dedicated ingredient-class evidence page despite PreserVision's category-leading 63% share.

## Indication
Slowing progression to advanced age-related macular degeneration (AMD) in patients already diagnosed with **intermediate AMD, or advanced AMD in one eye** — a secondary-prevention indication in an already-diagnosed, high-risk population. **Not indicated or evidenced for general AMD prevention, early AMD, or any other eye condition** (dry eye, cataracts, general vision support).

## Mechanism
Combines antioxidant vitamins (C, E), zinc/copper, and the macular carotenoids lutein and zeaxanthin, theorized to reduce oxidative stress and support macular pigment density, both implicated in AMD pathophysiology. Neither trial source reviewed here details the specific molecular mechanism beyond this general antioxidant/carotenoid rationale.

## Efficacy Evidence

**Original AREDS trial (NEI, 2001, Archives of Ophthalmology)** — high-risk AMD patients (intermediate AMD, or advanced AMD in one eye), average 6.5-year follow-up, formula: vitamin C 500mg, vitamin E 400 IU, beta-carotene 15mg, zinc 80mg + copper:
- **~25% relative risk reduction** in progression to advanced AMD versus placebo
- **~19% relative risk reduction** in AMD-related vision loss
- This is the foundational, landmark result establishing the entire "AREDS formula" eye-vitamin product category

**AREDS2 trial (NEI/JAMA 2013, PMID 23644932)** — 4,203 participants ages 50-85, 82 US sites, randomized (on top of the base AREDS formula) to added lutein 10mg + zeaxanthin 2mg, added omega-3 (DHA 350mg + EPA 650mg), both, or neither:
- **Adding lutein/zeaxanthin or omega-3 to the base AREDS formula showed NO significant additional overall benefit** in progression to advanced AMD (5-year progression: 31% placebo-add-on, 29% lutein/zeaxanthin, 31% omega-3, 30% both) — omega-3 fatty acids specifically do NOT add benefit for this indication, despite their evidence in other contexts (see [[omega-3-fish-oil]])
- **Critical substitution finding**: formulas using lutein/zeaxanthin with NO beta-carotene showed an **18% reduction** in advanced AMD progression compared to formulas with beta-carotene and no lutein/zeaxanthin — this drove the reformulation of commercial products from beta-carotene to lutein/zeaxanthin
- **Real, serious safety driver for that reformulation**: beta-carotene is independently documented (from separate lung-cancer-prevention trials, e.g., CARET) to **increase lung cancer risk in smokers and former smokers** — meaning the original 2001 AREDS beta-carotene formula carries a real contraindication for smokers, which the lutein/zeaxanthin-substituted "AREDS2" formula avoids
- A subgroup with low dietary lutein/zeaxanthin intake at baseline showed ~25% lower risk of advanced AMD when supplementing with lutein/zeaxanthin versus similar low-intake participants who did not supplement

**10-year follow-up (AREDS2 Report 28, 2022, PMID 35653117):** long-term data affirms the lutein/zeaxanthin-substituted AREDS2 formula remains effective and safe at 10 years.

## Contraindications
**Beta-carotene-containing formulas are contraindicated (or should be avoided) in current or former smokers** given the independently documented lung-cancer-risk increase from beta-carotene supplementation in this population — a real, serious, well-established safety issue distinct from the AMD-efficacy question itself.

## Key Safety Data
High-dose zinc (80mg, as used in both AREDS/AREDS2 formulas) is well above the general adult RDA and Tolerable Upper Intake Level for zinc — see [[zinc]] for general excess-zinc considerations (e.g., copper deficiency risk with prolonged high-dose zinc use, which is why AREDS/AREDS2 formulas include co-formulated copper specifically to offset this).

## Drug Interactions
Not itemized in the sources reviewed; see [[zinc]] and [[vitamin-c]] for those components' own general interaction notes.

## Special Population Flags
- **Current/former smokers:** should specifically avoid beta-carotene-containing AREDS formulas (lung cancer risk) — the lutein/zeaxanthin-based AREDS2 formula is the appropriate substitute
- **Patients without diagnosed intermediate/advanced (one-eye) AMD:** this evidence base does NOT support use for general prevention — a real, common risk of over-application to a broader population than the trials studied

## Overdose / Toxicity
Not addressed in the sources reviewed beyond the zinc/copper-balance note above.

## Pharmacy Times Context (recommendation share — NOT efficacy)
Ocular Nutritional Supplements (Pharmacy Times 2026 OTC Guide, ~1,700 pharmacists surveyed, 864,706 monthly recommendations): PreserVision 63%, Ocuvite 29%, TheraTears 8%. These are pharmacist recommendation shares, not response/cure rates. Confirmed via manufacturer sourcing: PreserVision AREDS 2 Formula matches the exact AREDS2-trial nutrient levels (vitamin C 500mg, vitamin E 400 IU, lutein 10mg, zeaxanthin 2mg, zinc 80mg as zinc oxide, copper 2mg as cupric oxide) — a direct, confirmed formula-to-trial match. Ocuvite (same manufacturer, Bausch + Lomb) sells multiple distinct formulas, not all of which match the AREDS2-trial formula — per independent sourcing, "PreserVision and Ocuvite are NOT interchangeable... Ocuvite vitamins support general eye health but do not contain the same formula that was studied in the AREDS2 trial" for at least some Ocuvite SKUs. TheraTears is primarily an artificial-tears/dry-eye brand (see [[artificial-tears]]) whose appearance in this specific category likely reflects a separate nutritional-supplement line, not independently confirmed against the AREDS2 formula in this pass.
