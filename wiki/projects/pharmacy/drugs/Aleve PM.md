## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Aleve PM is **naproxen sodium 220 mg + diphenhydramine HCl 25 mg** per tablet, matching [[naproxen]] and the newly built [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]. Naproxen's longer half-life (12-17h per [[naproxen]]) versus ibuprofen's ~2h means Aleve PM's analgesic component is better pharmacokinetically matched to an overnight-duration product than a shorter-acting NSAID would be — a genuine formulation-design logic point, not purely marketing.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label combining two individually well-evidenced ingredients for their respective primary uses
- Naproxen's long half-life is a real pharmacokinetic fit for an overnight sleep-aid/analgesic combination — likely to maintain analgesic coverage through the night better than a shorter-acting NSAID would, though no head-to-head overnight-coverage RCT between Aleve PM and its PM competitors was identified in this pass
- Addresses combined pain-with-sleeplessness in one product

## Cons
- **Same real, guideline-level "avoid in older adults" flag applies to the diphenhydramine component** (AGS Beers Criteria, per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]) as for Tylenol PM and Advil PM
- Carries naproxen's own contested cardiovascular-risk regulatory history (the 2004 FDA warning vs. the 2014 advisory-committee debate, documented on `drugs/Aleve.md` and [[naproxen]]) — same open questions apply here
- Combines NSAID risk (GI bleed, CV thrombotic risk per [[naproxen]]) with diphenhydramine's anticholinergic/elderly-risk profile simultaneously
- Smallest share (12%) of the three PM combination products in this database's data

## Use Cases
Combined temporary relief of headache/minor aches and pains WITH accompanying occasional sleeplessness, via naproxen's longer-acting NSAID profile.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label conventions matching the naproxen risk profile (per [[naproxen]]: GI bleeding, cardiovascular risk, avoid ≥30 weeks pregnancy) plus the diphenhydramine warnings shared across all PM products (avoid other diphenhydramine-containing products, alcohol, or sedatives without medical guidance; drowsiness will occur).

## Symptoms Treated
Headache, minor aches and pains, and occasional sleeplessness accompanying pain, with a longer-duration analgesic component than the other two PM products in this category.
