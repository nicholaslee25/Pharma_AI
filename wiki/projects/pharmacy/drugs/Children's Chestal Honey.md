## Overview
**Likely duplicate-brand-under-two-names discrepancy:** this file (Children's Cough category, 2% share) and `drugs/Boiron Children's Chestal Honey.md` (Children's Homeopathic Cough Products category, 15% share) almost certainly refer to the **same real-world product** — Boiron Children's Chestal Honey Cough Syrup — split across two Pharmacy Times category source files under slightly different naming (with/without the "Boiron" manufacturer prefix). Confirmed via manufacturer/DailyMed labeling: a **homeopathic** combination (Antimonium Tartaricum 6C HPUS, Bryonia 3C HPUS, Coccus Cacti 3C HPUS, Drosera 3C HPUS, Ipecacuanha 3C HPUS), with real honey among the inactive ingredients (citric acid, honey, purified water, sodium benzoate, sucrose), labeled for ages 2 and up. This mirrors the exact same apostrophe/naming-split pattern independently found for Hyland's 4 Kids Cough Syrup in this same research pass.
