## Overview
**Real, significant category-placement discrepancy found here:** despite being tracked in this database's "Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements" and "Cholesterol Management" categories (implying fish oil), MegaRed's core product line is confirmed to be **krill oil**, not fish oil — a genuinely different raw material (Antarctic krill, a small crustacean, versus oily fish), though both deliver EPA/DHA omega-3s. Krill oil's omega-3s are bound in phospholipid form, which the brand and some independent sourcing describe as more efficiently absorbed than the triglyceride/ethyl-ester forms typical of standard fish oil — a real, chemically distinct delivery mechanism, though this pass did not find independent (non-industry) confirmation of a clinically meaningful absorption advantage. MegaRed's own cited clinical study (a 2010 trial on 300mg MegaRed improving "omega-3 index" in 30 days) was **conducted by Aker Biomarine — a krill oil raw-material supplier, not an independent research body** — a real, confirmed industry-funding conflict-of-interest worth flagging, consistent with this pass's broader pattern of supplement brands citing manufacturer/supplier-funded rather than independent trials. MegaRed is manufactured by Schiff Nutrition, part of Reckitt (RB Health) since 2012. Cholesterol Management (7%) and Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements (3%) are covered by [[omega-3-fish-oil]] — that page's fish-oil-specific evidence may not directly transfer to krill oil given the different raw material, a real evidence-applicability gap this pass could not resolve without a dedicated krill-oil evidence page.
