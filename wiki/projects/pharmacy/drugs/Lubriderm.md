## Overview

Ingredients confirmed via product-ingredient sources: mineral oil, glycerin, dimethicone, cetyl/cetearyl alcohol, panthenol (vitamin B5), and standard emulsifier/preservative ingredients — a cosmetic occlusive/humectant moisturizer formulation, mechanistically similar to the general moisturizer functional categories documented on [[moisturizers-ceramides]] (occlusive/humectant/emollient), but with **no declared FDA active ingredient**, unlike [[AmLactin]] (ammonium lactate) elsewhere in this same subcategory.

No independent Lubriderm-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass.

## Pros

- Contains panthenol (vitamin B5), a generally well-tolerated humectant/skin-conditioning ingredient
- Standard, unremarkable formulation avoids exotic-ingredient irritation risks

## Cons
