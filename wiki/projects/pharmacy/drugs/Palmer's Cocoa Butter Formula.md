## Overview
Confirmed via product-ingredient sources: **Palmer's Cocoa Butter Formula** (the body-lotion/cream base line, distinct from the "Skin Therapy Oil with Vitamin E" product already researched — see [[Palmer's Cocoa Butter Skin Therapy Oil with Vitamin E]]) is a **cosmetic moisturizing lotion, not an FDA-regulated drug**. Cocoa butter (theobroma cacao seed butter) is the marketed key ingredient, alongside petrolatum, dimethicone, mineral oil, glycerin, and other standard cosmetic emollients — but no Drug Facts panel or declared active ingredient was found for this specific lotion SKU. Marketing claims focus on "48-hour deep hydration" and helping "smooth and blend unattractive marks and scars," which are cosmetic/moisturizing claims rather than a confirmed FDA drug claim.

This is now the **third Palmer's product researched in this database's Topicals pass**, each a cosmetic: the Lip Balms SPF stick (dimethicone/octinoxate/oxybenzone, a genuine sunscreen drug for that specific SKU — see [[Palmer's]]), the Skin Therapy Oil (cosmetic, [[Palmer's Cocoa Butter Skin Therapy Oil with Vitamin E]]), and this body-lotion formula (also cosmetic).

No independent Palmer's Cocoa Butter Formula-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass.
