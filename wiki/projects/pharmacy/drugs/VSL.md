## Overview
"VSL" refers to VSL#3, a high-potency, 8-strain probiotic combination (4 Lactobacillus strains, 3 Bifidobacterium strains, 1 Streptococcus thermophilus strain) with a genuinely strong, real, disease-specific clinical evidence base — one of the best-evidenced products found across this entire Probiotic Dietary Supplements category despite its smallest (1%) tracked share. A multicenter, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial (adults with mild-to-moderate ulcerative colitis, 3.6×10¹² CFU twice daily, 12 weeks) found **32.5% of VSL#3 patients achieved >50% improvement in UCDAI score versus 10% on placebo** — a real, substantial, statistically meaningful effect for an actual diagnosed medical condition, not a general wellness claim. Separate trials found VSL#3 reduced abdominal pain/bloating in both adult IBS-like symptoms (in UC remission) and pediatric IBS populations. This dose (3.6×10¹² to 450 billion CFU) is dramatically higher than most competitors in this category (Culturelle, Florajen, OLLY Probiotic all use CFU counts in the billions or hundreds of millions, several orders of magnitude lower).
