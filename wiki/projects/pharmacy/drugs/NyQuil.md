## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: NyQuil Cold and Flu = **acetaminophen + dextromethorphan HBr + doxylamine succinate** (liquid/capsule); NyQuil Severe Cold and Flu adds **phenylephrine HCl**. This confirms and corrects the earlier hedge, which guessed a pure dextromethorphan basis from the "Cough Suppressants" category placement — the actual core formula is a three-to-four-ingredient combination, not a single-ingredient cough product. Touches [[acetaminophen]], [[dextromethorphan]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]] (Severe SKU); doxylamine has no dedicated ingredient page in this database (same gap as diphenhydramine — both are sedating antihistamines used as sleep aids in nighttime combination products). Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/nyquil/dailymed-nyquil.md`.

## Pros
- The defining nighttime feature — doxylamine succinate — provides genuine sedative/sleep-aid support during illness, addressing a real symptom (difficulty sleeping while sick) that daytime-only products don't
- Broad symptom coverage: pain/fever, cough, congestion (Severe SKU), and sleep support in one product
- Long-established brand recognition and dosing familiarity

## Cons
- Doxylamine succinate has no dedicated ingredient page in this database — same research gap already flagged on the Benadryl and Theraflu brand pages for diphenhydramine; a future `ingredients/doxylamine.md` (or a combined first-generation-antihistamines page covering diphenhydramine, doxylamine, chlorpheniramine, and triprolidine together) would resolve this gap across several brands at once rather than one at a time
- Marked drowsiness is the intended effect, not a side effect to manage — should not be used before driving or during daytime activity, unlike its DayQuil counterpart
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication and dextromethorphan-MAOI-interaction risks apply, same as other combination products in this batch

## Use Cases
Nighttime multi-symptom cold/flu relief: fever, body aches, cough, nasal congestion (Severe SKU), and sleep support during illness.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. May cause marked drowsiness — avoid alcohol, do not drive or operate machinery. Ask a doctor before use with glaucoma, breathing problems, or enlarged prostate (antihistamine cautions); high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, or diabetes (Severe SKU's phenylephrine).

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, nasal congestion (Severe SKU), sleeplessness during illness.
