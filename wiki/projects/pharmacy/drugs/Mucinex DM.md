## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Mucinex DM (extended-release) = **guaifenesin 600mg + dextromethorphan HBr 30mg** per tablet; Maximum Strength = guaifenesin 1200mg + dextromethorphan HBr 60mg. This upgrades the earlier correct-but-unverified pointer to a full confirmation, touching both [[guaifenesin]] and [[dextromethorphan]] at specific, now-known doses. Note the guaifenesin dose here (600-1200mg) matches the same brand's plain-tablet doses already confirmed on [[guaifenesin]] (including the Bennett 2015 1200mg RCT) — meaning that specific trial's evidence plausibly does transfer to the Maximum Strength DM combination's guaifenesin component, more directly than it does to lower-guaifenesin-dose competitors like DayQuil (200mg) discussed elsewhere in this batch.

## Pros
- Extended-release 12-hour dosing (like plain Mucinex) reduces pill burden vs. immediate-release combination syrups
- The guaifenesin dose in the Maximum Strength variant (1200mg) matches the exact dose tested in the Bennett 2015 brand-specific RCT on [[guaifenesin]] — a stronger evidentiary link than most other guaifenesin-combination products in this batch can claim
- Addresses both unproductive cough (dextromethorphan) and mucus thinning (guaifenesin) in one tablet

## Cons
- Inherits dextromethorphan's full risk profile (MAOI interaction, misuse potential, and the "low-to-moderate" subjective-benefit evidence grade noted on [[dextromethorphan]]) on top of guaifenesin's generally mild profile
- Must track total daily guaifenesin (2,400mg/day ceiling) if combining with any other guaifenesin-containing product, including plain Mucinex
- Not for children <12y per the Mucinex label

## Use Cases
Cough suppression combined with chest congestion/mucus thinning in one product.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not for children <12y. Do not use with MAOIs. Do not exceed 2,400mg/day guaifenesin across all guaifenesin products combined.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, chest congestion.

No discrepancy with [[guaifenesin]] or [[dextromethorphan]] — doses match standard OTC monograph amounts already reflected on both ingredient pages, and the Maximum Strength dose directly matches the ingredient page's own cited RCT dose for guaifenesin specifically.
