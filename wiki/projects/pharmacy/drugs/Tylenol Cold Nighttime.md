## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tylenol Cold Nighttime family (e.g. Nighttime Cold and Flu Relief capsule) = **acetaminophen 325mg + dextromethorphan HBr 15mg + doxylamine succinate 6.25mg**; a "Nighttime Severe" variant adds phenylephrine HCl. This completes the earlier partial hedge ("built around acetaminophen... other actives not verified") — now confirmed as dextromethorphan + doxylamine (± phenylephrine in the Severe SKU). Touches [[acetaminophen]] and [[dextromethorphan]]; doxylamine remains an unaddressed ingredient-page gap in this database (recurring across NyQuil, Coricidin HBP Nighttime, Robitussin Nighttime, and this page). Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/tylenol-cold-nighttime/dailymed-tylenol-cold-nighttime.md`.

## Pros
- Same acetaminophen dose (325mg) as the daytime Tylenol Cold product, making total-daily-dose tracking straightforward if a user takes both a day and night Tylenol Cold product on the same day
- Doxylamine at a standard, well-established sleep-aid-adjunct dose (6.25mg) for nighttime symptom relief
- Part of a coordinated Day/Night product family designed for straightforward AM/PM switching without duplicate active ingredients beyond acetaminophen itself

## Cons
- If a user combines this with a *different* Tylenol daytime product with a higher acetaminophen dose (e.g. Extra Strength at 500mg/caplet) rather than the matched Tylenol Cold Daytime (325mg), the combined daily total could approach the 4,000mg ceiling faster than expected — worth being explicit about since "Tylenol" branding alone doesn't guarantee matched acetaminophen doses across its whole product range
- Doxylamine carries the same anticholinergic/sedation profile discussed for diphenhydramine on the Benadryl page — no dedicated ingredient page exists to check this brand's warnings against
- Severe SKU's phenylephrine carries the same OTC-efficacy-questioned status noted elsewhere in this batch (see [[oral-phenylephrine]])

## Use Cases
Nighttime relief of fever, body aches, cough, sleeplessness during a cold (± nasal congestion in the Severe SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. May cause marked drowsiness — avoid alcohol, do not drive. Ask a doctor before use with glaucoma, breathing problems, enlarged prostate, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, or diabetes (Severe SKU).

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, sleeplessness during illness, nasal congestion (Severe SKU).
