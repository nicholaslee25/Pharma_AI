## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Vicks Sinex Severe (Original/Moisturizing/VapoCOOL) Ultra Fine Mist = **oxymetazoline HCl 0.05%** — same active ingredient/concentration as Afrin, Dristan, and Mucinex Nasal Spray. This is a new confirmation touching [[oxymetazoline]] directly; no prior pointer existed. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/vicks-sinex/dailymed-vicks-sinex.md`.

## Pros
- "Ultra Fine Mist" delivery may distribute the spray more evenly across the nasal mucosa than a coarser spray, a plausible comfort/coverage advantage though not independently tested against other delivery formats in this pass
- Moisturizing variant adds humectant ingredients potentially reducing the dryness/irritation some users experience with decongestant sprays — not confirmed against a specific comparative study here
- Same well-established oxymetazoline efficacy as the category leader

## Cons
- Full oxymetazoline rebound-congestion risk applies identically — the 3-day limit is non-negotiable regardless of mist fineness or added moisturizers
- VapoCOOL variant adds menthol/cooling agents for sensory effect, not additional decongestant efficacy — worth being clear that the "severe"/"VapoCOOL" naming reflects formulation additives, not a stronger oxymetazoline dose

## Use Cases
Short-term (≤3 days) relief of nasal/sinus congestion.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not exceed 3 consecutive days of use. Ask a doctor before use with heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease, or enlarged prostate.

## Symptoms Treated
Nasal congestion, sinus congestion/pressure.
