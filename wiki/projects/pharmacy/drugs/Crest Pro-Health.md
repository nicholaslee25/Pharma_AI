## Appears In
Therapeutic Mouthwashes/Oral Rinses - 8%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Crest Pro-Health's mouthwash/rinse and toothpaste lines both use **stannous fluoride** as their active ingredient (delivered as stannous chloride providing the stannous fluoride active) — helping prevent cavities, prevent gingivitis, interfere with plaque's harmful effects, and control plaque bacteria, per the label's stated purposes. This upgrades [[stannous-fluoride]]'s prior "plausibly stannous-fluoride-based... not individually confirmed" placeholder to an independently confirmed finding via DailyMed. Crest markets multiple Pro-Health sub-lines (Clean Mint, Gum Detoxify Deep Clean, Maximum Cavity Protection, Advanced Antibacterial Protection) — all confirmed stannous fluoride based per the DailyMed listings located, a more internally consistent brand than several others in this category.

## Pros
- Carries stannous fluoride's real, two-independent-systematic-review evidence base for gingivitis/plaque reduction (Paraskevas 2006, Johannsen 2019, both documented on [[stannous-fluoride]]), plus fluoride's own separate strong anticaries evidence
- Multi-benefit formulation (anticavity + antigingivitis + antiplaque) in a single active ingredient, rather than requiring separate products for each benefit
- Confirmed consistent stannous-fluoride basis across multiple Pro-Health sub-lines, unlike Parodontax (where the toothpaste is stannous fluoride but the separate mouthwash is CPC — see `drugs/Parodontax.md`)

## Cons
- Per [[stannous-fluoride]]'s own evidence summary, both underlying systematic reviews flag substantial trial heterogeneity and call for better-quality RCTs — "moderate," not "strong," is the honest evidence characterization despite the multi-benefit marketing
- Tooth staining is a real, documented tradeoff of stannous fluoride (per Paraskevas 2006's finding of a notable staining increase vs. sodium fluoride), a genuine cosmetic downside for long-term daily use
- No brand-specific (as opposed to general stannous-fluoride ingredient-class) clinical trial evidence was identified for Crest Pro-Health specifically

## Use Cases
Cavity prevention, gingivitis prevention/reduction, and plaque control via stannous fluoride, across both toothpaste and mouthwash formats.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard fluoride precautions (do not swallow, supervise children, discontinue and consult a dentist if irritation occurs) per [[fluoride-toothpaste]]; stannous-fluoride-specific staining tradeoff per [[stannous-fluoride]] is cosmetic, not a safety warning.

## Symptoms Treated
Cavities, gingivitis (gum inflammation/bleeding), and plaque accumulation — a genuinely multi-purpose formulation within one active ingredient.
