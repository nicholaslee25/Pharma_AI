## Overview
Alli is genuinely distinct from every other product in this database's Diet Aids category: it is **the only FDA-approved OTC weight-loss drug** (60mg orlistat, an OTC-strength version of the 120mg prescription drug Xenical), confirmed via DailyMed label, approved for adults 18+ with BMI ≥25. This is a real, FDA-reviewed drug with an actual mechanism (inhibits intestinal fat absorption, blocking roughly 25% of dietary fat from being absorbed and digested) and real clinical trial support: most users lost 5-10 lbs over 6 months, with over 40% of users combining it with a calorie-restricted diet and exercise losing ≥5% of body weight within a year — a modest but real, FDA-reviewed effect size, unlike the unsupported or contested claims found for several competitors in this category. A real, recent safety update is directly relevant: **the FDA approved a 2026 label change specifically adding a warning for acute kidney injury/kidney stones**, a rare but confirmed orlistat side effect. Diet Aids (31% share) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database — orlistat's drug-class mechanism (lipase inhibitor) is distinct from every other ingredient class covered elsewhere.
