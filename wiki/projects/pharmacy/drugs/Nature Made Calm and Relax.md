## Overview
Nature Made Calm & Relax is confirmed to combine **ashwagandha and magnesium** — category-leading in this database's Mood Health Supplements listing (24% share). See [[mood-health-supplements]] for the category-wide evidence context: ashwagandha (particularly the well-studied KSM-66 branded extract, not confirmed to be the specific extract used here) has real supporting literature for "occasional stress" response, and magnesium has its own separate evidence base (see [[magnesium]]) though not specifically for mood/stress as its strongest-evidenced use case (migraine prevention is [[magnesium]]'s best-evidenced indication). No combination-specific RCT testing this exact ashwagandha + magnesium formula was located in this pass. Mood Health Supplements (24%) is covered by [[mood-health-supplements]].

## Pros
- Backed by [[Nature Made]]'s broader USP-Verified quality infrastructure (see that page), with the caveat the mark's coverage of this specific SKU was not confirmed
- Ashwagandha has real, independently-cited literature supporting a "healthy response to occasional stress" — a reasonable match to this product's positioning
- Magnesium has real ingredient-level evidence for several indications (though mood/stress-relief specifically is not its strongest-evidenced use per [[magnesium]])

## Cons
- No combination-specific clinical trial was located testing this exact ashwagandha + magnesium formula as a unit
- This pass did not confirm which specific ashwagandha extract/standardization is used, unlike the well-documented "KSM-66" branded extract confirmed for [[Nature's Bounty Anxiety & Stress Relief]]
- Per [[mood-health-supplements]], this product targets general "occasional stress," a milder and less rigorously studied population than the clinical depression/anxiety populations most of this category's strongest evidence (St. John's Wort, SAM-e) was generated in — not itself a discrepancy, but an important use-case distinction
- Shares [[Nature Made]]'s two real, recent brand-level negative findings (a Salmonella/Staph-testing-protocol recall and an active prenatal-line phthalate/BPA lawsuit) as general brand context, though neither is confirmed specific to this SKU
