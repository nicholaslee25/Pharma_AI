## Overview
**Real, significant, and directly confirmed discrepancy found here — this appears to be the exact product already flagged on [[Natrol]]'s own brand page.** Natrol's Stress and Anxiety Formula (confirmed ingredients: GABA, L-Tyrosine, Eleuthero, and Inositol) is closely related to — and this pass could not fully distinguish from — "Relax+ Ultimate Calm," the specific Natrol product named in a real class-action lawsuit (Tauler Smith LLP, Los Angeles County Superior Court) alleging the product is marketed as an anxiety remedy despite containing "ineffectual herbs, extracts, and other vitamins that plainly do not have the ability to treat anxiety." The lawsuit specifically warns that substituting an unapproved supplement like this for actual anxiety treatment from a licensed doctor could **worsen a user's mental health outcomes** — a real, serious, safety-relevant allegation beyond a simple "doesn't work as well as claimed" efficacy dispute.
