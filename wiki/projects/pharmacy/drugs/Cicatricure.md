## Overview
**A genuine "one brand, mixed regulatory status" finding.** Cicatricure (distributed by Genomma Lab USA Inc.) sells two very different product lines. Its **facial skincare/anti-aging line** (Cicatricure Facial Day with SPF, Gold Lift SPF 30, Aqua Defense Day, Brightening with SPF, Advanced Anti-Wrinkle Therapy) genuinely **is** FDA-regulated — these are listed on DailyMed as "HUMAN OTC DRUG LABEL" products with real chemical sunscreen actives (avobenzone, octinoxate, octisalate, octocrylene, oxybenzone, homosalate in varying combinations). But the specific product plausibly driving this brand's **Scar Treatments** share — **Cicatricure Advanced Scar Gel (Face & Body)** — is **not** one of those DailyMed-listed drug products. Its ingredient list (water, glycerin, carbomer, chamomilla recutita flower extract, allium cepa/onion bulb extract, centella asiatica extract, aloe barbadensis leaf extract, allantoin, bergamot fruit oil) is marketed as a **cosmetic**, with no confirmed Drug Facts panel or declared active ingredient specific to scar treatment.

This means Cicatricure's Scar Treatments-category share is most plausibly driven by a cosmetic product, even though the same brand name also carries several genuine FDA OTC sunscreen drugs in its adjacent facial-skincare line — a real, brand-level regulatory split this database has not previously documented, distinct from Mederma's cosmetic-device split because here one sub-line is a confirmed real drug and the other (the scar-relevant one) is not.

No independent, non-company-sponsored Cicatricure Scar Gel clinical trial was located; the "proven results in 2 weeks" marketing claim is not evaluated here as independent clinical evidence.
