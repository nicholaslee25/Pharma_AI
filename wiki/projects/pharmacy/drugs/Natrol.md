## Overview
This is the generic "Natrol" house-brand line (Herbal Supplement Brand, 4% share) — distinct from the brand's own named product-SKU pages already tracked separately in this database ([[Natrol Melatonin]], [[Natrol Cognium]], [[Natrol Omega-3 Fish Oil]], [[Natrol Stress & Anxiety]]). Natrol states it uses NSF International as a third-party annual auditor of its manufacturing facilities/processes against GMP standards, manufactures in an NSF/ANSI 455-2 GMP-certified facility, and sells at least one USP-Verified melatonin SKU — real, checkable quality claims, though this pass did not independently confirm the audit results themselves (only that NSF/USP relationships are claimed and, for the USP mark specifically, that it is a real third-party program). Set against that, Natrol carries a genuinely more active class-action litigation history than most brands reviewed in this pass. Herbal Supplement Brand itself has no dedicated ingredient evidence page in this database — single-herb efficacy varies enormously by which specific herbal product is sold under this generic 4%-share listing, which this pass could not resolve to a specific product.

## Pros
- Real, checkable third-party manufacturing oversight claimed (NSF-audited GMP facility, NSF/ANSI 455-2 certification) and at least one USP-Verified SKU (melatonin) — a real positive quality signal, though scoped to specific products rather than the whole brand
- Only one minor Class II recall found (a 2021 mislabeled-lot market withdrawal of ALA 300mg capsules, not a contamination or safety event)

## Cons
