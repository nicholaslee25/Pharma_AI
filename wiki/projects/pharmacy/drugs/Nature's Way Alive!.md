## Overview
**Real, confirmed, safety-relevant discrepancy found here — this is the specific product already flagged on [[Nature's Way]]'s own brand page.** A class-action lawsuit (Dass v. Nature's Way Products LLC, Case No. 1:20-cv-03906, S.D.N.Y., filed May 2020) alleges that **Nature's Way Alive! Once Daily Men's Ultra Potency Multivitamin contains "notable" amounts of gelatin not disclosed on the product label** — a real, confirmed undisclosed-ingredient allegation, not merely a marketing-language dispute. The plaintiff specifically alleged this caused real harm as a Hindu whose religious practice prohibits gelatin consumption, and that he paid a premium believing the product did not contain animal-derived gelatin. Separately, the National Advertising Division (NAD) advised Nature's Way in 2014 to change certain "Alive!" vitamin marketing claims — a second, independent instance of the brand's marketing being challenged, though for different (non-gelatin) claims. Multivitamins (3% share) is covered by [[multivitamins]].
