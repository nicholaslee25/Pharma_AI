## Overview
The Doctor's NightGuard is marketed as dentist-designed, using a patented two-layer construction (a soft top layer to cushion/absorb grinding force, and a firmer bottom layer to resist grinding/bite-through) plus a patented slanted-front profile intended to keep material away from the lips and gums for comfort. It is a boil-and-bite product that custom-molds to the wearer's bite within minutes, BPA-free, and reusable for approximately 6 months — materially similar in format and lifespan to Oral-B's entry in this same category. As a physical protective device, no active ingredient or pharmacological evidence applies.

## Pros
- Two-layer soft/firm construction is a specific engineering approach (shared conceptually with DenTek's Professional-Fit tier) intended to balance comfort with protection
- Dentist-designed positioning and slanted-front profile aim to address common night-guard comfort complaints (bulkiness, gum/lip irritation)
- BPA-free, similar reusable lifespan (~6 months) to the category-leading Oral-B product
