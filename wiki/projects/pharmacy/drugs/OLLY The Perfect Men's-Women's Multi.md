## Overview
This is OLLY's gummy multivitamin line. **This pass found a real, brand-wide (not product-specific) pattern of class-action litigation over unsupported marketing claims across multiple OLLY products**, relevant context for this specific SKU even though neither located suit concerns the Multivitamin line by name: (1) a lawsuit alleging OLLY's Metabolism Gummy Rings (apple cider vinegar-based) contain only 2.6% acetic acid — far below the 4% FDA Compliance Policy Guideline threshold for a product to be labeled "apple cider vinegar" — while being nearly half added sugar by weight; and (2) a separate 2022 class-action lawsuit alleging OLLY inaccurately dosed some melatonin-containing supplements (directly relevant to this database's separately-tracked [[OLLY Immunity Sleep]] page). No OLLY-Multivitamin-specific clinical trial or lawsuit was independently located, but this brand-wide pattern of specific-claim/dosing litigation is worth weighing.
