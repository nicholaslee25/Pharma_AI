## Overview
Irwin Naturals Ginkgo Smart uses ginkgo biloba as its primary active ingredient — see [[Nature's Bounty Ginkgo Biloba]] for the shared ingredient-level evidence: a 2026 Cochrane review (82 RCTs, 10,613 participants) found ginkgo has little-to-no benefit for mild cognitive impairment or subjective memory complaints, but may offer modest, short-term benefit specifically in diagnosed dementia. See [[Irwin Naturals]] for the parent brand's own separately-documented, real, and serious findings: a $2.65 million California Prop 65 settlement over lead contamination found at up to 10-14x the legal limit in other named Irwin Naturals products (System Six, Green Tea Fat Meltdown/Burner — not confirmed to involve this specific ginkgo product), and a separate pending class action alleging the "Naturals" brand name is misleading given synthetic ingredient content. Neither finding is confirmed specific to this exact SKU, but both are relevant brand-wide context. Memory Support (2% share, smallest tracked) is covered by [[brain-health-nootropics]] and shares the ginkgo evidence base with [[Nature's Bounty Ginkgo Biloba]].
