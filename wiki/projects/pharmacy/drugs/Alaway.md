## Overview
Confirmed: Alaway's (Bausch + Lomb) own FDA/DailyMed label lists **ketotifen fumarate 0.25 mg/mL (0.025%)** as its active ingredient — essentially the same drug and concentration as [[SYSTANE ZADITOR]] (Alcon's branded ketotifen) and generic ketotifen products, first FDA-approved 2006. Per [[ophthalmic-antihistamines]]'s cited meta-analysis, ketotifen shows comparable itch relief to olopatadine ([[Pataday]]) but somewhat higher hyperemia (redness) scores. No Alaway-brand-specific clinical trial beyond general ketotifen pharmacology was identified.

## Pros
- Comparable itch-relief efficacy to olopatadine ([[Pataday]]) per the cited meta-analysis
- Long, well-established market history (approved 2006) for the ketotifen active
- Essentially interchangeable with [[SYSTANE ZADITOR]] at the ingredient/concentration level, giving consumers brand choice without a meaningful efficacy tradeoff

## Cons
- Twice-daily dosing (every 8-12h) is less convenient than Pataday's once-daily options
- Per the cited meta-analysis, somewhat higher hyperemia (redness) than olopatadine, though itch relief is comparable
- No brand-specific outcome trial beyond the general ketotifen-vs-olopatadine class comparison exists

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of itchy eyes due to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander in adults and children 3+.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not use if allergic to ketotifen or benzalkonium chloride (a listed inactive ingredient); keep out of reach of children (contact Poison Control if swallowed).

## Symptoms Treated
Itchy eyes from seasonal/environmental allergens.
