# DenTek Canker Relief Canker Sore Patches

## Appears In
Canker Sore Treatments - 9%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (labeled brand name "Canker Cover," marketed to consumers as DenTek Canker Relief): active ingredient is **menthol 2.5mg** in an extended-release adhesive patch — a **materially different mechanism** from every benzocaine-based competitor in this category (Orajel, Anbesol, Kank-A). Rather than a topical gel/liquid rubbed onto the sore, this is a small patch that forms a "clear, gel-like bandage" over the sore within ~30 minutes, combining a physical barrier/protectant function with menthol's mild analgesic/cooling effect. DenTek's own consumer-facing product page describes the patch functioning "similar to a bandage on skin" and does not itself display a Drug Facts panel — the DailyMed-registered "Canker Cover" label is the actual FDA-facing OTC drug documentation for this product line.

## Pros
- Menthol-based patch avoids the benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk documented on [[topical-anesthetics]] entirely — a genuinely differentiated, lower-systemic-risk mechanism within this category
- Physical barrier/bandage function may reduce further mechanical irritation of the sore (e.g., from teeth, food) in a way a gel or liquid cannot
- Adhesive patch delivers several hours of low-maintenance protection versus needing to reapply gel multiple times daily

## Cons
- No brand-specific or menthol-patch-specific clinical trial evidence was identified for canker sore healing or pain relief — the "up to 3 times daily" dosing and barrier-formation claims are drawn from the product's own label/marketing rather than independent trial data
- Menthol's analgesic effect is generally understood to be milder/shorter-acting than benzocaine's true local anesthesia — a plausible pain-relief tradeoff for the safety benefit, not independently quantified here
- Small patch size (historically ~12mm diameter) may not adequately cover larger or awkwardly-located sores
- Age restriction: labeled for adults and children 5 years and older, narrower than some benzocaine competitors' 2+ threshold

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of pain associated with canker sores and mouth sores via an adhesive protective patch, for ages 5 and up.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: apply up to 3 times a day as needed; general OTC caution to discontinue and consult a dentist/doctor if the sore persists or worsens. No benzocaine-related methemoglobinemia warning applies to this menthol-only formulation.

## Symptoms Treated
Canker sore pain, via a combination physical-barrier/mild-analgesic mechanism rather than true local anesthesia.
