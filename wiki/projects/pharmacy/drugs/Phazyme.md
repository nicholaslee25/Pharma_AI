## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Phazyme is simethicone-based, consistent with [[simethicone]]'s general drug-class evidence — but spans a real, quantified 4x-plus dose spread across its own SKU line: **Ultra Strength (180mg)** and **Ultimate (500mg)**, alongside other real-market Phazyme strengths reported elsewhere (e.g. 125mg). This is a more extreme version of the one-brand-multiple-formulations pattern already flagged for Pepcid and Gelusil in this database's Gastrointestinal section — here spanning roughly a 4x concentration range (125mg to 500mg) under one brand name, a wider spread than most other multi-SKU brands checked in this pass.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA labels for both Ultra Strength and Ultimate formulations; genuinely simethicone-based, matching [[simethicone]]'s drug-class evidence
- Multiple strength tiers allow matching dose to symptom severity, similar in concept to Desitin's tiered system and Rolaids' multi-strength antacid line

## Cons
- **This database's category-mapping data ("Phazyme," 4% share) does not specify which strength tier (125mg/180mg/500mg — a 4x range) the recommendation share refers to** — the widest confirmed concentration spread among the multi-SKU brands checked in this Gastrointestinal research pass
- Per [[simethicone]]'s own Efficacy Evidence: the positive evidence base is a single old (1974), small (n=41) trial — applies equally regardless of which Phazyme strength is used, since higher simethicone doses have not been shown in this database's reviewed evidence to produce proportionally better outcomes
- No Phazyme-brand-specific RCT beyond the general simethicone evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases

## Allergies / Warnings
