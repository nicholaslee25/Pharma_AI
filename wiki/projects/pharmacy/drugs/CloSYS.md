## Overview
**Discrepancy flagged:** CloSYS markets itself around a patented "Cloralstan" **stabilized chlorine dioxide** formula, positioned as the brand's core identity and technology. However, per FDA OTC monograph convention, chlorine dioxide is not itself a recognized anticavity/antigingivitis "active ingredient" — on at least one confirmed SKU, **CloSYS Healthy Teeth Mouthwash**, the FDA Drug Facts panel lists **sodium fluoride 0.02%** as the active ingredient, with Cloralstan/stabilized chlorine dioxide relegated to the INACTIVE ingredients list. This means the ingredient CloSYS markets as its defining technology is, on its own FDA label, not the officially recognized active ingredient — an unusual, brand-specific version of the marketing-vs-FDA-label mismatch this database has documented elsewhere. Independent of the FDA-label technicality, a published RCT (Pham et al. 2018, Clinical and Experimental Dental Research) found a 0.1% chlorine dioxide mouthwash reduced oral malodor in a randomized, double-blind, 2-week crossover design (N=39) versus a saline control — real supporting evidence for chlorine dioxide's malodor-reduction mechanism specifically, even though it isn't the FDA "active ingredient" on the fluoride-containing CloSYS SKU.

## Pros
- The Healthy Teeth SKU carries fluoride's genuine, Cochrane-backed anticaries evidence base per [[fluoride-toothpaste]], regardless of the chlorine-dioxide-labeling nuance
- Independent published RCT evidence (Pham 2018) supports chlorine dioxide's oral-malodor-reduction mechanism specifically, a real if narrow evidence base not available for several competitors' flagship ingredients
- pH-balanced, alcohol-free positioning (per product marketing) may suit users seeking a gentler daily rinse than alcohol-containing alternatives like classic Listerine

## Cons
- The FDA-label-vs-marketing mismatch (chlorine dioxide as "the CloSYS technology" vs. fluoride as the actual labeled active ingredient on at least one SKU) could mislead a consumer into believing chlorine dioxide is FDA-recognized as this product's primary active mechanism
- CloSYS's product-line composition varies by SKU (some formulations may emphasize chlorine dioxide differently) — this database did not independently verify every SKU's Drug Facts panel, so the fluoride/chlorine-dioxide relationship documented here may not generalize to every CloSYS product
- The supporting chlorine-dioxide RCT (N=39) is modest in scale relative to the 32-trial, 5,000+-participant evidence base documented for Listerine's essential oils, or the 42,300-child Cochrane fluoride meta-analysis

## Use Cases
Cavity prevention (fluoride, the FDA-recognized active ingredient on the Healthy Teeth SKU) and oral malodor reduction (chlorine dioxide, independently trial-supported but not the FDA "active ingredient" designation on that same SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard fluoride precautions per [[fluoride-toothpaste]] (do not swallow, supervise children, discontinue if irritation occurs) for the Healthy Teeth SKU.

## Symptoms Treated
Cavity prevention (fluoride) and oral malodor (chlorine dioxide) — two distinct benefits from two differently-labeled ingredients within one product.
