## Overview
**Major finding, see [[camphor-menthol-eucalyptus]] for full category-wide detail:** confirmed via manufacturer labeling, Zarbee's Baby Soothing Chest Rub is **menthol-free and camphor-free** — a beeswax/shea-butter/castor-oil balm carrying eucalyptus and lavender essential oils, explicitly labeled "these statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration." This is NOT an FDA-regulated OTC drug and has no active ingredient of any kind, despite being the #2 ranked, 16%-share brand in a Pharmacy Times category ("Children's Topical Cough Suppressants, Ointments") whose #1 brand (Vicks VapoRub) and #3 brand (Mentholatum Children's Nighttime Vaporizing Rub) are genuine camphor/menthol/eucalyptus combination drugs. At least one labeled variant contains wintergreen leaf oil (methyl salicylate) — a real, separate salicylate-toxicity consideration if ingested, distinct from camphor toxicity.

## Pros
- Explicitly formulated without camphor and menthol, avoiding the real camphor-toxicity risk documented on [[camphor-menthol-eucalyptus]] that makes genuine vapor-rub drugs unsafe for children under 2 — this product is labeled safe from 2 months+
- Petroleum-free, simple essential-oil/wax-based formulation with a long safety track record for topical infant use in the general cosmetic-balm category
- No known drug-drug interaction risk given no active pharmaceutical ingredient

## Cons
- **Not an FDA-regulated drug — no active ingredient, no Drug Facts panel, no FDA efficacy review of any kind**, despite being ranked in a category alongside genuine OTC drugs at a higher recommendation share (16%) than the genuine-drug Mentholatum product (12%). A caregiver assuming "top-3 in this drug category" implies comparable regulatory status across all three products would be mistaken.
- No RCT or clinical efficacy evidence located for this specific essential-oil combination in infant cough/congestion relief — unlike Vicks VapoRub, which has at least one real (if industry-funded) pediatric RCT behind its camphor/menthol/eucalyptus formulation (see [[camphor-menthol-eucalyptus]])
- Contains wintergreen leaf oil (methyl salicylate) in at least one variant — a real toxicity risk if ingested in quantity, separate from and in addition to the standard "keep out of reach of children" caution
- Essential oils (lavender, eucalyptus) carry their own uncommon but real contact-sensitization/allergy risk, not addressed by an FDA-reviewed allergen framework the way major-allergen labeling is

## Use Cases
Marketed for soothing/comforting sensory relief of cold-related discomfort in infants too young for camphor-containing vapor rubs (ages 2 months+) — not a treatment for cough or congestion via any established pharmacological mechanism.

## Allergies / Warnings
For external use only; not for use on face, broken skin, or inside nostrils; not for babies under 2 months. Contains essential oils (lavender, eucalyptus, and wintergreen in some variants) that carry uncommon contact-sensitization risk. Methyl salicylate (wintergreen oil), if present, is toxic if ingested in quantity — keep out of reach of children.

## Symptoms Treated
No established pharmacological treatment of any specific symptom — marketed for general sensory/comfort relief during a cold, mechanistically unproven for actual cough or congestion relief.
