## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Advil Migraine is **solubilized ibuprofen equal to 200 mg ibuprofen (NSAID)**, purpose "pain reliever" — resolving this page's prior hedge into a confirmed finding, matching [[ibuprofen]]. Notably, the label's stated **Use is "Treats migraine"** verbatim, with migraine-specific warnings (consult a doctor for undiagnosed migraines or headaches differing from the usual pattern; medication-overuse-headache warning if used 10+ days/month). **Discrepancy/tension flagged:** Excedrin Migraine's own marketing states it is "the only OTC medication with FDA approval for the treatment of migraine" (per `drugs/Excedrin Migraine.md`), yet Advil Migraine's own FDA-reviewed Drug Facts label independently states "Treats migraine" as a Use. This database did not independently resolve the precise regulatory-pathway distinction (e.g., whether this reflects an OTC ibuprofen monograph allowance for a migraine-use claim vs. Excedrin's specific New Drug Application-level migraine indication) — both facts are presented here rather than silently reconciled, since they appear to be in tension.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established NSAID active ingredient (ibuprofen), backed by [[ibuprofen]]'s large-scale Cochrane meta-analytic evidence
- Solubilized/liquid-gel ibuprofen formulation, the same formulation category flagged favorably in the Derry 2009 Cochrane review's "soluble ibuprofen salts produced better efficacy estimates" finding (per `drugs/Advil.md`)
- Label includes genuinely useful migraine-specific guidance (medication-overuse-headache warning, doctor consultation for atypical/undiagnosed migraine presentations) beyond generic pain-reliever labeling

## Cons
- **Regulatory-claim tension, not independently resolved:** Excedrin Migraine markets itself as the sole FDA-approved OTC migraine medication, while Advil Migraine's own label separately states "Treats migraine" — flagged as an apparent discrepancy in how migraine-specific claims are made across brands, worth independent verification before treating either claim as the complete picture
- Carries the full ibuprofen risk profile documented on [[ibuprofen]]: boxed warnings for cardiovascular thrombotic events and GI bleeding, contraindicated perioperatively for CABG, aspirin-interaction timing issue
- No independent, ibuprofen-specific migraine RCT (comparable to Excedrin's dedicated Lipton 1998 trial) was identified in this pass — Advil Migraine's migraine claim relies on the general ibuprofen analgesic evidence base plus its own label's regulatory standing, not a dedicated migraine-specific trial located here
- Label explicitly warns against use 10+ days/month due to medication-overuse-headache risk — a genuine, easy-to-overlook limitation for frequent migraine sufferers who might reach for an OTC product too often

## Use Cases
