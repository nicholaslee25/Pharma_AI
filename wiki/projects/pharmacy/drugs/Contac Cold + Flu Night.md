## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (Contac Cold and Flu Night tablet): **acetaminophen 500mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg + chlorpheniramine maleate** (the antihistamine is added at night relative to the Day formula, which is acetaminophen + phenylephrine only — see `drugs/Contac Cold + Flu Day.md`). A related "Contac Cold and Flu" capsule uses dextromethorphan + doxylamine instead — a separate SKU. This is a new ingredient confirmation touching [[acetaminophen]] and [[oral-phenylephrine]]; chlorpheniramine (first-generation antihistamine) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database (same recurring gap as diphenhydramine/doxylamine/triprolidine). Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/contac-cold-flu-night/dailymed-contac-cold-flu-night.md`.

## Pros
- Directly complements the Day formula: same acetaminophen/phenylephrine base, with chlorpheniramine added specifically at night for drowsiness/allergy-symptom relief — a coherent day/night design
- Chlorpheniramine's antihistamine action may help dry a runny nose alongside the phenylephrine's vasoconstrictive decongestant effect — two different mechanisms targeting nasal symptoms simultaneously

## Cons
- Like other Contac products in this batch, does not contain a cough suppressant or expectorant — a consumer expecting broad "cold and flu" cough relief would need a separate product, same limitation flagged on the Day version
- Chlorpheniramine carries sedation and anticholinergic risk similar to the other first-generation antihistamines discussed throughout this batch (Benadryl, doxylamine-containing nighttime products) — no ingredient page exists to check dosing/warning specifics against
- Same oral-phenylephrine efficacy caveat as elsewhere: an FDA advisory panel questioned standard-dose OTC phenylephrine's efficacy in 2023
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication risk applies, worth watching if switching between the Day and Night Contac products on the same day (both use 500mg per caplet, so combined tracking is at least straightforward)

## Use Cases
Nighttime relief of fever, headache, minor aches/pains, nasal/sinus congestion, sore throat, and allergy-type symptoms (via chlorpheniramine) with expected drowsiness.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. May cause marked drowsiness — avoid alcohol, do not drive. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, diabetes, glaucoma, breathing problems, or enlarged prostate.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, headache, minor aches/pains, nasal congestion, sinus pressure, sore throat, allergy-type symptoms, sleeplessness (secondary to antihistamine drowsiness).
