## Overview
Confirmed: Tears Naturale's (Alcon) own FDA label (DailyMed) lists **dextran 70 0.1%, glycerin 0.2%, and hypromellose 0.3%** as active ingredients, using the POLYQUAD (polyquaternium-1) 0.001% preservative system. **This is the identical active-ingredient formulation to [[GenTeal Tears]] Moderate** (same manufacturer, Alcon) — GenTeal Tears was in fact the rebranding of the older "Tears Naturale Forte" product line, and current sources indicate the two names now largely refer to the same underlying formulation family sold under different product-line branding. Per [[artificial-tears]]'s cited Semp et al. 2023 review, this three-ingredient combination formulation sits in the favorably-evidenced "combination formulation" category, though hypromellose-based tears specifically underperformed PEG-containing tears like [[Systane]] in that same review. No Tears Naturale-brand-specific clinical trial beyond the shared dextran/glycerin/hypromellose evidence already discussed on [[artificial-tears]] and [[GenTeal Tears]] was identified.

## Pros
- Combination formulation (dextran 70 + glycerin + hypromellose) aligns with the formulation category the ingredient page's cited review found to outperform single-ingredient tears
- Long market history under the Alcon name (originally "Tears Naturale Forte"), reflecting an established, well-characterized product
- Identical, well-documented active-ingredient profile to sibling product [[GenTeal Tears]] Moderate — a consumer switching between the two names can expect consistent formulation

## Cons
- Hypromellose-based formulations were found in the cited systematic review to underperform PEG-containing tears like Systane in comparative outcomes
- **Direct formulation and manufacturer overlap with [[GenTeal Tears]]** means these are not truly independent product choices, despite being tracked as separate brands in this database's Pharmacy Times category data — a real "two brand names, one formulation" situation worth flagging for anyone comparing options
- Smaller/tied recommendation share (5%, tied with GenTeal Tears and Biotrue) — reflects a niche/legacy position relative to category leaders

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of burning, irritation, and discomfort due to dryness of the eye or exposure to wind or sun.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; discontinue and see a doctor for eye pain, vision changes, continued redness/irritation, or symptoms persisting beyond 72 hours.

## Symptoms Treated
Dry eye burning, irritation, discomfort from wind/sun exposure or reduced tear production.
