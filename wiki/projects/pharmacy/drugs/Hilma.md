## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed: Hilma's "Dairy and Gluten Digestive Enzymes" product genuinely contains lactase (correctly targeting lactose, unlike Beano researched separately in this pass) — but it is a **multi-enzyme combination product**, not a pure lactase supplement like Lactaid: confirmed ingredients also include lipase, a "Gluten Digestion Blend" (bacterial protease, acid-stable protease, peptidase), dandelion root, curcuminoids (turmeric extract), black pepper, and inulin. Lactase is one component among at least eight others. Manufacturer's own label explicitly states the product is **not intended for those with Celiac Disease or gluten allergies** — an important, self-disclosed limitation given the product's "Gluten Digestion" marketing name could otherwise be misread as making it safe for celiac disease.

## Pros
- Genuinely contains lactase, mechanistically correct for the Lactose Intolerance Products category (unlike Beano)
- Manufacturer proactively discloses the product is not for Celiac Disease or gluten allergy — a responsible label choice given how the "Gluten Digestion" marketing name could otherwise be misread
- Vegan, non-GMO, gluten-free formulation itself (per manufacturer), dye-free

## Cons
- **Lactase is only one of at least nine listed ingredients** — a consumer specifically seeking lactase for lactose intolerance is also taking several other enzymes/botanicals (dandelion root, curcuminoids, black pepper, inulin) with their own separate evidence bases not reviewed in this pass, none of which are documented on [[lactase]] or elsewhere in this database
- No FDA Drug Facts label or DailyMed entry located — as a dietary supplement, the FDA does not verify this product's label accurately reflects its actual ingredient content (a general dietary-supplement caveat, not specific evidence of a discrepancy here)
- No dose/potency (FCC units or equivalent) for the lactase component specifically was confirmed in this pass, unlike Lactaid's confirmed 4,500/9,000 FCC-unit strengths — a real gap in comparing this product's lactase-equivalent dose to competitors
- Gluten-digesting enzyme blends generally have limited independent evidence for meaningfully reducing gluten exposure risk in celiac disease or significant gluten sensitivity — the manufacturer's own "not for Celiac Disease" disclaimer is consistent with this, but a consumer with milder self-reported gluten sensitivity (not celiac) might still overestimate what enzyme supplementation can safely enable them to eat
- No Hilma-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Marketed for dairy AND gluten digestive support together — a broader combination use case than Lactaid's lactose-intolerance-only focus, per manufacturer.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Manufacturer explicitly states: not intended for those with Celiac Disease or gluten allergies** — a real, important limitation given the product's gluten-digestion marketing angle. No FDA-mandated Drug Facts warnings located (dietary supplement).

## Symptoms Treated
Dairy-related digestive discomfort (via lactase) and general gluten-digestion support (via the protease/peptidase blend, NOT a celiac-safe claim) — a broader but less-targeted use case than Lactaid.
