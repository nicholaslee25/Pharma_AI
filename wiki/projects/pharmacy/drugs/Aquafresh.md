## Appears In
Toothpaste, General Use - 6%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: **Aquafresh Cavity Protection** (the flagship line most plausibly driving this 6% General Use share) lists **sodium fluoride 0.25% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)** as its active ingredient, purpose "anticavity agent" — aids in the prevention of dental cavities via twice-daily brushing, a standard [[fluoride-toothpaste]] formulation. Other Aquafresh sub-lines (Extreme Clean, Whitening Intense White, Extra Fresh Plus Whitening, Multi Action Whitening, Ultimate White) are also confirmed sodium fluoride pastes per DailyMed — a more internally consistent core lineup than Crest or Colgate's split between sodium/stannous fluoride chemistries. **Brand-family note:** a separate Aquafresh Sensitive Maximum Strength product exists (potassium nitrate + sodium fluoride, the same combination as Sensodyne/Colgate Sensitive), but it does not appear in this database's Toothpaste, Sensitive Gums/Teeth subcategory data, so it is noted here for completeness rather than researched as a separate brand page.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established anticavity active ingredient (sodium fluoride), and — unlike Crest or Colgate — the core Aquafresh general-use lineup appears consistently sodium-fluoride-based across the sub-lines checked, rather than splitting across multiple fluoride chemistries
- Long-standing brand (marketed since 1973, currently under Haleon, formerly GSK/SmithKline Beecham) with wide market recognition

## Cons
- No brand-specific (as opposed to general fluoride ingredient-class) clinical trial evidence was identified for Aquafresh Cavity Protection specifically — searches surfaced only general antibacterial/odor marketing claims (e.g., a GSK-cited "kills 99.9% of odor-causing bacteria" figure for Extreme Clean) that are unrelated to cavity-prevention efficacy and were not independently verified in this pass
- Per [[fluoride-toothpaste]]'s own caveat, effect size depends on brushing frequency/technique/supervision — brand choice alone does not guarantee the ingredient-class benefit
- Aquafresh's own product family also includes a potassium-nitrate-based Sensitive Maximum Strength line not reflected in this database's General Use category data — a reminder that "Aquafresh" alone doesn't specify formulation without checking the specific SKU

## Use Cases
Cavity prevention via daily brushing (sodium fluoride, Cavity Protection and related whitening/multi-action lines).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: discontinue use if irritation develops; keep away from children under 6; contact Poison Control or seek medical assistance if more than the brushing amount is swallowed. Standard fluoride precautions per [[fluoride-toothpaste]] (pea-sized amount for ages 3–6, supervise brushing, dentist consultation under age 2).

## Symptoms Treated
Cavity prevention (all sodium-fluoride Aquafresh general-use lines). Does not address gum sensitivity under the core Cavity Protection formulation — see the separate Sensitive Maximum Strength SKU for that benefit.
