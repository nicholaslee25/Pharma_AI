## Overview
**Discrepancy flagged — non-drug product recommended in a "Headache Products" category:** Be Koool gel sheets (marketed by Kobayashi Americas, including "Migraine & Fever Cooling Gel Sheets" and "Kids Fever Cooling Gel Sheets") are confirmed **non-medicated** — the product is water, a polymer, menthol, and trace preservatives, with **no FDA-recognized active ingredient**, no Drug Facts panel, and no antipyretic or analgesic drug mechanism. The cooling sensation comes from evaporative physical cooling of the water-based gel plus menthol's cooling sensory effect on skin nerve endings — a real, physical sensation, but not a pharmacological fever reduction or pain relief in the way acetaminophen/ibuprofen/aspirin work. This is a genuine instance of this database's recurring "non-drug product miscategorized/recommended alongside drug products" pattern: Be Koool sits in this database's `drugs/` folder and in the Pharmacy Times "Headache Products" recommendation-share data at 4%, but it is a cosmetic/device-class comfort product, not a medication.

## Pros
- Genuinely safe to use alongside any medication, precisely because it has no active pharmaceutical ingredient and no drug-drug interaction potential
- Provides real, immediate physical cooling comfort (via evaporation) that many people find subjectively soothing during headache/fever/migraine discomfort, independent of any pharmacological effect
- No risk of the hepatotoxicity (acetaminophen), GI-bleed (NSAIDs), or other systemic drug risks documented for every other brand in this Headache Products category

## Cons
- **Provides no actual antipyretic or analgesic drug effect** — a caregiver using Be Koool in place of (rather than alongside) an actual fever-reducing medication for a genuine fever could under-treat the underlying condition; this product manages comfort/sensation, not the fever or headache mechanism itself
- No clinical trial evidence exists (or would be expected to exist) for this product's effect on headache/fever OUTCOMES, since it makes no such FDA-recognized therapeutic claim — its inclusion in a pharmacist-recommendation dataset for "Headache Products" should be understood as a comfort-adjunct recommendation, not an efficacy-equivalent one to the actual drug products in the same category
- Labeling as "fever reducing" or "migraine relief" gel sheets, without any FDA-regulated active ingredient behind those terms, risks a consumer over-interpreting the cooling sensation as equivalent to genuine fever reduction

## Use Cases
Comfort/cooling adjunct for headache, migraine, or fever discomfort — a physical/sensory measure, not a substitute for an actual antipyretic (e.g., acetaminophen, ibuprofen) when true fever reduction is medically needed.

## Allergies / Warnings
No FDA Drug Facts warnings apply, since this is a non-medicated product. General caution: menthol-containing topical products can occasionally cause skin irritation in sensitive individuals.

## Symptoms Treated
Subjective cooling comfort associated with headache, migraine, or fever — not a treatment for the underlying fever or pain mechanism itself.
