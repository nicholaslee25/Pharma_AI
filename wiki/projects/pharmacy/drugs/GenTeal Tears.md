## Appears In
Artificial Tears/Ophthalmic Lubricants - 5%

## Overview
Confirmed: GenTeal Tears' (Alcon) own FDA OTC Drug Facts labels (DailyMed) show the active ingredients vary by sub-formulation — **GenTeal Tears Moderate**: dextran 70 0.1%, glycerin 0.2%, hypromellose 0.3% (a three-ingredient combination); **GenTeal Tears Severe**: hypromellose 0.3% alone (a gel-viscosity single-ingredient formula). Per [[artificial-tears]]'s cited Semp et al. 2023 review, combination formulations outperformed single-ingredient ones, and HPMC/hypromellose-based tears specifically underperformed PEG-containing tears like [[Systane]] in the underlying comparative evidence — placing GenTeal's Moderate (combination) formula in a better relative position than its own Severe (single-ingredient HPMC) formula, and both somewhat behind PEG-based competitors per that same review. GenTeal Tears was formerly marketed as "Tears Naturale Forte" before rebranding; the current [[Tears Naturale]] product line (also tracked separately in this database) shares the identical dextran 70/glycerin/hypromellose formulation and manufacturer (Alcon) — worth noting as effectively sibling products under the same corporate formulation, not independent competitors. No GenTeal-brand-specific clinical trial beyond the general dextran/glycerin/hypromellose-ingredient evidence already on [[artificial-tears]] was identified.

## Pros
- Dextran 70/glycerin/hypromellose combination formulation (Moderate strength) aligns with the combination-formulation category the ingredient page's cited review found to outperform single-ingredient tears
- Multiple viscosity tiers (Moderate liquid drops vs. Severe gel) let users match product thickness to symptom severity — a real, verifiable product-line design choice
- Manufactured by Alcon, an established ophthalmic pharmaceutical company with a long track record in this specific product category (also makes [[Systane]] and [[Tears Naturale]])

## Cons
- Hypromellose (HPMC)-based formulations, including GenTeal's, were found in the cited systematic review to underperform PEG-containing tears like Systane in comparative outcomes — a real, source-supported formulation-tier distinction
- The Severe (gel) formula is single-ingredient hypromellose only, without the combination-formulation advantage of the Moderate liquid product — a meaningful within-brand formulation difference a consumer should be aware of when choosing between GenTeal sub-products
- Direct formulation overlap with sibling brand [[Tears Naturale]] (same manufacturer, same three actives) means the two are not truly independent choices despite being tracked as separate Pharmacy Times brands

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of burning, irritation, and discomfort due to dryness of the eye or exposure to wind or sun; gel formula (Severe) intended for more significant dryness given its higher viscosity.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; discontinue and see a doctor for eye pain, vision changes, continued redness/irritation, or symptoms persisting beyond 72 hours.

## Symptoms Treated
Dry eye burning, irritation, discomfort from wind/sun exposure or reduced tear production, across a range of severity via different viscosity sub-products.
