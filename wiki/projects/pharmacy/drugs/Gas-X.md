## Pros

## Cons
- Per [[simethicone]]'s own Efficacy Evidence: the positive evidence base is a single old (1974), small (n=41) trial — a genuinely thin evidence base for a 72%-recommended, category-dominant product
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Mechanism section: does not reduce actual gas production, only bubble surface tension — cannot address gas increases from lactose intolerance or medication side effects, a real mechanistic limitation worth setting expectations around
- Multiple strength tiers (Extra/Maximum/Ultra/Ultimate) exist under one brand name with this database's share data not specifying which — consistent with the multi-formulation-ambiguity pattern flagged on other brands
- No Gas-X-brand-specific RCT beyond the general simethicone evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of pressure, bloating, and fullness from gas — via the surfactant/bubble-coalescing mechanism detailed in [[simethicone]].

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: max 2 softgels (500mg)/24h without physician guidance for Maximum Strength. Per [[simethicone]]: simethicone allergy is the only itemized contraindication; minimal drug-interaction profile (one case report each of altered levothyroxine absorption and possible carbamazepine toxicity elevation).

## Symptoms Treated
Gas, bloating, pressure/fullness — via the mechanism described in [[simethicone]], not a treatment for the underlying cause of excess gas production.
