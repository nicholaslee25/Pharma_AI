## Overview
This category entry ("iHealRapid") matches the **iHealth COVID-19 Antigen Rapid Test** (iHealth Labs, FDA EUA) — the Pharmacy Times source table's brand name appears to be a shortened/concatenated form of "iHealth ... Rapid Test," noted here explicitly since the exact string doesn't match the manufacturer's own product name and could otherwise cause a mismatch. The manufacturer's lay-user clinical study reported **94.3% sensitivity / 98.1% specificity**, exceeding the WHO-recommended minimum guideline of ≥80% sensitivity / ≥97% specificity for antigen tests, and performed against an EUA-authorized molecular reference standard. This sensitivity figure is notably higher than the top of the brand-level range documented on [[at-home-covid-tests]] (34.3–91.3% symptomatic per the Dinnes 2022 Cochrane review) — consistent with the recurring pattern in this category of manufacturer-reported figures running above independently-derived pooled estimates (see [[BinaxNOW]] and [[CareStart]] for brands where an independent study specifically confirmed a lower real-world figure). No independent (non-manufacturer) validation study for this specific brand was located in this search.
