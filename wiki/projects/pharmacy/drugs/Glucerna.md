## Overview
Glucerna (Abbott Nutrition) is genuinely one of the most extensively and specifically clinically studied products across this entire Vitamins/Supplements research pass — sourcing states more than **50 clinical studies spanning 30 years** specific to this diabetes-focused nutritional-shake line, a real, checkable research investment well beyond what most brands in this database can claim. Real, quantified brand-specific results located: a controlled trial found Glucerna Protein Smart stimulated GLP-1 release 213% more and reduced post-meal blood glucose response by 80% over 4 hours compared to instant oatmeal; a separate study found Glucerna Hunger Smart taken twice daily was associated with a 47% reduction in post-meal peak glucose after breakfast. The product's CARBSTEADY technology (slow-release carbohydrates) is the stated mechanism, clinically linked to blunting post-meal glucose spikes — a genuinely well-differentiated, disease-specific evidence base relative to [[Ensure]] and [[Boost]]'s more general ONS-class evidence. - No independent (non-Abbott-affiliated) confirmation of these specific trial results was located in this pass — the sourcing found originates substantially from Abbott's own nutrition-research communications, a relevant transparency consideration even though the described trial designs (randomized, controlled, crossover) are methodologically reasonable
