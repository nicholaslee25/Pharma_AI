## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: both Dr. Smith's Diaper Rash Ointment and Diaper Rash Spray formulations are **zinc oxide 10%** — the lowest confirmed zinc-oxide concentration among this database's Diaper Rash Products brands (below Balmex's 11.3%), but consistent across both the ointment and spray formats, unlike several other brands in this category with real concentration splits between SKUs. **Manufacturer note:** DailyMed lists at least two different manufacturers of record for "Dr Smith's Diaper" products (Mission Pharmacal and MainPointe Pharmaceuticals) — plausibly reflecting a change of manufacturer/distributor over time for the same branded product line rather than a genuine formulation difference, though this database did not independently confirm whether both manufacturers' versions are identical in concentration.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts labels for both ointment and spray formats at a single, consistent 10% zinc-oxide concentration — no cross-SKU concentration ambiguity, a positive contrast with Desitin/Boudreaux's/Triple Paste's multi-tier splits
- Spray format offers a no-touch/no-rub application option similar in concept to Boogie Diaper Rash Spray, potentially reducing friction/discomfort on already-irritated skin
- Zinc oxide's barrier-protectant mechanism matches [[zinc-oxide]]'s general evidence base

## Cons
- Lowest confirmed zinc-oxide concentration among this category's brands checked in this pass (10%) — not necessarily inferior (concentration-dependent efficacy comparisons across brands were not established in [[zinc-oxide]]'s evidence review), but a real quantified difference worth noting
- This database's category-mapping data ("Dr Smith's Diaper Ointment/Spray," 5% share) combines both formats into one line item, so the recommendation share cannot be attributed to ointment vs. spray specifically
- Per [[zinc-oxide]]'s own evidence review: overall evidence quality for zinc-oxide barrier preparations is graded low-level (Grade C)
- No Dr. Smith's-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general zinc-oxide evidence base
- Multiple listed manufacturers of record (Mission Pharmacal, MainPointe Pharmaceuticals) were not independently reconciled to confirm identical formulation across both

## Use Cases
Prevention and treatment of diaper rash (irritant diaper dermatitis) via 10% zinc-oxide barrier protection, in either ointment or no-touch spray format.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for topical use only; avoid eye contact; if symptoms persist beyond 7 days, consult a healthcare provider. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control immediately if ingested.

## Symptoms Treated
