## Appears In
Infant Gas Products - 18%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Little Remedies Gas Relief Drops = **simethicone 20mg per 0.3mL** dose — an essentially identical active-ingredient dose and dosing schedule (0.3mL under 2 years/24 lbs, 0.6mL over, max 12 doses daily) to category-leading Infants' Mylicon Gas Relief, confirmed elsewhere in this database. Like Mylicon, the label's Uses section addresses gas discomfort from air-swallowing or diet only — **colic is not mentioned anywhere**, maintaining the same correctly-scoped indication and avoiding the colic-efficacy gap flagged on [[simethicone]]. Free of alcohol, artificial flavors, and artificial dyes (including Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6) per manufacturer labeling.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label matching Mylicon's dose and dosing schedule essentially exactly — a caregiver switching between these two top category brands would see no meaningful active-ingredient difference
- Correctly-scoped "gas" labeling with no colic claim, same positive finding as Mylicon
- Explicitly free of artificial flavors and dyes (including the specific dyes Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6) — a more detailed dye-disclosure than Mylicon's general "dye-free" labeling
- Labeled safe for newborns specifically, per manufacturer marketing

## Cons
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Efficacy Evidence: the ingredient's supporting positive trial tested adult gas/bloating symptoms (1974, n=41), not infants — this database has not located an infant-specific efficacy RCT for either this brand or Mylicon
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Mechanism section: does not reduce actual gas production, only bubble physics — a general ingredient-class limitation
- No Little-Remedies-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general simethicone evidence base
- Essentially a therapeutic equivalent to the category-leading Mylicon at the same dose — this database found no differentiating clinical advantage for either brand over the other

## Use Cases
Relief of infant gas discomfort due to excessive air swallowing or certain formulas/foods, newborns and up.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: keep out of reach of children. Per [[simethicone]]'s Contraindications: simethicone allergy. Free of alcohol, artificial flavors, and specific artificial dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5, Yellow 6) per manufacturer labeling.

## Symptoms Treated
Infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or diet — NOT labeled or evidence-supported for infant colic specifically.
