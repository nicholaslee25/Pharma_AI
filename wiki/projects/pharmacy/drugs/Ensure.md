## Overview
Ensure (Abbott Nutrition) is the category-dominant brand in this database's Nutritional Supplements listing (52% share) — an oral nutritional supplement (ONS) meal-replacement/calorie-and-protein-fortification drink, primarily marketed toward older adults and patients at risk of malnutrition. This pass located real, independent (non-Ensure-specific) randomized controlled trial evidence for the broader ONS product class: RCTs in malnourished older adults found real, statistically significant improvements in body weight, handgrip strength, knee-extension strength, gait speed, and activity/functional scores versus water/no-supplement controls, using a similar caloric dose (~200mL, ~247kcal/serving) to a standard Ensure serving. This pass did not fully isolate Ensure-brand-specific trial data from this broader ONS-class literature (some cited trials used "Ensure Plus" or unspecified branded ONS products), but the mechanism (calorie/protein supplementation in malnourished older adults) has real, independent RCT support at a category level. Nutritional Supplements (52%) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database, since this product class (multi-nutrient meal-replacement) does not map to a single active ingredient.

## Pros
- Real, independent RCT evidence exists for the ONS product class (including at least one trial using "Ensure Plus" specifically) showing genuine functional/strength/nutritional-status improvements in malnourished older adults — not merely a marketing claim
- Category-dominant, long-established brand (Abbott) with broad clinical/institutional adoption (hospitals, nursing homes)
- Offers formulation variants (e.g., Ensure Enlive, higher-calorie/nutrient-density options) for different degrees of nutritional need

## Cons
- This pass could not fully isolate a large-scale, brand-specific ("Ensure" by name) RCT from the broader ONS-class literature — some cited trials tested "Ensure Plus" or generic branded ONS products rather than confirming the exact core "Ensure" formulation
- Best-established evidence is specifically for malnourished/at-risk older adults — using this product as a general "nutritional supplement" for a non-malnourished, healthy individual is a different, less-evidenced use case than what the cited trials actually studied
- Contains added sugars in standard formulations — a real consideration for diabetic users, who are more appropriately served by [[Glucerna]] (Abbott's own diabetes-specific product line) per that product's own dedicated clinical evidence

## Use Cases
Calorie/protein supplementation for malnutrition risk, particularly in older adults — see Overview for the cited independent RCT evidence at the ONS-class level.

## Allergies / Warnings
Contains dairy and soy in most formulations — check the specific product's allergen statement. Standard (non-Glucerna) formulations contain meaningful added sugar, relevant for diabetic users.

## Symptoms Treated
Malnutrition risk/unintended weight loss, particularly in older adults — not a general wellness product per its strongest evidence base.
