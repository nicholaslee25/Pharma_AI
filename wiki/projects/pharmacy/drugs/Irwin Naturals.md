## Overview
Irwin Naturals has a real, confirmed, and fairly serious regulatory history: it settled a **$2.65 million California unfair-business-practices/false-advertising lawsuit** brought under Proposition 65 after testing found lead in its System Six and Green Tea Fat Meltdown products at up to 10x the legal limit, and its Green Tea Fat Burner at more than 14x the legal limit — a real, quantified contamination finding, not merely a labeling dispute. The same case also alleged its Hoodia-branded products did not actually contain Hoodia gordonii (though the company noted no validated test method exists to confirm Hoodia identity in softgel form, a genuine analytical-verification gap rather than a clear-cut fraud finding). A separate class action alleges the brand's "Naturals" name/marketing is misleading because its supplements contain synthetic ingredients. Irwin Naturals settled without admitting fault, and no product recall resulted from either case. Coenzyme Q10 Supplements (3%) is covered by [[coq10]] — none of the located contamination/labeling findings are specific to the CoQ10 line itself (they concern other named product lines: System Six, Green Tea Fat Meltdown/Fat Burner, Hoodia).
