## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Metamucil is psyllium husk (approximately 3.4g per 5.8g packet) — resolving [[fiber]]'s prior hedge and confirming Metamucil as the SPECIFIC fiber type ([[fiber]]'s cited van der Schoot 2022 meta-analysis: psyllium and pectin only) shown to have significant efficacy for chronic constipation at doses >10g/day for ≥4 weeks. Category-dominant across all three categories it appears in (56% Laxatives Bulk/Fiber, 46% Fiber Supplements, 12% Cholesterol Management) — genuinely the best-evidenced brand in its category, not merely the most popular.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA label matches [[fiber]]'s meta-analysis-supported fiber type exactly — the only brand in this database's bulk-fiber categories confirmed to use one of the two fiber types (psyllium, pectin) shown significantly effective in the cited 2022 meta-analysis, alongside Konsyl (also confirmed psyllium in this same pass)
- Category-dominant share across three separate categories
- Psyllium's soluble-fiber/cholesterol-lowering mechanism provides real secondary benefit beyond constipation relief, consistent with its separate Cholesterol Management category appearance

## Cons
- Per [[fiber]]'s own Key Safety Data: flatulence was significantly HIGHER with fiber supplementation vs. control in the cited meta-analysis — a real, quantified tradeoff even for the best-evidenced fiber type
- Per label: must be taken with at least 8oz of water/fluid — inadequate fluid intake risks throat/esophagus blockage/choking, and may cause allergic reactions in psyllium-sensitive individuals (inhaled or ingested)
- Should be taken 2+ hours apart from other drugs per label — a real, easily-overlooked interaction-timing consideration for anyone on other daily medications
- No Metamucil-brand-specific RCT beyond the general psyllium/fiber evidence base was independently located in this pass; the cholesterol-management indication was not covered by [[fiber]]'s own reviewed source (scoped to constipation)

## Use Cases
Relief of occasional constipation (bowel movement in 12-72 hours per label); secondary use for cholesterol management via psyllium's soluble-fiber mechanism, per its separate category appearance.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: mix with at least 8oz water/fluid — inadequate fluid may cause choking; may cause allergic reactions in those sensitive to inhaled or ingested psyllium; take 2+ hours before/after other drugs to avoid absorption interference.

## Symptoms Treated
