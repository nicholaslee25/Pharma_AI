## Overview
This is the same product family already researched on `drugs/NyQuil.md` (Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations, Nighttime category) — confirmed via DailyMed: **acetaminophen + dextromethorphan HBr + doxylamine succinate** (Cold and Flu), or the same three plus **phenylephrine HCl** (Severe Cold and Flu). Touches [[acetaminophen]], [[dextromethorphan]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]] (Severe SKU); doxylamine remains an unaddressed ingredient-page gap in this database. See `drugs/NyQuil.md` for the full Pros/Cons treatment.

## Pros
See `drugs/NyQuil.md` — the doxylamine component provides genuine sleep-aid support during flu illness, addressing a real symptom most daytime-only flu products don't.

## Cons
See `drugs/NyQuil.md` — marked drowsiness is the intended effect (not for daytime use); standard acetaminophen-duplication and dextromethorphan-MAOI risks apply; doxylamine has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.

## Use Cases
Nighttime flu symptom relief: fever, body aches, cough, nasal congestion (Severe SKU), sleep support during illness.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. May cause marked drowsiness — avoid alcohol, do not drive.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, nasal congestion (Severe SKU), sleeplessness during illness.
