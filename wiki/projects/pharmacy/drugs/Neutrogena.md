## Overview (Sun Burn Relief appearance only)
Confirmed via DailyMed: **Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun Medicated Relief Gel** uses **camphor 0.45%** (external analgesic) as its active ingredient — a lower concentration than [[BiteMD]]'s 3% camphor. Camphor has no dedicated ingredient evidence page in this database yet (same gap flagged on BiteMD); a broader counterirritant page is anticipated when this database's research reaches Thermal Relief Products. **A separate, non-drug SKU exists under nearly identical branding:** "Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun Replenishing Lotion" contains no active drug ingredient at all (per third-party ingredient listings — water, mineral oil, glycerin, shea butter, aloe vera, mint extract; no camphor or other Drug-Facts-listed analgesic) — it is a moisturizer, not a treatment. A consumer must check for the word "Medicated" to know which "Sun Rescue After Sun" product actually contains pain-relief medication.

## Pros (Sun Burn Relief appearance)
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label for the Medicated Relief Gel SKU, with a clearly disclosed single active ingredient
- Camphor provides a genuine, recognized cooling/counterirritant analgesic effect
- Brand offers a genuine choice between a medicated option (Relief Gel) and a non-medicated moisturizing option (Replenishing Lotion) for different post-sun needs

## Cons (Sun Burn Relief appearance)
- **Discrepancy flagged:** "Sun Rescue After Sun" branding spans one drug SKU (Medicated Relief Gel, camphor 0.45%) and one non-drug cosmetic SKU (Replenishing Lotion, no active ingredient) — nearly identical naming with a real difference in whether any pain-relief medication is present at all
- Camphor lacks a dedicated evidence page in this database — its independent safety/efficacy data is not documented here yet (gap, not fabricated)
- No brand-specific clinical trial for Neutrogena's sunburn products was identified beyond the general camphor ingredient-level context
- Flammable gel formulation; standard camphor/external-analgesic label cautions apply

## Use Cases (Sun Burn Relief appearance)
Temporary relief of pain and itching from sunburn (Medicated Relief Gel); post-sun skin moisturizing without pain-relief medication (Replenishing Lotion — a different use case entirely).

## Allergies / Warnings (Sun Burn Relief appearance)
Per Medicated Relief Gel label: external use only; flammable, keep away from fire/flame; avoid eyes; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur.

## Symptoms Treated (Sun Burn Relief appearance)
Pain and itching from sunburn (Medicated Relief Gel only — the Replenishing Lotion treats no symptom, it moisturizes).
