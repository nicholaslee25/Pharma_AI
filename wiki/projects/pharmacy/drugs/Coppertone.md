## Appears In
Sunscreen - 11%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Coppertone's core sunscreen line (Complete Sunscreen, standard SPF 30/50 lotions) uses a standard chemical-filter combination — **avobenzone 3%, homosalate 9-10%, octisalate 4.5-5%, octocrylene 5-8%** (percentages vary slightly by specific SPF/SKU) — see [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]] for the ingredient-class evidence (systemic absorption data, FDA GRASE data-gap status, skin-cancer-prevention RCT evidence). Coppertone also sells mineral-filter SKUs (e.g., "Sport Mineral").

**A real, well-documented, brand-specific safety event: in 2021, Coppertone (Beiersdorf) voluntarily recalled 12 lots of five aerosol sunscreen spray products** (Pure & Simple SPF 50, Pure & Simple Kids SPF 50, Pure & Simple Baby SPF 50, Sport Mineral SPF 50, and travel-size Coppertone Sport Spray SPF 50, manufactured January-June 2021) **after internal testing detected benzene, a known human carcinogen, as an unintended manufacturing contaminant** (not a formulated ingredient). The FDA's own safety assessment stated that daily exposure to benzene at the levels detected would not be expected to cause adverse health consequences based on standard regulatory exposure modeling, and Coppertone reported no adverse-event reports tied to the affected lots. This was part of a broader, multi-brand 2021 benzene-in-sunscreen-aerosol contamination event (independent lab Valisure's testing implicated several manufacturers), not unique to Coppertone, but it is a real, confirmed, brand-specific recall this database should document.

No independent Coppertone-brand-specific clinical trial (beyond the general chemical-sunscreen-filter literature) was located in this pass.

## Pros

## Cons
- **Real 2021 benzene-contamination recall** affecting 5 aerosol spray products/12 lots — a confirmed manufacturing-quality event, not a formulation-design flaw, but still a genuine safety-relevant history for this brand's aerosol product line specifically (non-aerosol lotions were not affected)
- Chemical-filter SKUs carry the class-wide FDA GRASE data-gap and systemic-absorption findings documented on [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]
- No independent Coppertone-brand-specific clinical trial was located

## Use Cases
Broad-spectrum sunburn prevention and, with regular use and other sun-protection measures, reduced skin cancer/early-aging risk, via chemical UV filters (core line) or mineral filters (Sport Mineral SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard chemical-sunscreen-filter warnings apply (see [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]). Aerosol spray users of the specific 2021-recalled lots should check lot numbers against the official recall notice; current production is not implicated.

## Symptoms Treated
