## Overview
Mentholatum spans multiple sub-lines with genuinely different active-ingredient concentrations, confirmed via DailyMed: **Mentholatum Original** = camphor 9% + menthol 1.3% (topical analgesic only, no eucalyptus, no cough-suppressant claim — labeled for muscle/joint aches, not cough); **Vaporizing Rub** lines (Cherry, No Mess, Lavender, Nighttime, Vapor Max) = camphor 5.3% + eucalyptus oil 1.3% + menthol 2.8% (cough suppressant + topical analgesic, directly comparable to Vicks VapoRub's formula). This is a genuinely important brand-internal distinction: "Mentholatum" the plain product is NOT the same formulation as "Mentholatum Vaporizing Rub" — a consumer should check the specific product name rather than assume all Mentholatum products treat cough. Same camphor/menthol ingredient-page gap as Vicks VapoRub. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/mentholatum/dailymed-mentholatum.md`.

## Pros
- The Vaporizing Rub line's formula (camphor 5.3%/eucalyptus 1.3%/menthol 2.8%) is very similar to Vicks VapoRub's (camphor 4.8%/eucalyptus 1.2%/menthol 2.63%), giving consumers a close alternative at potentially different pricing
- Multiple scent/format variants (Cherry, Lavender, No Mess, Nighttime) for preference

## Cons
- **Real risk of consumer confusion**: "Mentholatum Original" (higher camphor 9%, no eucalyptus, no cough claim) is a fundamentally different product from "Mentholatum Vaporizing Rub" (lower camphor 5.3%, has eucalyptus, cough-suppressant claim) — someone buying "Mentholatum" for a cough could end up with the Original formula, which doesn't carry that labeled use at all
- Same ingredient-page gap as Vicks VapoRub — no formal evidence grade for camphor/menthol/eucalyptus combinations in this database
- Same camphor toxicity-if-ingested/topical-only warnings apply as with any camphor-containing product

## Use Cases
Mentholatum Original: minor muscle/joint ache relief (topical analgesic only). Vaporizing Rub line: cough due to minor throat/bronchial irritation plus topical analgesic action.

## Allergies / Warnings
For topical use only — do not take by mouth or apply in/near the nostrils (Vaporizing Rub line). Ask a doctor before use in young children.

## Symptoms Treated
Muscle/joint aches (Original); cough, chest congestion sensation (Vaporizing Rub line).
