## Overview
**Fourth instance of the same likely-duplicate product identified in this database.** Per [[elderberry]]'s existing Regulatory/Categorization Note (established during earlier Sambucol brand-page research): "Sambucol Cold and Flu Relief" quick-dissolve tablets are confirmed via DailyMed to be an **actual homeopathic combination product** — Bryonia 6X, Gelsemium Sempervirens 6X/12X/30X, Sambucus Nigra 3X/6X, Sulphur 12X, Zincum Gluconicum 2X/6X — genuinely different from Sambucol's separate concentrated elderberry-extract syrup product. This exact tablet formulation now appears to underlie **four separate brand-name renderings across four different Pharmacy Times category tables** in this database: `drugs/Sambucol.md` (Homeopathic Cold Products 17%, Homeopathic Cough Products 41%, Immune Support 4%), `drugs/Sambucol Cold & Flu Relief.md` (Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations, Daytime, 1%), `drugs/Sambucol Black Elderberry Cold & Flu Relief.md` (Homeopathic Flu Products 40%), and this page (Children's Homeopathic Cough Products 12%) — the first pediatric-category instance of this same recurring naming pattern. This does NOT mean the actual tablet is being fabricated four times over; rather, this database's underlying category source data appears to track the same real product under enough name variants that a naive sum-of-shares across categories would substantially overstate the brand's true footprint.

## Pros
- Draws on the elderberry component's own dedicated evidence page ([[elderberry]]) for context, even though the specific homeopathic dilution levels in THIS product are far more dilute than the concentrated-extract studies (Hawkins 2019, Wieland 2021) that page reviews
- No serious adverse events reported for general homeopathic cold/flu products per the Cochrane-level review on [[homeopathic-cold-products]]

## Cons
- **This is the most extensive same-product-under-multiple-names finding in this entire research pass** — four distinct brand pages, four distinct Pharmacy Times categories, all pointing to what is very likely one real-world tablet formulation. Worth flagging to database maintainers as a systemic Sambucol-brand data-quality issue, not just a one-off duplicate pair (contrast with the two-way Hyland's/Chestal Honey duplicates found earlier in this same pass)
- **The elderberry evidence base ([[elderberry]] — Hawkins 2019, Wieland 2021) studied concentrated extract products, NOT this homeopathic-dilution tablet** — a parent choosing this product on the strength of "elderberry has some cold-symptom evidence" would be relying on evidence that does not actually apply to what they're buying, since Sambucus Nigra here is diluted to 3X/6X (far below meaningful concentration) alongside four other homeopathic constituents
- Per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s Cochrane-level review: no convincing evidence of benefit beyond placebo for general homeopathic cold/flu products at these dilutions
- No pediatric-specific dosing/age-range data for this exact SKU was independently confirmed in this pass
