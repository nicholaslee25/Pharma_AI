## Appears In

## Overview
**Discrepancy flagged:** Parodontax's brand identity is built almost entirely around **stannous fluoride** (its toothpaste line — Active Gum Repair, Complete Protection, Clean Mint, etc. — is confirmed stannous fluoride paste per DailyMed, and the manufacturer's own marketing extensively explains "why parodontax uses stannous fluoride"). However, the SEPARATE **Parodontax Active Gum Health Mouthwash** — most plausibly the product driving this database's Therapeutic Mouthwashes 4% share — is confirmed via its own DailyMed label to use **cetylpyridinium chloride (CPC) 0.07% w/w**, NOT stannous fluoride. A consumer who associates "Parodontax" with stannous fluoride specifically (reasonably, given the brand's toothpaste marketing) would not get that ingredient from the mouthwash product.

## Pros
- The toothpaste line carries stannous fluoride's real, two-independent-systematic-review evidence base for gingivitis/plaque reduction, documented on [[stannous-fluoride]]
- The mouthwash line's CPC mechanism is itself a legitimate, separately evidence-backed antigingivitis/antiplaque agent (same active-ingredient class as Cepacol Antibacterial and Crest Scope Original), so the brand's overall gum-health positioning is not unfounded — it simply uses two different active ingredients across its two product types
- Using CPC (rather than stannous fluoride) in the rinse avoids introducing an additional staining-risk exposure on top of whatever the paired toothpaste already contributes

## Cons
- **Brand-identity mismatch:** Parodontax's marketing and public identity center on stannous fluoride, but its mouthwash — filed in the same "gum health" product family — uses a completely different active ingredient (CPC) with a different evidence base and different staining profile
- Per [[stannous-fluoride]]'s evidence summary, the toothpaste's stannous-fluoride benefit carries real staining tradeoffs and "moderate," not "strong," evidence quality
- No brand-specific clinical trial evidence was identified for the Parodontax mouthwash's CPC formulation specifically, beyond the general CPC ingredient-class literature

## Use Cases
Gingivitis/plaque reduction and cavity prevention via stannous fluoride (toothpaste); gingivitis/plaque reduction via cetylpyridinium chloride (mouthwash) — two different active ingredients serving a similar overall "gum health" positioning.

## Allergies / Warnings
Toothpaste: standard stannous-fluoride precautions per [[stannous-fluoride]] and [[fluoride-toothpaste]] (staining tradeoff, do not swallow, supervise children). Mouthwash: standard CPC precautions (keep from children under 6, supervise 6-11, seek medical help if swallowed beyond rinsing use).

## Symptoms Treated
Gingivitis (gum inflammation/bleeding), plaque accumulation, and (toothpaste only) cavity prevention.
