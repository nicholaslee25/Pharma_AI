## Cons

- Label may cause stomach upset (mitigated by taking with/after meals per label) and can interfere with laboratory urine tests (glucose, ketones) — a real, practical clinical-testing-interference caution worth a user's/caregiver's awareness beyond the core phenazopyridine safety profile
- No Uricalm-specific clinical trial exists distinct from the general phenazopyridine literature

## Use Cases

## Allergies / Warnings
