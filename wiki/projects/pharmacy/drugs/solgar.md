## Overview
Solgar is a legacy supplement brand (owned by Nestlé Health Science alongside [[Nature's Bounty]], per that acquisition) with broad retail availability. It holds NSF GMP facility certification (manufacturing-process oversight) but, per independent brand-audit sources, **does not currently hold finished-product third-party verification** such as USP Verified, NSF Certified for Sport, or Informed Sport — a real, confirmed gap versus [[Nature Made]]. One independent brand-scoring source rated Solgar 60/100 ("B" grade, "medium" risk) on a composite trust score. Solgar has a documented, though old (2014), Salmonella-contamination recall (ABC Dophilus Powder, a probiotic product, pulled from pharmacies/drugstores) — no recent recalls were found in this pass. Iron Supplements (1%) is covered by [[iron]]; "Letter" Vitamin Brand has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.
