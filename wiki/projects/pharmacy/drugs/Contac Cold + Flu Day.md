## Appears In
Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations, Daytime - 3%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Contac Cold + Flu Day (caplet) = **acetaminophen 500mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg**. This is a new, previously-unlinked ingredient confirmation touching [[acetaminophen]] and [[oral-phenylephrine]]. Notably simpler (2 actives) than most competitors in this category (DayQuil, Theraflu, Tylenol Cold Daytime all add dextromethorphan and/or guaifenesin) — this product does not address cough or mucus at all, only pain/fever and congestion. A separate "Contac Cold + Flu Day & Night" kit and "Contac Cold and Flu" capsule (acetaminophen + dextromethorphan + doxylamine) are distinct SKUs not covered by this specific page's category entry. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/contac-cold-flu-day/dailymed-contac-cold-flu-day.md`.

## Pros
- Simpler 2-ingredient formula may suit someone who only has pain/fever + congestion and wants to avoid unnecessary cough-suppressant or antihistamine exposure
- 500mg acetaminophen per caplet is a moderate, standard OTC single-ingredient-equivalent dose

## Cons
- Does not address cough or chest congestion at all, despite being filed in a "Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations" category — a consumer expecting broad multi-symptom relief (as the category name might imply) would need a different product or an add-on for cough
- Same oral-phenylephrine efficacy question flagged elsewhere in this batch: an FDA advisory panel concluded in 2023 that phenylephrine at standard OTC doses is not significantly more effective than placebo — see [[oral-phenylephrine]]
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication risk applies

## Use Cases
Fever, headache, minor aches and pains, nasal/sinus congestion, sore throat associated with a cold or flu — explicitly not a cough product.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, diabetes, or enlarged prostate.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, headache, minor aches/pains, nasal congestion, sinus pressure, sore throat.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
No discrepancy — acetaminophen and phenylephrine doses match standard OTC monograph amounts on their respective ingredient pages. Worth flagging that this brand's category placement is somewhat misleading given it has no cough-relief ingredient at all, unlike its category-mates.
