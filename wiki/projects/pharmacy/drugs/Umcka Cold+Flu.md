## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Umcka Cold+Flu (chewable/syrup) = **pelargonium sidoides + aconitum napellus + bryonia + eucalyptus globulus + eupatorium perfoliatum + gelsemium sempervirens + ipecacuanha + phosphorus**. **Important finding, genuinely different from Umcka ColdCare**: this is NOT the same formulation as Umcka ColdCare (which this database's other page confirmed is pelargonium sidoides 1X alone). Umcka Cold+Flu combines real pelargonium with **seven additional true homeopathic-dilution ingredients** — a materially more complex, more homeopathic-leaning product than the plain ColdCare line. This directly extends the "1X labeling discrepancy" already flagged on [[pelargonium]] for Umcka ColdCare: within the same overall Umcka brand family, formulations range from "predominantly pelargonium" to "pelargonium blended with several genuine homeopathic remedies," and a reader should not assume all Umcka products share one evidence profile. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/umcka-cold-flu/dailymed-umcka-cold-flu.md`.

## Pros
- Includes actual pelargonium sidoides, which (per [[pelargonium]]) has real placebo-controlled RCT and meta-analysis support for acute bronchitis/cold cough symptoms, unlike the purely-homeopathic products elsewhere in this batch (Boiron ColdCalm, Chestal, Hyland's Defend)
- Chewable format may suit users who prefer not to swallow tablets or use liquid syrup

## Cons
- Because seven of the eight listed ingredients are homeopathic-dilution remedies (not concentrated extracts), and the exact proportion/dose of pelargonium relative to the whole formula is unclear from the ingredient list alone, this page cannot confirm how much of [[pelargonium]]'s RCT evidence (built around specific EPs 7630 doses of 30-90mg/day) actually applies to this specific combination product — the same "practical equivalence... has not been established" gap already flagged for Umcka ColdCare applies here, arguably more acutely given the additional homeopathic dilutants
- Same class-wide caveats about the weaker, uncertain evidence for the homeopathic-dilution components specifically

## Use Cases
Marketed to temporarily relieve cold/flu symptoms per the combined pelargonium + traditional homeopathic indications of its eight ingredients.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard FDA homeopathic-product disclaimer applies. Adults/children ≥12y: 1 chewable tablet 3x daily, chewed or dissolved in mouth (not swallowed whole).

## Symptoms Treated
Cold/flu symptoms per label claim (evidence strength varies by ingredient — see Cons).
