## Overview
Botanic Choice (Indiana Botanic Gardens, Inc., Hobart, Indiana) is a genuinely long-established, family-owned herbal brand dating to 1910, BBB-accredited, and states NSF certification plus cGMP/FDA-guideline testing and re-testing. Set against that legacy, this pass found two real, documented quality incidents: a **2015 finding of lead contamination and excessive rice filler** in at least one product (per an independent brand-review source — this pass could not confirm the specific product/lot or an official recall notice for that specific finding), and a separate **voluntary recall of one lot of Botanic Choice Prune & Senna Softgels for undeclared peanuts** — a real, confirmed undisclosed-allergen finding, not merely an efficacy dispute. No update or resolution confirming the 2015 lead/filler finding was subsequently addressed was located, which the source itself flags as a transparency gap.
