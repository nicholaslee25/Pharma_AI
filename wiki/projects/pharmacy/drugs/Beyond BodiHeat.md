# Beyond BodiHeat

## Appears In
Thermal Relief Products - 8%

## Overview
Confirmed: Beyond BodiHeat (Okamoto USA) uses **activated carbon + iron powder + vermiculite** — an air-activated exothermic heat-patch chemistry, the same general category as [[ThermaCare]] and common commercial hand warmers — NOT a chemical counterirritant drug. No DailyMed listing exists, consistent with a device rather than a drug. Activates upon opening, ready within 15-30 minutes, marketed for "up to 12 hours" of heat. **A meaningful usage distinction from ThermaCare: the manufacturer explicitly instructs the pad NOT be adhered directly to skin — it is designed to be worn over clothing** — while also warning it should not be used for more than 6 hours in any 24-hour period, despite the "up to 12 hours" duration claim (a real distinction between how long the pad stays warm and how long it should actually be worn).

## Pros
- No chemical active ingredient — avoids the methyl salicylate NSAID-class systemic risks and menthol/methyl salicylate burn risk documented on [[topical-counterirritants]] for chemical-rub competitors in the same category
- Over-clothing design (rather than direct skin adhesion) may reduce certain skin-contact risks compared to direct-adhesive heat wraps, though it introduces its own distinct usage instructions
- Simple, disposable, single-use format similar to [[ThermaCare]]

## Cons
- Real burn risk exists if used incorrectly — manufacturer explicitly warns against direct skin contact and against use beyond 6 hours per 24-hour period, with removal instructions if the pad becomes too hot or causes irritation
- Not for use on infants, frostbitten/desensitized skin, during sleep, or on recent (within 48 hours) bruising/swelling — real, specific contraindications
- No brand-specific clinical trial for Beyond BodiHeat specifically was identified in this pass, unlike [[ThermaCare]] which has real, dedicated RCT evidence (Nadler et al. 2002) for a similar heat-wrap mechanism — this is a gap, not a confirmed inferiority, since heat therapy's general mechanism is shared
- FDA device classification for this specific manufacturer/product was not independently confirmed against an FDA database record in this pass (unlike ThermaCare's confirmed Class II status)

## Use Cases
Sustained topical heat therapy (worn over clothing) for sore muscles and joints, up to approximately 6 hours of active wear per use.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no pharmacological allergy profile. Device-specific warnings: do not adhere directly to skin; do not use more than 6 hours per 24-hour period; not for infants, frostbitten/desensitized skin, use during sleep, or recent bruising/swelling; remove immediately if pad becomes too hot or irritation occurs — burns can result from incorrect use.

## Symptoms Treated
Sore muscle and joint pain via sustained heat delivery, not a pharmacological mechanism.
