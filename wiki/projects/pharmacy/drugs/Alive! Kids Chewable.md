## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via Nature's Way manufacturer/retailer labeling: Alive! Children's Chewable Multivitamin (tablet format, ages 2+) contains 15 essential vitamins/minerals plus an Orchard Fruits & Garden Veggies powder blend (150mg per 2-tablet serving) and **iron 5mg as ferrous fumarate (28% DV)**. Labeled free of the 8 major FDA-recognized allergens explicitly listed (crustaceans, wheat, eggs, fish, soybean, milk, tree nuts, peanuts) — sesame (the 9th allergen added under the 2023 FASTER Act) was not confirmed as explicitly addressed in the sources reviewed. **Unlike most brands in this category, "Chewable" here is a genuinely distinguishing, non-ambiguous SKU name**: Nature's Way separately sells "Alive! Kids Multivitamin Gummy" and "Alive! Premium Kids Gummy Multivitamin" — gelatin-free, pectin-based gummy formulations, materially different from this tablet's gelatin-based binder — so the category-mapping brand name ("Alive! Kids Chewable") does pin down the correct SKU here, a positive contrast with the SmartyPants/Flintstones/OLLY ambiguity problem repeatedly flagged elsewhere in this same category.

## Pros
- Brand name in the category data specifically identifies the Chewable (tablet) SKU, avoiding the formulation-ambiguity problem repeatedly flagged for other brands in this category
- Confirmed free of 8 of the 9 major FDA-recognized food allergens per manufacturer labeling
- Contains iron (ferrous fumarate 5mg, 28% DV) — relevant for iron-deficiency-risk children, though caregivers should coordinate with a pediatrician given the "one brand, iron vs. no-iron variant" confusion already documented for Flintstones and Enfamil Poly-Vi-Sol in this same category; confirm this specific SKU's iron content before combining with other supplements

## Cons
- Gelatin-based tablet binder (not vegetarian/vegan, unlike the brand's own separately sold pectin-based gummy line) — a distinction a caregiver choosing "Alive!" by brand name alone, without noting "Chewable" specifically, could miss
- Sesame-allergen-free status not explicitly confirmed in the sources reviewed in this pass (the 8-allergen list found predates or omits the FASTER Act's 2023 sesame addition) — flagged as an information gap, not a confirmed absence of disclosure
- Iron content (5mg ferrous fumarate) means this SKU should not be combined with other iron-containing supplements without pediatric guidance, standard for any iron-containing children's multivitamin
- Per [[multivitamins]]'s general evidence review: broad disease-prevention claims for multivitamin use are not established in a large modern adult RCT; no pediatric-specific outcome trial exists for this product
- No brand-specific pediatric RCT located beyond per-nutrient ingredient-page evidence

## Use Cases
General pediatric nutrient-gap supplementation, ages 2+ (1 tablet daily ages 2-3; 2 tablets daily ages 4+).

## Allergies / Warnings
Free of crustaceans, wheat, eggs, fish, soybean, milk, tree nuts, and peanuts per manufacturer labeling; sesame status not confirmed in this pass. Contains gelatin (tablet binder). Contains iron — keep out of reach of children; accidental iron overdose from supplements is a leading cause of fatal pediatric poisoning, a standing risk for any iron-containing children's product in this database, not specific to this brand.

## Symptoms Treated
General nutrient-gap correction; not a treatment for any specific disease or symptom.
