## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer/DailyMed labeling: a **homeopathic** combination product (7 dilute constituents: Allium Cepa 6X, Hepar Sulph Calc 12X, Hydrastis 6X, Natrum Muriaticum 6X, Phosphorus 12X, Pulsatilla 6X, Sulphur 12X HPUS) carrying the standard "not evaluated by the FDA" homeopathic disclaimer. This is a real category-placement pattern already documented in [[homeopathic-cold-products]]: a homeopathic-dilution product filed under a conventional-sounding "Children's Cough/Cold Combinations" drug category rather than (or in addition to) this database's dedicated "Homeopathic ___ Products" categories — the same cross-category surfacing already noted for SunBurnt, Hyland's Migraine Relief, and MagniLife Migraine Relief on that ingredient page. Per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s general evidence review, no convincing evidence of benefit beyond placebo has been established for general homeopathic cold/cough products at these dilutions, and no product-specific RCT for this exact 7-constituent combination was located.

## Pros
- No conventional drug active ingredients means it avoids the dextromethorphan/antihistamine/decongestant risk profiles carried by every combination-drug competitor in this same category (Dimetapp, Mucinex, Robitussin, Sudafed PE, PediaCare, Delsym) — a real safety-profile difference for parents specifically avoiding those ingredient classes
- No reported serious adverse events for general homeopathic cold products per the Cochrane review on [[homeopathic-cold-products]]

## Cons
- **No convincing evidence of a real treatment effect beyond placebo** — per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s own Cochrane-level review (Hawke 2018), studies rated LOW risk of bias found no benefit from homeopathic products generally, while only HIGHER-risk-of-bias studies reported benefit — an inverse quality-vs-result pattern consistent with a null true effect
- **Filed under a conventional drug category (Children's Cough/Cold Combinations) without disclosure in this database's category table that the product is homeopathic**, not a monograph OTC drug — the same real category-transparency gap already flagged for other homeopathic products surfacing outside their dedicated categories
- The primary documented risk of relying on this product class, per [[homeopathic-cold-products]], is not direct toxicity but **delaying more effective evaluation/treatment** while using a product without an established pharmacologic mechanism
- No product-specific RCT for this exact 7-constituent combination was located — general homeopathic-class evidence (or lack thereof) is being extrapolated to this specific formulation, which the Formulation Caveat on [[homeopathic-cold-products]] explicitly cautions against

## Use Cases
Marketed for relief of watery/runny nose, hacking cough, sore throat, sneezing, and chest/nasal congestion in children, via homeopathic (not conventional pharmacologic) mechanism.
