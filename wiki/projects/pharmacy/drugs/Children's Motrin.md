## Appears In
Children's Analgesics - 25%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Children's Motrin is **ibuprofen 100 mg per 5 mL** oral suspension, alcohol-free, for ages 2-11, lasting up to 8 hours per dose — resolving this page's prior hedge into a confirmed finding, matching [[ibuprofen]]. A related "Infants' Motrin" product is a more concentrated **50 mg per 1.25 mL** drop formulation for ages 6-23 months — a genuinely different concentration for a younger age range, analogous in principle (though at a different numeric ratio) to the historical infant/children's acetaminophen dual-concentration issue documented on [[acetaminophen]], so caregivers should not assume the Infants' and Children's Motrin products are interchangeable by volume.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label, backed by [[ibuprofen]]'s general analgesic/antipyretic/anti-inflammatory evidence base
- 8-hour duration per dose (per label) means less frequent dosing than a typical 4-6 hour acetaminophen interval — a real practical advantage for overnight fever management
- Second-largest share (25%) of Children's Analgesics in this database

## Cons
- Carries ibuprofen's NSAID-class risk profile (GI, renal, cardiovascular per [[ibuprofen]]) — a different risk profile than acetaminophen-based Children's Tylenol, relevant for a caregiver choosing between the two products for a child with any of these risk factors (e.g., dehydration increasing NSAID-related AKI risk, per [[ibuprofen]])
- The Infants' Motrin (50mg/1.25mL) vs. Children's Motrin (100mg/5mL) concentration difference is a real potential source of caregiver confusion analogous to the historical acetaminophen dual-concentration problem — caregivers should always confirm which specific product/concentration they are dosing from
- Not recommended for infants under 6 months without medical guidance (standard ibuprofen pediatric convention)
- No independent brand-specific RCT (beyond the general ibuprofen ingredient-class evidence) was identified for this specific pediatric formulation in this pass

## Use Cases
Pain relief and fever reduction in children ages 2-11 (Children's), or 6-23 months (Infants' Motrin, different concentration).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label conventions matching [[ibuprofen]]'s pediatric dosing guidance (5-10 mg/kg q6-8h per StatPearls); may cause severe allergic reaction especially in aspirin-allergic children; contains an NSAID which may cause stomach bleeding; use only the enclosed dosing device.

## Symptoms Treated
Pain and fever in children ages 2-11.
