## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tylenol Cold Daytime (capsule) = **acetaminophen 325mg + dextromethorphan HBr 10mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg**; a related caplet SKU adds guaifenesin 200mg. This confirms and completes the earlier partial hedge ("built around acetaminophen... other actives not verified") — the other two actives are now confirmed as dextromethorphan and phenylephrine. Touches [[acetaminophen]], [[dextromethorphan]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]]. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/tylenol-cold-daytime/dailymed-tylenol-cold-daytime.md`.

## Pros
- Non-drowsy daytime formulation (no antihistamine), covers pain/fever, cough, and congestion in one product
- 325mg acetaminophen dose (vs. Theraflu's 650-1000mg per dose) leaves more daily-dose headroom before hitting the 4,000mg/day ceiling if a second acetaminophen product is needed
- Part of the broader Tylenol Cold + Flu family with day/night split options for those wanting sleep support at night without daytime sedation

## Cons
- Still requires the same acetaminophen-duplication vigilance as any Tylenol product — the earlier hedge's warning about "watch for duplicate acetaminophen exposure if combined with other Tylenol products" remains fully valid and is reinforced here now that the other actives are confirmed
- Oral phenylephrine's OTC efficacy has been publicly questioned (FDA advisory panel, 2023) — see [[oral-phenylephrine]]
- Dextromethorphan MAOI interaction warning applies (serotonin syndrome risk)

## Use Cases
Daytime relief of fever, headache, body aches, cough, and nasal congestion associated with a cold.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, diabetes, or enlarged prostate (phenylephrine cautions). Do not use with MAOIs (dextromethorphan interaction).

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, headache, body aches, cough, nasal/sinus congestion.
