## Overview
Nature's Bounty is owned by Nestlé Health Science, which acquired it (along with Solgar, Osteo Bi-Flex, and Puritan's Pride) as part of a $5.75B acquisition of The Bountiful Company's core brands in 2021 — a large, well-resourced manufacturer, not an independent small brand. That scale has not translated into a clean regulatory/legal record: this database found a real, confirmed pattern of recalls and deceptive-marketing enforcement actions specific to this brand. Category-specific ingredient evidence: Calcium Supplements (6%) — see [[calcium]]; the other categories (CoQ10, cranberry, garlic, iron, magnesium, omega-3/fish oil, vitamin C) are covered at the general ingredient-class level by their respective pages but without Nature's-Bounty-specific trial data. "Letter" Vitamin Brand and Herbal Supplement Brand have no dedicated ingredient page in this database. Formulation not verified per SKU.

## Pros
- Backed by a large, well-capitalized manufacturer (Nestlé Health Science) with FDA-cGMP manufacturing oversight
- Calcium Supplements line has genuine ingredient-level evidence support — see [[calcium]]

## Cons
- **Real, brand-specific recalls:** a child-resistant-packaging recall (~12,000 bottles of Nature's Bounty/Natural Wealth multivitamins containing iron, a genuine pediatric-poisoning risk given iron's known toxicity in overdose) and a separate Nestlé Health Science voluntary recall of Nature's Bounty Kids Multivitamin Gummies
- **Real, brand-specific class-action history, several concluded/settled rather than merely alleged:** (1) a 2015 slack-fill-packaging suit specifically naming Nature's Bounty Cranberry with Hibiscus, Red Krill Oil, and Q-Sorb CoQ-10 Plus — directly overlapping this brand's Cranberry Supplements and Coenzyme Q10 Supplements categories tracked here; (2) a fish-oil mislabeling suit alleging a Nature's Bounty fish oil product contained "not a single milligram" of the omega-3s claimed on its label — directly relevant to this brand's Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements category (19% share); (3) an FTC settlement over deceptive weight-loss/bodybuilding/disease-treatment claims across 26 Nature's Bounty-family nutrient supplements; (4) a 2024 FTC "review hijacking" enforcement action returning $527,000+ in refunds to Amazon purchasers of Nature's Bounty and Sundown supplements over manipulated reviews
- No Nature's-Bounty-specific clinical trial was located for any of these 11 product lines beyond the general ingredient-level evidence in the linked pages
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF Certified for Sport, or similar) was found for Nature's Bounty in this pass — a real contrast to [[Nature Made]] (same broad category set, confirmed USP Verified on 90+ SKUs)
