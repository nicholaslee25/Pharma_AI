## Overview
**Real, significant, and repeated discrepancy found here.** Focus Factor (Factor Nutrition) has a real, multi-decade history of regulatory and legal challenges to its "clinically studied"/efficacy claims: a 2004 FTC action found the company (then Creative Health Institute) made unsubstantiated claims about the product's ability to improve focus, memory, mood, and concentration; more recently, the National Advertising Division (NAD) determined the company had **insufficient evidence to support its "America's #1 clinically studied" brain-health-supplement claim**; and current litigation alleges specific ingredients are present in amounts too small to plausibly produce the claimed effects — a practice sometimes called **"fairy dusting,"** where trace amounts of an active ingredient are included primarily to justify listing it on the label rather than at a clinically meaningful dose. This is consistent with the broader, striking pattern documented on [[brain-health-nootropics]] across this category's top brands. Brain Health (8%) and Memory Support (12%) are both covered by [[brain-health-nootropics]].
