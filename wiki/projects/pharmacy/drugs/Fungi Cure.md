## Overview
This is the same Kobayashi Healthcare International brand family as [[FUNGICURE]] (tracked separately by Pharmacy Times under the Jock Itch/Antifungal Products category, 5% share), which this pass already confirmed spans **two chemically distinct antifungal actives**: **clotrimazole** (FUNGICURE Intensive/Liquid Gel/Manicure & Pedicure Liquid Gel — see [[clotrimazole]]) and a separate **undecylenic acid** sub-line (see new ingredient page [[undecylenic-acid]], built this pass). As with [[Lamisil]]/[[Lamisil AT]], this database preserves the Pharmacy Times source's separate row naming ("Fungi Cure" for Toe/Foot vs. "FUNGICURE" for Jock Itch) rather than merging them, while noting the very likely shared underlying brand/product identity.

No independent Fungi Cure-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass beyond what's already documented on [[FUNGICURE]].

## Pros
See [[FUNGICURE]] — confirmed FDA Drug Facts labels across multiple SKUs; offers both an azole (clotrimazole, well-evidenced) and a fatty-acid (undecylenic acid, older evidence base) mechanism under one brand.

## Cons
See [[FUNGICURE]] — the same "two distinct active-ingredient mechanisms sold under one brand name" pattern applies here; a consumer must check the specific product name, not just the brand, to know which active is present. Per [[clotrimazole]]: not effective for onychomycosis (nail fungus) or scalp infections. Per [[undecylenic-acid]]: the undecylenic-acid sub-line's own FDA label claim (where nail-marketed) is limited to appearance improvement, not a cure — the same real effectiveness-expectation gap flagged on [[Fungi-Nail]].

## Use Cases
Treatment of athlete's foot, jock itch, and ringworm via clotrimazole's azole antifungal mechanism, or via undecylenic acid's fatty-acid mechanism on a separate sub-line, depending on the specific product chosen.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per [[clotrimazole]] (azole sub-line): external use only; avoid eyes; azole hypersensitivity; not effective for nail/scalp infections. Per [[undecylenic-acid]] (fatty-acid sub-line): external use only; not for children under 2 without doctor guidance.

## Symptoms Treated
Itching, burning, cracking, and scaling from athlete's foot, jock itch, and ringworm — mechanism depending on the specific Fungi Cure sub-line used.
