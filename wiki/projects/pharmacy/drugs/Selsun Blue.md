## Appears In
Dandruff Shampoo - 15%

## Overview
Selsun Blue is confirmed via DailyMed as **selenium sulfide 1%**-based across its main lines — Normal to Oily, Moisturizing, 2-in-1, and Medicated — per [[selenium-sulfide]]. Purpose/uses: antidandruff, used at least twice weekly.

**Genuine within-brand exception:** a specific SKU, "Selsun Blue Itchy Dry Scalp," is confirmed via DailyMed title as **pyrithione-zinc**-based instead — a completely different active ingredient/mechanism from the rest of the Selsun Blue line, per [[pyrithione-zinc]]. This is the same "one brand name, multiple mechanisms across SKUs" pattern documented on nearly every major multi-SKU brand in this pass (e.g., [[OXY]], [[Aveeno]], [[La Roche-Posay]]) — a consumer must check the specific product, not just the "Selsun Blue" name, to know which active ingredient they're using.

## Pros
- Confirmed 1% selenium sulfide concentration matches the standard OTC-monograph strength with real (if smaller-scale) clinical support, per [[selenium-sulfide]]
- Offering both a selenium-sulfide line and a pyrithione-zinc SKU under one brand gives a consumer a same-shelf alternative mechanism if one active doesn't work well or causes irritation
- Multiple formulation sub-lines (Moisturizing, 2-in-1) address secondary hair/scalp concerns alongside the core antidandruff action

## Cons
- **Two distinct, non-interchangeable active ingredients (selenium sulfide vs. pyrithione zinc) under one brand name**, with the exception SKU ("Itchy Dry Scalp") not obviously distinguishable from the rest of the naming convention (which otherwise describes hair type/texture, not active ingredient)
- Selenium-sulfide-specific clinical evidence located in this pass (see [[selenium-sulfide]]) is smaller-scale/less independently verified than the ketoconazole and pyrithione-zinc literature elsewhere in this category
- No Selsun-Blue-brand-specific clinical trial was located for either active in this pass — all evidence is at the shared-ingredient level
- If swallowed, the label carries an explicit "contact Poison Control right away" warning — a slightly more pointed ingestion caution than seen on some other brands' labels in this category, worth noting even though ingestion risk is presumably similar across products in this class

## Use Cases
Dandruff treatment via selenium sulfide's yeast-reducing/cytostatic action (most of the line) or pyrithione zinc's antifungal action (Itchy Dry Scalp SKU specifically) — mechanism depends on which specific product is purchased.

## Allergies / Warnings
Selenium-sulfide line: for external use only, avoid eye contact, if swallowed contact Poison Control immediately, per [[selenium-sulfide]]. Itchy Dry Scalp (pyrithione zinc) SKU: standard pyrithione-zinc warnings per [[pyrithione-zinc]] — external use only, avoid eyes, stop use if condition worsens.

## Symptoms Treated
Dandruff flaking and itching — via selenium sulfide (most SKUs) or pyrithione zinc (Itchy Dry Scalp SKU).
