## Appears In
Joint Health - 7%

## Overview
Move Free Advanced adds a proprietary "Uniflex" mineral complex and a "Joint Fluid" ingredient to its glucosamine/chondroitin base, and the brand cites a 2004 Miami-based randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study claiming the combination is "2x more effective than triple strength glucosamine & chondroitin alone," with 83% of participants reporting improved joint comfort. **This pass found the cited study was independent in design description but could not confirm independent (non-manufacturer-funded) sponsorship or peer-reviewed journal publication** — brand marketing materials are the primary source located for these specific statistics, a real transparency gap. This sits against [[glucosamine-chondroitin]]'s own well-documented landmark GAIT trial finding that the base glucosamine/chondroitin combination did NOT significantly reduce pain in the general knee osteoarthritis population — meaning Move Free's "2x more effective than glucosamine & chondroitin alone" claim would need independent verification to know whether it means genuinely better than a weak/null baseline, or simply an artifact of study design/funding. Joint Health (7%) is covered by [[glucosamine-chondroitin]].

## Pros
- Adds ingredients (Uniflex mineral complex, "Joint Fluid") beyond the base glucosamine/chondroitin combination, with a specific brand-cited claim of doubled effectiveness and a 2-week onset
- Manufactured by Schiff/Reckitt (RB Health), a large, established consumer-health company

## Cons
- **The brand's key "2x more effective" and "83%/78%/72% improvement" statistics were not independently traced to a peer-reviewed, published source in this pass** — they appear to originate from brand marketing materials citing an internal/company-associated 2004 study, a real evidence-transparency gap consistent with this database's broader pattern of manufacturer-sponsored-but-not-independently-published supplement trials
- The base glucosamine/chondroitin combination these added ingredients are compared against has its own well-documented null result in the general osteoarthritis population per the landmark GAIT trial (see [[glucosamine-chondroitin]]) — "2x better than glucosamine/chondroitin alone" is a comparison against a base that independent trial evidence suggests may not itself be significantly better than placebo
- No independent, peer-reviewed RCT specifically for the complete Move Free Advanced formulation (with Uniflex and Joint Fluid) was located in this pass
