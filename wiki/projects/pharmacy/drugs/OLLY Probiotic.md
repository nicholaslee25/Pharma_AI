## Appears In
Probiotic Dietary Supplements - 3%

## Overview
OLLY Probiotic is confirmed to use **Bacillus coagulans** in a gummy format, with the standard product listed at **500 million CFU** — a real, notably lower CFU count than several competitors in this same category ([[Culturelle]] and [[Florajen]] both use billions of CFU; OLLY's own separate "Extra Strength Probiotic" product line uses 6 billion CFU with 2 strains, suggesting the standard OLLY Probiotic gummy is deliberately positioned as a lower-potency entry product). This pass found real, relevant brand-wide context (not specific to this exact SKU): OLLY has faced a real class-action lawsuit alleging its Metabolism Gummy Rings (apple cider vinegar) contain far less active ingredient than implied, and a separate 2022 lawsuit alleging inaccurate melatonin dosing in other OLLY products — a documented pattern of claims/dosing scrutiny across this brand, relevant context though neither suit concerns this Probiotic SKU by name. Probiotic Dietary Supplements (3%) is covered by [[probiotics]].

## Pros
- Gummy format may improve adherence for users who dislike swallowing capsules
- Bacillus coagulans is a spore-forming, heat/acid-stable probiotic — a real, mechanistically favorable choice for a gummy format that isn't refrigerated

## Cons
- **Real, notably low CFU count (500 million) relative to several competitors** in the same category (Culturelle, Florajen both use billions of CFU) — OLLY's own separate "Extra Strength" product uses 12x the CFU count, suggesting the standard product is a deliberately lower-potency formulation that a consumer might not realize when comparing "probiotic" products by name alone
- **Real, brand-wide pattern of litigation over specific product claims and dosing accuracy** (ACV gummy content shortfall; melatonin dosing inaccuracy in other products) — not specific to this SKU, but relevant context given the pattern
- No OLLY-Probiotic-specific clinical trial was located; efficacy evidence is at the general Bacillus coagulans/probiotic-class level, not independently evidence-graded against a dedicated systematic review in this pass
- Gummy format typically includes added sugar
