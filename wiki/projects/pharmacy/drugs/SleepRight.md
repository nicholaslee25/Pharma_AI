## Overview
SleepRight (manufactured by Splintek) offers a multi-tier night-guard line including **Select-Comfort** and **Secure-Comfort** (no-boil, ready-to-use designs), **Ultra-Comfort** (a no-boil, soft, BPA-free premium option), **Dura-Comfort** (marketed for heavy grinders, implying a more durable/rigid material), and **ProRx** (a boil-and-bite custom-fit option using 150°F water and an included fitting tray). Materials across the line are described by the manufacturer as non-leaching and free of BPA, latex, silicone, and phthalates. As a physical protective device, no active ingredient or pharmacological evidence applies.
