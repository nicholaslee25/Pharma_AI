## Appears In
Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief - 1%

## Overview
**Real, confirmed sibling-product finding:** Jarrow Formulas sells TWO different women's probiotic products under closely related names — this database's tracked "Jarro-Dophilus Women" is confirmed via manufacturer data to contain a 4-strain blend (L. crispatus LbV 88, L. jensenii LbV 116, L. gasseri LbV 150N, L. rhamnosus LbV 96, 5-10 billion CFU depending on SKU). This is a genuinely DIFFERENT product from Jarrow's own **Fem-Dophilus**, which uses the specifically well-studied Lactobacillus rhamnosus GR-1 + Limosilactobacillus reuteri RC-14 combination — the most clinically documented vaginal-health-specific probiotic strain pairing that exists (see [[probiotics]]). No brand-specific RCT for the exact 4-strain "Jarro-Dophilus Women" combination was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Named, specific strains rather than an undisclosed proprietary blend, a real transparency advantage
- Comes from a manufacturer (Jarrow) that also makes the well-studied GR-1/RC-14 combination — general company-level probiotic manufacturing experience, though this does not transfer strain-specific clinical evidence to this particular product
- Available in both shelf-stable and refrigerated CFU-potency options per manufacturer

## Cons
- **Real evidence gap: this is NOT the GR-1/RC-14 combination** with the strongest direct vaginal-health trial pedigree — a consumer choosing "Jarrow" by brand-name association with vaginal-probiotic credibility could reasonably but incorrectly assume they are getting the same evidence-backed strain combination as Fem-Dophilus
- No dedicated clinical trial for this specific 4-strain blend's effect on yeast-infection prevention/relief was located in this pass
- Smallest independently-tracked share (1%, tied with Lagicam) in this category, consistent with less available independent coverage

## Use Cases
Marketed to support vaginal and urinary tract health via probiotic supplementation — not an antifungal treatment; positioned as flora-balance support rather than active yeast-infection treatment.

## Allergies / Warnings
No brand-specific recall or lawsuit located in this pass. General probiotic-class caution applies (rare bacteremia/fungemia risk in severely immunocompromised patients, per [[probiotics]]).

## Symptoms Treated
Marketed general vaginal/urinary flora support — no strain-specific trial evidence located in this pass confirming a yeast-infection-prevention effect for this exact combination.
