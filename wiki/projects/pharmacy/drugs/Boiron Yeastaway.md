## Overview
**Confirmed via DailyMed: Boiron Yeastaway (also sold/renamed as "YeastCalm") is homeopathic, not an antifungal.** Active ingredients are Borax 14X HPUS, Calendula officinalis 1X HPUS, Candida albicans 30X HPUS, and Hydrastis canadensis (goldenseal) 5X HPUS — an isopathic formula (using an ultra-diluted preparation of the pathogen itself, Candida albicans, alongside other traditional botanicals) rather than an azole or other pharmacologically active antifungal compound. This is the same finding pattern already documented for [[AZO]]'s "Yeast Plus" product elsewhere in this category — a real, recurring theme in this database's Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief research: multiple brands market products with "yeast" directly in the name or purpose that contain no actual antifungal active ingredient.

## Pros
- Free of preservatives and parabens per manufacturer claim
- No drug-interaction profile or azole-hypersensitivity contraindication, since it contains no antifungal drug

## Cons
- **Contains no antifungal active ingredient of any kind** — per this database's standing homeopathic-product finding, "the FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective," and this product has not been FDA-evaluated for safety or efficacy
- A consumer selecting this product specifically to treat (rather than merely experience symptom relief alongside) a diagnosed yeast infection would not be receiving an antifungal treatment, in direct contrast to [[Monistat]]/[[Lagicam]]/[[Vagistat]] (all confirmed genuine azole antifungals) tracked in this same category
- No independent clinical evidence (beyond traditional homeopathic-use claims) was located in this pass supporting any of the formula's four component ingredients for actual yeast-infection resolution
