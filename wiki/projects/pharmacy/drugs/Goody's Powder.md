## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Goody's Powder is consistently a **triple combination of acetaminophen + aspirin (NSAID) + caffeine**, the same active-ingredient category as BC Max and Excedrin — but the exact doses vary notably by specific SKU: Goody's Cool Orange (acetaminophen 325mg / aspirin 500mg / caffeine 65mg), Goody's Extra Strength (acetaminophen 260mg / aspirin 520mg / caffeine 32.5mg), Goody's Max (acetaminophen 500mg / aspirin 500mg / caffeine 130mg), and Goody's Extra Strength Headache Mixed Fruit Blast (acetaminophen 325mg / aspirin 500mg / caffeine 65mg). Unlike Excedrin's family (confirmed identical across Extra Strength/Tension Headache/Migraine), **Goody's own "Extra Strength" and "Max" sub-lines are NOT identical to each other** — real dose variation exists across the Goody's product family, so "Goody's Powder" does not denote one fixed dose the way some competitor brand families do. Like BC Powder, Goody's originated in and remains particularly associated with the American South.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts labels across multiple SKUs, all built on well-evidenced ingredients (acetaminophen, aspirin, caffeine) individually documented on [[acetaminophen]] and [[aspirin]]
- Powder format offers a genuine format-level alternative to tablet/caplet competitors for consumers who prefer it
- The triple-combination rationale (analgesic + NSAID + caffeine adjuvant) mirrors Excedrin's and BC Max's structurally similar, evidence-supported combination approach (per Excedrin's dedicated Lipton 1998 migraine trial, though that trial was specific to the Excedrin dose ratio, not independently re-run for Goody's specific ratios)

## Cons
- **Real dose inconsistency across the brand's own product line:** unlike Excedrin's confirmed-identical sub-brands, Goody's Extra Strength and Goody's Max use different aspirin/acetaminophen/caffeine ratios entirely — a consumer switching between Goody's sub-products should not assume equivalent dosing from the "Extra Strength" or "Max" naming alone
- Shares BC Powder's documented category-level chronic-use safety concerns (analgesic nephropathy risk literature, caffeine dependence/rebound headache potential) — see `drugs/BC Powder.md` for the specific case-report citations found in this pass; not independently re-confirmed for Goody's-branded products specifically, but the ingredient combination and chronic-use pattern are the same
- Combines three active ingredients simultaneously (acetaminophen hepatotoxicity + aspirin GI-bleed/Reye's risk + caffeine stimulant effects), a more complex safety profile than single-ingredient products
- No independent, Goody's-specific clinical trial was identified in this pass — brand relies entirely on the general ingredient-class evidence for its three components

## Use Cases
Headache pain relief via the acetaminophen + aspirin + caffeine combination.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label conventions for this ingredient combination: severe liver damage risk with excessive use or combination with other acetaminophen-containing products (per [[acetaminophen]]); GI bleeding and Reye's syndrome risk from the aspirin component (per [[aspirin]]); limit other caffeine sources while using.

## Symptoms Treated
Headache and general minor aches/pains.
