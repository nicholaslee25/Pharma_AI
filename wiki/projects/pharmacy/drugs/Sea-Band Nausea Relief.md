## Appears In
Motion Sickness Remedies - 6%

## Overview
Same underlying acupressure wristband product already documented on `drugs/Sea-Band.md` (tracked separately in this database under Children's Motion Sickness Products 18% and Nausea Remedies 4%) — this is a third category placement (Motion Sickness Remedies, Other, 6%) for the identical device. Sea-Band is a non-drug acupressure device applying continuous pressure to the Nei-Kuan (P6) point on each wrist, with no active pharmaceutical ingredient. Per the existing Sea-Band research: published independent evidence review found most trials do not support acupressure wristbands for motion-sickness prevention, and at least one study found neither the band NOR a placebo band prevented motion sickness regardless of correct or incorrect placement — a genuinely unflattering finding for the product's own proposed physiological mechanism.

## Pros
- Zero drug-interaction risk, no anticholinergic/sedation side effects — a real, meaningful safety differentiator from every antihistamine-based competitor in this category (Dramamine, Bonine)
- Reusable, no age-based dosing math required
- No overdose or allergic-reaction risk profile, since there is no active ingredient

## Cons
- **Independent evidence is weak and inconsistent** — at least one study found the band performed no better than a placebo band regardless of correct P6 placement, directly undercutting the plausibility of the manufacturer's own claimed mechanism
- Filed under a drug-focused "Motion Sickness Remedies" category despite being a device with no active ingredient — the same non-drug-in-a-drug-category pattern already documented for this product elsewhere in this database
- No independent adult-population-specific trial with hard efficacy numbers was located distinct from the general acupressure-wristband literature already reviewed on the existing Sea-Band page

## Use Cases
Prevention/relief of motion-sickness-related nausea via acupressure (per manufacturer claim; independent evidence for actual efficacy is weak and inconsistent).

## Allergies / Warnings
No pharmacologic contraindications (no active ingredient). Latex-free per label.

## Symptoms Treated
Motion-sickness-associated nausea (per manufacturer claim; independent evidence weak).
