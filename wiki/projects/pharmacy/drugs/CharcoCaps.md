## Overview
**Missing-ingredient-page and mismatched-regulatory-status finding.** CharcoCaps' active ingredient is **activated charcoal** — no dedicated ingredient page for it exists in this database. Per FDA guidance reviewed in this pass, activated charcoal's medicinal use for gas/indigestion has **not been evaluated or approved by the FDA as safe and effective**, and its effectiveness for this specific use case is genuinely uncertain (distinct from activated charcoal's well-established, separate use as an emergency poison/overdose decontamination agent in clinical toxicology, which is a completely different context and dosing regimen than this OTC gas-relief product). **Separate, more clinically significant finding: activated charcoal is a broad-spectrum absorbent that can meaningfully reduce the absorption of other oral medications taken around the same time** — including oral contraceptives, painkillers, sedatives, and antidepressants — a real drug-interaction risk mechanism that no other brand in this Antiflatulence Products category (Gas-X, Beano, Phazyme, Imodium Multi-Symptom Relief) shares to this degree, since simethicone has zero systemic absorption and minimal interaction risk per [[simethicone]].

## Pros

## Cons
- **FDA has not evaluated or approved activated charcoal's use for gas/indigestion as safe and effective** — genuinely uncertain efficacy for this specific indication, distinct from activated charcoal's well-established (but differently-dosed) emergency-overdose-decontamination use
- **Real, clinically meaningful drug-interaction risk: activated charcoal can reduce absorption of a broad range of co-administered oral medications** (oral contraceptives, analgesics, sedatives, antidepressants), with general guidance recommending 2-3 hours separation between charcoal and other oral medications — a consumer taking CharcoCaps alongside a daily oral contraceptive or other important medication without spacing doses risks reduced efficacy of that other medication, a real safety consideration this database's other Antiflatulence Products brands do not share
