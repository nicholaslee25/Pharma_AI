## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Ecotrin sells BOTH a 325mg regular-strength AND an 81mg low-strength enteric-coated aspirin tablet under the same brand name — see [[aspirin]] for the full ingredient evidence base. Both are marketed as "safety coated" to dissolve in the small intestine rather than the stomach, aiming to reduce direct gastric-mucosa contact. This is the same one-brand-multiple-doses pattern already documented for Bayer elsewhere in this database — a consumer must confirm which specific Ecotrin strength they are using, since 325mg (general pain relief) and 81mg (cardioprotective) serve genuinely different clinical purposes.

## Pros
- Enteric coating is a real, established pharmaceutical approach intended to reduce direct gastric irritation from aspirin, though it does not eliminate aspirin's systemic GI-bleeding risk (which operates via COX-1 inhibition throughout the body, not just local stomach contact) — a real, important distinction between LOCAL gastric protection and SYSTEMIC bleeding risk
- Both dose strengths available under the same trusted, long-established brand name

## Cons
- Enteric coating can delay and reduce the reliability of drug absorption, a real pharmacokinetic tradeoff versus immediate-release aspirin — relevant for anyone needing a fast onset (e.g., during a suspected heart attack, where immediate-release, chewed aspirin is the standard emergency recommendation, not an enteric-coated tablet)
- Same core aspirin safety profile applies regardless of coating: GI bleeding risk (systemic, not just local), Reye syndrome risk in children/teens with viral illness, bleeding-risk interactions with anticoagulants — enteric coating addresses local gastric irritation only, not these broader risks
- No dedicated Ecotrin-brand RCT distinct from the general enteric-coated-aspirin literature was located in this pass

## Use Cases
325mg: general pain/fever relief, minor arthritis pain. 81mg: doctor-directed secondary cardiovascular event prevention (this category's actual relevant use case).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard aspirin-class cautions apply per [[aspirin]]: Reye syndrome risk in children/teens with viral illness; GI bleeding risk; avoid with anticoagulants without medical guidance; avoid in 3rd trimester of pregnancy generally.

## Symptoms Treated
Secondary prevention of heart attack/stroke recurrence (81mg, doctor-directed); general pain/fever (325mg) — not the relevant use case for this category.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
Real finding, consistent with the Bayer pattern already documented on [[aspirin]]: "Ecotrin" spans two genuinely different doses/purposes (325mg general pain relief vs. 81mg cardioprotective) under one brand name — a consumer should confirm which specific product they have rather than assuming brand-name equivalence implies interchangeable use.
