## Overview
**Mismatched-ingredient finding, same pattern as Gaviscon.** Confirmed via DailyMed: Mylanta Maximum Strength is a **triple-combination product — aluminum hydroxide 800mg + magnesium hydroxide 800mg + simethicone 80mg** per 10mL dose — not calcium-carbonate-based like this category's dominant brand (Tums). The simethicone component explains this brand's dual-category appearance (Antacids 16%, Antiflatulence Products 3%): it is genuinely both an antacid AND an antigas product in one formulation, unlike Tums (antacid only). Each 10mL dose also contains 340mg magnesium and 7mg sodium per its own label.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA OTC label; combines two established antacid mechanisms ([[antacids]]) plus the antigas surfactant mechanism ([[simethicone]]) in one product — genuinely addresses both heartburn and gas/bloating in a single dose, unlike single-mechanism competitors (Tums antacid-only, Gas-X antigas-only)
- Aluminum/magnesium hydroxide combination may partially offset each salt's individual bowel-habit side effect (aluminum constipating, magnesium loosening) — a plausible formulation rationale, not independently confirmed by comparative trial in this pass

## Cons
- **Not calcium-carbonate-based despite sitting in the same Antacids category as Tums** — a consumer assuming category-mates share a mechanism would be mistaken, the same finding confirmed separately for Gaviscon and Gelusil in this pass
- Per [[antacids]]: aluminum-hydroxide component carries real toxicity risk in renal failure/infants (osteopenia, microcytic anemia, neurotoxicity); magnesium-hydroxide component can bind tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotics, impeding absorption
- Per [[simethicone]]: the antigas component has only a single old (1974), small (n=41) positive trial in the general evidence base, and is explicitly NOT shown effective for infant colic (not this product's marketed use case, but relevant context for how strong "simethicone works" claims should be read)
- 340mg magnesium and 7mg sodium per 10mL dose, with a labeled max of 60mL/24h — meaning a consumer at maximum labeled dose could take in over 2,000mg magnesium/24h, well above the [[magnesium]] page's adult Tolerable Upper Intake Level of 350mg for supplemental magnesium — a real, quantified excess-intake risk with sustained maximum-dose use, though this is a short-term antacid-use context (label caps use at 2 weeks) rather than chronic supplementation
- No Mylanta-specific RCT beyond the general antacid/simethicone evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of heartburn, acid indigestion, and sour stomach, PLUS gas/bloating pressure, in one product — a genuinely dual-mechanism use case among this category's brands.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: max 60mL (6 doses)/24h; do not use maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks. Per [[antacids]]: caution in renal failure (aluminum/magnesium accumulation), and with tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotics. Per [[magnesium]]: sustained maximum-dose use could approach or exceed the adult Tolerable Upper Intake Level for supplemental magnesium — flagged here as a real, quantified consideration for prolonged max-dose use.

## Symptoms Treated
Heartburn, acid indigestion, sour stomach, gas/bloating pressure — via a combined aluminum-hydroxide/magnesium-hydroxide antacid mechanism plus simethicone antigas mechanism, per [[antacids]] and [[simethicone]].

Also flagged: a quantified magnesium-intake concern at sustained maximum labeled dosing, cross-referenced to [[magnesium]]'s Tolerable Upper Intake Level data.
