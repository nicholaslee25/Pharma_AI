## Overview
This is [[Nature Made]]'s dedicated fish-oil/omega-3 product line, tracked here specifically in the Cholesterol Management category (28% share, category-leading). See [[Nature Made]] for the parent brand's full profile — genuinely strong USP-Verified quality infrastructure (first-ever USP Verified supplement brand), set against two real, recent negative findings (a Salmonella/Staph-testing-protocol recall and an active prenatal-line phthalate/BPA lawsuit, neither confirmed specific to this fish-oil SKU). For the cholesterol/triglyceride mechanism specifically, see [[omega-3-fish-oil]]: the strongest cardiovascular-outcome evidence (REDUCE-IT trial, 25% composite CV event reduction) used a high-dose (4g/day) prescription icosapent ethyl formulation, not standard OTC fish oil; a 2020 Cochrane review of 86 RCTs (162,796 participants) found standard-dose omega-3 supplementation reduces triglycerides by ~15% but has NO effect on all-cause mortality, overall cardiovascular events, stroke, or arrhythmia — a real, important distinction between the modest triglyceride-lowering effect this OTC product can plausibly deliver and the much stronger hard-outcome evidence that applies only to a specific high-dose prescription product.
