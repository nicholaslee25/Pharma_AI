## Overview
Doctor's Best (founded 1990) positions itself around branded, science-based ingredient sourcing, manufactures exclusively at cGMP-certified US facilities, and states its products undergo third-party testing. Independent ConsumerLab testing found 13 of 14 reviewed Doctor's Best products "Approved" for quality — a strong, real independent-testing track record.
