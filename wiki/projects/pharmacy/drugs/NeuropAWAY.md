## Overview
No FDA Drug Facts label was found for NeuropAWAY Maximum Strength Gel — it is marketed as a "non-menthol" topical gel with no active-ingredient/percentage claim, consistent with a cosmetic or dietary-supplement-style classification rather than an OTC monograph drug. Its ingredient list is built around nutraceutical/dietary-supplement-type components typically taken orally in other contexts — **CoQ10** (see [[coq10]]), Vitamin D3 (see [[vitamin-d]]), Acetyl-L-Carnitine, Vitamin B5, Vitamin B6, MSM (methylsulfonylmethane), silk peptides, allantoin, and Vitamin E — reformulated here as a topical gel rather than an oral supplement. This is a genuinely different route of administration from how CoQ10 and vitamin D are otherwise covered in this database (their existing ingredient pages address oral supplementation, not topical/transdermal delivery of these same nutrients) — no topical-delivery evidence for any of these ingredients was located in this pass. NeuropAWAY also separately sells an oral "Nerve Support Capsule" product, a different SKU from the topical gel and not evaluated here. No brand-specific clinical trial for either SKU was found.
