## Appears In
Sleep Aids (non-melatonin) - 10%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Simply Sleep is diphenhydramine HCl 25mg — see [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]] for the full evidence base. Marketed as being "from the makers of Extra Strength Tylenol PM," but manufacturer materials explicitly confirm it contains **no pain reliever** — a single-ingredient sleep aid distinct from the Tylenol PM combination product despite the shared corporate-family branding association.

## Pros
- Clear, explicit "no pain reliever" positioning avoids the acetaminophen-related liver-injury risk that comes with combination products like Tylenol PM — a real, meaningful safety differentiator for users who need a sleep aid but not an analgesic (avoiding unnecessary acetaminophen exposure)
- Lower dose (25mg) than the FDA-recognized maximum OTC sleep-aid dose (50mg) — may produce less pronounced next-day grogginess for some users, though this database did not independently verify a dose-response comparison specific to this product

## Cons
- Shares diphenhydramine's full documented safety profile per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: AGS Beers Criteria "avoid in older adults" flag, anticholinergic side-effect profile, general OTC-sleep-aid-class efficacy-evidence gap
- The "from the makers of Tylenol PM" branding association, while accurately disclosed as pain-reliever-free, could create initial consumer uncertainty about whether the product contains acetaminophen — worth explicit label-reading given the close brand-family association
- No Simply-Sleep-brand-specific RCT distinct from the general diphenhydramine OTC-sleep-aid literature was located in this pass

## Use Cases
Occasional sleeplessness (single-ingredient, no analgesic).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard diphenhydramine-class cautions apply per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: avoid combining with other diphenhydramine-containing products; avoid alcohol/sedatives without consulting a doctor; AGS Beers Criteria recommends avoidance in most/all older adults.

## Symptoms Treated
Occasional sleeplessness — same low/mixed evidence tier documented on [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]].

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
No discrepancy found — confirmed to be exactly what it claims: a single-ingredient (diphenhydramine-only) sleep aid, genuinely free of the acetaminophen its brand-family association (Tylenol PM) might suggest.
