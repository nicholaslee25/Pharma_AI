## Appears In
Ear Wax Removal - 2%

## Overview
**Discrepancy flagged:** Similasan Ear Wax Relief's own FDA/DailyMed label confirms its active ingredients are **Causticum 12X, Graphites 15X, Lachesis 12X, and Lycopodium Clavatum 12X** — homeopathic dilutions, not a conventional water/oil/peroxide-based cerumenolytic like every other brand in this category ([[Debrox]], [[NeilMed ClearCanal]], [[Earwax MD]]). This is mechanistically unrelated to the chemistry [[cerumenolytics]] describes, and no rigorous trial specific to homeopathic earwax treatment was located (the related evidence on [[homeopathic-ear-products]], covering Similasan's sibling earache product, similarly found no convincing effect beyond placebo for homeopathic ear preparations generally). Similasan also sells "Earache Relief" (a different homeopathic formulation, Chamomilla/Mercurius/Sulphur — see [[Similasan Earache Relief]]) — the two products target different symptoms with entirely different homeopathic formulations despite sharing a brand name.

## Pros
- No serious adverse events identified for this product type
- Simple, well-tolerated formulation consistent with the low-risk profile of homeopathic dilutions generally
- Offers users who prefer to avoid a chemical/peroxide cerumenolytic an alternative option

## Cons
- **Category-placement/mechanism mismatch:** unlike every other brand in Ear Wax Removal, Similasan's active ingredients are homeopathic dilutions with no established conventional mechanism for softening or removing earwax, per [[cerumenolytics]] and [[homeopathic-ear-products]]
- No brand-specific or homeopathic-earwax-specific RCT was identified
- FDA has not evaluated this product for safety or efficacy (standard homeopathic-drug label disclaimer)

## Use Cases
Marketed for earwax buildup relief (per label); mechanism and evidence base differ fundamentally from the other four brands in this category.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: homeopathic dilutions generally described as non-toxic; standard ear-product caution to discontinue and see a doctor if pain, discharge, or new symptoms develop; avoid with perforated eardrum.

## Symptoms Treated
Marketed for earwax buildup/dryness/itching (per label); no established conventional pharmacologic mechanism for actually softening or removing wax.
