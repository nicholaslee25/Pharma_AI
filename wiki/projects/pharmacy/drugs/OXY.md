## Appears In
Acne Products - 2%

## Overview
OXY (manufactured by The Mentholatum Company) is confirmed via multiple DailyMed labels to span **two distinct acne-active mechanisms** across its product line — resolving this ingredient page's prior "plausible, not confirmed" flag on [[benzoyl-peroxide]]:

- **Salicylic acid line** — 3-in-1 Acne Cleansing Pads and Deep Pore Acne Cleansing Pads (2% salicylic acid), Sensitive Skin Acne Cleansing Pads (0.5%), plus swab/pad SKUs at unspecified concentration. Confirmed active: **salicylic acid 2%**, purpose "acne treatment," per the OXY Advanced Care Maximum Strength 3-in-1 Acne Pads label pulled from DailyMed.
- **Benzoyl peroxide line** — OXY 10 Acne Cleanser and Face Wash Maximum Action (10% benzoyl peroxide, the FDA OTC ceiling concentration), OXY Advanced Care Sensitive Skin Acne Cleanser and Sensitive Skin Acne Cream Cleanser (5% benzoyl peroxide) — a separate, real product family under the same brand name.
- **Non-drug line** — 8-in-1 Pimple Patches are hydrocolloid (no listed acne active, same non-drug-device category as [[Band-Aid Acne Patches]]/[[Mighty Patch]]), and a Vitamin C/hyaluronic-acid serum is a cosmetic with no Drug Facts panel.

This is the same "one brand name, multiple mechanisms/concentrations across SKUs" pattern already documented on [[La Roche-Posay]], [[Cetaphil]], [[Clearasil]], and [[AcneFree]] — a consumer picking "OXY" off a shelf could get salicylic acid, benzoyl peroxide, or neither, at concentrations ranging from 0.5% to 10%, depending on which specific product they grab.

No OXY-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass (see [[salicylic-acid]] and [[benzoyl-peroxide]] for the general ingredient-level evidence bases, both of which are large and independently substantiated).

## Pros
- Confirmed salicylic-acid (0.5%/2%) and benzoyl-peroxide (5%/10%) concentrations across the line fall within FDA-monograph OTC ranges
- Offering both mechanisms under one brand gives consumers/dermatologists a same-shelf option to switch actives if one causes irritation, without switching brands
- 10% benzoyl peroxide face wash sits at the top of the FDA-permitted concentration range, matching [[PanOxyl]]'s maximum-strength positioning

## Cons
- **Two distinct, non-interchangeable active-ingredient families (salicylic acid vs. benzoyl peroxide) sold under one brand name** with no single unifying "OXY = X ingredient" identity — a consumer must read the specific product label, not just the brand name, to know what they're using
- Combining an OXY salicylic-acid pad with an OXY benzoyl-peroxide wash (plausible if a consumer assumes "more OXY" helps) would combine two independently drying/irritating actives — a real combination-use risk neither ingredient page nor this pass found addressed in brand-specific OXY marketing
- No OXY-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass for either mechanism — evidence is entirely ingredient-level, not brand-level
- Concentration is not disclosed on every SKU name (e.g., "Deep Pore Cleansing Pads — salicylic acid swab" with concentration only confirmed via the underlying DailyMed label, not the product name)

## Use Cases
Acne spot/area treatment via salicylic acid (keratolytic, pad/cloth format) or benzoyl peroxide (antibacterial/keratolytic, wash/cleanser format) — consumer's or product's choice depending on which specific OXY SKU is purchased.

## Allergies / Warnings
Salicylic-acid line: per [[salicylic-acid]] — external use only; avoid eyes, lips, mouth; do not combine with other topical acne medications without care (increased irritation/dryness risk); consult a doctor if pregnant/nursing; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed. Benzoyl-peroxide line: per [[benzoyl-peroxide]] — external use only; avoid eyes/lips/mucous membranes; may bleach fabric/hair; increased sun sensitivity; discontinue if excessive irritation occurs.

## Symptoms Treated
Acne pimples, blackheads, and whiteheads — via salicylic acid (pad/cloth line) or benzoyl peroxide (wash/cleanser line), mechanism depending on specific product chosen.
