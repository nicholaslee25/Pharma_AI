## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tylenol Cold Plus Flu Severe spans multiple SKUs — the 30mL daytime liquid = **acetaminophen 650mg + dextromethorphan HBr 20mg**; a tablet/Day-Night kit version = **acetaminophen + dextromethorphan + guaifenesin + phenylephrine** (day) with doxylamine added at night. This completes the earlier partial hedge, confirming dextromethorphan, guaifenesin, and phenylephrine as the other actives beyond acetaminophen. ## Pros
- Broad symptom coverage across the day/night kit format, coordinated dosing between formulations under one product line
- Acetaminophen dose in the daytime liquid (650mg) sits comfortably under the 4,000mg/day ceiling with room for a second dose within label limits

## Cons
- The most fragmented Tylenol sub-family in this batch — daytime liquid, tablet, and Day/Night kit versions all carry different exact ingredient combinations, so a consumer must check the specific SKU rather than assume "Tylenol Cold + Flu Severe" means one fixed formula
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication risk, dextromethorphan-MAOI interaction, and oral-phenylephrine efficacy caveats all apply depending on the exact SKU

## Use Cases
Multi-symptom flu relief: fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion, nasal congestion (SKU-dependent), sleep support (night kit).

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, or diabetes.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion, nasal congestion, sleeplessness during illness (SKU-dependent).
