## Overview
Confirmed via Zarbee's/Kenvue manufacturer labeling: "Zarbee's" children's multivitamin gummies split into at least two distinct SKUs — "Children's Complete Multivitamin + Probiotic" (13 vitamins/minerals plus **Bacillus coagulans probiotic, 500 million–1 billion live cells**) and "Children's Complete Multivitamin + Immune" (elderberry + vitamin C + zinc, no probiotic) — the same one-brand-multiple-formulations pattern already confirmed for Flintstones, Enfamil Poly-Vi-Sol, One A Day Kids, OLLY Kid's Multi, SmartyPants, and Culturelle Kids elsewhere in this category. Both SKUs are labeled formulated without milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, wheat, peanut, soy, and sesame (all 9 major FDA-recognized allergens), and are gluten-free, gelatin-free (pectin-based, vegetarian), and free of high-fructose corn syrup — a positive allergen profile matching OLLY Kid's Multi's clean showing in this same category. **Ingredient note:** the Probiotic SKU's "other ingredients" list includes honey (in a tapioca syrup/honey blend) — not a concern at the labeled ages 2+ (honey's infant botulism risk applies only under 12 months), but worth flagging since this database's [[honey-pediatric-cough]] page already tracks Zarbee's Naturals as a honey-containing brand in a different category (Children's Cough); this is the same manufacturer family using honey across product lines, not a new brand-level finding but a useful cross-reference.

## Pros
- **Confirmed free of all 9 major FDA-recognized food allergens** per manufacturer labeling, matching OLLY Kid's Multi as a second allergen-clean brand in this category, in contrast to Nature Made Kids First Multivitamin (fish) and L'il Critters Gummy Vites (tree nut/coconut)
- Probiotic SKU combines multivitamin and digestive-support ingredients in one product, similar in concept to OLLY Kid's Multi's Probiotic variant and Culturelle Kids elsewhere in this category — and unlike Culturelle Kids' unconfirmed strain-dose specifics, this SKU's probiotic dose (500 million–1 billion CFU Bacillus coagulans) is explicitly labeled
- Gelatin-free, pectin-based, vegetarian formulation; no high-fructose corn syrup

## Cons
- **Same brand-name/SKU ambiguity flagged repeatedly in this category**: this database's category-mapping data ("Zarbee's Multi," 2% share) does not specify whether the Probiotic or Immune (elderberry) SKU applies, and the two have materially different ingredient sets and use-case framing
- If the Probiotic SKU applies: Bacillus coagulans is a spore-forming probiotic species distinct from the Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium strains more commonly discussed in this database's [[probiotics]] page — this database has not independently reviewed Bacillus coagulans-specific pediatric efficacy evidence
- If the Immune SKU applies: elderberry's evidence base is not itemized in this database under a dedicated ingredient page in this pass — efficacy claims for elderberry in a pediatric immune-support context are not independently verified here
- Per [[multivitamins]]'s general evidence review: broad disease-prevention claims for multivitamin use are not established in a large modern adult RCT; no pediatric-specific outcome trial exists for either Zarbee's Kids formulation

## Use Cases
General pediatric nutrient-gap supplementation, ages 2+, with the Probiotic SKU adding digestive-health framing and the Immune SKU adding elderberry-based immune-support framing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Free of the 9 major FDA-recognized food allergens per manufacturer labeling. Gluten-free, gelatin-free. Probiotic SKU's inactive-ingredient blend includes honey — not a concern at the labeled ages 2+ (honey/infant botulism risk applies only under 12 months). Keep out of reach of children.

## Symptoms Treated
General nutrient-gap correction; Probiotic SKU adds digestive-health support framing; Immune SKU adds elderberry-based immune-support framing. Not a treatment for any specific disease.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**New finding, added to both this page and [[multivitamins]]:** Zarbee's Multi is the sixth brand in this Children's Multivitamins category confirmed to span multiple formulations under one umbrella name (Probiotic vs. Immune/elderberry split), but — like OLLY Kid's Multi — is also confirmed allergen-free across all 9 major FDA-recognized allergens, reinforcing that category-wide allergen-safety and formulation-ambiguity are two independent, both-recurring findings rather than a single brand's isolated issue.
