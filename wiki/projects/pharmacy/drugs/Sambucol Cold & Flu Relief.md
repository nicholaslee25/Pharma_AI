## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: "Sambucol Cold and Flu Relief" is an actual **homeopathic combination product** (quick-dissolve tablets), not a straightforward elderberry extract. Active ingredients, all at homeopathic dilution: Bryonia 6X, Gelsemium Sempervirens 6X/12X/30X, Sambucus Nigra 3X/6X, Sulphur 12X, Zincum Gluconicum 2X/6X. This is the same product identified under `drugs/Sambucol Black Elderberry Cold & Flu Relief.md` (Homeopathic Flu Products category) — likely the identical SKU listed under two slightly different name renderings across different Pharmacy Times subcategory tables in this database's source data. This corrects the earlier flat "see ingredients/elderberry.md" pointer, which implied this product was governed by the concentrated-extract elderberry evidence; it is not — see [[elderberry]]'s updated Regulatory/Categorization note.

## Cons

- Only 1% recommendation share in this category, the smallest of the 11 products in Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations Daytime — reflecting limited pharmacist confidence relative to conventional combination products in the same list (DayQuil, Mucinex, etc.)
