## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer labeling: Umcka ColdCare Kids Syrup = **Pelargonium sidoides 1X**, single active ingredient, cherry-flavored, marketed "phenylephrine free," ages 1+ (a separate Chewables variant is labeled ages 6+). Per [[pelargonium]]'s existing, detailed evidence page: pelargonium is **explicitly NOT a homeopathic dilution** in the ultra-dilute sense — it's a pharmacologically active standardized herbal extract (EPs 7630) with a real, if mixed, RCT evidence base (a positive 2022 meta-analysis of 2195 participants including a pediatric acute-bronchitis subgroup; but the largest, most recent, most pragmatic 2026 trial found no significant primary-endpoint benefit and more adverse events than usual care). However, [[pelargonium]] ALSO already documents an unresolved gap specific to this exact brand: Umcka's FDA-registered label uses homeopathic-style "1X" dilution notation, and this database has **not established how much actual standardized EPs 7630 material a labeled "1X" dose delivers** — meaning the practical equivalence between this children's product's dose and the 30-90mg/day EPs 7630 doses used in the positive RCT evidence has not been confirmed. This product is filed under "Children's Homeopathic Cough Products" despite pelargonium's own evidence page explicitly cautioning against treating it as equivalent to true homeopathic dilutions — the same categorization tension [[homeopathic-cold-products]] already flags for pelargonium and elderberry generally.

## Pros
- Unlike the true homeopathic multi-remedy products in this same category (Hyland's, Boiron ColdCalm Kids, Similasan Kids, Genexa), pelargonium has a real, non-trivial pediatric RCT evidence base per the 2022 Kardos meta-analysis's dedicated children/adolescent acute-bronchitis subgroup (79.6% vs. 41.0% achieving ≥50% cough-intensity reduction at day 7 vs. placebo)
- "Phenylephrine free" positioning is a genuine, verifiable differentiator from combination products in adjacent categories that DO contain phenylephrine (Children's Mucinex, Children's Sudafed PE, Vicks Children's combos) — sidesteps that ingredient's FDA-flagged efficacy problem entirely
- Ages 1+ labeling (syrup) is broader than most competitors in this category (Boiron ColdCalm Kids and Similasan Kids both require age 2-4+)

## Cons
- **The dose-equivalence gap already flagged on [[pelargonium]] applies directly and unresolved to this exact pediatric product**: this database cannot currently confirm what fraction (if any) of the positive pediatric-bronchitis trial evidence actually transfers to Umcka ColdCare Kids' specific "1X" labeled dose — a real, acknowledged limitation, not a minor caveat
- **The largest, most pragmatic trial located (PHYTOBRONCH 2026, 332 adults)** found no significant benefit on its primary endpoint and significantly MORE adverse events (32.3% vs. 21.5%, all mild) than usual care — this more rigorous recent evidence should be weighted alongside, not overridden by, the older positive meta-analysis data
- Filed under a "Homeopathic" category label despite pelargonium's own evidence page explicitly stating it should not be conflated with true homeopathic dilutions — a real category-framing tension, not a factual contradiction, per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s existing caution against grouping pharmacologically active herbal extracts with true homeopathic products
- No pediatric-specific dosing table or FDA label exists (dietary supplement, not an FDA-regulated drug) — dosing is manufacturer-set, not FDA-reviewed

## Use Cases
Marketed to shorten duration and reduce severity of cold symptoms (cough, congestion, sore throat) in children ages 1+, via a real (though incompletely dose-confirmed) pharmacologically active herbal mechanism rather than true homeopathic dilution.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not FDA-evaluated as a drug (dietary supplement). Per [[pelargonium]]'s general safety data: adverse events reported in RCTs were mild-to-moderate and dose-dependent; no serious adverse events reported across the largest dose-finding trial (406 adults). Pediatric-specific contraindication data was not located.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, cold-symptom duration/severity reduction, per manufacturer marketing claims not independently verified against an FDA-reviewed standard.
