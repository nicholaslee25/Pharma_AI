## Overview
AZO is a large brand family (i-Health, Inc.) spanning multiple genuinely different active-ingredient mechanisms under one name, confirmed across this database's research passes: (1) **AZO Urinary Pain Relief** — phenazopyridine HCl 99.5mg (Maximum Strength), the category's core urinary-analgesic mechanism, see [[phenazopyridine]]; (2) **AZO Cranberry** — a cranberry-extract dietary supplement, see [[cranberry]]; (3) **AZO Boric Acid Vaginal Suppositories** — boric acid 600mg-class antifungal-adjacent product, see [[boric-acid]] and `drugs/AZO Boric Acid Vaginal Suppositories.md`; (4) **AZO Complete Feminine Balance** — a probiotic capsule line (Intelliflora multi-strain blend), see [[probiotics]]; and (5) **AZO Yeast Plus**, confirmed via DailyMed as a **homeopathic** product (Candida albicans 30X HPUS, Kreosotum/wood creosote 30X HPUS, Natrium muriaticum 12X HPUS, Sulphur 12X HPUS) — this is the product driving AZO's 16% share of Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief in this database. **This is a real, significant finding: "AZO Yeast" does NOT contain an antifungal ingredient of any kind** — despite the brand name directly evoking yeast-infection treatment, and despite sitting in a category (Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief) alongside genuinely antifungal miconazole/tioconazole-based competitors like [[Monistat]] and [[Vagistat]] — its own label states "This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy" and that "the FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective." The 68% Urinary Health figure, by contrast, is driven by the genuinely FDA-monograph phenazopyridine product.

## Pros
- The phenazopyridine-based Urinary Pain Relief line has real, cited symptomatic-relief evidence per [[phenazopyridine]] (though it treats symptoms only, not the underlying infection)
- Brand-family breadth lets AZO capture multiple genuinely distinct women's-urinary/vaginal-health use cases under one recognized name
- The Complete Feminine Balance probiotic line: no recall or lawsuit was located in this pass (a real, if modest, clean-record finding)

## Cons
- **AZO Yeast Plus is homeopathic, not an antifungal** — a consumer choosing "AZO" by brand-name association with the effective phenazopyridine product, or assuming "Yeast" in the name implies a miconazole/tioconazole-class treatment, would be genuinely misled about this specific sub-product's mechanism
- Phenazopyridine's own well-documented limitation applies to the Urinary Pain Relief line: it treats symptoms only, does NOT cure or treat the underlying UTI — per [[phenazopyridine]]'s cited survey data, 71% of consumers were unaware a bladder infection was causing their symptoms and 38% used the product as a substitute for medical care, a real, documented behavior risk this brand's marketing does not appear to specifically counteract
- Five genuinely different active-ingredient mechanisms (or none, for the homeopathic line) under one brand name is the most extensive single-brand mechanism-spread documented in this database's Women's Health research, a real navigation burden for consumers
- AZO's own phenazopyridine-suppository/boric-acid mg doses were not independently re-confirmed against their own DailyMed labels in every sub-line during this pass (see [[boric-acid]] for the specific dose-verification gap flagged there)

## Use Cases
Urinary pain/burning/urgency relief (phenazopyridine line); cranberry-based urinary-tract wellness support (AZO Cranberry); vaginal odor/pH support (AZO Boric Acid); vaginal probiotic balance (Complete Feminine Balance); yeast-infection SYMPTOM relief only, not treatment, per homeopathic labeling (AZO Yeast Plus).

## Allergies / Warnings
Phenazopyridine line: see [[phenazopyridine]] for G6PD-deficiency hemolysis risk, renal-impairment contraindication (GFR <50), and methemoglobinemia signal. AZO Yeast Plus: standard homeopathic-product "ask a doctor if pregnant/nursing" convention; no antifungal drug-interaction profile since it contains no antifungal active ingredient.

## Symptoms Treated
Urinary pain/burning/frequency (phenazopyridine); vaginal yeast-infection-associated itching/burning/discharge/odor SYMPTOMS only, not the infection itself (AZO Yeast Plus, homeopathic).
