## Overview
Florajen is confirmed to use multiple, specifically-identified (substrain-level) probiotic strains — Florajen Digestion contains Lactobacillus acidophilus NCFM, L. acidophilus La-14, Bifidobacterium lactis Bi-07, B. animalis ssp. lactis HN019, and B. longum Bl-05, totaling 15 billion CFU/capsule — real, checkable substrain-level identification that resolves [[probiotics]]'s own noted "genus alone isn't a sufficient identifier" concern for this brand specifically. Florajen's key differentiator is refrigeration: the brand states a "Cold Chain Commitment" maintaining refrigeration from manufacture through purchase, a real, mechanistically relevant claim since probiotic viability (live CFU count) genuinely degrades faster at room temperature for many strains — though this pass did not independently verify comparative CFU-retention data against non-refrigerated competitors like [[Culturelle]]. Florajen also has a separate product line (Florajen Eczema) with a cited clinical trial showing improved eczema symptoms and reduced reliance on topical steroids — a real, brand-specific trial for a use case entirely outside general "digestive health." Probiotic Dietary Supplements (10%) and Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief (9%) are both covered by [[probiotics]].
