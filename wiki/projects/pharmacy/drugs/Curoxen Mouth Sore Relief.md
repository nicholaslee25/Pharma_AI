## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via its DailyMed "Curoxen Oral Care" listing: this is a **homeopathic** ointment with active ingredients **Calendula 3X HPUS, Clove Essential Oil 1X HPUS, and Olive Extract (Oleuropein) 3X HPUS** — explicitly marketed as "benzocaine-free," positioning itself against the dominant benzocaine-based competitors in this same category (Orajel, Anbesol, Kank-A). It carries an FDA National Drug Code (NDC), which reflects manufacturer self-registration under the homeopathic OTC framework rather than FDA premarket efficacy review — the product's own marketing states it is "not FDA evaluated," a distinction worth preserving rather than letting the NDC registration imply agency-reviewed efficacy. This mirrors the same NDC-registration-vs-efficacy-review discrepancy already documented elsewhere in this database for other homeopathic products (see [[homeopathic-cold-products]]).

## Pros
- Explicitly benzocaine-free, avoiding the methemoglobinemia risk documented on [[topical-anesthetics]] for the category's dominant competitors
- All-natural/organic ingredient positioning may appeal to consumers avoiding synthetic anesthetics or petrolatum-derived products
- Manufactured under FDA Good Manufacturing Practices per the product's own claims (a manufacturing-quality standard, distinct from efficacy review)

## Cons
- No clinical trial evidence (RCT or otherwise) was identified for this specific product's efficacy in treating canker sore pain or healing — "not FDA evaluated" per the product's own labeling means no premarket efficacy determination was made
- Homeopathic dilution nomenclature (3X, 1X) reflects the homeopathic potency system rather than standard pharmaceutical concentration — consumers unfamiliar with homeopathic labeling conventions may not realize how dilute these "active" ingredients are
- Clove oil (eugenol) has a plausible pharmacologic basis for topical oral analgesia in conventional (non-homeopathic) dentistry, but this product's specific 1X HPUS dilution has not been independently verified against that conventional-dose evidence base

## Use Cases
Marketed for temporary relief of canker sores and other mouth sores, via a non-benzocaine, homeopathic ointment.

Product-specific warnings not independently retrieved beyond general homeopathic-product labeling conventions; no benzocaine-related methemoglobinemia risk applies given the confirmed benzocaine-free formulation. Standard precaution: discontinue and consult a dentist/doctor if the sore persists or worsens.
