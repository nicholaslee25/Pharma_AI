## Overview
This page and [[Ester-C]] (tracked separately in this database's High-Potency Vitamin C Supplements category at 6% share) refer to the same underlying branded product — "Ester C" vs. "Ester-C" is a naming-formatting inconsistency across the two Pharmacy Times source tables this database draws from, not two different products. See [[Ester-C]] for the full research: a combination of calcium ascorbate and vitamin C metabolites (including L-threonate), with a genuinely mixed evidence picture on its headline bioavailability/retention claim — one small independent study found no absorption difference from standard ascorbic acid, while the manufacturer's stronger bioavailability claim rests on an unpublished study; confirmed better GI tolerability than standard ascorbic acid; and at least one study found an association with fewer colds/shorter severe-symptom duration versus placebo, directly relevant to this page's Immune Support category placement. Immune Support (2%) is covered by [[vitamin-c]].
