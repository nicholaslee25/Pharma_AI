## Pros
- Confirmed single active ingredient (colloidal oatmeal) consistently across all Eczema Therapy sub-line SKUs checked — genuinely simpler than most other multi-category brands in this pass
- Real supporting RCT evidence: per [[colloidal-oatmeal]], a 2017 double-blind, randomized, active-controlled trial (n=90, ages 6mo-18yr) found a 1% colloidal-oatmeal OTC cream **non-inferior** to a prescription ceramide-based barrier repair cream on EASI score improvement at week 3 — a genuinely strong comparator (steroid-free OTC product performing on par with a prescription-tier product)
- Non-inferiority finding replicated in a Black/African American pediatric sub-analysis, broadening the population base for the evidence
- Steroid-free mechanism, relevant for long-term/maintenance use where topical corticosteroid overuse is a recognized concern

## Cons

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of itching and dryness associated with eczema/atopic dermatitis, as a steroid-free daily-maintenance moisturizer and flare-relief treatment.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per FDA skin-protectant monograph: for external use only; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days; avoid eyes and open/deep wounds. Oat/cereal-grain allergy: a theoretical but recognized consideration for a plant-derived topical, not itemized as a specific label warning.

## Symptoms Treated
Itching, dryness, and skin irritation associated with eczema/atopic dermatitis — via colloidal oatmeal's occlusive/moisturizing and avenanthramide-mediated anti-inflammatory mechanism (see [[colloidal-oatmeal]]).
