## Overview
OptiMind is a nootropic supplement marketed for memory, focus, and energy. **This pass found a real, but different-flavored discrepancy than most competitors in this category: rather than an efficacy-specific lawsuit, this brand has a documented pattern of consumer-billing complaints related to its free-trial/autoship marketing model** — customers signing up for what was marketed as a 14-day trial reportedly received full-price bottle shipments and recurring charges, with reported difficulty canceling. This is a real, consumer-protection-relevant finding distinct from (though related to) the efficacy-substantiation problems found across most of this category's other brands on [[brain-health-nootropics]] — no specific FTC action or class-action lawsuit naming OptiMind was independently confirmed in this pass, though the FTC has taken action against other supplement companies for materially similar undisclosed-autoship practices, providing relevant regulatory context.
