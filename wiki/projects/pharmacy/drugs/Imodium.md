## Use Cases
Control of acute nonspecific diarrhea, traveler's diarrhea symptoms, and IBS-associated chronic diarrhea — via the peripherally-restricted opioid mechanism detailed in [[loperamide]].

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label/[[loperamide]]: do not exceed 4 caplets/24h (adults); age-specific lower limits for children 6-11y; do not use under age 2; stop and see a doctor if diarrhea persists beyond 2 days, if fever develops, or if blood/mucus appears in stool. Real overdose risk of QT prolongation/torsades de pointes with excess dosing — FDA imposed package-size/unit-dose limits in 2019 specifically to curb high-dose misuse access.
