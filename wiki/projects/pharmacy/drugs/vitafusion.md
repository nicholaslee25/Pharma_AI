## Overview
This is the generic "vitafusion" house-brand line — distinct from the brand's separately-tracked named-product pages in this database ([[Vitafusion Brain Food]], [[Vitafusion Fiber Well]], [[Vitafusion Melatonin Gummies]], [[vitafusion Infusions]], [[vitafusion MultiVites]], [[vitafusion PreNatal]]). vitafusion is manufactured by Church & Dwight (a large, publicly traded consumer-products company) under FDA cGMP oversight, but **does not participate in the USP Dietary Supplement Verification Program** — a real, confirmed contrast to [[Nature Made]] in the same broad gummy/multivitamin space. The brand had a real, brand-specific 2021 voluntary recall (select gummy lots recalled after consumer reports of possible metallic mesh material; FDA confirmed the recall was completed with no reported illness/injury). Calcium Supplements (2%) is covered by [[calcium]]; High-Potency Vitamin C Supplements (1%) by [[vitamin-c]]; "Letter" Vitamin Brand has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.

## Pros
- Manufactured by a large, FDA-cGMP-audited consumer company (Church & Dwight) rather than a small/unverified operation
- Offers sugar-free formulations for some product lines (e.g., Women's Sugar-Free Multivitamin Gummy) alongside standard formulations sweetened with glucose syrup/sucrose/fruit juice concentrates rather than only synthetic sweeteners
- The 2021 recall was handled transparently (voluntary, FDA-confirmed completion, no injuries) rather than concealed

## Cons
- **Confirmed not USP Dietary Supplement Verified** — a real, checkable quality-assurance gap versus [[Nature Made]], which holds more USP-Verified SKUs than any competitor in the exact same "Letter" Vitamin Brand / general-multivitamin space
- Gummy formulation inherently carries **added sugar per dose** (e.g., ~3g sugar per 2-gummy MultiVites serving) — a real, product-form-specific consideration not shared by tablet/capsule competitors, worth flagging for diabetic or sugar-restricted users even though the amount is modest
- Real 2021 recall for possible foreign material (metallic mesh) in select gummy lots — resolved, but a documented manufacturing-quality incident
- No vitafusion-specific clinical trial was located for any of its three tracked generic-line categories — efficacy evidence is at the general-ingredient level only

## Allergies / Warnings
Gummy base typically contains gelatin (or pectin in some lines) and added sugars/sweeteners — check the individual product's Supplement Facts and allergen statement, particularly for gelatin-avoidant or sugar-restricted users. General ingredient-level warnings are in [[calcium]] and [[vitamin-c]].
