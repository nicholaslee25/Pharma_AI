## Overview
**Real, brand-specific formulation complexity confirmed**: "Feosol" is not one single formulation but spans at least two chemically distinct products under one brand name — Feosol Original uses **ferrous sulfate** (325mg, 65mg elemental iron), while a separate Feosol SKU uses **carbonyl iron**, a different iron form that is 100% elemental iron by weight (versus ferrous sulfate's ~20% elemental iron by weight) — meaning per-tablet elemental iron content is not directly comparable across "Feosol" SKUs without checking the specific product's label. Carbonyl iron is independently documented to have a different absorption/toxicity profile than ferrous salts (generally regarded as having a wider margin of safety in accidental pediatric ingestion specifically, since its absorption is more tightly regulated by the gut, though this pass did not independently verify a primary source for that specific safety claim). Iron Supplements (16%) is covered by [[iron]] — that page names Feosol's sister product Slow Fe specifically, but not Feosol's own dual-formulation structure.

## Pros
- 70+ year manufacturing history, positioned as an established, high-potency iron brand
- Offers both ferrous sulfate (most commonly recommended/best-studied form) and carbonyl iron (potentially wider safety margin) formulations, giving consumers a choice depending on tolerability/safety priorities — though see Cons regarding the risk of not distinguishing between them

## Cons
- **Real brand-name-spans-multiple-formulations risk**: "Feosol" covers at least two chemically different iron sources (ferrous sulfate vs. carbonyl iron) with different elemental-iron content and different absorption/safety profiles under the same brand name — a consumer or caregiver could reasonably assume all "Feosol" products are interchangeable when they are not
- Standard ferrous sulfate (the "Original" formulation) carries the same documented GI-tolerability limitations found across this ingredient class in [[iron]] and independent meta-analysis sourcing
- No Feosol-specific clinical trial was located; efficacy evidence is at the general-ingredient level, split across two different iron-salt evidence bases depending on which specific Feosol SKU is used
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located for Feosol in this pass
