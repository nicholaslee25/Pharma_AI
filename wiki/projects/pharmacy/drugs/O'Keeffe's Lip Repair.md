## Appears In
Lip Balms - 1%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: **O'Keeffe's Lip Repair Seal & Heal** lists **Petrolatum 30%** as its sole declared active ingredient, purpose "Lip protectant." Beeswax, shea butter, dimethicone, and various oils appear only as inactive ingredients. A separate SKU, **O'Keeffe's Lip Repair SPF 35 Stick**, adds a chemical sunscreen combination — **avobenzone, dimethicone, octinoxate, and octocrylene** (specific percentages not confirmed in this pass) — meaning this brand follows the same "core skin-protectant SKU plus separate SPF SKU with a different active set" pattern seen elsewhere in this subcategory ([[Aquaphor Lip Repair]], [[Nivea]]). See [[petrolatum]] for the ingredient evidence page.

No O'Keeffe's-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Confirmed, clearly declared active ingredient (petrolatum 30%) for the core Seal & Heal SKU, with beeswax/shea correctly identified as inactive rather than miscategorized
- SPF 35 variant offers a notably higher SPF than most other lip-balm sunscreen SKUs found in this subcategory (most are SPF 15-30)
- Strong general petrolatum evidence base (see [[petrolatum]])

## Cons
- **The 1% share figure spans at least two different formulations** (non-SPF Seal & Heal vs. SPF 35 Stick) that the source data does not distinguish between
- Dedicated evidence pages for avobenzone/octinoxate/octocrylene have not yet been built in this database — deferred to the Sunscreen subcategory pass
- No O'Keeffe's-brand-specific clinical trial was located
- Smallest tied share in this subcategory (1%)

## Use Cases
Seal & Heal: skin protectant for chapped/cracked lips via petrolatum. SPF 35 Stick: broad-spectrum-adjacent sun protection for lips via chemical UV filters, plus dimethicone.

## Allergies / Warnings
Seal & Heal (per label): external use only; avoid eye contact; stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur shortly after clearing. SPF 35 Stick: standard chemical-sunscreen warnings apply.

## Symptoms Treated
Chapped, cracked, or dry lips (Seal & Heal); sunburn prevention on lips (SPF 35 Stick).
