## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Lanacane First Aid Spray combines **benzethonium chloride 0.2%** (first-aid antiseptic) with **benzocaine 20%** (pain-relieving anesthetic) — the same antiseptic + anesthetic combination pattern already confirmed for [[Dermoplast]]'s "First Aid" SKU line elsewhere in this database. Per [[topical-anesthetics]], the 20% benzocaine concentration places this product on the higher-methemoglobinemia-risk side of that page's benzocaine/lidocaine divide (benzocaine implicated in ~66% of 242 reviewed methemoglobinemia case-report episodes vs. ~5% for lidocaine). The label also carries the same "intentional inhalation misuse" warning found on [[New-Skin]]'s pressurized-spray formulation, reflecting the volatile aerosol delivery format shared by both products.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with clearly disclosed dual-action formulation (antiseptic + anesthetic)
- Combines infection-risk reduction (benzethonium chloride) with pain/itch relief (benzocaine) in one product, addressing two needs simultaneously for minor cuts/scrapes/burns
- Substantial share (13%) in Topical Anesthetics, reflecting broad consumer/pharmacist familiarity with the Lanacane brand

## Cons
- Contains benzocaine 20%, carrying the disproportionate methemoglobinemia risk documented on [[topical-anesthetics]] — the same class-level concern as [[Dermoplast]], [[BiteMD]], and [[Sting-Kill]] in this database
- Pressurized aerosol format carries a specific "intentional inhalation misuse can pose serious health risks" label warning, plus flammability precautions
- Do not apply over large body areas — a real limitation for treating more extensive minor injuries
- No brand-specific clinical trial for Lanacane First Aid Spray specifically was identified beyond the general benzocaine/benzethonium chloride ingredient-level evidence

## Use Cases
First aid for temporary relief of pain and itching, and to help prevent infection, in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: external use only; flammable (avoid heat/flame/fire/smoking); do not apply in eyes or over large body areas; avoid puncturing/incinerating the pressurized container; do not store above 120°F; intentional inhalation misuse poses serious health risks; consult a doctor for deep wounds, animal bites, or serious burns; discontinue if symptoms worsen, persist beyond 7 days, or recur; keep from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed. See [[topical-anesthetics]] for the quantified benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk data.

## Symptoms Treated
