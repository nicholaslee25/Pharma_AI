## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (Blaine Labs Inc.): Tineacide Antifungal Cream is **miconazole nitrate 2%** — the same azole active and ingredient page as [[Desenex]], see [[miconazole-nitrate-topical]]. Labeled to cure most athlete's foot (tinea pedis), jock itch (tinea cruris), and ringworm (tinea corporis). **A real, brand-name-vs-formulation nuance worth noting**: Blaine Labs also sells a separately-named "Tineacide Physician Formula"/"Tineacide" product using **undecylenic acid** rather than miconazole (per DailyMed) — meaning, similar to [[FUNGICURE]] and [[Fungi-Nail]]/[[Fungi Cure]] elsewhere in this same subcategory, the "Tineacide" name is not perfectly one-to-one with a single active ingredient across the full product line, though the specific SKU tracked here ("Tineacide Antifungal Cream," not "Physician Formula") was confirmed as miconazole nitrate 2%.

No independent Tineacide-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass; the relevant evidence is the general miconazole nitrate literature on [[miconazole-nitrate-topical]].

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label, miconazole nitrate 2% at the standard OTC monograph concentration
- Backed by a large Cochrane review (129 trials, 18,086 participants) at the ingredient-class level, per [[miconazole-nitrate-topical]]
- Broadest labeled indication set among this category's smaller brands (athlete's foot, jock itch, AND ringworm on one label)

## Cons
- **Same-brand-name, multiple-active-ingredients pattern**: "Tineacide" spans both this miconazole-nitrate cream and a separately-named undecylenic-acid "Physician Formula" product — a consumer must check the specific product name closely, the same recurring pattern already documented for [[FUNGICURE]]/[[Fungi Cure]] in this subcategory
- No independent Tineacide-brand-specific clinical trial was located
- Not effective for onychomycosis (nail fungus) or scalp infections, the standard azole-class limitation
- Smallest tied share in this subcategory pass so far (2%)

## Use Cases
Treatment of athlete's foot, jock itch, and ringworm via miconazole nitrate's azole antifungal mechanism (this specific "Antifungal Cream" SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per [[miconazole-nitrate-topical]]: external use only; avoid eyes and mucous membranes; discontinue and consult a doctor if irritation develops or no improvement within the labeled treatment duration; azole hypersensitivity consideration.

## Symptoms Treated
Itching, burning, cracking, and scaling from athlete's foot, jock itch, and ringworm.
