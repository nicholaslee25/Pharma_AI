## Overview
Mucinex InstaSoothe is a multi-SKU sub-line with genuinely varied active ingredients, confirmed via DailyMed: **Sore Throat Plus Pain Relief spray** = benzocaine 7% + menthol 1%; **Sore Throat Plus Cough Relief lozenge** = dextromethorphan HBr + hexylresorcinol; **Sore Throat Plus Soothing Comfort lozenge** and a separate **Pain Relief lozenge** = hexylresorcinol alone. **Hexylresorcinol is a distinct oral anesthetic** not covered by [[topical-anesthetics]] (which addresses benzocaine and lidocaine) — a genuine new data gap, distinct from the dyclonine gap already flagged on the Sucrets brand page. This is a new confirmation touching [[topical-anesthetics]] (spray SKU, benzocaine) and [[dextromethorphan]] (cough lozenge SKU). Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/mucinex-instasoothe/dailymed-mucinex-instasoothe.md`.

## Pros
- Offers a genuine choice between two different oral anesthetic classes (benzocaine spray vs. hexylresorcinol lozenges) under one brand — potentially useful for someone with a known benzocaine sensitivity wanting an alternative anesthetic
- Cough Relief SKU combines an anesthetic (hexylresorcinol) with an actual cough suppressant (dextromethorphan), broader symptom coverage than a plain lozenge

## Cons
- The benzocaine spray SKU carries the full methemoglobinemia risk profile documented on [[topical-anesthetics]] — same real risk as Cepacol, Orajel, and Chloraseptic
- Hexylresorcinol has no dedicated ingredient page in this database — its own safety/efficacy profile can't currently be checked against an evidence-graded page, a genuine research gap
- Brand-name overlap with oral Mucinex (guaifenesin) and Mucinex Nasal Spray (oxymetazoline) — three "Mucinex" products with three different active ingredient classes under one umbrella brand, real potential for consumer confusion

## Use Cases
Sore throat pain relief (benzocaine spray or hexylresorcinol lozenges), combined pain + cough relief (Cough Relief lozenge).

## Allergies / Warnings
Benzocaine spray: watch for methemoglobinemia signs (pale/gray/blue skin, lips, nail beds; shortness of breath; fatigue; rapid heart rate). Cough lozenge: standard dextromethorphan-MAOI interaction warning.

## Symptoms Treated
