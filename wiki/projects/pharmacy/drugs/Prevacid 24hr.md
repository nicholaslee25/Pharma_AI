## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Prevacid 24 HR (lansoprazole delayed-release capsule, 15mg) resolves [[proton-pump-inhibitors]]'s prior hedge about lansoprazole identity. Notably lower per-capsule dose (15mg) than Prilosec OTC/Nexium 24HR (20mg each) — a real, quantified strength difference between OTC PPI brands not previously itemized on the ingredient page, though this reflects lansoprazole's own labeled OTC dose rather than an under-dosing concern. Package contains 42 capsules (three 14-day courses) vs. the 14-capsule single-course packaging typical of Prilosec/Nexium OTC — a packaging difference worth noting for a consumer comparing per-box value or planning repeat courses.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA OTC label; same PPI mechanism/evidence base as [[proton-pump-inhibitors]]
- 42-capsule package provides three full 14-day courses in one purchase, convenient for a consumer who anticipates needing to repeat the course (per label, up to every 4 months)

## Cons
- **Capsule contains several synthetic dyes** (D&C red no. 28, D&C yellow no. 10, FD&C blue no. 1, FD&C red no. 40) per its own DailyMed inactive-ingredient list — relevant for dye-sensitive individuals, and not something the [[proton-pump-inhibitors]] page's general evidence discussion would surface since it is capsule-shell-specific to this brand
- Lower per-unit dose (15mg) than Prilosec OTC/Nexium 24HR (20mg) — this database found no evidence that 15mg lansoprazole is therapeutically inferior at the OTC heartburn indication (it is the FDA-approved OTC lansoprazole strength), but a consumer comparing "which PPI is strongest" by looking only at mg-per-capsule across brands would be comparing different molecules with different per-mg potencies, not a valid direct comparison
- Same full PPI-class safety-signal list applies (hypomagnesemia, C. diff/infection risk, rebound acid hypersecretion)
- No Prevacid-24HR-specific RCT beyond the general PPI-class evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Treatment of frequent heartburn (≥2 days/week) via a full 14-day course, repeatable up to every 4 months — same use pattern as the rest of the PPI class.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not use with trouble/pain swallowing, vomiting blood, or bloody/black stools. Children under 18: ask a doctor first. Pregnant/breastfeeding: ask a health professional. **Contains synthetic dyes** (see Cons) — a dye-sensitive consumer should check the inactive-ingredient list specifically, since this is not called out on the [[proton-pump-inhibitors]] class page.

## Symptoms Treated
Frequent heartburn / acid indigestion, via the PPI mechanism described in [[proton-pump-inhibitors]].
