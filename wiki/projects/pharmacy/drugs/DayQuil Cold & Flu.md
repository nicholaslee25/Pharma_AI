## Overview
This is the same product family already researched on `drugs/DayQuil.md` (Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations, Daytime category) — confirmed via DailyMed: **acetaminophen 325mg + dextromethorphan HBr 10mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg** (Cold and Flu SKU), or the same three plus **guaifenesin 200mg** (Severe Cold and Flu SKU). Touches [[acetaminophen]], [[dextromethorphan]], [[oral-phenylephrine]], and [[guaifenesin]] (Severe SKU). See `drugs/DayQuil.md` for the full Pros/Cons treatment — duplicated only briefly here since this is the identical brand appearing under a second Pharmacy Times category (Flu Products vs. Cough/Cold/Flu Combinations Daytime) rather than a distinct product.

## Pros
See `drugs/DayQuil.md` — non-drowsy daytime formula covering pain/fever, cough, and congestion (± mucus, Severe SKU) in one product.

## Cons
See `drugs/DayQuil.md` — acetaminophen-duplication risk, oral phenylephrine's questioned OTC efficacy (see [[oral-phenylephrine]]), and the guaifenesin dose (200mg) being well below the 1200mg dose in [[guaifenesin]]'s own cited RCT.

## Use Cases
Daytime flu symptom relief: fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion (Severe SKU), nasal congestion.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, or diabetes.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion (Severe SKU), nasal/sinus congestion.
