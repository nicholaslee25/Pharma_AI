## Overview
Access Bio's **CareStart COVID-19 Antigen Home Test** holds an FDA EUA (authorized for testing twice over 2–3 days, at least 24h and not more than 48h apart) and an 8% share of this category. This brand offers the clearest, most concretely quantified illustration in this category of [[at-home-covid-tests]]'s central warning about manufacturer-vs-independent performance gaps: the FDA-validated clinical study reported **88.4% sensitivity / 100% specificity**, but two independent real-world community-testing studies found sensitivity of only **49.0% (95% CI 34.8–63.4%)** and **64.9% (95% CI 47.5–79.8%)** respectively — both markedly lower, with the lowest figure observed specifically in asymptomatic individuals. This nearly 40-percentage-point gap between the manufacturer-supporting figure and the lowest independent figure is a real, named number, not a hypothetical caveat — directly consistent with (and now supplying a specific data point for) the manufacturer-funding bias pattern already documented for blood glucose meters on [[blood-glucose-monitors]] (King et al. 2018, OR ~22 for manufacturer-vs-independent "pass" rates). Specificity remained consistently high (~99%+) across all studies located.

## Pros
- Specificity consistently high (99.3–100%) across both manufacturer and independent studies — false positives were not the concern here
- Authorized specifically for serial (two-test) use over 2–3 days, a design that partially compensates for single-test sensitivity limitations, consistent with the serial-testing mitigation strategy [[at-home-covid-tests]] recommends generally
- Two independent, published, non-manufacturer real-world studies exist for this brand — more independent scrutiny than several competitors (e.g. [[FlowFlex]], for which no independent study was located in this pass)

## Cons
- **The largest documented manufacturer-vs-independent sensitivity gap found for any brand in this category:** 88.4% (FDA-validated) vs. as low as 49.0% (independent, asymptomatic-heavy community testing) — nearly a 40-point spread
- Both independent studies found meaningfully reduced sensitivity specifically in asymptomatic testing populations, consistent with (and a real-world confirmation of) the general asymptomatic-sensitivity penalty already documented on [[at-home-covid-tests]] (54.7% pooled asymptomatic sensitivity across the antigen-test class)
- Smallest share (8%) of the five COVID test brands in this category, though this reflects market position rather than being directly tied to the performance data above

## Use Cases
Rapid at-home qualitative detection of SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid antigen from an anterior nasal swab in symptomatic or asymptomatic individuals, authorized specifically for serial (two-test) use.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no allergy profile. Given the documented real-world sensitivity gap, a negative result — especially in an asymptomatic person or from a single test rather than the authorized serial-testing protocol — should be treated with particular caution and not assumed to rule out infection; follow the authorized two-test serial protocol and confirm with molecular testing if clinically indicated.
