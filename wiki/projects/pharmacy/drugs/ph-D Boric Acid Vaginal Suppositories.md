## Cons

- Same general boric-acid safety profile applies: toxic if swallowed, requires careful storage; not indicated for treating sexually transmitted infections; may compromise latex condoms/diaphragms during treatment
- Per [[boric-acid]]'s broader evidence base, boric acid is positioned as a second-line option for recurrent/azole-resistant candidiasis specifically — its evidence does not establish it as a general-purpose "vaginal odor" product for causes of odor unrelated to yeast overgrowth
