## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (manufacturer: Bayer HealthCare LLC — the same corporate parent as [[Lotrimin]] elsewhere in this category): Tinactin is **tolnaftate 1%** across its cream, aerosol powder, and powder-spray (talc-free) formats. See [[tolnaftate]] (new ingredient page built this pass) for the full evidence picture. Tolnaftate shares the same squalene-epoxidase mechanism target as [[terbinafine]] and [[butenafine]] (both used by higher-share competitors in this category) but is chemically a distinct, older thiocarbamate — and per [[tolnaftate]]'s dedicated research, its efficacy literature is older and has not been updated with the same rigorous modern comparative trials that show butenafine/terbinafine outperforming clotrimazole. Cure rates in the older literature (93% mycologic eradication, 53-70% clinical cure) are respectable but the drug has genuinely declined in dermatology practice relative to newer agents.

No Tinactin-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass beyond the general tolnaftate ingredient-level historical data.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label, consistent tolnaftate 1% formulation across cream/aerosol/powder formats
- Long-established, well-tolerated agent with a genuinely low-toxicity mechanism (fungal-membrane-specific)
- Available in multiple formats (cream, aerosol powder, talc-free powder spray) for different preferences, including a foot-powder format useful for moisture control alongside antifungal action

## Cons
- **Per [[tolnaftate]]'s dedicated evidence review: the drug's efficacy literature is older and has been superseded by newer agents in dermatology practice** — this category's top two brands ([[Lotrimin]]'s butenafine sub-line and [[Lamisil AT]]'s terbinafine) both have modern comparative-trial evidence showing stronger cure rates than the general clotrimazole/tolnaftate-era baseline
- No Tinactin-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass
- Not effective for onychomycosis (nail fungus) or scalp infections — the same limitation as [[clotrimazole]]
- Smaller recommendation share (6% Jock Itch, 3% Toe/Foot) than the category leaders, consistent with its comparatively dated evidence position

## Use Cases
Treatment of mild-to-moderate athlete's foot (tinea pedis), jock itch (tinea cruris), and ringworm (tinea corporis) via tolnaftate's squalene-epoxidase-inhibiting antifungal mechanism.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: external use only; not for children under 2 without doctor advice/supervision; avoid eye contact; discontinue and consult a doctor if irritation occurs or no improvement within 2 weeks (jock itch) or longer for athlete's foot per label duration; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Symptoms Treated
Itching, chafing, and burning from jock itch, athlete's foot, and ringworm.
