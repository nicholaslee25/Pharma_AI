## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Mylicon Infants Gas Relief (Dye Free and Original formulas both identified) is **simethicone 20mg per 0.3mL** dose, labeled "Antigas" for discomfort from infant gas due to air swallowing or certain formulas/foods. **Positive finding worth flagging explicitly:** the label does NOT mention colic anywhere — a genuinely conservative, correctly-scoped indication consistent with [[simethicone]]'s own Efficacy Evidence section, which cites StatPearls stating plainly that simethicone is NOT effective for infantile colic despite how frequently simethicone drops are marketed/used for that exact purpose. Mylicon, as the category-dominant brand (63% share), avoids this over-claiming trap that this database's [[simethicone]] page specifically warns about.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a correctly-scoped "gas discomfort" indication that does NOT claim colic relief — a genuine positive contrast with brands elsewhere in this category (see [[simethicone]] cross-reference) that lean on "colic" framing despite the ingredient-class evidence not supporting it for that specific use
- Category-dominant share (63%) among Infant Gas Products in this database
- Available in a dye-free formula, relevant for caregivers seeking to minimize additive exposure in infants
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Key Safety Data: minimal toxicity profile given zero systemic absorption — a reassuring general safety baseline for this ingredient class

## Cons
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Efficacy Evidence: the ingredient's own supporting positive trial (a 1974 double-blind RCT, n=41) tested adult gas/bloating/heartburn/distension, not infants specifically — this database has not located an infant-specific simethicone efficacy RCT for gas relief either, so even the correctly-scoped "gas" claim (as opposed to colic) rests on adult-population evidence extended to infants by dosing convention rather than a dedicated pediatric trial
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Mechanism section: simethicone does not reduce actual gas production, only bubble physics — it cannot address gas increases from an underlying cause (e.g., formula intolerance), a general ingredient-class limitation rather than a Mylicon-specific one
- No Mylicon-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general simethicone evidence base

## Use Cases
Relief of infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or certain formulas/foods, ages under 2 (0.3mL) and over 2 (0.6mL per label, up to 12 doses daily).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: keep away from children; overdose requires immediate medical attention (Poison Control 1-800-222-1222). Per [[simethicone]]'s Contraindications: simethicone allergy. Zero systemic absorption per [[simethicone]]'s Pharmacokinetics section supports a generally benign safety profile at labeled doses.

## Symptoms Treated
Infant gas discomfort (bloating, flatulence-related fussiness) due to air swallowing or diet — NOT labeled or evidence-supported for infant colic specifically, a distinction this product's own label correctly maintains.
