## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: ZzzQuil Nighttime Sleep-Aid (the classic liquid product tracked in this Non-Melatonin category) is diphenhydramine HCl — see [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]] for the full evidence base. **Real, significant brand-line finding:** ZzzQuil also markets an entirely separate "Pure Zzzs" product line that is melatonin-based (gummies, liquid, and capsules containing melatonin plus botanicals like chamomile, lavender, valerian root, and lemon balm) — a genuinely different active ingredient tracked under this database's separate Melatonin Sleep Aids category, not this Non-Melatonin category. A single brand name ("ZzzQuil") therefore spans both a melatonin-based and a non-melatonin (diphenhydramine) product line, a real potential source of consumer confusion given this database's own category structure treats melatonin and non-melatonin sleep aids as separate subcategories.

## Pros
- Liquid dosage form may suit users who prefer not to swallow tablets/capsules
- Product-line breadth (classic diphenhydramine liquid plus the separate Pure Zzzs melatonin line) offers consumers a choice between two different sleep-aid mechanisms under one recognized brand

## Cons
- **Real brand-name-spans-two-mechanisms finding:** "ZzzQuil" is not synonymous with one active ingredient — the classic Nighttime Sleep-Aid liquid (diphenhydramine, tracked in this Non-Melatonin category) and Pure Zzzs (melatonin, a different ingredient class tracked separately) share a brand name but not a mechanism; a consumer shopping by brand name alone in a store could reasonably but incorrectly assume interchangeability
- Shares diphenhydramine's full documented safety profile per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: AGS Beers Criteria "avoid in older adults" flag, anticholinergic side-effect profile, general OTC-sleep-aid-class efficacy-evidence gap
- No ZzzQuil-brand-specific RCT distinct from the general diphenhydramine OTC-sleep-aid literature was located in this pass

## Use Cases
Occasional sleeplessness (classic diphenhydramine liquid, this category); melatonin-based sleep support (Pure Zzzs line, a different category/mechanism not covered further here).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard diphenhydramine-class cautions apply per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: avoid combining with other diphenhydramine-containing products; avoid alcohol/sedatives without consulting a doctor; AGS Beers Criteria recommends avoidance in most/all older adults.
