## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Mommy's Bliss Gas Relief Drops (MOM Enterprises, LLC) is **simethicone 20mg per 0.3mL** — the same active-ingredient dose and dosing schedule as Mylicon and Little Remedies Gas Relief Drops, both confirmed elsewhere in this database. **Minor labeling-metadata discrepancy noted:** the DailyMed listing's own product-title/header field reads "dimethicone solution/drops," while the actual Drug Facts panel (the legally operative label content) unambiguously lists "Simethicone 20 mg" as the active ingredient — dimethicone and simethicone are chemically related but functionally different (simethicone = dimethicone + silica, giving it its antifoam/antigas action; plain dimethicone lacks this specific mechanism). This is almost certainly an administrative/metadata labeling error in the DailyMed record rather than an actual formulation difference, since the Drug Facts panel itself is consistent and unambiguous — flagged for transparency rather than treated as a real formulation concern. Separately, the product is marketed prominently around "real ginger extract" as a selling point, though ginger extract appears only as an INACTIVE ingredient — ginger is not the FDA-recognized active ingredient (simethicone is), an active/marketed-ingredient distinction similar to the pattern already flagged for other brands elsewhere in this database (e.g., CloSYS, Arm & Hammer toothpaste).

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label matching Mylicon's and Little Remedies' dose and dosing schedule
- Label correctly addresses "gas" discomfort from air-swallowing/diet without a colic claim, consistent with the category's two other simethicone-based brands
- Clean Label Project certified per manufacturer marketing, an independent third-party quality-testing certification distinct from FDA approval

## Cons
- **Marketing prominently features "ginger extract" despite it being an inactive, not active, ingredient** — a caregiver choosing this product specifically for its ginger content should understand the FDA-recognized therapeutic action comes from simethicone, not ginger, per the product's own Drug Facts panel
- Minor DailyMed record metadata inconsistency (header says "dimethicone," Drug Facts panel says "simethicone") — likely an administrative error, not independently resolved with the manufacturer in this pass
- Per [[simethicone]]'s Efficacy Evidence and Mechanism sections: the same general limitations apply (adult-population supporting trial, not effective for colic specifically, does not reduce actual gas production) — this product makes no colic claim, so this limitation is less consequential here than for Hyland's Baby Gas Drops or Colic Calm Plus
- No Mommy's-Bliss-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general simethicone evidence base

## Use Cases
Relief of infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or certain formulas/foods — same indication as Mylicon and Little Remedies.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: in case of overdose, get medical help or contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) immediately even without symptoms. Per [[simethicone]]'s Contraindications: simethicone allergy.

## Symptoms Treated
Infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or diet — NOT labeled for colic, consistent with Mylicon and Little Remedies' correctly-scoped approach.
