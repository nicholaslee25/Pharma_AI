## Overview
Himalaya MindCare uses **Bacopa monnieri (Brahmi)** as its primary active ingredient — genuinely one of the better-evidenced ingredients found across this entire Brain Health/Memory Support research pass, a real positive exception to the pattern documented on [[brain-health-nootropics]]. Multiple independent, peer-reviewed, double-blind, placebo-controlled RCTs support Bacopa: a 12-week trial (125mg twice daily) found significant improvement in mental control, logical memory, and paired-associate learning; a separate 76-participant trial found a significant effect on retention of new information and a reduced rate of forgetting; and a six-week trial in medical students (using the standardized "Bacognize" extract) found cognitive-function improvements. This is a real, multi-trial, independently-reproduced evidence base — though this pass did not confirm whether Himalaya's specific MindCare formulation uses the same standardized extract/dose as the cited trials, and no brand-specific (Himalaya-named) RCT was located.
