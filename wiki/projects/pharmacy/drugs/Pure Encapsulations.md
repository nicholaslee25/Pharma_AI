## Overview
Pure Encapsulations (founded 1991) is now owned by Nestlé Health Science (acquired via the 2017 Atrium Innovations purchase), and has a genuinely strong, multi-layered quality profile: it runs its own cGMP-compliant manufacturing facility (Sudbury, MA), is NSF-GMP registered in the US and GMP-certified in Canada, and states its manufacturing standards exceed USP requirements. The brand's defining positioning is hypoallergenic formulation — products are formulated free of common allergens (wheat, gluten, eggs, peanuts, artificial additives) using only hypoallergenic plant-fiber and vegetarian capsule material, with raw materials tested for identity, potency, microbial contaminants, heavy metals (mercury, arsenic, cadmium, lead), and 32 solvent residues.
