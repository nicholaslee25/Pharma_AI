## Overview
**Major mismatched-ingredient finding.** Confirmed via DailyMed: Hyland's Baby Gas Drops is **NOT simethicone-based at all** — its active ingredients are nine homeopathic dilutions (Argentum Nitricum 12X HPUS, Asafoetida 6X HPUS, Carbo Vegetabilis 12X HPUS, Chamomilla 6X HPUS, Cinchona Officinalis 6X HPUS, Colocynthis 6X HPUS, Lycopodium 30X HPUS, Nux Moschata 6X HPUS, Sepia 12X HPUS), mechanistically unrelated to Mylicon and Little Remedies Gas Relief Drops, the two genuine simethicone-based category leaders (81% combined share) confirmed elsewhere in this database. **This product's own FDA label explicitly claims relief of "colic"** among its listed uses — directly claiming the exact use case [[simethicone]]'s Efficacy Evidence section documents StatPearls stating simethicone is NOT effective for, except this product isn't even simethicone-based, so its colic claim rests entirely on homeopathic tradition with no simethicone evidence to lean on at all. **The product's own DailyMed label carries the standard FDA homeopathic disclaimer verbatim: "This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy. FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective."** No dedicated homeopathic-GI-products ingredient page exists in this database (a data gap — [[homeopathic-cold-products]] is scoped to cold/flu, [[homeopathic-ear-products]] to ear conditions, neither to gas/colic).

## Pros
- No antihistamine/anticholinergic or systemic drug exposure — homeopathic dilutions this high (up to 30X) are generally regarded as containing negligible-to-no molecules of the original substance, consistent with [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s general safety framing that serious adverse events are rare for this product class
- Free of artificial dyes per manufacturer labeling
- Manufacturer's own label is transparent about the "not evaluated by FDA" status (a regulatory requirement for homeopathic products, not a voluntary disclosure, but still present and legible on the label rather than buried)

## Cons
- **This database's most direct mismatched-active-ingredient finding in the Infant Gas Products category:** filed alongside genuine FDA-monograph simethicone drugs (Mylicon, Little Remedies) at a meaningful 10% share, despite containing no simethicone and no established conventional pharmacologic mechanism for gas relief
- **Explicitly claims relief of "colic"** — a claim with no simethicone evidence behind it (since this isn't a simethicone product) and, per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s general Cochrane-level evidence review of the broader homeopathic product class, no convincing evidence of benefit beyond placebo for homeopathic preparations generally, though that specific review is scoped to cold/respiratory products rather than gas/colic — a genuine evidence-category gap this database has not independently closed for homeopathic GI-symptom products specifically
- Contains Argentum Nitricum (silver nitrate) at 12X dilution — plausibly negligible at that dilution level, but worth noting given silver nitrate's own toxicity profile at non-homeopathic doses, a distinction a caregiver unfamiliar with homeopathic dilution notation might not appreciate
- Per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s Key Safety Data: the primary documented risk with pediatric homeopathic products generally is delaying effective evaluation/treatment while relying on a product without established efficacy — a real consideration for a caregiver treating what could be a more serious underlying issue (e.g., cow's milk protein intolerance, GERD) as simple "gas" for 7+ days per the product's own "stop and ask a doctor" threshold
- Dosing schedule (up to 8 doses every 15 minutes, then every 2 hours) is notably more frequent than Mylicon/Little Remedies' schedule (max 12 doses/day after meals/bedtime) — a real, if minor, usability/adherence difference

## Use Cases
Per its own FDA-required label: "temporarily relieves the symptoms of gas, stomach pressure and pain, swollen tummy, restlessness, crying, burping, fussiness, groaning, gas discomfort, colic, constipation, disturbed sleep, hard tummy, and bloating" — a notably broader symptom list than the simethicone-based competitors' narrower "gas from air-swallowing or diet" framing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms persist more than 7 days or worsen. Per the product's own label: "This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy. FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective." No major food-allergen ingredients identified in this pass.
