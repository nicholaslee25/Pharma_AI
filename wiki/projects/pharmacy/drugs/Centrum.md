## Overview
**Real, unusually direct brand-to-trial link found here.** Centrum Silver is the actual product used in the landmark COSMOS trial (21,442 US adults 60+, median 3.6-year follow-up) that anchors this database's own [[multivitamins]] evidence page — Haleon (Centrum's manufacturer) supplied the actual Centrum Silver tablets and placebos for this NIH-funded, independently-conducted (Brigham and Women's Hospital-Harvard, Columbia University investigators) trial, though the manufacturer had no role in study design, data interpretation, or authorship. This is the same directness-of-evidence pattern already found for [[Cosamin DS]] (GAIT trial). Per [[multivitamins]]: the companion Sesso et al. 2022 paper found daily multivitamin use did NOT significantly reduce cancer, cardiovascular disease, or all-cause mortality risk; but the companion COSMOS-Mind/COSMOS-Web papers (Baker et al. 2023, and a 2023 follow-up) found a real, statistically significant cognitive benefit (global cognition, memory, executive function), most pronounced in participants with cardiovascular disease history. Category-leading share (33%) in this database's Multivitamins listing.

## Pros
- **Genuinely the most direct brand-to-clinical-trial link in this database's Multivitamins category**: Centrum Silver is the actual tested product in a large, NIH-funded, independently-conducted RCT — not an inference from "same product category"
- Real, statistically significant cognitive benefit (global cognition, memory, executive function) demonstrated in the COSMOS-Mind/COSMOS-Web sub-studies, with a larger effect in those with cardiovascular disease history
- No safety concerns were identified in the large COSMOS trial population

## Cons
- **The same trial found NO significant reduction in cancer, cardiovascular disease, or all-cause mortality** — the two most commonly implied "general health"/prevention benefits of daily multivitamin use were not supported in this rigorous, large RCT
- The cognitive-benefit finding, while statistically significant, comes from a trial population (60+) that may not generalize to younger multivitamin users, and the study's own authors note confirmation in a more diverse cohort is still needed
- Formulation used in the trial (Centrum Silver specifically) should not be assumed to represent every "Centrum" product line (e.g., Centrum PreNatal, Centrum Kids) without independent verification

## Use Cases
General nutrient-deficiency correction; per [[multivitamins]]'s COSMOS evidence, Centrum Silver specifically has real support for cognitive/memory benefit in older adults, but not for cancer/cardiovascular/mortality prevention.
