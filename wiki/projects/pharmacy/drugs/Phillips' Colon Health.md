This is another instance of the "Phillips'" brand name spanning multiple, mechanistically unrelated products already flagged in this database (see [[Phillips' Fiber Good Gummies]]'s cross-reference to Phillips' Milk of Magnesia and bisacodyl stimulant-laxative lines) — this probiotic product shares no active ingredient with either of those other Phillips'-branded products. No combination-specific (LGG + BB-12 as a unit) RCT was located in this pass; efficacy evidence is at the general genus/strain level per [[probiotics]] and the broader LGG literature already noted on [[Culturelle]].
