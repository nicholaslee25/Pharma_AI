## Appears In
Children's Multivitamins - 8%

## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer/CVS ingredient labeling: a standard gummy multivitamin. **Confirmed allergen finding**: contains **tree nuts (coconut, used in the coconut/palm oil blend with beeswax/carnauba wax)**, and is processed in a facility that also handles egg, fish, shellfish, soy, and tree nuts more broadly — a real, labeled cross-contamination risk profile worth flagging for tree-nut-allergic children specifically, distinct from the fish allergen already flagged for Nature Made Kids First Multivitamin elsewhere in this category. No high-fructose corn syrup, no synthetic (FD&C) dyes, no dairy, no gluten per manufacturer marketing.

## Pros
- No synthetic FD&C dyes and no high-fructose corn syrup — verifiable manufacturing claims independent of therapeutic efficacy
- Long-established brand with broad market presence in this category (8% share)
- Dairy-free and gluten-free formulation may suit children with those specific sensitivities

## Cons
- **Confirmed to contain coconut (classified as a tree nut allergen under US labeling law) and is processed in a facility handling egg, fish, shellfish, soy, and additional tree nuts** — a real, specific allergen consideration a caregiver of a tree-nut-allergic child should check before use, not obvious from "Gummy Vites" branding alone
- Per [[multivitamins]]'s general evidence review: broad disease-prevention claims for multivitamin use are not established in a large modern adult RCT; no pediatric-specific outcome trial exists for this product
- No brand-specific pediatric RCT located beyond per-nutrient ingredient-page evidence
- Gummy format's specific nutrient panel (vitamins/minerals and doses) was not fully itemized in the sources reviewed in this pass — only the inactive-ingredient/allergen list was confirmed with certainty

## Use Cases
General pediatric nutrient-gap supplementation.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Contains coconut (tree nut) — not appropriate for tree-nut-allergic children.** Processed in a facility with egg, fish, shellfish, soy, and tree nuts — a cross-contamination risk for children with any of those allergies even if the product itself doesn't list them as direct ingredients. Keep out of reach of children (general gummy-vitamin overconsumption risk, independent of any specific mineral content not confirmed in this pass).

## Symptoms Treated
