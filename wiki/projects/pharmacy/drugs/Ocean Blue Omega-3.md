## Overview

See that page for the brand's genuine, confirmed third-party quality credentials (ConsumerLab Quality Certification Program approval, Friend of the Sea sustainable-sourcing certification, molecular distillation for contaminant removal). For the cholesterol/triglyceride mechanism, see [[omega-3-fish-oil]] — standard OTC-dose fish oil offers a modest (~15%) triglyceride reduction per Cochrane evidence, without confirmed hard-cardiovascular-outcome benefit at that dose.
