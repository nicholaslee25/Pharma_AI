## Overview
**Real, significant, and independently-corroborated discrepancy found here.** Premier Protein (BellRing Brands/Premier Nutrition Co.) faces a real class-action lawsuit (filed June 2023, N.D. Cal.) alleging its ready-to-drink protein shakes and powders contain undisclosed lead at levels **more than 2x California's Prop 65 maximum allowable dose**, while being marketed as "healthful" — a real, serious contamination-vs-marketing-claim allegation. This pass located independent, non-litigation corroboration: **2026 third-party testing found Premier Protein's dairy-based powder at 0.38 micrograms of lead per serving and its RTD shakes at 0.59 µg/serving — the RTD shake result sitting 18% above California's Prop 65 safe-harbor limit of 0.5 µg/day** — meaning this is not merely a plaintiff's allegation but a finding independently reproduced by later testing. This is directly relevant to protein-powder/shake contamination concerns documented more broadly (Consumer Reports has separately reported on lead in protein powders/shakes as a category-wide issue, not unique to this brand, though this pass did not confirm Premier Protein's relative standing against competitors on that broader test).
