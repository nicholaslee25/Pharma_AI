## Overview
Vitafusion Brain Food is confirmed to use **ashwagandha (125mg)**, **phosphatidylserine (100mg)**, and B vitamins (B12, B6) in a gummy format — notably, this pass confirmed it does NOT contain ginkgo, distinguishing it from [[Nature's Bounty Ginkgo Biloba]] and [[Irwin Naturals Ginkgo Smart]] in this same category. Phosphatidylserine is one of the two ingredients in [[Neuriva Brain Health]] that independent expert re-analysis found performed no better than placebo in that brand's own cited trial (see [[brain-health-nootropics]]) — though that finding concerned Neuriva's specific combination/dose, not phosphatidylserine as a standalone compound generally, so it should not be assumed to directly transfer to this different product without its own verification. Ashwagandha has a real, separate stress/anxiety evidence base (not independently evidence-graded on a dedicated page in this database) more than a memory-specific one. See [[vitafusion]] for the parent brand's own confirmed profile (not USP Verified, real 2021 recall on other gummy products). Memory Support (7% share) is covered by [[brain-health-nootropics]] for general category context.

## Pros
- Transparent, disclosed ingredient list (not an undisclosed proprietary blend)

## Cons
- **Phosphatidylserine, one of this product's two named active ingredients, is the same compound independent expert analysis found (in a different brand's specific trial, [[Neuriva Brain Health]]) to perform no better than placebo** — this pass could not confirm whether that finding is specific to Neuriva's dose/formulation or reflects a more general phosphatidylserine efficacy question; treated here as an open, unresolved concern rather than a confirmed transfer of that finding
- Ashwagandha's strongest independent evidence is for stress/anxiety, not memory specifically — the "Brain Food" positioning may imply a more direct memory-enhancement mechanism than the ingredient's actual best-evidenced use case
- No combination-specific RCT was located testing this exact ashwagandha + phosphatidylserine + B-vitamin formula as a unit
- Gummy format contains added sugar (glucose syrup, sugar)
- See [[vitafusion]] for the parent brand's own confirmed gaps (no USP Verified status, 2021 recall on other products)
