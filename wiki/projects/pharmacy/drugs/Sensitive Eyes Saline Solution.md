## Appears In

## Overview
**Discrepancy/distinction flagged:** Sensitive Eyes Saline Solution (Bausch + Lomb) is materially different from the other two brands in this Pharmacy Times category — confirmed via manufacturer packaging/product-insert information, it is a **rinsing and storage saline only**, explicitly labeled **"not to be used as a disinfectant."** It contains boric acid, sodium borate, potassium chloride, and sodium chloride, preserved with polyaminopropyl biguanide (0.00003%) and edetate disodium (0.025%) — a preservative system sufficient to keep the saline itself sterile in the bottle, but not marketed or tested as a lens-disinfecting system in the way [[OPTI-FREE Puremoist]]'s dual POLYQUAD/ALDOX system is. This is a real, safety-relevant product-type distinction, not a minor formulation difference: a user substituting this saline for a true multipurpose disinfecting solution in their lens-care routine would be skipping the disinfection step entirely, a documented risk factor for microbial keratitis per [[contact-lens-solutions-saline]]'s epidemiological evidence. Still Class II device-regulated (21 CFR 886.5928). No brand-specific clinical trial was identified, as expected for this device type.

## Pros
- Clear, explicit labeling that it is not a disinfectant reduces the risk of a well-informed user misusing it as a stand-alone lens-care system
- Simple, well-characterized saline formulation with a long safety record for its intended rinsing/storage purpose
- Often used alongside a separate disinfecting/cleaning step (e.g. hydrogen peroxide systems, which require a saline rinse after neutralization) — a legitimate, complementary role in some lens-care regimens rather than a standalone system

## Cons
- **Category-placement risk**: being grouped in the same Pharmacy Times "Contact Lens Solutions, Saline" category (25% share) as true multipurpose disinfecting solutions could lead a consumer to assume interchangeability with [[OPTI-FREE Puremoist]] or [[Biotrue]] — it is not interchangeable, and using saline alone as a complete lens-care regimen omits the disinfection step entirely
- No antimicrobial disinfectant system in the same sense as an MPS — its preservatives (polyaminopropyl biguanide, edetate disodium) protect the bottled solution from contamination, not the lens from pathogen buildup during wear
- Smallest recommendation share in this three-brand category (25%, vs. OPTI-FREE's 45% and Biotrue's 30%)

## Use Cases
Rinsing and storing soft and gas-permeable contact lenses, typically as a component of a broader lens-care regimen that includes a separate disinfecting step (e.g. paired with a peroxide-based disinfection system) — not intended as a stand-alone disinfecting solution.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no allergy/ingredient profile in the pharmacological sense. Manufacturer label explicitly states: not to be used as a disinfectant. Never substitute for a true multipurpose or peroxide-based disinfecting system as the sole lens-care product; discontinue and seek care for eye pain, redness, or vision changes.

## Symptoms Treated
Not applicable — a lens rinsing/storage product, not a symptom treatment or disinfectant.
