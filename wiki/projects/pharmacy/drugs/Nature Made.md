## Overview
Nature Made (brand owned by Pharmavite LLC) is a genuine outlier in the supplement-quality landscape this database has otherwise found to be weakly regulated: it was the **first supplement brand ever to achieve USP Verified Mark certification** and, per USP's own count, still holds more USP-Verified products (90+) than any other vitamin/supplement brand. The USP Verified Mark is a real, meaningful differentiator — USP independently audits manufacturing facilities against standards stricter than FDA's baseline cGMP requirement, confirms label-claimed ingredients/potency, tests for specified contaminants, and continues **off-the-shelf retesting** of already-certified products pulled from store shelves (not just one-time certification). Not every Nature Made SKU carries the mark — it should be checked per-product, since this brand spans 13 categories in this database (see Appears In) — but the brand-wide quality-control posture is confirmed real and substantive, unlike most house brands in this pass which show no third-party verification at all. Category-specific ingredient evidence: Calcium Supplements (24%) — see [[calcium]]; Coenzyme Q10 Supplements (51%) — see [[coq10]]; Multivitamins (22%) — see [[multivitamins]]; Cranberry, Garlic, High-Potency Vitamin C, Iron, Magnesium, Omega-3/Fish Oil, and Probiotic lines are covered at the general ingredient-class level by their respective pages but without Nature-Made-specific trial data. "Letter" Vitamin Brand, Flax Seed Oil Supplements, and Herbal Supplement Brand are not covered by any ingredient page in this database. Formulation not verified per SKU against the USP-Verified list.

## Pros
- Genuinely differentiated quality control: first-ever USP Verified supplement brand, largest current portfolio of USP-Verified SKUs of any brand in this database — a real, checkable, independent signal most competitors in this same set of categories (Nature's Truth, Sundown, NOW, Botanic Choice, etc.) do not have
- USP's off-the-shelf retesting model addresses a real, common supplement-industry criticism (label accuracy drifting after initial certification) that this database has flagged repeatedly across other brands
- Spans 13 categories with several backed by at least moderate ingredient-level evidence (calcium, CoQ10, multivitamins — see linked pages)

## Cons
- The USP Verified Mark applies per-SKU, not brand-wide — this database did not verify which of the 13 category-specific Nature Made products in this list actually carry the mark versus which are unverified sister SKUs sold under the same house-brand name
- No Nature-Made-specific clinical trial was located for any of these 13 product lines; all efficacy evidence is at the general-ingredient level (see each linked ingredients/ page)
- "Letter" Vitamin Brand, Flax Seed Oil Supplements, and Herbal Supplement Brand categories have no dedicated ingredient evidence page in this database at all — single-vitamin and single-herb efficacy varies enormously by which specific vitamin/herb is sold under this generic listing, and this pass could not resolve that ambiguity
