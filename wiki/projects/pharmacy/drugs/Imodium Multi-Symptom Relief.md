## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Imodium Multi-Symptom Relief (formerly Imodium A-D Advanced) is **loperamide HCl 2mg + simethicone 125mg** per caplet — resolving both [[loperamide]]'s and [[simethicone]]'s prior hedge ("exact formulation not independently pulled/verified"). This is a genuine combination product, not a simple rebrand: it pairs [[loperamide]]'s antidiarrheal mechanism with [[simethicone]]'s antigas mechanism in one caplet, explaining its placement in Antiflatulence Products (4% share) despite being brand-related to Imodium (the category-dominant Antidiarrheals brand at 81%, researched separately).

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA label; genuinely combines two established, complementary mechanisms (antidiarrheal + antigas) for the common real-world symptom cluster of diarrhea WITH cramping/gas — addresses two symptoms in one product where a consumer might otherwise need both plain Imodium and a separate simethicone product
- Simethicone's zero-systemic-absorption profile ([[simethicone]]) means it does not add meaningful new systemic risk on top of loperamide's own profile

## Cons
- **Shares all of loperamide's real risks** per [[loperamide]]'s Boxed Warning: torsades de pointes/sudden death with higher-than-recommended doses, plus the same age-2 contraindication and bloody-diarrhea/bacterial-infection contraindications — a consumer choosing this product for its "multi-symptom" branding should not assume it is gentler or lower-risk than plain Imodium
- Per [[simethicone]]'s own Efficacy Evidence: the antigas component's positive evidence is a single old, small (n=41) trial — a thin evidence base for the added ingredient
- A consumer already taking plain Imodium (loperamide only) who switches to or combines with this product without realizing the loperamide dose is the same (2mg/caplet) risks confusion about total daily loperamide intake, though not necessarily double-dosing if substituted rather than combined
- No brand-specific RCT beyond the general loperamide/simethicone evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of diarrhea plus associated cramps, pressure, bloating, and gas — via the combined loperamide/simethicone mechanism.

## Allergies / Warnings
