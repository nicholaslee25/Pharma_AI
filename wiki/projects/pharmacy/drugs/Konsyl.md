## Appears In
Laxatives, Bulk/Fiber - 3%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Konsyl Original Formula is psyllium husk (labeled "Psyllium Hydrophilic Mucilloid," 6g per teaspoon) — the same evidence-supported fiber type as Metamucil (confirmed separately in this pass), one of only two fiber types ([[fiber]]'s cited meta-analysis: psyllium and pectin) shown to produce significant treatment-response improvement for chronic constipation. Konsyl is a single-ingredient psyllium product (per manufacturer, "one simple ingredient"), a genuine differentiator from Metamucil's more heavily flavored/sweetened formulations.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA OTC label; genuinely psyllium-based, sharing Metamucil's meta-analysis-supported evidence base per [[fiber]]
- Single-ingredient formulation (psyllium husk powder only) — a real differentiator for consumers wanting to avoid added flavors, sweeteners, or other ingredients present in Metamucil's flavored product lines
- Available in sugar-free and flavored (orange) variants, offering format flexibility

## Cons
- Per [[fiber]]'s own Key Safety Data: flatulence was significantly higher with fiber supplementation (including psyllium specifically) vs. control in the cited meta-analysis
- Per label: must be mixed with at least 8oz water/fluid to avoid choking risk, same general bulk-fiber caution as Metamucil
- Much smaller recommendation share (3%) than Metamucil (56%) despite sharing the same evidence-supported active ingredient — likely reflects brand recognition/marketing scale rather than any evidence-based difference, though this database did not independently confirm that inference
- No Konsyl-brand-specific RCT beyond the general psyllium/fiber evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of occasional constipation, inducing regularity — via the same psyllium mechanism as Metamucil, per [[fiber]].

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: mix with at least 8oz water/fluid to avoid choking; may cause allergic reactions in those sensitive to inhaled or ingested psyllium (same general psyllium-class warning as Metamucil).

## Symptoms Treated
Occasional constipation — via psyllium's soluble-fiber mechanism, per [[fiber]], the same evidence-supported fiber type as Metamucil.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
No discrepancy found — Konsyl is genuinely psyllium-based, sharing Metamucil's meta-analysis-supported evidence.
