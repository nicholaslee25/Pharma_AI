## Overview
Nordic Naturals has a genuinely strong, independently-corroborated third-party quality-testing record for its dominant Omega-3/Fish Oil line (12% share): the brand submits products (including its flagship Ultimate Omega line) to the independent IFOS (International Fish Oil Standards) program, which grades purity, potency, and freshness on a five-star scale, with Nordic Naturals consistently earning five-star ratings and publishing batch-specific certificates of analysis searchable by lot number. Independent reporting corroborates the brand's contaminant testing showing heavy metals/environmental contaminants at levels roughly 10x below the stringent European Pharmacopeia/CRN/GOED/IFOS thresholds. The brand also proactively responded to broader industry PCB-contamination regulatory concerns by continuing to publish third-party lab analyses — a transparency posture not found for most other brands in this pass. Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements (12%) is covered by [[omega-3-fish-oil]]; Magnesium Supplements (1%) by [[magnesium]] (no Nordic-Naturals-specific quality data was located for the magnesium line specifically — the IFOS/PCB testing findings above are scoped to the fish-oil line).

## Pros
- Real, independently-verifiable third-party purity/potency/freshness testing (IFOS five-star ratings, publicly searchable batch-specific certificates of analysis) for its fish-oil line — a genuine, checkable quality signal most competitors in the Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements category (Nature's Bounty Fish Oil, Sundown fish oil, Schiff MegaRed) do not have documented in this pass
- Contaminant levels reported roughly 10x below multiple independent industry standards (European Pharmacopeia, CRN, GOED, IFOS)
- Proactive public transparency in response to fish-oil-industry PCB contamination concerns generally, rather than only responding defensively to brand-specific incidents

## Cons
- The strong, independently-verified quality record found in this pass is specific to the fish-oil product line; **no comparable Nordic-Naturals-specific testing data was located for its Magnesium Supplements line** (1% share) — the quality claims should not be assumed to extend automatically across the whole brand
- No Nordic-Naturals-specific clinical efficacy trial (beyond the general omega-3 evidence base in [[omega-3-fish-oil]]) was located — the IFOS program verifies purity/potency/freshness, not clinical efficacy
- IFOS certification, while a real independent program, is still an industry-standard testing service the brand voluntarily submits to and pays for, not a government regulatory requirement
