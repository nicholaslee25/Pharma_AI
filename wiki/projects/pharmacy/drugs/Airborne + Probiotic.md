## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer ingredient listing: Airborne + Probiotic (Airborne Plus Probiotic Gummies) combines the brand's standard vitamin/herbal immune-support blend (vitamin A/C/E, magnesium, zinc, selenium, manganese, plus a proprietary herbal blend including echinacea and traditional Chinese medicine botanicals — Lonicera japonica, Forsythia suspensa, Schizonepeta tenuifolia, Vitex trifolia, Isatis tinctoria) with **Bacillus coagulans** as its probiotic strain. See [[Airborne]] for the parent brand's own confirmed, real 2008 $30 million FTC false-advertising settlement (unsubstantiated "clinically proven to treat colds" claims) and 2026 dosage-labeling lawsuit — both relevant brand-wide context. Bacillus coagulans is a spore-forming probiotic with real, distinct heat/acid-stability properties versus non-spore-forming Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium strains, though this pass did not locate a combination-product-specific trial testing this exact multi-ingredient formulation as a unit. Probiotic Dietary Supplements (5%) is covered by [[probiotics]] — Bacillus coagulans is a different genus than the Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium strains [[probiotics]]'s own cited AAD-prevention evidence directly covers.

## Pros
- Bacillus coagulans is a spore-forming probiotic with real, favorable heat/acid-stability properties (survives gastric transit and shelf storage better than some non-spore-forming strains) — a genuine, mechanistically relevant formulation choice
- Combines probiotic and immune-support ingredients in one product, a real convenience factor

## Cons
