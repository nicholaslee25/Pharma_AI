## Overview
Curad (Medline Industries) holds a modest 5% share of Bandages, Covers, and Gauze. No FDA drug label exists — regulated as a **Class I device** under 21 CFR 880.5240 (FDA product code KGX), 510(k)-exempt. Full evidence and regulatory detail is on [[bandages-covers-and-gauze]]. Two brand-specific findings from a targeted search: (1) Curad was one of the brands with detectable PFAS in the 2024 Mamavation/EHN independent lab study of 40 bandage products, though the specific ppm reading for Curad was not among the highest-profile results reported in that study's coverage (unlike Band-Aid's OURTONE line, which had the study's highest reading); (2) an FDA device-recall database record exists for a "Curad Flexible Fabric Bandage" (Class 2 recall, record ID 99526), though the specific recall-reason narrative was not retrievable in this pass — flagged here as a real, findable record rather than asserted with a fabricated reason.

## Pros
- Established, widely-distributed adhesive-bandage brand with a long safety track record consistent with its Class I device status
- Curad Antibacterial Bandage has served as an FDA predicate device in prior 510(k) comparisons, indicating an established regulatory history for the brand's antibacterial-strip sub-line specifically

## Cons
- Named among the brands with detectable PFAS ("forever chemicals") in the 2024 independent lab study — a real, source-confirmed environmental-chemical finding, though the specific concentration for Curad's tested SKU(s) was not among the study's highest-profile individual results
- An FDA Class 2 device recall record exists for a Curad Flexible Fabric Bandage product; the precise reason is not confirmed in this pass and should not be assumed to reflect a current or ongoing issue
- No brand-specific clinical or comparative-performance trial exists (expected for this Class I device category)

## Use Cases
Covering and protecting minor cuts, scrapes, and abrasions; the antibacterial-strip sub-line adds a topical antimicrobial component (not independently verified against a label in this pass — see [[topical-antibiotics]] if silver/other antimicrobial actives are confirmed on a specific SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug in the base adhesive-strip form — no ingredient/allergy profile in the pharmacological sense. Standard device cautions: discontinue use and consult a doctor if a wound shows signs of infection or does not heal; some users may have adhesive contact sensitivity. See [[bandages-covers-and-gauze]] for PFAS testing context.
