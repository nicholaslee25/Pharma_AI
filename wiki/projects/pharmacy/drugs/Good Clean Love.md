## Overview
Good Clean Love "Almost Naked" is confirmed via manufacturer ingredient disclosure to be a 95%-organic-aloe, water-based lubricant (organic aloe barbadensis leaf juice, xanthan gum, agar, lactic acid, potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate) with a manufacturer-stated osmolality range of 250–400 mOsm/kg. This is a genuinely, substantially lower osmolality than the three top-selling brands in this category — [[K-Y]] (2,463 mOsm/kg), [[Replens]] (2,143 mOsm/kg), and [[Astroglide]] (5,848 mOsm/kg) — per the Edwards & Panay 2015 review cited on [[vaginal-lubricants-moisturizers]]. However, the top of Good Clean Love's own stated range (400) still slightly exceeds the WHO's strict 380 mOsm/kg ceiling, even as it sits far below the category's other top performers.

## Pros
- **Real, meaningful positive osmolality contrast** relative to this category's three top-selling brands — a genuinely lower-irritation-risk formulation by the same measure that flags K-Y/Replens/Astroglide as concerning
- Organic, paraben-free, glycerin-free formulation, addressing several specific ingredient concerns raised elsewhere in this category's research
- Lactic-acid inclusion is consistent with supporting normal vaginal pH (3.8–4.5), a genuine, if modest, formulation rationale distinct from the polycarbophil-bioadhesive mechanism used by Replens/RepHresh

## Cons
- The manufacturer's own stated range (250–400 mOsm/kg) still touches or slightly exceeds the WHO's strict 380 mOsm/kg recommendation at its upper bound — "iso-osmolar" marketing language is directionally accurate relative to competitors but not a guarantee of falling entirely within the WHO's most conservative threshold
- No independent (non-manufacturer) osmolality testing of this specific product was located in this pass — the 250–400 mOsm/kg figure comes from manufacturer/retail sources, not an independent peer-reviewed measurement of the kind available for K-Y/Replens/Astroglide
- No dedicated brand-specific RCT was located in this pass beyond the general osmolality/formulation data above

## Use Cases
Personal lubrication for sexual activity (per label), marketed specifically around organic/clean-ingredient positioning.

## Allergies / Warnings
No brand-specific recall or lawsuit located in this pass. Contains potassium sorbate and sodium benzoate (preservatives) — generally low-risk but worth noting for anyone with specific preservative sensitivities.

## Symptoms Treated
Vaginal dryness/friction during sexual activity (comfort use only).
