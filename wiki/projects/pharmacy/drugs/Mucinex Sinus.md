## Appears In
Saline Nasal Moisturizers - 5%

## Overview
**Discrepancy note (naming, not mechanism):** Mucinex Sinus Saline Nasal Spray & Sinus Rinse contains **only purified water and sodium chloride** — no preservatives (no benzalkonium chloride or phenylcarbinol, per manufacturer) — a simpler formulation than several competitors. This is distinct from other "Mucinex Sinus-Max" branded products (e.g., Mucinex Sinus-Max Severe Congestion Relief), which contain **oxymetazoline HCl**, a genuine vasoconstrictor decongestant drug — a completely different product under a very similar "Mucinex Sinus" family name. A consumer must check the specific SKU carefully; this database's tracked "Mucinex Sinus" (Saline Nasal Moisturizers category) is the plain saline version, not the oxymetazoline one. See [[saline-nasal-sprays]] for the general isotonic-saline evidence. No brand-specific clinical trial was identified.

## Pros
- Minimalist two-ingredient formulation (water + sodium chloride only) avoids preservatives some users are sensitive to
- 2-in-1 nozzle design (gentle mist + power-jet) offers flexibility for mild vs. tough congestion within one product
- Backed by [[saline-nasal-sprays]]'s general Cochrane-level evidence for allergic rhinitis symptom benefit

## Cons
- **Naming collision risk:** the "Mucinex Sinus" / "Mucinex Sinus-Max" product family includes both this drug-free saline spray AND a separate oxymetazoline-containing decongestant spray — a real risk of confusing the two given how similar the names and packaging family are
- No preservatives means a shorter recommended use window after opening is typical for this formulation type (general saline-product handling caution, not independently confirmed against this specific label in this pass)
- No brand-specific outcome trial exists beyond the general saline-class evidence

## Use Cases
Nasal congestion relief and sinus rinsing for colds and everyday allergens; gentle-mist nozzle for ages 2+, power-jet for ages 6+.

## Allergies / Warnings
No active ingredient, not FDA-evaluated as a drug. General saline-class cautions apply. **Important: verify the specific Mucinex SKU** — the oxymetazoline-containing "Sinus-Max" decongestant products in the same family carry the standard vasoconstrictor 3-day-use warning and rebound-congestion risk that this plain saline product does not.

## Symptoms Treated
Nasal congestion, dryness, allergy/sinus symptoms (mechanical/osmotic saline action only — no drug effect).
