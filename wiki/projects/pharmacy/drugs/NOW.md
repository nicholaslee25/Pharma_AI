## Overview
NOW (NOW Foods) has a real, substantive third-party quality-assurance record: it was one of the first dietary-supplement manufacturers (2000) to seek GMP certification, is certified by Eurofins Healthcare Assurance to the NSF/ANSI 455-2 GMP standard, is separately audited/certified by Intertek against the Supplement Safety & Compliance Initiative (SSCI) benchmarks (CFR 111 conformance), runs ISO/IEC 17025-accredited in-house labs, and holds Informed Sport certification on selected products. ConsumerLab reported 39 of 45 tested NOW products "Approved" for quality — a real, mostly-favorable independent testing record, though not a clean sweep. Against that, this pass found real, recent quality lapses: a January 2024 phosphatidylserine label-claim recall traced to lot-homogeneity issues at a contract manufacturer, a separate January 2024 sub-label-potency recall, and an unresolved 2024 magnesium-labeling class action. Herbal Supplement Brand (1% share, this page's only tracked category) has no dedicated ingredient evidence page in this database, and the specific herb sold under this listing was not identified in this pass.

## Pros
- Genuinely broad, multi-program third-party quality infrastructure (NSF/ANSI 455-2 via Eurofins, Intertek/SSCI audit, in-house ISO/IEC 17025 labs, Informed Sport on select products) — more layered than most brands reviewed in this pass
- Favorable independent ConsumerLab testing record (39/45 products Approved) — a real, checkable outcome, not just a certification claim
- Long track record as an early GMP-certification adopter (since 2000)

## Cons

- 6 of 45 ConsumerLab-tested products were NOT approved — a real minority failure rate even within NOW's own favorable overall record
