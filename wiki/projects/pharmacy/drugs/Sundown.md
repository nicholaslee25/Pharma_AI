## Overview
Sundown (originally Rexall Sundown) has passed through several corporate owners — Royal Numico, then NBTY (2003), which later became The Nature's Bounty Co. and then The Bountiful Company, whose core brands (including Sundown and [[Nature's Bounty]]) were acquired by Nestlé Health Science in 2021. Sundown is therefore now a corporate sibling of [[Nature's Bounty]], not an independent competitor, and shares that brand's confirmed 2024 FTC "review hijacking" enforcement action — the FTC's $527,000+ refund action for manipulated Amazon reviews explicitly named both Nature's Bounty **and Sundown** products. High-Potency Vitamin C Supplements (2%) is covered by [[vitamin-c]]; Omega-3/Fish Oil Supplements (2%) by [[omega-3-fish-oil]]; Herbal Supplement Brand (1%) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.
