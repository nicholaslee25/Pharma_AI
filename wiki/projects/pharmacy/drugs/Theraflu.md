## Overview
Theraflu is another large multi-SKU family, all confirmed via DailyMed to be **acetaminophen-based** with varying add-ons: Max Strength Daytime (powder) = acetaminophen 1000mg + dextromethorphan 30mg; Daytime Severe Cold and Cough = acetaminophen 650mg + dextromethorphan 20mg + phenylephrine 10mg; Severe Cold Relief Chest Congestion Daytime (syrup) = acetaminophen 650mg + dextromethorphan 20mg + guaifenesin 400mg; Severe Cold Relief Nighttime (syrup) = acetaminophen 650mg + diphenhydramine 25mg. This is a new ingredient confirmation touching [[acetaminophen]], [[dextromethorphan]], [[guaifenesin]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]] — no prior pointer existed. Note the acetaminophen dose varies meaningfully by SKU (650mg to 1000mg per serving), which matters for total daily-dose tracking. ## Pros
- Powder/liquid formats dissolved in hot water may provide a soothing drinking experience beyond the pharmacology itself, a real (if subjective) comfort benefit during illness
- Day/Night split lets users avoid the sedating diphenhydramine component during work/school hours
- Multiple symptom-targeted variants (chest congestion vs. plain cold/cough) let users match the product to their specific symptoms

## Cons
- **The single highest acetaminophen dose found across all brands reviewed in this batch**: Max Strength Daytime powder delivers 1000mg acetaminophen per dose — approaching a quarter of the entire 4,000mg/day adult ceiling in one serving. A user taking this twice plus any other acetaminophen-containing product risks exceeding the safe daily limit quickly; this deserves explicit emphasis on this page given how close to the ceiling a single dose sits
- Nighttime variant uses diphenhydramine (first-generation antihistamine) rather than doxylamine — carries the same sedation/anticholinergic profile discussed on the Benadryl brand page, with no dedicated ingredient page in this database to cross-reference (same gap noted there)
- All the acetaminophen-liver-damage warnings on [[acetaminophen]] apply, with less margin for error given the high per-dose amount in some SKUs

## Use Cases
Daytime and nighttime multi-symptom cold/flu relief: fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion (select SKUs), nasal congestion (select SKUs), sleep support (nighttime SKU).

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products — this warning carries extra weight here given the 1000mg-per-dose daytime powder. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease. Nighttime variant: may cause marked drowsiness, avoid alcohol.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, chest congestion, nasal congestion, sleeplessness during illness (nighttime variant).
