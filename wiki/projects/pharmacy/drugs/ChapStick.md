## Appears In
Lip Balms - 9%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (ChapStick Classic Original, labeler Haleon US Holdings LLC, marketed by Pfizer): **White Petrolatum 45%**, the sole declared active ingredient, purpose "skin protectant." Camphor appears in this SKU's ingredient list but **only as an inactive ingredient** (not declared with an active percentage) — a real, confirmed contrast with [[Carmex]] (camphor 1.70% declared active) and [[Blistex]]'s lip-ointment SKU (camphor declared active alongside dimethicone/menthol/phenol), both of which use camphor as a genuine counterirritant active. ChapStick's other SKUs (12 Days of Christmas kit, Cherry, Supreme Classic Cherry, and separately-labeled Medicated and SPF variants) were not individually verified in this pass; Classic Original is presumed the primary driver of this brand's 9% share as the flagship product. See [[petrolatum]] for the ingredient evidence page.

No ChapStick-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Confirmed, simple single-active formulation (white petrolatum 45%) for the flagship Classic Original SKU
- Strong general petrolatum evidence base (see [[petrolatum]])
- Long-standing, widely recognized brand with broad retail availability
- Non-SPF formulation avoids chemical-sunscreen-filter allergy/irritation risk for consumers who don't need sun protection

## Cons
- **Multiple sub-formulations exist under the ChapStick name** (Medicated, SPF variants, seasonal kits) not individually verified in this pass — the 9% share figure cannot be attributed to a single confirmed formulation with certainty
- No sunscreen protection in the Classic Original formulation
- No ChapStick-brand-specific clinical trial was located
- Camphor's presence as an unlabeled-strength inactive ingredient (vs. a declared active in competing brands) means Classic Original does not deliver the same counterirritant/cooling therapeutic claim that camphor-active competitors do, despite camphor being present in the formula — a real, if subtle, points-of-comparison gap for a consumer comparing ingredient lists across brands without reading the Drug Facts panel closely

## Use Cases
Skin protectant for temporarily protecting and relieving chapped or cracked lips (Classic Original formulation); other SKUs may add sun protection or additional counterirritant/analgesic actives not verified in this pass.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: external use only; keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Symptoms Treated
Chapped, cracked, or dry lips (Classic Original).
