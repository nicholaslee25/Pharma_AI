## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Sarna's product line is built consistently around **pramoxine HCl** as the core anesthetic, with concentration and companion ingredients varying by SKU — **"Sarna Original"** is pramoxine HCl 0.5% + menthol 0.5% (per secondary reference sourcing); the reformulated **"Sarna Calm + Cool"** is pramoxine HCl 1% + menthol 0.5% (now also enriched with witch hazel extract per the 2024 Crown Laboratories rebrand); and **"Sarna Sensitive"** is pramoxine HCl alone, without menthol, for users who find menthol's cooling/tingling sensation irritating. All are labeled "steroid free" and indicated for temporary relief of pain and itching from minor burns, sunburn, cuts, scrapes, insect bites, minor skin irritations, and poison ivy/oak/sumac rashes. This is a consistent single-anesthetic-family brand (all SKUs use pramoxine, unlike brands that switch mechanisms entirely across sub-lines), with menthol concentration/presence as the main formulation variable — a real but modest across-SKU strength difference (0.5% vs. 1% pramoxine) worth noting for consumers assuming all "Sarna" products are equally strong.

No Sarna-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass; evidence rests on the general pramoxine/menthol ingredient literature. Sarna markets itself as the "#1 Dermatologist-Recommended" topical anti-itch lotion brand per its own manufacturer materials — a marketing claim not independently verified against third-party recommendation data in this pass beyond this database's own Pharmacy Times-sourced category share (3%, a comparatively modest share relative to that self-description, though Sarna spans additional non-poison-ivy categories like general eczema/dry-skin itch not captured in this specific category row).

## Pros
- Confirmed, consistent core anesthetic (pramoxine HCl) across all identified SKUs, with a Sensitive (menthol-free) option for users sensitive to cooling agents
- Reformulated Calm + Cool line uses the higher 1% pramoxine concentration plus witch hazel extract, a genuine formulation upgrade over the original 0.5% strength
- Steroid-free positioning may appeal to users seeking to avoid the skin-atrophy/tachyphylaxis risks documented for hydrocortisone-based competitors on [[hydrocortisone]]

## Cons

- Pramoxine concentration varies meaningfully across sub-lines (0.5% Original vs. 1% Calm + Cool) — a consumer assuming uniform strength across "Sarna" products would be incorrect
- No Sarna-brand-specific clinical trial was located; the "#1 Dermatologist-Recommended" marketing claim was not independently verified against third-party data in this pass
- Modest 3% share in this specific category (Topical Poison Ivy/Oak Remedies) despite broader brand marketing claims — context suggests Sarna's overall market position may rest more heavily on general eczema/dry-skin-itch use than poison-ivy-specific use
