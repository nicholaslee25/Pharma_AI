## Overview
up4 Women's Probiotic is confirmed via manufacturer product data as a 50-billion-CFU, 9-strain proprietary probiotic blend (including Lactobacillus acidophilus DDS-1, Bifidobacterium lactis, L. rhamnosus, L. plantarum, and Saccharomyces boulardii among named strains) PLUS whole cranberry powder — a combination product spanning both mechanisms this database covers on [[probiotics]] and [[cranberry]] separately. Per [[probiotics]]' Vaginal/Urinary Health Use section, this 9-strain blend is not the specifically well-studied GR-1/RC-14 vaginal-health combination — no brand-specific RCT for this exact strain combination was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Combines two ingredient classes with independent general evidence bases ([[probiotics]] for AAD prevention specifically; [[cranberry]] for UTI-risk reduction in some populations) into one product, a reasonable formulation logic even without a dedicated combination trial
- High stated CFU count (50 billion) and strain diversity (9 strains) relative to some single-strain competitors
- Shelf-stable per manufacturer claim, a practical usability advantage

## Cons
- **No dedicated up4-brand RCT for either the full 9-strain blend or the specific probiotic+cranberry combination was located in this pass** — efficacy rests on the individual ingredients' general (and, per [[cranberry]], often "very-low-certainty" per Cochrane) evidence rather than a product-specific trial
- Not the GR-1/RC-14 vaginal-health-specific probiotic combination with the strongest direct trial pedigree for this category's implied use case
- Cranberry-powder PAC (proanthocyanidin) content was not disclosed in the sources reviewed — the same industry-wide labeling gap already flagged on [[cranberry]] for other brands
