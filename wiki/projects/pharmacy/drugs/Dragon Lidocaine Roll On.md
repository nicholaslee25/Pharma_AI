## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: **Dragon Pain Numbing Roll-On** (brand family also marketed as "Pomada Dragon," Genomma Lab) carries **Lidocaine HCl 4%** as its active ingredient, purpose "topical analgesic" — the same confirmed concentration as [[Aspercreme with Lidocaine]]. The same brand also sells a lidocaine 4% patch format under the "Dragon Pain Relief" name, at the same concentration. This brand is already referenced on [[topical-anesthetics]] under its "Pomada Dragon" name with a 1% share of the general Topical Anesthetics category — this page adds its separate 2% share of the Diabetic Neuropathy Products category specifically. ## Pros
- Confirmed active ingredient (lidocaine 4%) matching [[Aspercreme with Lidocaine]]'s concentration — a genuine, labeled alternative at the same dose
- Lidocaine carries a materially safer methemoglobinemia profile than benzocaine per the Guay 2009 review on [[topical-anesthetics]]
- Roll-on applicator format offers a no-mess alternative to creams for direct application to hands/feet affected by neuropathy

## Cons
- Small recommendation share (2%) in this category relative to Aspercreme with Lidocaine's 33%, suggesting comparatively limited pharmacist familiarity or availability
- No brand-specific RCT distinct from the general lidocaine-molecule evidence was identified
- Standard local-anesthetic cautions apply (avoid broken/irritated skin, avoid excessive application over large areas given systemic absorption risk)
- Provides only temporary symptomatic numbing, not a treatment for the underlying diabetic neuropathy

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of minor muscle/joint pain and neuropathic burning/tingling/numbness discomfort via topical numbing.

## Allergies / Warnings
For external use only; do not apply to wounds, broken/irritated skin, or use with a heating pad. Do not use if allergic to lidocaine or other amide-class local anesthetics. Ask a doctor before use if pregnant/breastfeeding. See [[topical-anesthetics]] for systemic toxicity (LAST) and special-population data.

## Symptoms Treated
Minor muscle/joint pain; neuropathic burning, tingling, and numbness discomfort (symptomatic relief only).
