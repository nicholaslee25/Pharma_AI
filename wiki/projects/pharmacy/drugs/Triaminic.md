## Appears In

## Overview
**Same underlying brand as [[Children's Triaminic]]** (filed there as "Children's Triaminic - 2%" under Children's Cough), tracked here as its own page because the category-mapping data lists it under a distinct name string ("Triaminic," Children's Cough/Cold Combinations - 4%) — consistent with how this database handles other one-product/multiple-listings cases (e.g. the A+D family). **New finding beyond what [[Children's Triaminic]] captured:** Triaminic is not a single formulation but a small product line, confirmed via PDR/DailyMed/Kaiser Permanente drug-reference listings — "Triaminic Day Time Cold and Cough" (dextromethorphan HBr + phenylephrine HCl, the formulation [[Children's Triaminic]] documents), "Triaminic Night Time Cold and Cough" (**diphenhydramine HCl + phenylephrine HCl — a sedating first-generation antihistamine, not dextromethorphan**), and "Triaminic Multi-Symptom Fever and Cold" (acetaminophen + chlorpheniramine maleate + dextromethorphan HBr + phenylephrine HCl — a four-ingredient combination adding a fever reducer and a second antihistamine). This is the same brand-name-spans-multiple-formulations pattern already recurring across this database's multivitamin and cough-cold-combination brands, but here it's compounded by the brand's own likely-discontinued status (see below) — this database cannot confirm which, if any, of these three formulations is the one still being pharmacist-recommended at 4% share.

## Pros
- If Day Time formulation: cough-plus-congestion coverage in one product (dextromethorphan + phenylephrine)
- If Night Time formulation: antihistamine (diphenhydramine) may help with allergy-driven runny nose/sneezing symptoms alongside decongestion, though at the cost of sedation
- Historically one of the most recognized pediatric cough/cold brand names in the US, which may explain a lingering pharmacist-recommendation-share entry even if current retail availability has changed

## Cons
- **Brand discontinuation discrepancy, same as [[Children's Triaminic]]:** multiple secondary sources (Drugs.com) state the Triaminic brand name has been discontinued in the United States. This database could not independently confirm current retail availability, and if accurate, this means a "4% pharmacist recommendation share" entry may reflect stale survey data rather than a currently purchasable product — flagged, not silently resolved.
- **New multi-formulation ambiguity (beyond [[Children's Triaminic]]'s single-formulation documentation):** this category-mapping entry doesn't specify Day Time (DXM+PE), Night Time (diphenhydramine+PE, sedating), or Multi-Symptom (acetaminophen+chlorpheniramine+DXM+PE) — three materially different risk/side-effect profiles filed under one 4%-share line item
- **Historical PPA (phenylpropanolamine) safety issue** — same finding as [[Children's Triaminic]]: older Triaminic formulations contained PPA, later withdrawn from all OTC products in the early 2000s after being linked to increased hemorrhagic stroke risk in women. Resolved via reformulation, not an active current risk, but a real documented episode for this brand.
- **2013 child-resistant-cap recall** — same finding as [[Children's Triaminic]]: Triaminic Syrups (with Theraflu Warming Relief Syrups, same manufacturer family) were recalled over child-resistant caps failing to function, risking unintentional pediatric ingestion.
- If diphenhydramine-containing (Night Time or Multi-Symptom): **standing 2008 FDA/CHPA pediatric cough-cold under-4 labeling history applies**, as with any antihistamine/decongestant-containing pediatric cough-cold combination in this database
- If phenylephrine-containing (all three sub-formulations): inherits [[oral-phenylephrine]]'s FDA-flagged efficacy problem and the pending proposed removal order

## Use Cases
Relief of cough, nasal/sinus congestion, and (Night Time/Multi-Symptom) sneezing/runny nose/fever due to the common cold — specific use case depends on which of the three sub-formulations applies, which this database cannot confirm from the category data alone.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label conventions for this ingredient class: not for children under the labeled age threshold; do not combine with other decongestant-, antihistamine-, or cough-suppressant-containing products; if Night Time or Multi-Symptom, expect antihistamine-driven sedation.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, nasal/sinus congestion, and (formulation-dependent) sneezing/runny nose/fever.
