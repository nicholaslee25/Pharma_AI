## Overview
Solaray (a Nutraceutical Corporation brand) has a real manufacturing-quality credential — its parent's Ogden, UT facilities appear on NSF/ANSI 455-2 GMP certification listings, a more substantive standard than a generic self-reported cGMP claim. Set against that, this pass found a real, confirmed 2023 recall: 85,300 units of Solaray Liposomal Multivitamins (Liposomal Women's, Universal Multivitamin, Liposomal Women's 50+, in multiple counts) were recalled because the iron-containing product's packaging failed to meet the Poison Prevention Packaging Act's child-resistant requirement — a genuine pediatric-poisoning-risk compliance failure, though no injuries were reported and the company faced no legal action over it. That recall is for a different product line (multivitamins) than this page's tracked High-Potency Vitamin C Supplements (3%), which is covered by [[vitamin-c]].

## Pros
- Parent company (Nutraceutical Corporation) holds real NSF/ANSI 455-2 GMP certification at its Ogden, UT manufacturing facilities — a genuine, checkable standard above generic cGMP self-reporting
- The 2023 child-resistant-packaging recall was handled transparently (voluntary recall, public CPSC notice, no reported injuries, no litigation) rather than concealed

## Cons
- **Real, confirmed 2023 recall for a genuine pediatric-safety compliance failure** (non-child-resistant packaging on an iron-containing product) — though this affected the brand's multivitamin line, not the vitamin-C line tracked on this specific page
- No Solaray-specific clinical trial was located for its High-Potency Vitamin C Supplements line — efficacy evidence is at the general-ingredient level only ([[vitamin-c]])
- No independent finished-product third-party certification (USP Verified, ConsumerLab) was located specifically for the vitamin-C line
