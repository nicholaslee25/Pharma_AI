## Appears In
Decongestants, Oral - 4%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Allegra-D 24 Hour = **fexofenadine HCl 180mg (immediate-release) + pseudoephedrine HCl 240mg (extended-release)**, dosed once daily. This confirms the earlier "widely known as... not independently confirmed" hedge exactly as guessed. Note the pseudoephedrine dose here (240mg) is meaningfully higher than the 120mg found in Zyrtec-D and Mucinex D (both 12hr products) — because this is a 24-hour formulation delivering roughly double the daily pseudoephedrine over the full day, which is internally consistent (not a discrepancy) but worth a reader knowing before assuming all "-D" products dose pseudoephedrine identically. Touches [[second-generation-antihistamines]] (noting that page uses cetirizine as its representative agent, not fexofenadine specifically) and [[pseudoephedrine]].

## Pros
- Once-daily dosing for the full combination — more convenient than Zyrtec-D's twice-daily schedule for a user prioritizing dosing simplicity
- Same evidenced-decongestant advantage as other pseudoephedrine-based combination products in this batch
- Fexofenadine's own class-level profile (per [[second-generation-antihistamines]]) is generally considered relatively non-sedating

## Cons
- Behind-the-counter purchase restriction applies due to the pseudoephedrine component
- 240mg pseudoephedrine in one extended-release tablet is a large total daily dose — must not be combined with any other pseudoephedrine-containing product (Sudafed, Mucinex D, Zyrtec-D, etc.) without careful total-dose accounting
- Must be taken with water, not fruit juice, per fexofenadine's known absorption interaction (noted on the plain Allegra brand page)

## Use Cases
Combined allergy symptom relief and nasal/sinus congestion relief, once-daily dosing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Ask a doctor before use with kidney disease (fexofenadine), high blood pressure, heart disease, thyroid disease, or diabetes (pseudoephedrine). Avoid dosing with fruit juices.

## Symptoms Treated
Sneezing, runny nose, itchy/watery eyes, nasal/sinus congestion.
