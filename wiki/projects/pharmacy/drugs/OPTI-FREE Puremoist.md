## Overview
**OPTI-FREE Puremoist** (Alcon, category leader with 45% share) is a true multipurpose disinfecting solution — no FDA drug label (Class II device, 21 CFR 886.5928, per [[contact-lens-solutions-saline]]). Confirmed formulation: a dual-disinfectant preservative system of **POLYQUAD (polyquaternium-1) 0.001% and ALDOX (myristamidopropyl dimethylamine) 0.0006%**, plus HydraGlyde Moisture Matrix (a proprietary wetting/lubricating block copolymer for silicone hydrogel lenses), sodium citrate, sodium chloride, boric acid, sorbitol, aminomethylpropanol, and disodium EDTA. Manufacturer marketing states the dual-disinfectant system "kills 99.9% of germs" based on ISO 14729 testing against the standard 5-organism panel — an accurate characterization of that specific test, but per [[contact-lens-solutions-saline]]'s class-level evidence, ISO 14729 panel testing does not establish protection against Acanthamoeba specifically, a documented limitation of multipurpose solutions as a class (not a defect unique to this brand). No OPTI-FREE-brand-specific clinical trial isolating its disinfection performance from the general MPS class-level evidence was identified, though the brand appears in an active clinical trial registry (NCT02965820, presbyopic contact lens wearer study; NCT03466060, lens-material/solution-type comparison) whose results were not retrieved in this pass.

## Pros
- Dual-disinfectant system (POLYQUAD + ALDOX) is a genuine, verifiable formulation feature, ISO 14729-tested against the standard bacterial/fungal panel
- HydraGlyde Moisture Matrix wetting agent is specifically designed for modern silicone hydrogel lens materials, a real formulation consideration for lens-material compatibility
- Largest recommendation share in the category (45%), reflecting broad pharmacist/optometrist familiarity

## Cons
- Per [[contact-lens-solutions-saline]], the ISO 14729 "kills 99.9% of germs" claim, while accurate for the standard test panel, does not extend to Acanthamoeba protection — a real, documented class-wide limitation that applies to this brand as much as any other multipurpose solution, worth being explicit about given how the marketing claim could be read as broader than it is
- No independent brand-specific outcome trial (e.g. comparative infection rates vs. competing MPS brands) was located
- As with all multipurpose solutions, efficacy depends heavily on correct user technique (full lens rub-and-rinse, adequate soak time, no "topping off" old solution) — a compliance-dependent factor rather than a fixed product attribute

## Use Cases
Daily cleaning, disinfecting, rinsing, and storage of soft (including silicone hydrogel) contact lenses.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no allergy/ingredient profile in the pharmacological sense. Standard MPS safety cautions: never use as an eye drop; do not reuse or "top off" old solution; replace lens case regularly; discontinue and seek care for eye pain, redness, or vision changes, which may indicate infection requiring prompt evaluation.

## Symptoms Treated
Not applicable — a lens hygiene/disinfection product, not a symptom treatment.
