## Overview
Breathe Right (Kenvue) is an **external adhesive nasal strip** — a spring-tension strip worn on the outside of the nose bridge that physically pulls the nostrils open. FDA-cleared as a device for temporary relief of nasal congestion, the indication with the most consistent supporting evidence. Category leader by a very wide margin (86% share). Per [[snore-aid-devices]], a 2016 meta-analysis of 14 studies/147 OSA patients found **no statistically significant AHI improvement** (28.7→27.4 events/hr, p=0.64) and no significant change in lowest oxygen saturation or snoring index for OSA patients specifically — external strips also performed worse than internal nasal dilators in that meta-analysis's subgroup comparison. Separate smaller trials in non-OSA populations (chronic rhinitis, habitual snorers) did find reduced snoring frequency and improved sleep efficiency without a significant AHI change — meaning Breathe Right may genuinely help subjective snoring/sleep quality in simple snorers even though it does not measurably reduce apnea severity in OSA patients.

## Pros
- FDA-cleared for its core indication (temporary nasal congestion relief), the claim with the most consistent evidence
- Non-invasive, no chemicals/drugs, minimal risk beyond possible adhesive skin irritation
- Some RCT evidence for reduced snoring frequency and improved sleep efficiency in non-OSA, habitual-snorer/chronic-rhinitis populations

## Cons
- Per [[snore-aid-devices]]'s cited meta-analysis, **no significant AHI improvement in OSA patients** — this product should not be relied on as an OSA treatment despite "snore aid" category framing
- Performed worse than internal nasal dilators (like [[Mute]]) in that meta-analysis's device-subtype subgroup comparison
- Category-dominant share (86%) is concentrated in the device subtype with the weakest objective-AHI evidence among the three mechanisms in this category

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of nasal congestion; may reduce snoring frequency/improve subjective sleep quality in simple snorers without diagnosed OSA. Not a treatment for diagnosed sleep apnea.

## Allergies / Warnings
Adhesive may irritate sensitive skin. Not a substitute for medical evaluation of suspected sleep apnea (loud/frequent snoring with witnessed pauses in breathing, excessive daytime sleepiness).

## Symptoms Treated
Nasal congestion (FDA-cleared indication); snoring frequency/sound in simple snorers (per non-OSA trial evidence) — not diagnosed OSA severity.
