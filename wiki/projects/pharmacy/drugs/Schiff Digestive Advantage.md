## Overview
Confirmed: Digestive Advantage "Lactose Defense Formula" combines **lactase** (mechanistically correct for lactose intolerance, per [[lactase]]) with **Bacillus coagulans GBI-30, 6086 ("BC30"), a probiotic** — a genuine two-mechanism combination rather than a pure lactase product like Lactaid. This is a real, relevant cross-reference to [[lactase]]'s own Efficacy Evidence section: the Shaukat et al. 2010 AHRQ systematic review that page cites explicitly reviewed probiotics as a separate treatment category for lactose intolerance and found **evidence for probiotics was also insufficient** — meaning half of this product's dual mechanism (the probiotic component) has the same "insufficient evidence" verdict from this database's own cited systematic review as the lactase component itself.

## Pros
- Contains genuine lactase, mechanistically correct for lactose intolerance
- The added probiotic component (BC30) has a broader digestive-health rationale beyond lactose specifically, and Bacillus coagulans strains have their own separate probiotic evidence base (see [[probiotics]]) not limited to the lactose-intolerance indication reviewed on [[lactase]]
- Manufacturer's stated dosing (1 capsule per dairy-containing meal, up to 3/day) is more specific and food-triggered than a generic "once daily" supplement schedule

## Cons
- **Per [[lactase]]'s own cited systematic review (Shaukat et al. 2010): evidence for probiotics specifically in treating lactose intolerance was found insufficient** — the same verdict as for lactase/lactose-reduced products generally. A consumer choosing this combination product believing "two mechanisms are better than one" should know that, per this database's own reviewed evidence, neither mechanism has a strong established efficacy case for lactose intolerance specifically as of that review
- Manufacturer's own label carries the standard dietary-supplement disclaimer: "these statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration... not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease"
- No FDA Drug Facts label/DailyMed entry located, consistent with its dietary-supplement regulatory status (same pathway distinction as Lactaid and Hilma)
- No lactase potency (FCC units) was confirmed for this specific product in this pass, preventing direct comparison to Lactaid's confirmed 4,500/9,000 FCC-unit strengths
- No Schiff-Digestive-Advantage-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Marketed to support lactose digestion (via lactase) and general gut-bacteria balance (via the BC30 probiotic) together — taken with dairy-containing meals.

## Allergies / Warnings
No FDA-mandated Drug Facts warnings located (dietary supplement). Manufacturer's own label includes the standard "not evaluated by FDA" disclaimer.

## Symptoms Treated
Lactose-intolerance-related bloating/gas/discomfort (via lactase) plus general digestive-balance support (via probiotic) — though per [[lactase]]'s cited evidence review, neither component has strong established efficacy specifically for lactose intolerance.
