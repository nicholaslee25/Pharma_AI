## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer labeling: OLLY's children's line includes "Kids Multivitamin & Probiotic" (Vitamins A, C, D, E, B-vitamins, zinc, PLUS a probiotic strain) and a separate "Kids Multi" gummy-worm format (zinc, D, and B vitamins, no stated probiotic) — another instance of one brand name spanning multiple formulations, this time with a probiotic-vs-no-probiotic split rather than an iron split (contrast with Flintstones, Enfamil, One A Day Kids above). OLLY markets its supplements broadly as free of the top 9 major food allergens (soy, egg, peanut, tree nut, fish, shellfish, wheat, milk, sesame) — a genuine, verifiable positive distinguishing it from Nature Made Kids First Multivitamin (fish) and L'il Critters Gummy Vites (tree nut/coconut), both flagged with real allergens elsewhere in this same category.

## Pros
- **Confirmed free of all 9 major FDA-recognized food allergens** per manufacturer labeling — a genuine differentiator from two other brands in this exact category found to contain specific allergens (fish, tree nuts) in this same research pass
- Probiotic-containing SKU combines multivitamin and digestive-support ingredients in one product, similar in concept to Zarbee's Multi and One A Day Kids' Probiotic variant elsewhere in this category
- Beef gelatin base (rather than pork-derived) may be relevant for some dietary/religious restrictions, though this was not independently confirmed as a labeled claim in this pass

## Cons
- **Same brand-name/SKU ambiguity already flagged repeatedly in this category**: this database's category-mapping data ("OLLY Kid's Multi," 8% share) does not specify whether the Probiotic or non-Probiotic gummy-worm formulation applies, and the two have different ingredient sets
- If the Probiotic SKU applies: the specific probiotic strain/species was not confirmed in this pass, and per [[probiotics]]'s evidence page, efficacy is strain/species-dependent — this database cannot confirm whether OLLY's specific strain has dedicated supporting evidence
- Per [[multivitamins]]'s general evidence review: broad disease-prevention claims for multivitamin use are not established in a large modern adult RCT; no pediatric-specific outcome trial exists for either OLLY Kids formulation
- Contains beef gelatin (a specific animal-product consideration relevant to vegetarian/vegan or some religious dietary restrictions, distinct from the top-9-allergen-free claim)

## Use Cases
General pediatric nutrient-gap supplementation, with the Probiotic SKU adding digestive-health support framing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Free of the top 9 FDA-recognized major food allergens per manufacturer labeling. Contains beef gelatin. Keep out of reach of children.
