## Appears In
Antibacterial Soaps - 4%

## Overview
Softsoap (Colgate-Palmolive) is confirmed via DailyMed as using **benzalkonium chloride 0.13%** across its antibacterial-labeled hand-soap SKUs (e.g., Softsoap Antibacterial Kitchen Fresh Hands), matching the concentration confirmed for [[Dove]]'s Care & Protect line and [[Cetaphil]]'s antibacterial soap, per [[benzalkonium-chloride]]. Purpose: "antibacterial"; use: "helps eliminate bacteria on hands." As with other brands in this category, non-antibacterial Softsoap SKUs (plain liquid hand soaps without the "Antibacterial" label) also exist and were not individually confirmed as carrying a Drug Facts panel in this pass — only the specifically-labeled antibacterial SKU was checked.

## Pros
- Confirmed 0.13% benzalkonium chloride concentration is consistent with other brands in this category, suggesting no unusual under- or over-dosing relative to market norms
- Liquid pump format may encourage more consistent hand-washing volume/technique than a bar soap, though this is a general hand-hygiene consideration rather than a Softsoap-specific finding
- Simple, standard directions (wash hands, rinse) with no unusual application requirements

## Cons
- **Benzalkonium chloride carries no final FDA GRASE determination** for antiseptic-wash use, per [[benzalkonium-chloride]] — Softsoap's antibacterial claim rests on an ingredient the FDA has neither approved nor banned, nearly a decade after its 2016 deferral
- No Softsoap-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass — evidence is entirely ingredient-level
- Only one specific antibacterial SKU (Kitchen Fresh Hands) was checked against a DailyMed label in this pass; the broader Softsoap antibacterial product range (multiple scent/moisturizer variants) was not each individually re-verified for identical concentration
- Non-antibacterial Softsoap SKUs exist under the same brand name with presumably no Drug Facts panel — not independently confirmed this pass, but consistent with the general "Dove"/"Cetaphil"-style split of medicated vs. cosmetic SKUs seen elsewhere in this category

## Use Cases
Daily hand washing intended to reduce surface bacteria, per Drug Facts wording ("helps eliminate bacteria on hands") — general hygiene use, not a substitute for medical antisepsis.

## Allergies / Warnings
For external use only; avoid contact with eyes (flush with water if contact occurs); keep out of reach of children except under adult supervision; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Symptoms Treated
Not a treatment for an existing condition — a preventive hygiene product intended to reduce bacterial load on hands during normal washing.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Confirmed finding, consistent with this category's dominant pattern:** Softsoap's antibacterial line uses benzalkonium chloride 0.13%, the same active and concentration class as [[Dove]] and [[Cetaphil]] in this same category — all three (plus [[Dial]] at a slightly lower 0.10%) share the same unresolved-GRASE-status caveat documented on [[benzalkonium-chloride]].
