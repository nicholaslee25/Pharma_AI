## Appears In

## Overview
Ferro-Sequels is confirmed to combine iron (65mg elemental, timed-release) with **vitamin C** (to enhance nonheme iron absorption, consistent with [[iron]]'s own noted absorption-enhancer mechanism) and **docusate sodium**, a stool softener, specifically to counteract iron-induced constipation — a real, mechanistically sound dual-action formulation directly addressing one of the most common iron-supplement side effects. This combination approach (iron + absorption enhancer + stool softener) is a more comprehensive tolerability strategy than either Slow Fe's extended-release-only approach or a plain ferrous salt tablet. Iron Supplements (7% share) is covered by [[iron]].

## Pros
- **Real, mechanistically sound combination formulation**: vitamin C for absorption enhancement plus docusate sodium (an established, independently-evidenced stool softener) specifically to counteract iron-associated constipation — addresses two separate real iron-supplementation pain points in one product
- Timed-release delivery, similar in principle to [[Slow Fe]], intended to reduce GI irritation
- Vitamin C co-formulation has a real, independently-documented mechanism (keeping iron in the more-absorbable ferrous form) rather than being an arbitrary addition

## Cons
- No Ferro-Sequels-specific clinical trial was located testing the combination product as a whole (versus the individual, separately-evidenced mechanisms of iron, vitamin C, and docusate)
- Docusate's own evidence as a stool softener is itself modest per general medical literature (not independently re-verified in this pass against this database's own docusate evidence, though a [[docusate]] ingredient page does exist in this database and should be consulted for that mechanism's own evidence grade)
- Still carries the same fundamental iron-supplement risks documented in [[iron]] (real pediatric overdose/poisoning risk)
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located for Ferro-Sequels in this pass

## Use Cases
Iron-deficiency anemia treatment/prevention with built-in constipation mitigation — see [[iron]] and [[docusate]] for the respective evidence bases.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Real pediatric poisoning risk from iron overdose** — keep out of reach of children. See [[iron]] for drug interactions (levodopa, levothyroxine, reduced absorption with PPIs).

## Symptoms Treated
Iron-deficiency anemia, with adjunctive constipation prevention — see [[iron]] and [[docusate]].
