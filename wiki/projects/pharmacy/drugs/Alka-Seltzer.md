## Overview
**Major mismatched-category finding.** Confirmed via DailyMed: Alka-Seltzer Original's actual active ingredients are **citric acid 1000mg + aspirin 325mg + sodium bicarbonate 1916mg** per effervescent tablet — the product is filed in this database under three GI-symptom categories (Antacids, Nausea Remedies, Upset Stomach Remedies) with no category name signaling that it also contains a **full adult analgesic dose of aspirin** in every tablet. This is genuinely different from every other brand confirmed in this same research pass (Tums, Gaviscon, Mylanta, Gelusil, Rolaids), none of which contain aspirin or any NSAID. **Separate, equally important finding: each tablet contains ~567mg of sodium** — nearly double Zegerid OTC's already-flagged 303mg/capsule sodium load — and the labeled adult dosing of up to 2 tablets per dose means a single dose can deliver over 1,100mg of sodium plus 650mg of aspirin, entirely apart from any other medication the user may be taking for pain, fever, or blood-thinning.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA OTC label; the antacid components (citric acid, sodium bicarbonate) provide genuine acid-neutralization consistent with [[antacids]]'s general mechanism
- The aspirin component adds a real analgesic/anti-inflammatory effect (per [[aspirin]]'s Cochrane-level evidence) that pure antacid competitors (Tums, Gaviscon, Mylanta, Gelusil) do not provide — genuinely useful for a consumer with both an upset stomach AND a headache/body ache, if they are aware of and comfortable with taking aspirin
- Long-standing OTC product with an extensive real-world use history

## Cons
- **Contains a full 325mg dose of aspirin per tablet (650mg at the labeled 2-tablet adult dose) — the single most important finding in this database's Antacids/Nausea/Upset-Stomach categories**, since none of the category names ("antacid," "nausea remedy," "upset stomach remedy") signal NSAID content to a consumer. Per [[aspirin]]: this carries real GI bleeding risk, aspirin-hypersensitivity/anaphylaxis risk, Reye syndrome risk in children/teens with viral illness, and clinically significant interactions with anticoagulants, methotrexate, and other NSAIDs — none of which apply to any other brand confirmed in this same research pass
- **~567mg sodium per tablet (up to ~1,100mg+ at labeled adult dosing)** — a real, quantified sodium load exceeding even Zegerid OTC's already-flagged 303mg/capsule warning; a consumer on a sodium-restricted diet or with heart failure/hypertension using Alka-Seltzer for "upset stomach" without realizing its aspirin/antacid combination carries this sodium load would be making a genuinely uninformed choice
- Contraindicated in the third trimester of pregnancy per [[aspirin]]'s label warning — a consideration entirely absent from this database's other Antacids-category brands
- Not appropriate for children/teenagers with flu-like symptoms or chickenpox given the Reye syndrome risk — a real pediatric safety consideration this brand shares with bismuth subsalicylate (Pepto-Bismol) but not with any other brand in this same category grouping
- No Alka-Seltzer-Original-specific RCT beyond the general aspirin/antacid evidence base was independently located in this pass
- Distinct from "Alka-Seltzer Plus" and "Alka-Seltzer Plus Cold & Flu" (separately researched in this database, see [[Alka-Seltzer Plus]]) — those are multi-symptom cold/flu combination products with different SKU-dependent formulations (some acetaminophen-based, one aspirin-based), and this plain "Alka-Seltzer" brand page covers the Original antacid/analgesic formulation specifically

## Use Cases
Relief of headache, body aches, and upset stomach/heartburn/sour stomach together — via a combined aspirin-analgesic and citrate/bicarbonate-antacid mechanism, genuinely different from every other brand in this category (all of which are pure antacids with no analgesic component).

## Allergies / Warnings
**Real, brand-specific warnings not shared by other Antacids-category brands in this database: contains aspirin (NSAID) — allergic reaction risk (hives, facial swelling, asthma/wheezing, shock), GI bleeding risk, Reye syndrome risk in children/teens with viral illness, avoid in the last 3 months of pregnancy unless directed by a doctor.** Contains ~567mg sodium/tablet — relevant for sodium-restricted diets, heart failure, or hypertension. Ask a doctor before use if taking a prescription drug (particularly anticoagulants, methotrexate, or other NSAIDs), given aspirin's real interaction profile per [[aspirin]].

## Symptoms Treated
Headache, body aches, upset stomach, heartburn, sour stomach — via a combined analgesic (aspirin) and antacid (citric acid/sodium bicarbonate) mechanism.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Major finding, added to both this page and [[antacids]] / [[aspirin]]:** plain "Alka-Seltzer" contains a full adult dose of aspirin and a substantial sodium load per tablet, neither of which is signaled by any of the three GI-symptom categories it is filed under in this database (Antacids, Nausea Remedies, Upset Stomach Remedies). This is a more consequential category-name mismatch than the calcium-vs-aluminum/magnesium mismatches found for Gaviscon/Mylanta/Gelusil in this same pass, since it introduces a real analgesic/NSAID safety profile (bleeding risk, Reye syndrome, pregnancy contraindication) into categories a consumer would reasonably expect to be NSAID-free.
