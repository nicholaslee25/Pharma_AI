## Overview
Nature's Way Prenatal Multivitamin is confirmed (via manufacturer ingredient listing) to contain **400mcg folic acid** — at the lower bound of the WHO NTD-prevention range (400-800mcg) cited in [[prenatal-vitamins]], and notably below the 1mg StatPearls cites as "typical" prenatal-vitamin folate content on that same page — plus iron (9mg, on the lower end for a prenatal-specific iron dose), calcium, and a standard mineral panel, in a gelatin-capsule delivery. See [[Nature's Way]] for the parent brand's own separately-documented, real findings: a $3.4M Ginkgold false-advertising settlement, a "Made in USA" mislabeling suit, and — most directly relevant to this exact product category — a **real, litigated allegation that a different Nature's Way multivitamin (Alive! Once Daily Men's) contains undisclosed gelatin** (see [[Nature's Way Alive!]]); this Prenatal Multivitamin is itself confirmed to use a gelatin capsule, relevant to the same dietary/religious-restriction consideration even though no lawsuit was specifically located for this exact SKU. Prenatal Vitamins (3% share) is covered by [[prenatal-vitamins]].

## Pros
- Real, third-party certification coverage exists for at least some Nature's Way products (ConsumerLab, Labdoor, Non-GMO Project, NSF GMP, USDA Organic), per [[Nature's Way]]'s own research, though not independently confirmed for this specific prenatal SKU

## Cons
- **Folic acid dose (400mcg) sits at the low end of the WHO-effective range and notably below the 1mg "typical" prenatal-vitamin content** cited in [[prenatal-vitamins]] — still within the evidence-supported range, but lower than several category competitors (e.g., [[OLLY The Essential Prenatal]]'s 800mcg)
- Uses a gelatin capsule — while not the specific product named in the undisclosed-gelatin lawsuit against [[Nature's Way Alive!]], this shared brand-wide pattern (gelatin use, sometimes not clearly flagged) is relevant context for vegetarian/vegan/kosher/halal/religious-dietary-restriction users
- See [[Nature's Way]] for the brand's other real, documented litigation history (Ginkgold settlement, "Made in USA" suit) — general brand-wide context
- No independent third-party quality certification specific to this exact prenatal SKU was confirmed in this pass
