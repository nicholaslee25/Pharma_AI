## Overview
ACON Laboratories' **Flowflex COVID-19 Antigen Home Test** holds an FDA EUA and the second-largest share (33%) of this category. The manufacturer's supporting clinical study (an all-comers design, March–May 2021, 108 self- or pair-collected nasal swabs from symptomatic patients within 7 days of onset, tested unassisted in a simulated home setting) reported **93% sensitivity / 100% specificity** against an EUA-authorized RT-PCR comparator. A separate variant-specific analysis found the test detected 100% of live-virus Omicron samples at Ct-value 25.6 (higher viral load) but did not reliably detect Omicron at lower viral concentrations (Ct >25.6) — a concrete, brand-specific illustration of [[at-home-covid-tests]]'s general point that sensitivity depends heavily on viral load/timing, here demonstrated for a specific variant rather than only in the abstract. FDA also has since authorized a combination "Flowflex Plus COVID-19 and Flu A/B Home Test," a related but distinct product from the single-target COVID test evaluated here — not independently assessed in this pass. No independent (non-manufacturer) real-world validation study for the original single-target test was located in this search, unlike [[BinaxNOW]] and [[CareStart]], which both have independent studies showing lower real-world sensitivity than their own EUA data.

## Pros
- Manufacturer-reported 93% sensitivity is toward the high end of the brand-level range documented on [[at-home-covid-tests]] (34.3–91.3% symptomatic per Dinnes 2022 — notably, this manufacturer figure actually exceeds that meta-analysis's own upper bound, consistent with the general pattern that company-sponsored studies report higher performance than independent ones)
- Study design specifically simulated home/unassisted use (not a clinical-staff-assisted study), a genuinely relevant real-world validity feature
- Second-largest market share, indicating broad availability and pharmacist familiarity
- Combination Flu A/B + COVID product now available under the same brand family for patients wanting a single multi-target test

## Cons
- No independent (non-manufacturer-funded) real-world validation study was located in this pass — unlike [[BinaxNOW]] and [[CareStart]], both of which have published independent data showing their real-world sensitivity runs below their own EUA figures. The absence of an independent study here is a genuine gap, not evidence the manufacturer figure is unrepresentative — but it also means no independent check exists
- Demonstrated reduced/absent detection of Omicron at lower viral loads (Ct >25.6) in the variant-specific study — a concrete, quantified limitation rather than a general caveat
- The manufacturer study population was exclusively symptomatic (within 7 days of onset) — no brand-specific asymptomatic-population sensitivity data was located, despite the test being authorized for asymptomatic use

## Use Cases
Rapid at-home qualitative detection of SARS-CoV-2 nucleocapsid antigen from a self-collected nasal swab, for symptomatic and (per authorization) asymptomatic serial testing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no allergy profile. Standard diagnostic cautions apply: a negative result does not rule out infection; sensitivity is lower with reduced viral load (demonstrated variant-specific in this pass); confirm unexpected results with molecular testing per current public health guidance.

## Symptoms Treated
Not applicable — a diagnostic test, not a treatment.
