## Overview
Align (Procter & Gamble) is confirmed to use a single, specific, well-identified strain — **Bifidobacterium longum 35624** (formerly classified as Bifidobacterium infantis 35624), marketed as "Bifantis." This pass located a genuinely notable, real finding: an independent review of probiotic trial research for IBS symptoms found this strain was **the only probiotic shown to demonstrate significant efficacy versus placebo for IBS symptoms** among the probiotics reviewed — a real, quantified, comparative distinction, not a generic "probiotics may help IBS" claim. Multiple RCTs (three placebo-controlled, plus open-label studies) found significant improvement in a composite score of abdominal pain/discomfort, bloating/distention, and bowel movement difficulty, with anti-inflammatory properties proposed as the mechanism. This is one of the more specifically-differentiated evidence bases found in this category — most competitors' evidence is genus-level (Lactobacillus/Bifidobacterium generally, for antibiotic-associated diarrhea), while Align's is strain-specific for a different, real indication (IBS).
