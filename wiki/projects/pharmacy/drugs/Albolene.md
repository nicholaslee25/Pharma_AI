## Overview
**No FDA Drug Facts panel or declared active ingredient was found.** Albolene Moisturizing Cleanser's confirmed ingredient list is remarkably short: **mineral oil, petrolatum, paraffin, ceresin (a mineral wax), and beta-carotene** — a five-ingredient, soap-free, fragrance-free, preservative-free cleansing balm/cream that removes makeup and cleanses via an oil-dissolves-oil mechanism rather than surfactant lathering. Despite containing petrolatum (an FDA-monograph skin-protectant active elsewhere in this database — see [[petrolatum]]), no FDA drug claim or Drug Facts panel was identified for this specific product; it is marketed and functions as a cosmetic cleansing balm, not a drug.
