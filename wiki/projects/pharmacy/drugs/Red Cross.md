## Appears In
Oral Pain Relievers, Adult - 1%

## Overview
**Discrepancy confirmed and cross-referenced:** Confirmed via DailyMed, this "Red Cross" listing (Oral Pain Relievers, Adult category) corresponds to **Red Cross Oral Pain Relief**, which is **benzocaine 20%** liquid — a completely different active ingredient from **Red Cross Toothache Medication** (eugenol 85%, already documented in `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`, Toothache Products category). This is the same brand-name-spans-multiple-formulations pattern already flagged repeatedly in this database (Crest, Colgate, Sensodyne, Parodontax, etc.), now confirmed for Red Cross: "Red Cross" alone does not specify whether a product uses benzocaine (Oral Pain Relief/Canker Sore lines) or eugenol (Toothache Medication line) — two mechanistically and safety-profile distinct active ingredients under one brand name.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established local-anesthetic active ingredient (benzocaine 20%), matching [[topical-anesthetics]]'s existing documentation
- Broader labeled use (toothaches, canker sores, minor mouth irritation, denture/brace-related injuries) than the narrower cavity-specific Red Cross Toothache Medication (eugenol) product
- Gives the Red Cross brand two mechanistically different options (benzocaine local anesthesia vs. eugenol) depending on which specific product a consumer selects

## Cons
- **Same brand, two entirely different active ingredients across its oral-pain product lines** — a consumer who had a reaction to (or specifically sought/avoided) one Red Cross product's active ingredient should not assume the same applies to a differently-named Red Cross product
- Carries the full, disproportionate methemoglobinemia risk documented on [[topical-anesthetics]] for benzocaine specifically — this "Oral Pain Relief" product shares that risk, while the separate eugenol-based Red Cross Toothache Medication does not (though it carries its own distinct high-concentration local-irritation risk, per `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`)
- Not for teething or children under 2, per label and per [[topical-anesthetics]]'s pediatric flag
- No independent brand-specific RCT was identified for this specific product in this pass

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of toothaches, canker sores, minor mouth irritation, and denture/brace-related mouth and gum injuries via local anesthetic action.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: may cause methemoglobinemia — stop use and seek immediate medical attention if pale/blue skin, headache, rapid heartbeat, breathing difficulty, dizziness, or fatigue occur; avoid if allergic to local anesthetics ("caine" family); not for teething or children under 2; limit to 7 days unless directed by a dentist/doctor; discontinue if symptoms persist beyond 7 days or swelling/rash/fever develops.

## Symptoms Treated
Toothache, canker sore pain, minor mouth irritation, denture/brace-related gum injury pain.
