## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Sun Bum's lip-balm line spans **at least three different active-ingredient combinations across flavor/format variants** — a real, confirmed inconsistency, not an assumption. **Sunscreen Lip Balm SPF 30 (Coconut)**: avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octocrylene 8%, octisalate 5%. A **different Sun Bum lip balm SKU** (flavor/version not fully distinguished in this pass): avobenzone 2%, homosalate 15%, octinoxate 7.5%, octisalate 5% — a materially different filter set and concentration profile from the Coconut version despite both being labeled "Sunscreen Lip Balm Broad Spectrum SPF 30." **Sun Bum Signature Mineral Sunscreen Lip Balm**: **zinc oxide 8%** as the sole active — a physical/mineral filter, mechanistically unrelated to the chemical-filter SKUs. This is the same "same product name, different chemical filter combinations" pattern already flagged for [[Aquaphor Lip Repair]] in this subcategory, but with a third, mineral-mechanism variant added on top.

This pass covers **Lip Balms only** (1% share); Sun Bum's much larger 6% share in the Sunscreen category is not yet researched and is deferred to that subcategory's dedicated pass, where the chemical sunscreen filter actives (avobenzone, homosalate, octocrylene, octisalate, octinoxate) will get their own ingredient-evidence page(s). Zinc oxide has an existing ingredient page in this database, [[zinc-oxide]] — but that page is scoped to zinc oxide's diaper-rash barrier-protectant use, not its UV-filter/sunscreen mechanism, so it should not be read as covering Sun Bum's sunscreen efficacy.

No Sun Bum-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Offers both chemical-filter and mineral (zinc oxide) sunscreen mechanisms under one lip-balm sub-line, giving consumers a choice (e.g., for chemical-filter sensitivity)
- All checked SKUs are broad-spectrum SPF 30, a solid, consistent SPF level across the line
- Zinc oxide variant avoids the systemic-absorption/environmental concerns raised elsewhere in the literature about chemical filters like oxybenzone (not present in any Sun Bum lip SKU found in this pass)

## Cons
- **Confirmed same-name, different-actives inconsistency**: two SKUs both labeled "Sunscreen Lip Balm Broad Spectrum SPF 30" use different filter combinations and concentrations (one includes octocrylene 8%/octisalate 5%, the other octinoxate 7.5%/higher homosalate 15%) — a consumer cannot tell from the product name alone which filters a given flavor/stick actually contains
- Dedicated evidence pages for avobenzone/homosalate/octocrylene/octisalate/octinoxate have not yet been built — deferred to the Sunscreen subcategory pass
- No Sun Bum-brand-specific clinical trial was located
- Smallest tied share in this subcategory (1% for Lip Balms; the brand's much larger 6% Sunscreen-category share is a separate, not-yet-researched line item)

## Use Cases
Broad-spectrum SPF 30 sun protection for lips, via either a chemical-filter combination (varies by SKU — see Overview) or a mineral zinc-oxide-8% formulation.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard chemical-sunscreen-filter warnings apply to the avobenzone/homosalate/octocrylene/octisalate/octinoxate SKUs (specific warnings not itemized in this pass, deferred to the Sunscreen subcategory page). Zinc oxide variant: standard mineral-sunscreen precautions (avoid eyes, external use only).

## Symptoms Treated
Sunburn prevention on lips.
