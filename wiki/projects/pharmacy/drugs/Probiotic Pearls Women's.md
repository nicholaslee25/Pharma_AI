## Overview
Probiotic Pearls Women's (Nature's Way/Schiff) is confirmed via manufacturer supplement facts as a 1-billion-CFU blend of Lactobacillus acidophilus NCFM, L. rhamnosus Lr-32, and L. plantarum Lp-115, delivered in a triple-layer softgel designed to protect the organisms from heat, air, moisture, and stomach acid. Per this database's [[probiotics]] page (Vaginal/Urinary Health Use section), this is a distinct strain blend from the specifically well-studied GR-1/RC-14 vaginal-health combination — no brand-specific RCT for this exact 3-strain blend was located in this pass.

## Pros
- Named, specific strains (NCFM, Lr-32, Lp-115) rather than an undisclosed "proprietary blend" — a real transparency advantage
- Triple-layer softgel delivery technology is a genuine, verifiable manufacturing approach aimed at improving organism survival through the GI tract, addressing a real, general probiotic-category challenge (acid/bile degradation before reaching the intestine)
- Also marketed for general digestive/immune health, giving the product a broader evidence-adjacent rationale (per [[probiotics]]' AAD-prevention findings, which apply to probiotics generally, though not confirmed for these specific strains) beyond the vaginal-health claim alone

## Cons
- **1 billion CFU is a comparatively low dose** relative to several other probiotic products in this database's broader Vitamins/Supplements research (which found CFU counts ranging from 5 billion to 90+ billion across various brands) — this database did not independently verify whether 1 billion CFU is sufficient for the specific vaginal-health claim marketed
- **No dedicated RCT for this exact 3-strain combination's vaginal/urinary-health effect was located in this pass** — the same evidence-gap pattern already flagged for other non-GR-1/RC-14 women's probiotic products in this category ([[Jarro-Dophilus Women]], [[up4 Probiotics Women's]])
- Marketed simultaneously for "vaginal & digestive health" — a broad combination claim spanning two different physiological systems, without a single trial demonstrating both outcomes together for this specific strain blend

## Use Cases
Marketed to support vaginal, urinary tract, and digestive health, and to protect against occasional bloating and constipation (per manufacturer).

## Allergies / Warnings
No brand-specific recall or lawsuit located in this pass. Contains fish gelatin (softgel capsule) — relevant for anyone avoiding animal-derived gelatin or with fish allergy/dietary restrictions, though fish gelatin itself is generally considered non-allergenic in this context (a different protein than fish flesh), a distinction worth noting rather than assuming equivalence to a fish allergy.
