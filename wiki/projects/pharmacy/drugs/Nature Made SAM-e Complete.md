## Overview
Nature Made SAM-e Complete is confirmed (via manufacturer product listings) to be sold in 200mg and 400mg enteric-coated tablet formulations, with company guidance to gradually increase "up to a maximum of 1,600mg/day" for full benefit. **Real, significant dosing nuance found here**: per [[mood-health-supplements]]'s cited SAM-e literature, the clinical trials showing real antidepressant benefit (comparable to tricyclic antidepressants) generally used doses in the 800-1,600mg/day range — meaning a user taking only the base 400mg (or 200mg) tablet as a single daily dose, without following the manufacturer's own up-titration guidance, may be significantly under-dosed relative to what the supporting evidence actually tested. The manufacturer's own marketing claim ("restore healthy mood in as little as 7-14 days") is broadly consistent with SAM-e's real, substantial (50+ trial) evidence base, though only one trial has ever compared SAM-e against a modern SSRI, finding no advantage for either over placebo. ## Pros
- SAM-e has a genuinely substantial evidence base (50+ published trials, real superiority over placebo, equivalence to older tricyclic antidepressants) — one of the better-evidenced single ingredients in this entire Mood Health Supplements category
- Manufacturer provides real, specific up-titration dosing guidance (toward the higher, trial-tested dose range) rather than implying the lowest dose alone is sufficient
- Enteric-coated formulation is a real, appropriate delivery choice for SAM-e, which can otherwise degrade in stomach acid

## Cons
- **Real dosing gap**: the base product doses (200mg, 400mg) sit below the 800-1,600mg/day range used in the clinical trials showing genuine antidepressant benefit — a user not following the manufacturer's own up-titration guidance to the higher dose may not be taking an evidence-matched amount
- Only one published trial has ever compared SAM-e to a modern SSRI, finding no advantage for either over placebo — a real, important evidence gap for the comparison most relevant to today's standard antidepressant care
- No FDA review of SAM-e's effectiveness exists (dietary supplement status) — unlike an actual antidepressant medication, efficacy claims rest on the supplement literature alone
- Must be taken on an empty stomach (30+ minutes before a meal) for absorption — a real, specific adherence requirement easy to overlook

## Use Cases
Mood support, with real evidence specifically for major depressive disorder at doses (800-1,600mg/day) at or above the manufacturer's own recommended maximum — see [[mood-health-supplements]] for the full evidence context, including the real gap in SSRI-comparison data.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Should not be combined with other serotonergic medications (SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs) without physician guidance** — a real serotonin syndrome risk consideration for a compound with real antidepressant-level activity, not merely a "supplement." Take on an empty stomach per label.
