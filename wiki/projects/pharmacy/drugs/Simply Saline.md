## Appears In
Saline Nasal Moisturizers - 38%

## Overview
Confirmed: Simply Saline is an **isotonic saline solution (sodium chloride 0.65%)** with no active drug ingredient — some SKUs add disodium/monosodium phosphate buffers and benzalkonium chloride as a preservative. See [[saline-nasal-sprays]] for the full class evidence: Cochrane reviews support a real, if modest, symptom-severity benefit for allergic rhinitis and some benefit for acute URI symptoms. As an isotonic (not hypertonic) formulation, Simply Saline provides moisturizing/rinsing action without the additional osmotic decongestant effect that hypertonic products like [[Vicks Sinex Saline]] have shown in trials. It is the category leader by a wide margin (38% share). No Simply Saline-brand-specific clinical trial was identified.

## Pros
- Category leader by a wide margin (38% share)
- Isotonic formulation is gentle and broadly suitable across ages, per [[saline-nasal-sprays]]'s general safety data
- Backed by real Cochrane-level evidence for allergic rhinitis symptom-severity benefit, unlike some other high-share/thin-evidence categories in this database

## Cons
- Isotonic-only formulation does not have the additional congestion-specific osmotic benefit that hypertonic saline products ([[Vicks Sinex Saline]], NeilMed Extra Strength) have shown in meta-analyses
- Some SKUs contain benzalkonium chloride, a preservative a small subset of users may be sensitive to
- No brand-specific outcome trial beyond the general saline-class evidence exists

## Use Cases
Relief of stuffy nose, post-nasal drip, nasal dryness/irritation from colds, flu, allergies, or environmental pollutants.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not FDA-evaluated as a drug (no active ingredient); general saline-class cautions apply — mild stinging or nosebleed possible per [[saline-nasal-sprays]]; discontinue and consult a doctor if symptoms don't improve.

## Symptoms Treated
Nasal congestion, dryness, post-nasal drip, general nasal irritation.
