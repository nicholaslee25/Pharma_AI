## Overview

The label carries the standard verbatim FDA homeopathic disclaimer: "This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy." This is the same general evidence category as [[oscillococcinum]] and [[Theraworx]] elsewhere in this database — no dedicated ingredient page exists for any of these three specific homeopathic botanicals. **Important brand-line distinction:** this Neuropathy SKU's active ingredients are entirely different from the same brand's Foot/Arthritis Pain Relief SKUs, which use conventional Menthol 2% instead — both share nearly identical packaging/branding (frankincense-myrrh essential oil blend as the common inactive base), creating a real risk that a consumer believes they are buying the same active formulation across the line when they are not.
