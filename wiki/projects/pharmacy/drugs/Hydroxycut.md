## Overview
**Real, serious, historical safety finding confirmed here.** In May 2009, the FDA issued a public warning for consumers to stop using Hydroxycut products after receiving 23 reports of serious health problems — ranging from jaundice and elevated liver enzymes to liver damage requiring transplantation, and one reported death from liver failure — along with additional reports of seizures, cardiovascular disorders, and rhabdomyolysis (a serious muscle-breakdown condition that can cause kidney failure). This led to a real, confirmed recall by the manufacturer (Iovate Health Sciences). The FDA's own investigation could not conclusively determine which specific ingredient(s), doses, or factors caused the liver injuries, though the pre-2009 formulation containing Garcinia cambogia rind extract was one hypothesis considered. The product has since been reformulated and remains on the market, but this pass did not independently verify the current formulation's safety data separately from the historical 2009 event, meaning this real, serious history should be weighed even though it concerns a discontinued prior formulation. Diet Aids (6% share) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database, and Hydroxycut's current stimulant-blend formulation was not independently pulled from a label in this pass.
