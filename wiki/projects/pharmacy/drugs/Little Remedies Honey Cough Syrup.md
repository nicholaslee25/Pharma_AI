## Overview
Confirmed: a **dietary supplement, not an FDA-regulated OTC drug** — three ingredients only (honey, purified water, a natural preservative), no conventional drug active ingredient, marketed for ages 12 months and up. Draws directly on [[honey-pediatric-cough]]'s moderate-certainty Cochrane evidence for honey specifically. **Real, significant safety finding located in this pass: on 2025-06-17, manufacturer Medtech Products Inc. issued a nationwide voluntary recall of 5 lots (lot numbers 0039, 0545, 0640, 0450, 1198; expiration dates November 2025–December 2026) of this exact product due to *Bacillus cereus* contamination and loss of shelf-stability.** The affected product (UPC 7-56184-10737-9) had been distributed nationwide from December 14, 2022 through June 4, 2025. Per FDA/manufacturer reporting, no serious adverse events had been reported as of the recall announcement, but *B. cereus* exposure can cause nausea, vomiting, stomach cramps, and diarrhea, and high-level exposure carries a rare but real death risk in vulnerable individuals. This is exactly the kind of brand-specific recall this database's research protocol is designed to surface and had not previously captured for this product.

## Pros
- Simplest possible formulation (honey + water + preservative) — no added antihistamine, decongestant, or dextromethorphan, avoiding those ingredient classes' side-effect profiles entirely
- Built on honey specifically, which per [[honey-pediatric-cough]]'s Cochrane review carries real moderate-certainty evidence for short-term (≤3 day) nighttime cough benefit in children 12 months+, a stronger evidence footing than most homeopathic competitors in adjacent categories

## Cons
- **Real 2025 nationwide recall for microbial (Bacillus cereus) contamination — a genuine, brand-specific manufacturing-quality finding, not a hypothetical risk.** Consumers with product from the affected lot range (expiring Nov 2025–Dec 2026) should have discarded it per the recall; this database flags it explicitly rather than presenting the product as risk-free.
- Not an FDA-regulated drug — no FDA efficacy review applies to this product, unlike every conventional combination-drug competitor in the Children's Cough category (Delsym, Dimetapp, Mucinex, Robitussin)
- **The under-12-months honey/botulism contraindication applies directly** — per [[honey-pediatric-cough]], this product's own age-12-months-and-up labeling reflects that real, physiologically-grounded restriction; must not be given to infants under 12 months under any circumstance
- Per the Cochrane review's duration finding: benefit is strongest ≤3 days of use; beyond that window, honey has not been shown to outperform other options for cough severity or sleep impact

## Use Cases
Short-term (≤3 days optimal per evidence) relief of nighttime cough associated with the common cold, ages 12 months and up.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Not for infants under 12 months** (botulism risk, per [[honey-pediatric-cough]]). Recalled lots (0039, 0545, 0640, 0450, 1198) should not be used — check any remaining stock against these lot numbers and expiration dates (Nov 2025–Dec 2026) before use. General honey-allergy caution.
