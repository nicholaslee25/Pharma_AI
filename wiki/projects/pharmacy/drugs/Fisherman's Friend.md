## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Fisherman's Friend lozenges = **menthol**, dosed 10mg/lozenge (Original Extra Strong) or 6.9mg/lozenge (Sugar Free Cherry). Same recurring finding as Halls/Ricola — **no dedicated ingredient page exists for menthol**, see `drugs/Halls.md` for the fuller discussion. Notably, the Original Extra Strong variant's 10mg/lozenge is the highest menthol dose confirmed among the brands checked in this category, consistent with its "Extra Strong" branding being a genuine dosing difference, not just marketing. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/fishermans-friend/dailymed-fishermans-friend.md`.

## Pros
- Confirmed genuinely higher menthol dose (10mg) than most competitors checked in this pass — a real, label-backed basis for its "extra strong" positioning
- Approved down to age 2 per label (with adult supervision/doctor consultation implied for the youngest ages), broader than some competitor age floors

## Cons
- Same ingredient-page gap as Halls/Ricola — no formal evidence grade or safety profile available for menthol in this database
- Highest confirmed menthol dose in this category means the strongest sensory/cooling intensity, but also the most potential for oral/throat irritation in sensitive users — not independently studied in this pass

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of occasional minor sore throat and cough due to minor throat/bronchial irritation from a cold or inhaled irritants.

## Allergies / Warnings
Adults/children ≥2y: 1 lozenge dissolved slowly, repeat every 2 hours as needed. Children <2y: ask a doctor.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, sore throat, minor throat/bronchial irritation.
