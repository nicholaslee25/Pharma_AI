## Overview
Unlike every other product in this database's Cholesterol Management category (all fish-oil/omega-3-based), CholestOff is a **plant sterol/stanol** product (1.8g/day free plant sterols and stanols) — a mechanistically distinct approach that reduces intestinal absorption of dietary cholesterol rather than modulating triglycerides via omega-3 fatty acids. This pass located genuine, brand-specific clinical trial support from the manufacturer (Pharmavite/[[Nature Made]]): a double-blind crossover study (n=32, ages 21–79, elevated LDL) combining 1.8g/day CholestOff with a TLC (Therapeutic Lifestyle Changes) diet over 6 weeks found average reductions of **4.9% LDL, 3.6% non-HDL, and 2.8% total cholesterol** versus control; the manufacturer states three double-blind studies total support this effect. These are real, quantified, brand-specific results, though from a manufacturer-funded/affiliated study population (a real, if common, industry-funding consideration) and modest in absolute magnitude compared to statin therapy. This is consistent with broader plant-sterol meta-analysis findings (up to 12% LDL reduction at ~2g/day from food or supplement sources combined) cited independently of the brand-specific trial. Cholesterol Management (9%) has no dedicated plant-sterol ingredient page in this database — [[omega-3-fish-oil]] does not apply to this product's actual mechanism.
