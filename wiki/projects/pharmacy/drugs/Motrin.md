## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Motrin IB's active ingredient is **ibuprofen 200 mg** (NSAID), pain reliever/fever reducer — resolving this page's prior hedge ("not individually name-confirmed... the way Advil is") into a confirmed finding, matching [[ibuprofen]]. Motrin PM combines ibuprofen with diphenhydramine citrate; Motrin IB Migraine is a liquid-filled ibuprofen capsule; Children's Motrin is an ibuprofen suspension.

**Significant brand-specific safety history found in this pass, beyond general ibuprofen ingredient-class risk:** Motrin (manufactured by McNeil/Johnson & Johnson) has been the subject of multiple large product-liability jury verdicts for the rare but real Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS)/toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN) adverse reaction already documented generically on [[ibuprofen]] ("Serious skin reactions... can be life-threatening"). Notable cases: **Samantha Reckis** — given Motrin at age 7, developed TEN, lost 90% of her skin, was blinded; a Massachusetts jury awarded $63M (later reported with interest at $109M), upheld on appeal. **Christopher Trejo** — developed SJS after taking Motrin as a teenager; a Los Angeles jury awarded $48.1M. **Brianna Maya** — a 12-year-old given Children's Motrin in 2000, suffered TEN with burns over 84% of her body and blindness in one eye; a Philadelphia jury awarded $10M. Separately, McNeil (Motrin's manufacturer) was subject to a 2010 multistate consumer-fraud settlement ($33M to ~40 states + DC) tied to manufacturing quality-control lapses that triggered recalls across several McNeil brands (Tylenol, Motrin, Benadryl, Sudafed) — see also `drugs/Tylenol.md`.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established NSAID active ingredient, backed by [[ibuprofen]]'s large-scale Cochrane meta-analytic evidence
- Leading share of Oral Anti-Inflammatory Products (24%) and Oral Arthritis Pain Relievers (21%) in this database, reflecting broad clinical/pharmacist trust despite the litigation history below
- The manufacturing-quality-control issue underlying the 2010 settlement was addressed via recall/settlement and does not reflect an ongoing active safety defect in current product

## Cons
- **Real, high-profile jury verdicts document the rare SJS/TEN risk materializing with severe, life-altering outcomes (blindness, extensive skin loss) in this specific brand** — while SJS/TEN is a known, disclosed, ingredient-class-wide risk for ANY NSAID (not unique to Motrin chemically), these cases are notable, large-dollar, brand-specific legal outcomes that a family should be aware exist, distinct from the ordinary GI-bleed/CV risk more commonly discussed for ibuprofen
- The 2010 McNeil quality-control recall/settlement (affecting Motrin alongside Tylenol, Benadryl, Sudafed) is a real historical brand-trust issue, resolved but worth knowing about
- Carries the full ibuprofen risk profile documented on [[ibuprofen]] (CV thrombotic boxed warning, GI bleed boxed warning, CABG contraindication, aspirin-interaction timing)
- No independent brand-specific efficacy RCT (as opposed to the ingredient-class evidence) was identified for Motrin specifically in this pass

## Use Cases
General analgesia, fever reduction, and anti-inflammatory relief — headache, muscular aches, minor arthritis pain, toothache, backache, common cold aches, menstrual cramps.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: may cause severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin; contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding. Full boxed-warning/contraindication detail per [[ibuprofen]] applies. SJS/TEN is a rare but documented serious skin reaction — discontinue immediately at first sign of rash and seek medical attention, per [[ibuprofen]]'s existing warning.
