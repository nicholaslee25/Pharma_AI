## Appears In
Sleep Aids (non-melatonin) - 10%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Boiron SleepCalm is homeopathic — Hyoscyamus niger 3C HPUS, Nux Moschata 4C HPUS, Passiflora Incarnata 3X HPUS, Stramonium 6X HPUS. This is a distinct SleepCalm-branded combination product, NOT the same as Boiron's separate single-remedy Coffea Cruda 30C product (also marketed by Boiron for sleeplessness but sold under its own name, not part of SleepCalm's formula) — worth noting since both are Boiron sleep-related homeopathic products that could be confused with one another. Notably, two of SleepCalm's four ingredients — Hyoscyamus niger (henbane) and Stramonium (Jimson weed) — are, in their crude/undiluted form, recognized toxic anticholinergic plants; at the stated 3C/6X homeopathic dilutions, this database understands them to be diluted far below any pharmacologically active exposure level, consistent with general homeopathic-dilution principles already documented elsewhere in this database.

## Pros
- Marketed and formulated as melatonin-free and non-habit-forming — a real, if modest, differentiator for users specifically wanting to avoid both melatonin and habit-forming sedatives
- No known drug-interaction profile at the stated ultra-dilute concentrations
- Passiflora incarnata (passionflower) has some independent, non-homeopathic-dose herbal literature suggesting mild anxiolytic/sedative properties — though this database did not verify whether SleepCalm's 3X dilution delivers a comparable dose to studies of standard (non-homeopathic) passionflower extract, and the two should not be assumed equivalent

## Cons
- **No FDA evaluation of safety or efficacy** — same standing homeopathic-product finding as every other homeopathic product in this database; the product's own regulatory status carries the standard "FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective" caveat
- Two of its four source ingredients (Hyoscyamus niger, Stramonium) are toxic plants in their non-homeopathic form — while this database understands the stated dilutions to be far below active exposure, this is worth a user's/caregiver's awareness, particularly given SleepCalm Kids and SleepCalm Pellets variants also exist under the same brand for pediatric use, per DailyMed listings
- No product-specific clinical trial evidence was located in this pass supporting efficacy for sleeplessness

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of restless sleep, sleeplessness, intermittent awakening due to nervousness, worries, exhaustion, overactive mind, jet lag, or night-shift work (per label).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard homeopathic-product "ask a doctor if pregnant/nursing" convention applies. Contains lactose (inactive ingredient).

## Symptoms Treated
Restless sleep, sleeplessness, intermittent awakening (per label claim, without FDA efficacy evaluation).

Also flagged: this product should not be confused with Boiron's separately-sold single-remedy Coffea Cruda sleep product, a different Boiron item with a different (single-ingredient) formula.
