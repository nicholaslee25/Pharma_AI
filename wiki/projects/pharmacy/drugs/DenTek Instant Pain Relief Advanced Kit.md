## Appears In
Toothache Products - 8%

## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer/retailer sourcing (CVS, Amazon, Walmart product listings; consistent with the DenTek Instant Pain Relief Maximum Strength DailyMed label): this kit contains **benzocaine 20%** liquid gel with 50 disposable applicators, an applicator handle, and a carrying case — mint-flavored, maximum-strength, for quick numbing of toothaches, sore gums, canker sores, and cold sores. This is the same active ingredient and concentration as Orajel and Anbesol's core mouth-pain products, packaged with a distinctive multi-use-applicator delivery system rather than a tube or bottle applied directly. A separate DenTek product, "Temparin Max," addresses temporary filling/lost-crown repair — a mechanically different (non-drug) product not to be confused with this pain-relief kit despite similar packaging/branding.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA-recognized active ingredient (benzocaine 20%), the same well-established local-anesthetic mechanism as the category's other benzocaine-based leaders (Orajel, Anbesol, Kank-A)
- The disposable-applicator delivery system may offer more precise, hygienic application to a specific tooth/gum area than a gel squeezed directly from a tube, particularly useful for treating a single problem tooth repeatedly
- 50-treatment kit format provides an extended supply relative to a single small tube, useful for a recurring toothache before a dental appointment can be scheduled

## Cons
- Full benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk applies per [[topical-anesthetics]] — same disproportionate risk profile as every other benzocaine-based competitor in this category
- No brand-specific clinical trial evidence was identified for this specific kit beyond the general benzocaine ingredient-class evidence
- The DenTek brand also sells a similarly-named, non-drug "Temparin Max" filling-repair product — a consumer should distinguish between DenTek's pain-relief and dental-repair product lines rather than assuming interchangeability

## Use Cases
Quick, temporary numbing relief of toothaches, sore gums, canker sores, and cold sores via a disposable-applicator benzocaine liquid gel.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard benzocaine precautions per [[topical-anesthetics]]: do not use in children under 2; do not use for teething; do not use for more than 7 days unless directed by a dentist/doctor; methemoglobinemia risk — stop and seek immediate medical attention for pale/gray/blue skin, headache, rapid heart rate, shortness of breath, dizziness, or fatigue; "caine"-family anesthetic allergy contraindication.

## Symptoms Treated
Temporary numbing/pain relief for toothaches, sore gums, canker sores, and cold sores.
