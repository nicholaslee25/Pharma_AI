## Overview
Confirmed: Lastacaft's own FDA label lists **alcaftadine ophthalmic solution 0.25%** as its active ingredient — a once-daily H1-antihistamine that completed a full prescription-to-OTC switch in December 2021, the most recent of the four dual-acting antihistamines in this category to go OTC. Marketed as "Original Prescription Strength," reflecting that the OTC formulation is identical to the prior Rx version. Per [[ophthalmic-antihistamines]], no head-to-head trial of alcaftadine against ketotifen or olopatadine was located in this pass — its efficacy data comes from its own placebo-controlled approval trials rather than a direct within-class comparison, unlike the olopatadine-vs-ketotifen meta-analysis available for its two main competitors.

## Pros
- Once-daily dosing, matching [[Pataday]]'s convenience advantage over twice-daily ketotifen products
- OTC formulation identical to the prior prescription-strength product — no dose reduction in the Rx-to-OTC switch
- Newest entrant, benefiting from the most recent formulation/manufacturing standards among this category's antihistamines

## Cons
- Smallest share among the four pure antihistamines in this category (6%, tied with [[Lumify]]) — likely reflecting its more recent OTC availability (2021) vs. the decades-long OTC history of Zaditor/ketotifen
- No direct head-to-head trial against ketotifen or olopatadine was located, unlike the meta-analysis-level evidence comparing those two — Lastacaft's relative efficacy position within this specific class is less independently characterized
- No brand-specific outcome trial beyond its own approval-package placebo-controlled data was identified

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of itchy eyes due to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander (allergic conjunctivitis).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not use if allergic to alcaftadine; not for use with contact lenses if eye is red; discontinue and consult a doctor if irritation worsens or persists.

## Symptoms Treated
Itchy eyes from seasonal/environmental allergens.
