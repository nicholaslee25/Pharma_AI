## Overview

This upgrades the earlier hedge ("possibly dextromethorphan-based... not independently confirmed") to a full confirmation; the ingredient page was already using Delsym as its representative labeled product. Note this plain "Delsym" (54% share, Cough Suppressants) is the single-ingredient extended-release product — distinct from "Delsym Cough + Cold Night Time" (a separate, more ambiguous combination SKU already researched on its own page).

## Pros
- Extended-release (polistirex) technology allows 12-hour dosing vs. the 4-hour interval typical of immediate-release dextromethorphan syrups — meaningfully less frequent dosing, a real formulation advantage of this specific brand/technology
- Single-ingredient formula avoids the acetaminophen-duplication and multi-ingredient interaction risks that combination products in this batch carry
- The single largest recommendation share (54%) of any product in the Cough Suppressants category in this database

## Cons
- Per [[dextromethorphan]]'s own headline RCT (Ramsay et al. 2008), the evidence grade for dextromethorphan generally is "low-to-moderate" — it objectively reduces cough-reflex sensitivity in a validated challenge model, but the best-designed placebo-controlled trial located found **no subjective symptom benefit over placebo**. This tempers how much real-world symptom relief a user should expect regardless of the convenient dosing schedule.
- Not for children <4y per the label; MAOI interaction risk (serotonin syndrome) applies
- Structurally similar to opioids (though not opioid-receptor-active) and carries recognized dissociative misuse potential at supratherapeutic doses

## Use Cases
Cough due to minor throat/bronchial irritation from a cold; helps control cough impulse to aid sleep, per the label's own framing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with MAOIs or within 2 weeks of stopping one. Children <4y: do not use. Ask a doctor if cough persists >7 days, recurs, or is accompanied by fever/rash/persistent headache.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation.
