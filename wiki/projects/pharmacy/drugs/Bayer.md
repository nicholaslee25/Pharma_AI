## Cons

Additionally: aspirin's anti-inflammatory effect at OTC-labeled doses (325–650mg q4-6h) is real but generally considered weaker/less consistently dosed for sustained anti-inflammatory effect than prescription-level NSAID dosing (per [[aspirin]]'s dosing table, true anti-inflammatory/RA dosing runs 2–4g/day divided, well above typical OTC single-dose use) — a consumer using standard OTC Bayer Aspirin for inflammation specifically should not expect prescription-strength anti-inflammatory dosing from label-directed use alone.
