## Overview
DenTek's night-guard line spans at least three tiers with different fitting mechanisms: **Comfort-Fit** (ready-to-wear, no boiling/microwaving required, a 5-point adjustment system, FDA-cleared per manufacturer/retailer claims, BPA- and latex-free), **Professional-Fit** (boil-and-bite thermoplastic that molds to the teeth, with a soft cushioned upper layer and a firmer "grind shield" bottom layer), and **Ultimate** (a further tier not independently detailed in this pass). As with Oral-B's entry in this category, this is a physical protective device with no active ingredient — the relevant research question is device fit/material/FDA-clearance status rather than pharmacology.

## Pros
- Offers multiple fitting mechanisms (ready-to-wear vs. boil-and-bite) under one brand, letting a consumer choose based on convenience preference vs. desired fit precision
- FDA-cleared (per manufacturer/retailer sourcing) and confirmed BPA- and latex-free across at least the Comfort-Fit tier
- The two-layer Professional-Fit design (soft cushioned upper + firm grind-shield lower) is a specific engineering approach to balancing comfort with bite-through resistance

## Cons
- No clinical trial evidence was sought or is applicable to this device category in the way it would be for a drug
- The ready-to-wear Comfort-Fit tier, while more convenient, is generally understood in dental literature to offer a less individualized fit than boil-and-bite or professionally fitted alternatives, a plausible comfort/retention tradeoff not independently quantified in this pass
- "DenTek" spans multiple tiers with different fit mechanisms and price points under one brand name — a consumer should identify which specific tier they are purchasing rather than assume uniform fit/comfort across the line

## Use Cases
Protecting teeth from wear/damage caused by nocturnal teeth grinding or clenching (bruxism), across ready-to-wear and boil-and-bite formats.

## Allergies / Warnings
No pharmacological allergy profile; BPA- and latex-free per manufacturer claims for at least the Comfort-Fit tier; standard device precautions (discontinue and consult a dentist if pain, jaw discomfort, or poor fit occurs).
