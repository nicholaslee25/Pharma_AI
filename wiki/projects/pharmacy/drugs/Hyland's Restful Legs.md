## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Hyland's Restful Legs is homeopathic — Arsenicum Album 12X HPUS, Lycopodium 6X HPUS, Pulsatilla 6X HPUS, Rhus Toxicodendron 6X HPUS, Sulphur 6X HPUS, Zincum Metallicum 12X HPUS — see [[homeopathic-leg-cramp-relief]]. **Genuinely different formula from its sibling [[Hyland's Leg Cramps]]** — no Cinchona/quinine ingredient at all, despite sharing the "Hyland's" brand name. **Real, label-confirmed finding: the product's own label explicitly states it is NOT intended to treat Restless Leg Syndrome**, despite the "Restful Legs" name directly evoking that condition to a consumer.

## Pros
- No quinine/Cinchona ingredient, unlike its sibling Hyland's Leg Cramps product — avoids that specific historical-safety-signal association entirely
- Sublingual dissolving-tablet format may appeal to users who prefer not to swallow a standard tablet

## Cons
- **No FDA evaluation of safety or efficacy** — same standing homeopathic-product finding as every other product in this category
- **Real, worth-flagging name-vs-label tension:** "Restful Legs" evokes Restless Leg Syndrome (RLS), a recognized medical condition, but the product's own label explicitly disclaims treating RLS — instead framing its claim narrowly around "occasional symptoms of agitation and the compelling urge to jerk or move legs." A consumer with diagnosed RLS seeking treatment based on the product name alone would be reaching for a product that explicitly does not claim to treat their condition
- No product-specific clinical trial evidence was located in this pass

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of occasional agitation and the urge to jerk/move legs, itching/tingling/crawling leg sensations (per label) — explicitly NOT a treatment for diagnosed Restless Leg Syndrome.
