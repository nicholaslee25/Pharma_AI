## Overview
**Same mismatched-ingredient category as Hyland's Baby Gas Drops (see [[simethicone]] for the general finding), plus a real, separate manufacturing/regulatory finding.** Confirmed via DailyMed: Colic Calm Plus (marketed by CalmCo LLC, manufactured by Denison Pharmaceuticals, LLC) is a homeopathic liquid combining 12 constituents (aloe, asafoetida, carbo vegetabilis, carum carvi, chamomilla, cinchona officinalis, foeniculum vulgare, magnesia phosphorica, melissa officinalis, mentha piperita, prunus spinosa, zingiber officinale) — four more than the original "Colic Calm" (8 ingredients). The manufacturer's own marketing explicitly confirms the product does **NOT** contain simethicone, sugar, sodium bicarbonate, herbal oils, soy, dairy, wheat, gluten, yeast, animal products, or artificial flavors/colors — an unusually direct confirmation that this is mechanistically unrelated to Mylicon/Little Remedies. **Separate, more serious finding: on 2023-08-09, the FDA issued a warning letter to CalmCo LLC (previously Ketomi LLC) citing Colic Calm and "Colic Calm +" specifically** for (1) making illegal unapproved-new-drug claims (e.g., "Colic Calm eases baby's discomfort often associated with gas, colic, hiccups and teething" was cited as an impermissible disease-treatment claim) and (2) referencing a related, more serious CGMP (current good manufacturing practice) warning letter issued to the actual manufacturer, Denison Pharmaceuticals, LLC, on 2023-07-26, which found the firm failed to adequately investigate unexplained discrepancies or batch/component failures to meet specifications. The FDA explicitly stated these products were "particularly concerning from a public health perspective" given the CGMP violations combined with their intended use in infants. This database identifies "Colic Calm Plus" (this brand's category-mapping name) with "Colic Calm +" (the FDA warning letter's own naming) as the same product with high confidence, though the exact string match was not independently re-verified against the primary warning letter document in this pass.

## Pros
- No simethicone, sugar, sodium bicarbonate, or major allergen ingredients (soy, dairy, wheat) per manufacturer's own explicit exclusion list
- Non-habit-forming per manufacturer labeling
- Homeopathic dilutions generally carry a low direct-toxicity risk profile per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s general safety framing (though that page is scoped to cold/flu products, a data gap for gas/colic-specific homeopathic evidence)

## Cons
- **Real 2023 FDA warning letter citing both illegal drug claims AND manufacturing quality-control (CGMP) violations at the manufacturing facility, specifically flagged by the FDA as concerning given infant use** — this is a genuine, documented regulatory action, not a theoretical concern, and this database found no evidence in this pass of subsequent FDA closeout or resolution confirmation
- **Not simethicone-based despite category placement alongside genuine simethicone drugs (Mylicon, Little Remedies)** — no established conventional pharmacologic mechanism for gas/colic relief
- Marketing claims (colic, hiccups, teething relief) were specifically the subject of the FDA's unapproved-drug-claim citation — a caregiver relying on these claims should know they were flagged by the FDA as impermissible, not merely aggressive marketing language
- No dedicated homeopathic-GI-products ingredient page exists in this database (same gap flagged for Hyland's Baby Gas Drops)
- Shares no active-ingredient chemistry with any other product in this category — a caregiver cannot compare this product's "strength" or dosing logic to the simethicone-based competitors in any meaningful way

## Use Cases
Per manufacturer marketing (the subject of the FDA's warning letter): relief of discomfort associated with gas, colic, hiccups, and teething — claims the FDA determined constitute impermissible unapproved-new-drug disease claims as of the August 2023 warning letter.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per manufacturer: free of simethicone, sugar, sodium bicarbonate, herbal oils, soy, dairy, wheat, gluten, yeast, animal products, artificial flavors/colors. **Real regulatory concern:** the manufacturing facility (Denison Pharmaceuticals, LLC) received a CGMP warning letter in July 2023 for failing to adequately investigate batch/specification discrepancies, and the FDA explicitly flagged this as a public-health concern given the product's infant-directed use — caregivers should independently check current FDA status/resolution before use.
