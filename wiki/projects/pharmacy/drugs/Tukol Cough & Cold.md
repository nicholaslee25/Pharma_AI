## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tukol Cough & Cold Honey Flavor Syrup = **dextromethorphan HBr 10mg + guaifenesin 100mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg** per 5mL — a three-ingredient combination confirming the earlier hedge's guess that both categories (Cough Suppressants and Decongestants, Oral) reflect one combination product rather than two unrelated ones. A "Tukol Max Action Cold, Sore Throat and Cough" SKU adds acetaminophen 650mg (per 20mL) — a separate, more complex formulation. This is a new ingredient confirmation touching [[dextromethorphan]], [[guaifenesin]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]]. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/tukol/dailymed-tukol.md`.

## Pros
- Single product addresses cough, mucus, and congestion together — matches the earlier hedge's structural guess, now confirmed
- Also sold in a sugar-free variant (Tukol Sugar Free Cough and Cold, dextromethorphan + guaifenesin, no phenylephrine) for users wanting to avoid sugar, similar to the pattern seen with Safe Tussin DM and diabetic-labeled Robitussin variants
- Pediatric-strength version exists (Tukol Cough, half-strength doses) for children, separate from the adult syrup

## Cons
- Contains phenylephrine, whose OTC oral efficacy has been publicly questioned by an FDA advisory panel (2023) — see [[oral-phenylephrine]] — a real limitation for the decongestant component specifically
- Standard dextromethorphan MAOI-interaction risk applies
- The smallest recommendation shares of any brand in both of its categories in this database (1% and 2%) — reflects limited pharmacist familiarity/confidence relative to the major national brands (Mucinex, Robitussin, DayQuil) it competes against, not necessarily a safety or efficacy difference

## Use Cases
Combined cough suppression, mucus thinning, and nasal decongestion for cold symptoms.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with MAOIs. Ask a doctor before use with high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, diabetes, or enlarged prostate (phenylephrine cautions).

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, chest congestion, nasal/sinus congestion.
