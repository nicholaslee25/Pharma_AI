## Appears In
Dandruff Shampoo - 4%

## Overview
DHS Zinc Shampoo (Person and Covey) is confirmed via DailyMed as **zinc pyrithione 2%** — **double the 1% concentration** confirmed for the two much-larger-share brands using the same active ingredient, [[Head & Shoulders]] (28% share) and [[Dove DermaCare Scalp]] (6% share), per [[pyrithione-zinc]]. Purpose/uses: helps prevent recurrence of scalp itching and flaking associated with seborrheic dermatitis and dandruff.

**No head-to-head trial comparing 1% vs. 2% zinc pyrithione was located in this pass** — unlike ketoconazole, where a direct 1%-vs-2% dose-response study exists (see [[ketoconazole]]), whether DHS's 2x concentration translates to meaningfully better real-world results, or is simply a different manufacturer's formulation choice within the FDA-monograph-allowable range, is a genuine open question this pass could not resolve.

## Pros
- Highest confirmed pyrithione-zinc concentration in this category (2%, vs. 1% for Head & Shoulders/Dove) — plausibly relevant for a consumer who has not responded well to standard-strength 1% products, though this is an inference from concentration alone, not a confirmed clinical advantage
- Simple, standard directions (wet, lather, leave several minutes, rinse, repeat) consistent with category norms
- Sold as a dedicated "medicated" shampoo (Person and Covey) rather than a mass-market cosmetic-adjacent brand, which may appeal to a consumer seeking a more clinical-feeling product

## Cons
- **No trial evidence located confirming the 2% concentration is actually more effective than 1%** for zinc pyrithione specifically — the concentration difference is confirmed, but its clinical significance is not, a genuine evidence gap
- Higher concentration could plausibly carry a higher irritation risk than the 1% products, though this was not confirmed against any comparative safety data in this pass — flagged as a reasonable but unverified inference
- No DHS-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass — evidence is entirely ingredient-level
- Much smaller market/recommendation share (4%) than the chemically-similar Head & Shoulders (28%) or Dove DermaCare Scalp (6%), suggesting most pharmacists/consumers default to the standard-strength products despite DHS's higher concentration being available

## Use Cases
Dandruff and seborrheic-dermatitis-associated scalp itching/flaking, via pyrithione zinc's antifungal action, at double the standard OTC concentration used by the category's top two pyrithione-zinc brands.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; do not get into eyes (rinse thoroughly with water if contact occurs); if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control right away.
