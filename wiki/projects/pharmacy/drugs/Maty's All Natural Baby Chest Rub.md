## Appears In

## Overview
**Same category-level finding as Zarbee's Naturals Soothing Baby Chest Rub — see [[camphor-menthol-eucalyptus]] for full detail.** Confirmed via manufacturer labeling: Maty's All Natural Baby Chest Rub is **menthol-free, camphor-free, and petroleum-free** — a sunflower/castor/coconut-oil-based balm with essential oils of chamomile, lavender, dill weed, patchouli, eucalyptus radiata, and coriander, carrying a "these statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration" disclaimer. This is the third of five brands in this category confirmed to have no FDA-recognized active ingredient at all. **New allergen finding:** contains coconut oil, classified as a tree-nut allergen under US labeling law — the same allergen already flagged for L'il Critters Gummy Vites in this database's Children's Multivitamins category, here appearing in a completely different product category (topical, not oral) under the same brand-agnostic ingredient.

## Pros
- Explicitly camphor-free and menthol-free, avoiding the real camphor-toxicity risk documented on [[camphor-menthol-eucalyptus]] — labeled safe for babies 3 months+
- Petroleum-free, "clean label"-style formulation with pediatric/dermatological testing claimed by the manufacturer
- No known drug-drug interaction risk given no active pharmaceutical ingredient

## Cons
- **Not an FDA-regulated drug — no active ingredient, no Drug Facts panel, no FDA efficacy review**, despite being ranked in the same Pharmacy Times category as genuine OTC drugs (Vicks VapoRub, Mentholatum Children's Nighttime Vaporizing Rub) at a comparable recommendation share
- **Contains coconut oil — a tree-nut allergen under US labeling law** — not obvious from "All Natural" branding, and a genuine consideration for tree-nut-allergic children even in a topical (rather than oral) product, since topical exposure can still trigger contact reactions in sensitized individuals
- No RCT or clinical efficacy evidence located for this specific essential-oil combination in infant cough/congestion relief
- Essential oils (patchouli, dill weed, coriander, lavender, chamomile) carry their own uncommon but real contact-sensitization/allergy risk profile, distinct from and broader than the single coconut-oil allergen finding above

## Use Cases
Marketed for soothing/comfort relief of cold-related discomfort in infants too young for camphor-containing vapor rubs (ages 3 months+) — not a treatment for cough or congestion via any established pharmacological mechanism.

## Allergies / Warnings
For external use only. **Contains coconut oil (tree-nut allergen) — caregivers of tree-nut-allergic children should check before use.** Contains multiple essential oils (chamomile, lavender, dill weed, patchouli, eucalyptus radiata, coriander) with individual, uncommon contact-sensitization risk profiles not addressed by a single unified allergen framework.

## Symptoms Treated
No established pharmacological treatment of any specific symptom — marketed for general sensory/comfort relief during a cold, mechanistically unproven for actual cough or congestion relief.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
