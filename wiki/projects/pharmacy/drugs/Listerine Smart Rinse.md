## Appears In
Children's Mouthwashes - 15%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (Pink Lemonade and Bubble Blast flavors): Listerine Smart Rinse Anticavity Fluoride Rinse is **sodium fluoride 0.02% (0.01% w/v fluoride ion, 0.1mg/mL)**, the same concentration as this category's #2 brand, Colgate Kids (see `drugs/Colgate Kids.md`) — and **half the fluoride-ion concentration of the category leader, ACT Kids Anticavity** (sodium fluoride 0.05%, 0.02% w/v fluoride ion; see `drugs/ACT Kids Anticavity.md`). This extends the fluoride-concentration comparison already flagged between ACT Kids and Colgate Kids to a third brand in the same category, confirming a real two-tier split (0.02% vs. 0.05% NaF) across the four ranked Children's Mouthwashes brands rather than a uniform industry standard.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established anticavity active ingredient (sodium fluoride), backed by [[fluoride-toothpaste]]'s strong general fluoride evidence base (rinse-specific RCT evidence not independently confirmed, same caveat as ACT Kids/Colgate Kids)
- Alcohol-free formulation, ADA-accepted per manufacturer labeling — relevant given alcohol content is a common caregiver concern for pediatric oral rinses
- Same fluoride-ion concentration as the #2 brand (Colgate Kids), a lower-potency option than ACT Kids Anticavity that may be preferred for younger/lower-risk children per a dentist's guidance

## Cons
- Lower fluoride-ion concentration than the category leader (ACT Kids Anticavity) — a caregiver assuming all children's anticavity rinses in this category deliver equivalent fluoride dosing would be incorrect
- Labeled for ages 6+ with adult supervision required for under 12; under 6, consult a dentist or doctor — not appropriate for younger children given fluorosis risk during tooth development (per [[fluoride-toothpaste]]'s general fluorosis-risk framing)
- Contains FD&C Red No. 40 and D&C Red No. 33 (Pink Lemonade flavor) as inactive ingredients — synthetic dyes some parents specifically try to avoid, not disclosed as a marketed feature
- No independent, Listerine-brand-specific rinse efficacy RCT was identified in this pass — relies on the general fluoride ingredient-class evidence, which is itself toothpaste-focused rather than rinse-focused per [[fluoride-toothpaste]]'s own sourcing

## Use Cases
Cavity prevention via daily fluoride mouth rinse, ages 6+, twice daily after brushing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: keep out of reach of children; if more than used for rinsing is accidentally swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away; do not swallow; avoid eating/drinking for 30 minutes after use; children under 12 require adult supervision; under 6, consult a dentist or doctor. Contains synthetic dyes (FD&C Red No. 40, D&C Red No. 33) in at least the Pink Lemonade flavor — relevant for dye-sensitive individuals, though not a recognized major food allergen.
