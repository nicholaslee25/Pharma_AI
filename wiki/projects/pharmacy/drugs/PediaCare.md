## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: "PediaCare" is an umbrella brand spanning **at least three distinct formulations** — "Children's Cough and Cold" (brompheniramine maleate + dextromethorphan HBr + phenylephrine HCl, a triple combination), "Children's Cough & Cold" (dextromethorphan + guaifenesin only, no antihistamine/decongestant), and "Children's Cough & Congestion" (brompheniramine + dextromethorphan + phenylephrine). This is the most extreme example yet in this batch of a single brand name spanning multiple, materially different active-ingredient combinations — more formulations under one name than Dimetapp, Mucinex, Delsym, Robitussin, or Sudafed PE above. This database's category-mapping data lists a single "PediaCare" entry for each of Children's Cough (2%) and Children's Cough/Cold Combinations (4%) without SKU specificity. Note also: `drugs/PediaCare Gas Relief Drops.md` is a completely unrelated simethicone-based product filed separately under Infant Gas Products — the PediaCare brand extends across mechanistically unrelated product categories entirely, not just formulation variants within one category.

## Pros
- Broad product-line coverage lets a caregiver select a formulation matched to a child's specific symptom profile (cough-only via guaifenesin/DXM, vs. cough-plus-allergy-plus-congestion via the triple combination) if the correct SKU is identified
- Every confirmed active ingredient (dextromethorphan, guaifenesin, brompheniramine, phenylephrine) has some evidence-page coverage in this database, even where evidence grades are weak

## Cons
- **The most pronounced brand-name/SKU ambiguity documented in this research batch**: with three-plus distinct PediaCare cough/cold formulations confirmed, and no SKU-level specificity in this database's category-mapping data, the actual active-ingredient exposure behind PediaCare's 2%/4% shares cannot be determined with any confidence — this is a genuine, material data gap, not a minor caveat
- If a phenylephrine-containing SKU applies: inherits [[oral-phenylephrine]]'s FDA-flagged efficacy problem and the pending November 2024 proposed removal order
- If a brompheniramine-containing SKU applies: inherits the same no-dedicated-ingredient-page gap already flagged for Dimetapp and PediaCare's own triple-combination sibling products
- **Standing 2008 FDA/CHPA pediatric cough-cold labeling history applies** to any of PediaCare's combination SKUs
- No brand-specific pediatric RCT located for any PediaCare formulation beyond individual-ingredient evidence

## Use Cases
Cough relief (guaifenesin/DXM SKU); combined cough, congestion, and allergy-symptom relief (triple-combination SKUs) — specific use case depends entirely on which PediaCare product is purchased.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label (all confirmed SKUs): do not use with any other product containing dextromethorphan, guaifenesin, an antihistamine, or a decongestant; ask a doctor before use if the child has heart disease, high blood pressure, thyroid disease, diabetes, or a breathing problem; age restrictions vary by specific SKU (verify against the individual product's own label before use).

## Symptoms Treated
Cough (all SKUs); nasal/sinus congestion and allergy symptoms (phenylephrine/brompheniramine-containing SKUs only).

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**New finding for this database:** PediaCare's brand-name/SKU ambiguity is the most extensive documented in this Children's Cough/Cold Combinations research pass, following the same pattern already raised for Delsym, Robitussin, and Sudafed PE — collectively, this suggests this database's brand-level (rather than SKU-level) category-mapping data systematically understates real-world formulation heterogeneity within the Children's Cough/Cold Combinations category specifically. Worth flagging as a category-wide methodology note, not just a PediaCare-specific one.
