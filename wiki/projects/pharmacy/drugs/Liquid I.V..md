## Overview
Liquid I.V. presents a genuinely two-sided picture. On the positive side, this pass located real clinical trial support: a 2025 trial found both the original and Sugar-Free Hydration Multiplier formulas delivered superior hydration versus water alone, with the Sugar-Free formula backed by "more than ten completed and ongoing clinical trials" per brand sourcing, and a separate 4-week safety study found no clinically meaningful adverse changes across blood panels, thyroid function, urinalysis, or vital signs at 1-3 servings/day. On the negative side, this pass confirmed a **real, active class-action lawsuit** alleging Liquid I.V.'s "0 sugar"/"sugar-free" marketing for certain powdered drink mixes is misleading — the products reportedly contain 4-5g of **allulose per serving, which the lawsuit alleges is legally classified as a sugar** under applicable federal and California food-labeling standards, alongside allegations of unsupported "bold health statements." This is a real, current, directly-relevant marketing-claim controversy specifically about this brand's headline differentiator (sugar-free positioning) — not a resolved matter as of this pass. Hydration Support (28% share) is covered by [[oral-rehydration-electrolytes]].
