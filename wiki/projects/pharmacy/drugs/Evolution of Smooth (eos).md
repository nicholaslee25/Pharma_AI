## Overview
**No FDA Drug Facts panel or declared active ingredient was found for eos lip balm products.** eos's own marketing explicitly states its lip balms are "petrolatum free," and the ingredients found (sunflower seed oil, beeswax, coconut oil, shea butter, jojoba oil, tocopherols) are cosmetic emollients/moisturizers with no skin-protectant or sunscreen drug claim identified — consistent with the same cosmetic-not-drug pattern found on [[e.l.f.]] and [[Nivea]]'s base line in this subcategory.

**A real, documented consumer-safety event exists for this specific brand:** in January 2016, a class-action lawsuit was filed (Los Angeles) alleging eos's "Summer Fruit" lip balm caused a consumer's lips to blister and the surrounding skin to develop a severe rash requiring medical care; several related class actions followed. **eos settled the consolidated lawsuits, without admitting liability**, agreeing to add a warning to packaging/website and to allow affected consumers to file compensation claims. eos maintained (and maintains) that its products are safe. This is a confirmed, real regulatory/legal event, not a current safety finding against the product's ingredients — no FDA recall or ingredient-hazard determination was identified alongside it.

No eos-brand-specific clinical trial (efficacy or safety) beyond the lawsuit history above was located.
