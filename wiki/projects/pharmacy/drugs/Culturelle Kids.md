## Appears In
Children's Multivitamins - 3%

## Overview
Confirmed via manufacturer/retailer labeling: "Culturelle Kids" is itself a multi-SKU sub-line, not a single product — at minimum "Kids Complete Chewable Multivitamin + Probiotic," "Kids Multivitamin + Probiotic Chewables," and a separate probiotic-only "Kids Daily Probiotic Packets" (no multivitamin content at all) were identified. The multivitamin-containing chewables combine 11 vitamins/minerals (Vitamins A, C, D, E, B6, folate, B12, biotin, pantothenic acid, iodine, zinc) with the probiotic strain **Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG (LGG)** — the same specific, well-studied strain the adult "Culturelle" brand is built on per its market identity (see [[probiotics]]'s Brands Using This Ingredient note, previously unconfirmed against a label; this pediatric-line confirmation is the first label-level strain confirmation for the Culturelle brand family in this database). Since the category-mapping data ("Culturelle Kids," 3% share) is filed under Children's Multivitamins specifically, this should refer to one of the multivitamin+probiotic chewable SKUs, not the probiotic-only packets — but which specific multivitamin+probiotic SKU was not independently confirmed.

## Pros
- **First label-level confirmation of Culturelle's probiotic strain (Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG) in this database** — LGG is one of the more extensively studied individual probiotic strains generally, though this database's [[probiotics]] page notes efficacy is strain/species-dependent and does not have a pediatric-multivitamin-combination-specific trial for this exact product
- Combines multivitamin and probiotic supplementation in one product, similar in concept to OLLY Kid's Multi's probiotic SKU and Zarbee's Multi elsewhere in this category
- Sugar-free, non-GMO, gluten-free, vegan, and dairy-free formulation per manufacturer labeling (multivitamin+probiotic chewable SKU)

## Cons
- **Same brand-name/SKU ambiguity flagged repeatedly in this category**: "Culturelle Kids" spans at least three distinct SKUs, one of which (Kids Daily Probiotic Packets) contains no multivitamin content at all and would not belong in this category by ingredient — this database cannot confirm the exact multivitamin+probiotic SKU behind the 3% share figure
- **Dairy cross-contamination note**: though no dairy-derived ingredients are listed, the product is manufactured in a facility that also handles dairy — a real (if lower-severity) consideration for dairy-allergic children, similar in kind to the tree-nut/fish facility cross-contamination findings already flagged for L'il Critters Gummy Vites and Nature Made Kids First Multivitamin in this same category
- Per [[multivitamins]]'s general evidence review: broad disease-prevention claims for multivitamin use are not established in a large modern adult RCT; no pediatric-specific outcome trial exists combining LGG with this specific multivitamin panel
- Per [[probiotics]]'s general evidence review: probiotic efficacy is strain/species/indication-dependent — LGG's own evidence base is not itemized in this database's [[probiotics]] page beyond brand-identity association, so this database cannot make a specific efficacy claim for this combination product

## Use Cases
General pediatric nutrient-gap supplementation combined with probiotic/digestive-health support framing, ages 3+.

## Allergies / Warnings
Sugar-free, gluten-free, dairy-free formulation (multivitamin+probiotic chewable SKU) per manufacturer labeling, but manufactured in a facility that also handles dairy ingredients. Keep out of reach of children.

## Symptoms Treated
General nutrient-gap correction plus probiotic/digestive-health support framing; not an established treatment for any specific disease.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**New finding, added to both this page and [[multivitamins]]/[[probiotics]]:** Culturelle Kids is the sixth brand in this Children's Multivitamins category confirmed to span multiple formulations under one umbrella name, uniquely because one of its sibling SKUs (Kids Daily Probiotic Packets) doesn't contain a multivitamin at all — a more extreme version of the ambiguity problem than a simple iron/no-iron or probiotic/no-probiotic split. Also the first label-confirmed probiotic strain (LGG) for the Culturelle brand family in this database, useful for whoever researches the separate adult Culturelle/Culturelle Probiotic pages later.
