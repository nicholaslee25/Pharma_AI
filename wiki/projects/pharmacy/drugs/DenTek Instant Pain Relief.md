## Appears In
Oral Pain Relievers, Adult - 3%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: DenTek Instant Pain Relief (Maximum Strength) is **benzocaine 20%** liquid, sold as a 50-treatment kit with disposable applicators — a standard benzocaine oral-pain-reliever formulation matching [[topical-anesthetics]]'s existing documentation for this ingredient class. Same active ingredient/concentration as Orajel 2X, Anbesol Maximum Strength, and Red Cross Oral Pain Relief (see `drugs/Red Cross.md`), all already documented in this database.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established local-anesthetic active ingredient, matching [[topical-anesthetics]]'s benzocaine documentation
- Disposable-applicator kit format (50 treatments) avoids repeated finger/cotton-swab contact with the oral lesion, a hygiene-adjacent usability feature distinct from tube/gel competitors
- Same proven 20% concentration as the category's other confirmed benzocaine leaders (Orajel, Anbesol)

## Cons
- **Carries the full, disproportionate methemoglobinemia risk documented on [[topical-anesthetics]]** for benzocaine specifically (Guay 2009: ~66% of 242 reviewed methemoglobinemia cases involved benzocaine; risk can occur even with prior uneventful use, and can spike ~20-fold with repeat exposure within a week) — the label's own methemoglobinemia warning explicitly states this can occur "even if you have used this product before"
- Not for use in children under 2, or for teething, per label and per [[topical-anesthetics]]'s pediatric KIDs List flag
- Do not use for more than 7 days without dentist/doctor direction (standard benzocaine-category limit)
- No independent brand-specific RCT (as opposed to the general benzocaine ingredient-class evidence) was identified for DenTek specifically in this pass

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of toothache and gum pain via local anesthetic action.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition reducing blood oxygen — stop use and seek immediate medical attention if pale/blue skin, headache, rapid heartbeat, breathing difficulty, dizziness, or fatigue occur, even with prior uneventful use; do not use if allergic to local anesthetics (procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, other "caine" anesthetics); not for teething or children under 2; do not use more than 4 times daily or for more than 7 days without professional guidance.

## Symptoms Treated
