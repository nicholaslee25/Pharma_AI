## Appears In
Ear Pain Relief - 7%

## Overview
Confirmed: Eosera Ear Pain MD's own label lists **lidocaine HCl monohydrate 4%** as its active ingredient — a genuine, FDA-monograph-recognized local anesthetic, mechanistically unrelated to the other four (homeopathic-dilution) brands in this category. See [[topical-anesthetics]] for the ingredient-class evidence: lidocaine carries a substantially lower methemoglobinemia risk than benzocaine per the Guay et al. 2009 review (lidocaine implicated in only ~5% of 242 reviewed methemoglobinemia cases vs. ~66% for benzocaine), though that review's data was drawn mostly from oral/dermal use rather than otic (ear-canal) application specifically — no otic-specific methemoglobinemia data was located. A separate "Ear Pain MD +PLUS" SKU adds an antibacterial component for an infection-protection claim, not present in the base formula. No Eosera-brand-specific clinical trial beyond general lidocaine pharmacology was identified.

## Pros
- Real, conventional-pharmacology active ingredient (lidocaine 4%) with an established local-anesthetic mechanism, unlike the four homeopathic-dilution competitors in this category
- Substantially lower methemoglobinemia risk than benzocaine-based anesthetics per [[topical-anesthetics]]'s cited safety review
- Fast-acting per marketing (numbing mechanism should act faster than a homeopathic product's claimed multi-day/week timelines)

## Cons
- Smallest recommendation share in the category (7%, tied with Ring Relief) despite being the one product with an established conventional mechanism — recommendation share does not track evidence quality here
- Explicitly does not treat underlying infection — masks pain only, and untreated ear infection can worsen if pain relief delays proper evaluation
- No otic-specific (ear-canal) methemoglobinemia or safety data was located; [[topical-anesthetics]]'s safety data is drawn mostly from oral/dermal lidocaine use, not this specific route of administration

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of minor ear pain/discomfort in adults and children 2+; not intended to address underlying infection. The +PLUS SKU adds an antibacterial claim for infection protection.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: contraindicated with known lidocaine/amide-anesthetic hypersensitivity; do not use with a perforated eardrum, ear tubes, or active drainage; discontinue and see a doctor if pain worsens or persists beyond a few days, since this product does not treat infection.

## Symptoms Treated
Minor ear pain/discomfort (does not treat infection or underlying cause).
