## Overview
**Real, significant discrepancy found here, sourced from the company's own study.** ONNIT Alpha BRAIN is marketed as supporting memory, focus, and processing speed, citing a 2016 self-funded clinical study. A current class-action lawsuit alleges this is false, misleading, and deceptive advertising because, per the full text of the company's own cited study: across **26 total tests performed, only 1 showed a statistically significant difference from placebo**, and the placebo group actually **outperformed** the Alpha BRAIN group on several measures. This is a particularly stark instance of the pattern documented on [[brain-health-nootropics]] — the negative finding comes not from an outside critic's re-analysis alone, but from a plain reading of the company's own self-funded trial's full results. The proposed class claims were dismissed without prejudice (a procedural outcome, meaning the same allegations could be refiled), not a ruling that the underlying claims are accurate. ONNIT is now owned by Unilever.
