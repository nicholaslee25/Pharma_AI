## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (full brand name: "Boogie Bottoms No-Rub Diaper Rash Spray," from the makers of Boogie Wipes, manufactured by Eleeo Brands LLC): **dual active ingredients zinc oxide 25% and dimethicone 20%**, in a spray/no-rub format — the highest zinc-oxide concentration confirmed among this category's non-"maximum-strength" products (higher than Balmex 11.3%, Desitin's standard 13%, Triple Paste's standard 12.8%, and close to Boudreaux's standard 16%), and the only spray-format, dual-active-ingredient product confirmed in this category so far. Dimethicone (a silicone-based skin protectant) is a second, distinct barrier mechanism not seen in any other Diaper Rash Products brand checked in this pass. Lanolin appears as an INACTIVE ingredient (not one of the two actives) — a real, if secondary, contact-allergy consideration despite not being marketed as an active component.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a dual-active (zinc oxide + dimethicone) formulation — combining a barrier/astringent mechanism (zinc oxide, per [[zinc-oxide]]) with a silicone-based occlusive mechanism (dimethicone), a formulation approach not seen elsewhere in this category
- No-rub spray application format may reduce friction/discomfort during application on already-irritated infant skin, a genuine usability advantage distinct from active-ingredient efficacy
- Free of parabens, dyes, and talc per manufacturer labeling
- Relatively high zinc-oxide concentration (25%) among non-maximum-strength products in this category

## Cons
- **Contains lanolin as an inactive ingredient** — the same known (if uncommon) contact-allergy risk already flagged for A&D, A+D Original Ointment, and Calmoseptine elsewhere in this category, here present despite lanolin not being one of the two marketed active ingredients (zinc oxide, dimethicone) — a caregiver checking only the "active ingredients" line on the Drug Facts panel could miss this
- No dedicated dimethicone ingredient page exists in this database — its evidence base as a skin protectant for diaper rash specifically was not independently reviewed here, so this database cannot make a dimethicone-specific efficacy claim beyond zinc oxide's own evidence
- Per [[zinc-oxide]]'s own evidence review: overall evidence quality for zinc-oxide barrier preparations is graded low-level (Grade C) — applies to this product's zinc-oxide component regardless of the additional dimethicone
- No Boogie-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Prevention and treatment of diaper rash (irritant diaper dermatitis) via a combined zinc-oxide/dimethicone barrier mechanism, applied via no-rub spray.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label conventions for this ingredient class: for external use only; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or does not improve. **Contains lanolin (inactive ingredient) — a known, if uncommon, contact allergen** not disclosed among the product's marketed active ingredients. Free of parabens, dyes, and talc.

## Symptoms Treated
Diaper rash / irritant diaper dermatitis — via combined zinc-oxide barrier/astringent and dimethicone occlusive mechanisms.
