## Pros
- Confirmed FDA label matches [[famotidine]]'s existing sourcing exactly
- Category-dominant share (83%) in H2 Receptor Antagonists
- Famotidine has a comparatively clean drug-interaction profile relative to Tagamet HB 200 (cimetidine) — see that page's CYP450 discussion — a real differentiator within this same H2RA category
- Preferable to other H2RAs during lactation per [[famotidine]]'s Special Population Flags (lower breast-milk concentration than other H2RAs)

## Cons
- **This database's category-mapping data ("Pepcid," 35%/83% share) does not specify which strength (10mg Original vs. 20mg Maximum Strength) the recommendation share refers to** — a real, if minor, formulation-ambiguity gap consistent with the pattern flagged on other multi-SKU brands in this database
- Per [[famotidine]]'s own Key Safety Data: a real, documented thrombocytopenia risk exists with famotidine, requiring CBC monitoring with long-term use — worth restating on the brand page most consumers actually encounter
- No Pepcid-brand-specific RCT beyond the general famotidine evidence base was independently located in this pass
- Label directs not combining with other acid reducers or using >2 tablets/24h without doctor guidance — easy to violate unintentionally if a consumer also takes an antacid or PPI without realizing they overlap mechanistically

## Use Cases
Prevention and relief of heartburn associated with acid indigestion and sour stomach; can be taken 10–60 minutes before trigger foods/beverages for prevention — a genuinely different use pattern than a PPI's fixed 14-day course, since famotidine acts fast enough (within ~1h per [[famotidine]]) for as-needed dosing.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not use with difficulty/pain swallowing, vomiting blood, bloody/black stools, or in combination with other acid reducers. Max 2 tablets/24h. Pregnant/breastfeeding: ask a health professional. Per [[famotidine]]: avoid in patients with a documented cimetidine hypersensitivity history (H2RA cross-sensitivity risk).

## Symptoms Treated
Heartburn, acid indigestion, sour stomach — via the H2-receptor-antagonist mechanism detailed in [[famotidine]].
