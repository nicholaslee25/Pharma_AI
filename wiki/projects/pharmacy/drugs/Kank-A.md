## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Kank-A Mouth Pain Liquid (Blistex-manufactured) lists **benzocaine 20% + benzoin resin 0.5%** as its active ingredients — benzoin resin acts as a protective polymer-forming binder rather than a separate anesthetic, so benzocaine remains the sole active-drug mechanism, consistent with Orajel and Anbesol. Labeled for canker sores, minor mouth/gum irritation from dentures or orthodontic appliances, and minor mouth/gum injury — matching its three category appearances here. A separate "Kank-A Triple Action" gel SKU (benzocaine + menthol) and a "Baby Kank-A" teething product (benzocaine) also exist under the same brand per DailyMed, meaning Kank-A spans multiple companion-ingredient combinations even though all confirmed SKUs share the same benzocaine active.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with benzocaine as the active ingredient, a consistent mechanism across its SKUs (no materially different active-ingredient sibling product identified, unlike Orajel or Anbesol)
- Labeled specifically for denture/orthodontic-appliance irritation in addition to canker sores — a slightly broader labeled use than some competitors
- Manufactured by Blistex, a company with an established OTC oral-care manufacturing track record

## Cons
- Full benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk applies per [[topical-anesthetics]] — same disproportionate risk profile as every other benzocaine-based competitor in this category
- Label explicitly states "not for use during pregnancy or lactation" — a more explicit pregnancy caution than seen on the Orajel/Anbesol labels reviewed in this pass
- A "Baby Kank-A" teething SKU exists under the same brand despite benzocaine's FDA-flagged pediatric teething risk (per [[topical-anesthetics]]'s StatPearls-sourced KIDs List flag, teething use should be avoided altogether in children) — flagged as a genuine safety concern for this brand extension, though not independently confirmed here whether this SKU remains in current production or complies with post-2018 FDA teething-labeling changes
- No brand-specific (vs. ingredient-class) clinical trial identified for Kank-A

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of pain from canker sores, minor mouth/gum irritation caused by dentures or orthodontic appliances, and minor mouth/gum injury.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: methemoglobinemia risk (stop use, seek immediate medical attention for pale/gray/blue skin, headache, rapid heart rate, shortness of breath, dizziness, fatigue); do not use during pregnancy/lactation without consulting a doctor; do not use for more than 7 days; benzocaine "caine"-family allergy contraindication; discontinue if no improvement within 72 hours or if swelling develops.

## Symptoms Treated
Temporary numbing/pain relief for canker sores and mouth/gum irritation from dental appliances or minor injury.
