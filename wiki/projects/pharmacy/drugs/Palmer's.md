## Overview
Confirmed via product-ingredient sources: **Palmer's Cocoa Butter Formula Lip Balm SPF 15** lists **dimethicone 1.5%, octinoxate 7.5%, and oxybenzone 4.5%** as active ingredients (a sunscreen combination). This pass could not independently confirm a Drug Facts panel for the non-SPF "Cocoa Butter Formula" original lip balm stick — cocoa butter (theobroma cacao seed butter) is itself an FDA-monograph-approved skin-protectant active ingredient, but the non-SPF SKU's ingredient listings found in this pass show it in the *inactive*/emollient ingredient position rather than confirmed as a declared active, so this remains unresolved rather than assumed either way.

This pass covers **Lip Balms only**; Palmer's separate 2% share in Therapeutic Skin Care, Moisturizers is not yet researched.

No Palmer's-brand-specific clinical trial was located.

## Pros
- SPF 15 SKU provides a confirmed, labeled sunscreen active-ingredient combination for lip sun protection

- Established brand with a specific niche (cocoa-butter-based formulations) distinct from the petrolatum-based majority of this subcategory

## Use Cases
SPF 15 SKU: broad-spectrum-adjacent sun protection for lips (per its declared UV-filter actives) plus cocoa-butter moisturization. Non-SPF SKU: cocoa-butter lip moisturization (drug-claim status unconfirmed in this pass).

## Allergies / Warnings
SPF 15 SKU: standard chemical-sunscreen warnings apply (oxybenzone in particular has drawn separate absorption/environmental scrutiny in the broader literature, not evaluated in depth here). Non-SPF SKU: standard cosmetic-ingredient precautions (fragrance, cocoa butter sensitivity).

## Symptoms Treated
