## Overview
Confirmed: Vitafusion Fiber Well gummies use **polydextrose** as the fiber source (5g/serving) — not inulin/chicory root as initial marketing searches suggested, and not psyllium or pectin, the only two fiber types shown significantly effective in [[fiber]]'s cited 2022 meta-analysis. A gummy-vitamin-format dietary supplement (same brand family as this database's other Vitafusion products), not an FDA-monograph OTC drug.

## Pros
- Gummy format may improve compliance for users who dislike mixing fiber powders or swallowing capsules
- Polydextrose has its own separate prebiotic/soluble-fiber rationale, distinct from but conceptually similar to inulin's mechanism

## Cons
- **Not psyllium or pectin** — per [[fiber]]'s own cited meta-analysis, polydextrose was not one of the two fiber types shown to produce significant treatment-response improvement for chronic constipation, the same pattern found for most other bulk-fiber brands in this database's research pass
- **Not an FDA-approved OTC drug** — a dietary supplement gummy, meaning no FDA Drug Facts label review, consistent with the regulatory-pathway distinction already flagged for several other gummy/supplement-format products in this database (DiaResQ, Beano, Hilma, Schiff Digestive Advantage)
- Contains sugar alcohols (sorbitol) per ingredient list — a real, separate consideration for gas/bloating/osmotic-diarrhea risk at higher intake, distinct from and additive to the fiber-related flatulence risk noted on [[fiber]]
- Gummy-format fiber supplements generally deliver a smaller per-serving fiber dose than powder/capsule alternatives (5g here vs. Metamucil's higher psyllium content) — a real dose consideration for a consumer comparing "how much fiber am I actually getting"
- No Vitafusion-Fiber-Well-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Marketed for digestive health, regularity, and prebiotic gut support via polydextrose — a gummy-format alternative to powder/capsule fiber supplements.

## Allergies / Warnings
No FDA Drug Facts label located (dietary supplement). Contains sorbitol (sugar alcohol) — may cause additional GI upset/gas at higher intake, separate from the fiber-related flatulence risk noted generally on [[fiber]].

## Symptoms Treated
Marketed for digestive regularity/gut health support via polydextrose fiber — a different specific fiber type than Metamucil's psyllium, per [[fiber]], with a smaller per-serving fiber dose than powder-format competitors.
