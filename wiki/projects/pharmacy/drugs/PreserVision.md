## Overview
PreserVision (Bausch + Lomb) is the category-dominant brand in this database's Ocular Nutritional Supplements listing (63% share) and is confirmed to be a **direct, exact match to the actual NEI-funded AREDS2 clinical trial formula**: vitamin C 500mg, vitamin E 400 IU, lutein 10mg, zeaxanthin 2mg, zinc 80mg (as zinc oxide), and copper 2mg (as cupric oxide) — a genuinely rare case in this database of a commercial product formulated to exactly match the clinically-tested dose, not merely using the same general ingredient class. See the new [[areds2-eye-vitamins]] ingredient page built specifically for this category: two large NEI randomized trials (AREDS 2001, AREDS2 2013) found this exact combination reduces progression to advanced AMD by ~25% (original AREDS) and confirms the lutein/zeaxanthin-based reformulation avoids beta-carotene's real lung-cancer risk in smokers while maintaining efficacy — but critically, this evidence applies **only to patients already diagnosed with intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye**, not to general AMD prevention or early-stage disease. The brand has since also launched an AREDS3 variant (adding a B-vitamin complex) and other soft-gel/mini-gel variants (with CoQ10, "OCUSorb") whose additions were not independently verified against trial-level evidence in this pass.

## Pros
- **Confirmed, exact formula-to-trial match** for the core AREDS 2 Formula product — a genuine, rare positive-alignment case in this database's supplement research, where the commercial product's dosing precisely mirrors what was actually tested in a large, NEI-funded RCT
- Real, quantified efficacy: ~25% reduction in progression to advanced AMD (original AREDS), with the lutein/zeaxanthin-based AREDS2 reformulation confirmed effective and safe at 10-year follow-up
- Category-dominant share (63%) reflects genuine, evidence-justified pharmacist/ophthalmologist familiarity, not merely marketing reach

## Cons
- **This evidence applies specifically to patients with intermediate AMD, or advanced AMD in one eye — NOT to general AMD prevention or early-stage disease** — a real, important scope limitation that "eye health" marketing could obscure for a broader population than the trials actually studied
- Newer brand variants (AREDS3 with B-vitamins, formulas with added CoQ10 or "OCUSorb" absorption technology) add ingredients beyond the exact AREDS2 trial formula — these additions were not independently verified against trial-level evidence in this pass, meaning not every "PreserVision" SKU carries the same trial-matched guarantee as the core AREDS 2 Formula product
- High-dose zinc (80mg) requires co-formulated copper to offset a real zinc-induced copper-deficiency risk — present in the correct ratio in the confirmed formula, but worth noting as a real, dose-dependent consideration

## Use Cases
Slowing progression to advanced age-related macular degeneration in patients already diagnosed with intermediate AMD or advanced AMD in one eye — see [[areds2-eye-vitamins]] for the full evidence base. Not evidenced for general AMD prevention.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Current or former smokers should confirm their specific PreserVision SKU uses lutein/zeaxanthin rather than beta-carotene** — beta-carotene is independently documented to increase lung cancer risk in smokers (the original 2001 AREDS formula used beta-carotene; the AREDS2-based reformulation, which PreserVision's core AREDS 2 Formula product uses, does not). See [[areds2-eye-vitamins]] and [[zinc]] for general safety data.
