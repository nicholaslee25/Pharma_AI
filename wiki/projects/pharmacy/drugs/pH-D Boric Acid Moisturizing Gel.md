## Allergies / Warnings
Toxic if swallowed. Not for treating sexually transmitted infections. May compromise latex condoms/diaphragms. Consult a doctor before use if pregnant, nursing, or experiencing unusual vaginal bleeding, sores, fever, or pelvic pain.
