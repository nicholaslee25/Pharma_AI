## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Rolaids IS genuinely calcium-carbonate based (matching [[antacids]]'s representative-agent framing, unlike Gaviscon/Mylanta/Gelusil confirmed as aluminum/magnesium-based elsewhere in this same research pass) — but spans a real, quantified multi-SKU concentration spread: **Original** (calcium carbonate 675mg + magnesium hydroxide 135mg), **Ultra Strength 1200** (calcium carbonate 1000mg + magnesium hydroxide 200mg), and **Max Strength 1500** (calcium carbonate 1250mg + magnesium hydroxide 250mg) — nearly a 2x spread in calcium-carbonate dose across the same brand name, the same one-brand-multiple-formulations pattern already documented for Desitin and other multi-SKU brands in this database. A separate combination line, "Rolaids Advanced/Multi-Symptom Antacid Plus Anti-Gas," adds simethicone (see below).

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA label; genuinely calcium-carbonate-primary, consistent with [[antacids]]'s existing sourcing and Tums' mechanism — unlike several other brands checked in this same category pass
- Also contains magnesium hydroxide at every strength tier, which may help offset calcium carbonate's constipating tendency (a plausible formulation rationale, not independently confirmed by trial in this pass)
- Multiple strength tiers allow matching dose to symptom severity, similar in concept to Desitin's tiered zinc-oxide system

## Cons
- **This database's category-mapping data ("Rolaids," 3% share) does not specify which of the three strength tiers (675/135mg, 1000/200mg, or 1250/250mg) the recommendation share refers to** — nearly a 2x calcium-carbonate dose spread across SKUs under one brand name
- Per [[antacids]]'s own Key Safety Data: calcium carbonate carries a real milk-alkali syndrome risk with excessive/chronic use; magnesium hydroxide can cause diarrhea and, per [[antacids]], binds tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotics
- At the Max Strength 1500 tier (1250mg calcium carbonate/tablet), even a modest few-tablet dose could approach [[antacids]]'s cited calcium-carbonate antacid-use maximum of 8000mg/day — worth noting as a real ceiling relevant to heavy users of the higher-strength tiers specifically
- No Rolaids-specific RCT beyond the general calcium-carbonate/antacid evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of heartburn, acid indigestion, sour/upset stomach via calcium-carbonate/magnesium-hydroxide acid neutralization — same general use case as Tums, at a choice of three strength tiers.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not use maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks without medical supervision; ask a doctor/pharmacist before use if taking a prescription drug. Per [[antacids]]: caution in renal failure, heart failure, hyperparathyroidism; magnesium content relevant to tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotic absorption.

## Symptoms Treated
Heartburn, acid indigestion, sour stomach, upset stomach — via the calcium-carbonate/magnesium-hydroxide mechanism described in [[antacids]].
