## Overview
This generic "Nature's Way" page covers the brand's Garlic Supplements (3%) and Herbal Supplement Brand (2%) lines — distinct from the brand's separately-tracked named-product pages in this database ([[Nature's Way Alive!]] in Multivitamins, [[Nature's Way Prenatal Multivitamin]] in Prenatal Vitamins, and the missing-file [[Nature's Way St. John's Wort]] in Mood Health Supplements). Nature's Way (owned by Schwabe North America) holds real, checkable third-party credentials — one brand-audit source lists ConsumerLab, Labdoor, Non-GMO Project, NSF GMP, and USDA Organic among its verified certifications, more documented third-party coverage than most brands in this pass. Set against that, the brand carries a real, multi-incident history: a 2002 lead-contamination recall, a 2015 excessive-lead recall on an herbal allergy-relief product (four lots), a 2008 FDA warning tied to red yeast rice, an Australia milk-allergen recall, a **$3.4 million Ginkgold false-advertising class-action settlement** (ginkgo biloba efficacy claims), a "Made in USA" mislabeling suit (ingredients allegedly sourced abroad), and — directly relevant to this brand's own tracked Multivitamins product — a suit alleging **Nature's Way Alive! Once Daily Men's Ultra Potency Multivitamin contains undisclosed gelatin not listed on the label**, a real undisclosed-allergen finding. Garlic Supplements (3%) is covered by [[garlic]]; Herbal Supplement Brand (2%) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.
