## Overview
Boost (Nestlé Health Science) is [[Ensure]]'s primary direct competitor — a nearly identical oral nutritional supplement (ONS) product category (calorie/protein-fortified meal-replacement drink), sold under the same corporate umbrella (Nestlé Health Science) as several other brands already researched in this database ([[Nature's Bounty]], [[Solgar]], [[Osteo Bi-Flex]]). This pass located the same category-level independent RCT evidence documented on [[Ensure]] (real, significant functional/strength/nutritional-status improvements in malnourished older adults from ONS supplementation generally) but did not locate a Boost-brand-specific trial distinguishing it from Ensure or the general ONS literature. Comparative consumer sourcing frames Ensure and Boost as largely interchangeable products differentiated mainly by taste, specific formulation variants, and minor nutrient-profile differences rather than by differential clinical evidence. Nutritional Supplements (19% share) has no dedicated ingredient page in this database.
