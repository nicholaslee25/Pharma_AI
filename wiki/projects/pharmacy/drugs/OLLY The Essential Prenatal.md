## Overview
**Real, significant formulation gap confirmed here.** OLLY The Essential Prenatal contains 800mcg folic acid (at the upper end of the WHO NTD-prevention range cited in [[prenatal-vitamins]]) plus DHA and vitamin D, but is confirmed via manufacturer ingredient disclosure to contain **no added iron** — a deliberate formulation choice the brand frames as avoiding GI upset, but a real, significant omission given iron is a standard core component of prenatal supplementation (pregnancy substantially increases iron requirements to support increased maternal blood volume and fetal development). A user relying on this product as their sole prenatal supplement would need a separate iron source to meet standard prenatal iron recommendations — a real, non-obvious gap a consumer might not notice from general "prenatal vitamin" marketing. Separately, [[OLLY The Perfect Men's/Women's Multi]] and [[OLLY Immunity Sleep]] both document a real, brand-wide pattern of litigation over product claims/dosing accuracy (ACV gummies, melatonin dosing) — not confirmed specific to this prenatal SKU, but relevant brand context. This sits against [[prenatal-vitamins]]'s own category-wide GAO label-accuracy finding (11 of 12 tested products had at least one nutrient level differing from label). ## Pros
- Folic acid dose (800mcg) is at the well-evidenced upper end of the WHO NTD-prevention range cited in [[prenatal-vitamins]]
- Includes DHA, a nutrient with its own separate fetal-cognitive-development evidence base
- The no-added-iron formulation choice is transparently disclosed (not hidden), and is a real, legitimate response to a genuine, common prenatal-vitamin tolerability complaint (iron-induced GI upset/nausea)

## Cons
- **Real, significant, and non-obvious formulation gap: contains no added iron**, despite iron being a standard, medically-important component of prenatal supplementation for most pregnant users — anyone using this as their sole prenatal supplement needs a separate iron source, a real risk of under-supplementation if not understood
- Same real, brand-wide pattern of litigation over product claims/dosing accuracy documented on other OLLY pages ([[OLLY The Perfect Men's/Women's Multi]], [[OLLY Immunity Sleep]]) — not confirmed specific to this SKU, but relevant context
- Gummy format contains added sugars (glucose syrup, beet sugar)
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located for this specific product in this pass

## Use Cases
Prenatal nutritional support via folic acid/DHA/vitamin D specifically — **not a complete iron-inclusive prenatal solution**; users should discuss separate iron supplementation with their obstetric provider given this product's confirmed no-iron formulation.

## Allergies / Warnings
