## Overview
**Discrepancy flagged — a materially different product category than its Pharmacy Times placement suggests:** Dentemp is primarily a **temporary dental filling/repair material**, not a pain-relief liquid or gel like most other products in the "Oral Pain Relievers, Adult" category. Dentemp Maximum Strength (for lost fillings/loose caps) is a **zinc oxide + eugenol (ZOE) cement** — a well-established, classical dental-cement chemistry, formulated here with calcium sulfate, barium sulfate, dimethoxytetraethylene glycol, and ethylmethacrylate polymer as fillers/binders. A related product, DenTek/Dentemp-family "Temparin Max Advanced Repair Kit," uses a similar zinc-oxide/eugenol-plus-mineral-filler formulation (aluminum phosphate, aluminum sulfate, calcium sulfate, eugenol, iron oxides, menthol, paraffin, petrolatum, potassium alum, zinc oxide). The eugenol component provides secondary analgesic/antiseptic benefit (the same active ingredient documented for Red Cross Toothache Medication, per `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`), but the product's PRIMARY function is physically repairing/refilling a lost filling or loose cap — pain relief is a secondary effect of the eugenol component within a repair cement, not the product's main mechanism the way it is for benzocaine gels/liquids.

## Pros
- Addresses the underlying physical problem (a lost filling or loose cap) rather than only masking pain — a genuinely different value proposition than pure analgesic products in this category, potentially bridging a gap until a dentist visit is possible
- Zinc oxide eugenol (ZOE) cement is a long-established, real dental material with decades of clinical dentistry use as a temporary restorative
- Eugenol component provides some genuine analgesic/antiseptic benefit alongside the repair function, per the same mechanism documented on `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`

## Cons
- **Categorization mismatch:** this product's placement in "Oral Pain Relievers, Adult" alongside benzocaine liquids/gels (DenTek Instant Pain Relief, Red Cross, Finafta) understates that Dentemp is fundamentally a repair/filling material, not a dedicated analgesic — a consumer expecting pure pain relief comparable to a benzocaine gel would get a different kind of product
- Self-applied temporary dental cement is explicitly a bridge to professional care, not a substitute for it — improper self-repair of a lost filling/cap carries its own risks (incomplete seal, further tooth damage) beyond what a simple analgesic product would carry
- Eugenol's known local-tissue-irritation risk at higher concentrations (documented on `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`) applies to the eugenol component here as well
- No independent brand-specific clinical trial evidence was identified for Dentemp specifically in this pass — reliance is on zinc oxide eugenol cement's general, long-established dental-material history rather than a dedicated RCT

## Use Cases
Temporary self-repair of a lost filling or loose cap, with secondary pain/antiseptic relief from the eugenol component, until professional dental treatment is available.

## Allergies / Warnings
Contraindicated for anyone with a known eugenol (clove oil) allergy, per the same consideration documented on `drugs/Red Cross Toothache Medication.md`. This is a temporary measure only — a dentist should be seen to properly address the underlying lost filling/cap.

## Symptoms Treated
Physical gap from a lost filling or loose cap (primary), with secondary toothache/sensitivity relief from the eugenol component.

Flagged here rather than treated as directly comparable to DenTek Instant Pain Relief, Red Cross, or Finafta.
