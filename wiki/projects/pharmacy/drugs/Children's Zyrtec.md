## Allergies / Warnings
Per [[second-generation-antihistamines]]: known hypersensitivity to cetirizine or hydroxyzine (cross-reactivity); consult a doctor for use in children with liver/kidney disease; overdose case data includes an infant (180mg) presenting with restlessness/irritability/drowsiness and a 6-year-old accidental ingestion presenting with anticholinergic toxicity signs (fixed/dilated pupils, tachycardia, agitation, hyperthermia, hallucinations).
