## Overview
**Discrepancy/complexity flagged:** "Sleepwell" is sold as at least two distinct product types that could plausibly both fall under this Pharmacy Times category. (1) A retail **internal nasal dilator** infused with essential oils (lavender, chamomile, ylang ylang) — mechanically the internal-dilator subtype covered on [[snore-aid-devices]]. (2) A separate **mandibular advancement mouthpiece** (custom-fit silicone oral appliance) marketed under dental/sleep-clinic channels — mechanically the MAD subtype on the same page, with a materially different (and generally stronger) evidence base for actual OSA-severity reduction. The sources reviewed in this pass could not confirm with certainty which specific SKU drives this database's tracked 5% share, and it's possible these are two unrelated companies using a very similar generic name ("sleep well") rather than one brand's two product lines — a real naming-collision risk, the same pattern flagged for [[Clinere]] in Ear Wax Removal. The essential-oil infusion in the nasal-dilator SKU has no independent snoring-efficacy trial located in this pass; per [[snore-aid-devices]], it should be treated as an aromatherapy-level addition, not a mechanism responsible for any airway-opening effect.

## Pros
- If the nasal-dilator SKU: drug-free, chemical-free positioning per manufacturer, minimal risk

## Cons
