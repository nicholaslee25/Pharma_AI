## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Sominex (Max strength) is diphenhydramine HCl 50mg — see [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]] for the full evidence base. A single-ingredient sleep aid at the full FDA-recognized maximum OTC diphenhydramine dose.

## Pros
- Single-ingredient formulation (no added analgesic), similar to Simply Sleep, avoiding unnecessary acetaminophen/NSAID exposure for users who need only a sleep aid
- Full 50mg dose matches the FDA-recognized maximum OTC diphenhydramine sleep-aid dose, per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]

## Cons
- Shares diphenhydramine's full documented safety profile per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: AGS Beers Criteria "avoid in older adults" flag, anticholinergic side-effect profile, general OTC-sleep-aid-class efficacy-evidence gap
- At the full 50mg dose, some evidence cited on [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]] suggests higher doses may increase anticholinergic side effects without proportionally greater efficacy versus lower doses — a real dose-related consideration
- No Sominex-brand-specific RCT distinct from the general diphenhydramine OTC-sleep-aid literature was located in this pass

## Use Cases
Occasional sleeplessness.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard diphenhydramine-class cautions apply per [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]]: avoid combining with other diphenhydramine-containing products; avoid alcohol/sedatives without consulting a doctor; AGS Beers Criteria recommends avoidance in most/all older adults.

## Symptoms Treated
Occasional sleeplessness — same low/mixed evidence tier documented on [[oral-diphenhydramine-sleep-aid]].
