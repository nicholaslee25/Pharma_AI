## Appears In
Dandruff Shampoo - 6%

## Overview
Dove DermaCare Scalp (Unilever) is confirmed via multiple DailyMed labels as **pyrithione zinc 1.0%**-based across its line — Pure Daily Care, Invigorating Mint, Dryness and Itch Relief, and the Men+Care Dandruff Defense/Deep Clean sub-lines, per [[pyrithione-zinc]]. Purpose: "anti-dandruff"; uses: helps prevent and control recurrence of itching, flaking, and irritation associated with dandruff. **This is the identical active ingredient and concentration confirmed for [[Head & Shoulders]]** elsewhere in this category — the two brands are pharmacologically the same product under different corporate/marketing identities (Unilever vs. P&G).

Note this is a genuinely different Dove sub-line from the [[Dove]] brand page's "Care & Protect" antibacterial-soap products (benzalkonium chloride) documented in the Antibacterial Soaps category — "Dove" as a name spans body-wash antibacterial, cosmetic body-wash, and dandruff-shampoo product families with three unrelated formulations, tracked as separate brand pages in this database given the distinct product lines and categories involved.

## Pros
- Confirmed 1.0% pyrithione zinc concentration matches the standard OTC-monograph strength with real RCT support (see [[pyrithione-zinc]])
- Multiple sub-lines (Pure Daily Care, Dryness and Itch Relief, Men+Care variants) let a consumer select for secondary concerns while keeping the same core active
- Consistent single-mechanism formulation across every SKU checked this pass — no within-brand dual-active surprise

## Cons
- Chemically identical to [[Head & Shoulders]] (both 1% pyrithione zinc) — a consumer paying a brand premium for either product is not getting a pharmacologically distinct formulation
- No Dove DermaCare Scalp-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass — evidence is entirely ingredient-level, shared with every other pyrithione-zinc brand in this category
- Same head-to-head gap noted for Head & Shoulders applies here: ketoconazole 2% modestly outperformed zinc pyrithione 1% in the comparative trial cited on [[pyrithione-zinc]]/[[ketoconazole]], though not in a fair 1%-vs-1% comparison
- The "Dove" brand name spanning three unrelated product/category identities (antibacterial soap, cosmetic soap, dandruff shampoo) makes it easy for a consumer to assume more formulation overlap across "Dove" products than actually exists

## Use Cases
Dandruff treatment and recurrence prevention via pyrithione zinc's antifungal action against *Malassezia* yeast; Men+Care and Dryness/Itch Relief sub-lines address associated secondary scalp symptoms within the same core formulation.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; avoid contact with eyes (rinse thoroughly with water if contact occurs); stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or does not improve after regular use as directed.

## Symptoms Treated
Dandruff flaking, itching, and irritation, and prevention of their recurrence, via pyrithione zinc.
