## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Clean & Clear spans **both major OTC acne mechanisms** — the Advantage Acne Spot Treatment and Essentials Deep Cleaning Astringent use **salicylic acid** (2%), while the Continuous Control line uses **benzoyl peroxide** (10%, "the number-one pharmacist-recommended OTC acne ingredient" per manufacturer marketing — a marketing claim, not independently verified against a specific ranking source in this pass). This is the same dual-mechanism pattern already documented on [[Cetaphil]], [[Clearasil]], and [[AcneFree]] elsewhere in this pass. The brand's Therapeutic Skin Care Cleansers appearance (3%) most likely corresponds to the same or an adjacent Essentials cleanser line rather than a distinct product; this pass did not independently confirm a separately-labeled non-acne cleanser SKU.

## Pros
- Confirmed salicylic-acid (2%) and benzoyl-peroxide (10%) concentrations fall within FDA-monograph OTC ranges, per [[salicylic-acid]] and [[benzoyl-peroxide]]
- Manufacturer guidance around combining both actives (one AM, one PM, per manufacturer's own site) shows some awareness of the compounded-irritation risk of using both simultaneously — a genuinely more transparent approach to the dual-mechanism-brand problem than some competitors
- Long-standing teen/young-adult-focused brand identity with a wide range of formats (spot treatment, astringent, face wash)

## Cons
- Same brand-name-spans-two-mechanisms pattern as [[Cetaphil]], [[Clearasil]], and [[AcneFree]] — a consumer must check the specific product name to know which active is present
- 10% benzoyl peroxide (Continuous Control) sits at the top of the FDA-approved concentration range, carrying [[benzoyl-peroxide]]'s Cochrane-documented higher withdrawal-due-to-adverse-effects rate (RR 2.13 vs. placebo)
- The Therapeutic Skin Care Cleansers category entry (3%) was not independently distinguished from the Acne Products entry in this pass — a genuine ambiguity about whether this is a separately-formulated non-medicated cleanser or the same acne-line product double-counted across two Pharmacy Times categories
- No Clean & Clear-brand-specific RCT beyond the general benzoyl-peroxide/salicylic-acid evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Treatment of mild-to-moderate acne vulgaris via salicylic acid (keratolytic, Advantage/Essentials lines) or benzoyl peroxide (bactericidal/sebostatic, Continuous Control line).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per [[salicylic-acid]]: avoid eyes/mucous membranes, do not combine with other topical acne medications without doctor's advice. Per [[benzoyl-peroxide]]: external use only, may bleach fabric/hair, discontinue if excessive irritation. Manufacturer guidance suggests alternating AM/PM use of salicylic-acid and benzoyl-peroxide products rather than combining them in one application.

## Symptoms Treated
Acne vulgaris — via salicylic acid or benzoyl peroxide depending on the specific Clean & Clear SKU selected.
