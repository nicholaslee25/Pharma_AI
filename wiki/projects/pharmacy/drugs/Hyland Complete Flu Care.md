## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: active ingredients include **Cairina moschata heart/liver autolysate**, bryonia alba root, eupatorium perfoliatum flowering top, euphrasia stricta, gelsemium sempervirens root, and potassium iodide. **Notable finding**: Cairina moschata (Muscovy duck) heart/liver extract is the same category of raw material as Oscillococcinum's Anas barbariae (Muscovy/wild duck) heart and liver extract — this product uses essentially the same traditional homeopathic duck-organ-extract basis as Boiron's Oscillococcinum, combined here with four additional homeopathic botanicals/minerals in one product, rather than as a single-remedy preparation. This is a previously-unlinked, genuinely useful cross-reference the earlier hedge ("no adequate product-specific RCT located") didn't surface. ## Cons
- No dedicated RCT for this exact five-ingredient combination was located in this pass or the earlier one — [[oscillococcinum]]'s own evidence (Cochrane review, "not compelling") concerns the single-remedy Boiron product specifically, not this multi-remedy combination, so that evidence should not be directly transferred here despite the shared duck-extract tradition
- Same class-wide evidence limitation as [[homeopathic-cold-products]]: no convincing evidence of clinically meaningful benefit at homeopathic dilutions generally
- Combining five different remedies makes attributing any observed effect to a specific ingredient especially difficult
