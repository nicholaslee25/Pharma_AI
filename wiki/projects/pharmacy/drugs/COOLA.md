## Appears In
Sunscreen - 4%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: COOLA sells two clearly, honestly distinguished product lines, unlike the mislabeled "Mineral" SKU found on [[Sun Bum]]. **COOLA Classic** (Face/Body, Fragrance-Free and scented variants): a standard chemical-filter formulation, **avobenzone 3%, homosalate 10%, octisalate 4.5%, octocrylene 8%** — see [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]. **COOLA Mineral** (Body Sunscreen Spray, Sunscreen Stick, Sunscreen Lotion): genuine **zinc oxide and titanium dioxide** mineral filters (e.g., zinc oxide 14% in one lotion SKU; titanium dioxide 7.3% + zinc oxide 4.3% in another) — see [[mineral-sunscreen-filters]]. Both lines are formulated with 70%+ certified-organic supporting ingredients (antioxidant plant oils/extracts) and marketed using proprietary "oleosome technology," a delivery-vehicle claim not independently evaluated in this pass. Unlike Sun Bum's "Daily Mineral" product, COOLA's "Mineral" line name was confirmed to accurately reflect its actual mineral-only active ingredients.

No independent COOLA-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass; the relevant evidence for each line is the general chemical- or mineral-filter literature documented on the two ingredient-class pages above.

## Pros
- **Honest, verified naming**: unlike a real discrepancy found elsewhere in this subcategory ([[Sun Bum]]'s "Daily Mineral" product containing no mineral filters), COOLA's "Classic" (chemical) vs. "Mineral" (zinc oxide/titanium dioxide) product-line names were confirmed to accurately describe their actual active ingredients
- Offers both major sunscreen mechanisms under clearly differentiated names, letting a consumer reliably choose based on the label
- High organic-ingredient content (70%+) in supporting/inactive ingredients may appeal to consumers prioritizing that formulation characteristic (not an efficacy claim)

## Cons
- Chemical-filter (Classic) line carries the same class-wide FDA GRASE data-gap and systemic-absorption findings documented on [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]
- No independent COOLA-brand-specific clinical trial was located for either line
- Smallest share tied in this subcategory (4%, alongside [[Cetaphil]])
- "Oleosome technology" and "70% organic" formulation claims were not independently verified for any efficacy benefit beyond the confirmed active-ingredient identities

## Use Cases
Broad-spectrum sunburn prevention via either chemical UV filters (Classic line) or mineral/physical UV filters (Mineral line), depending on the specific product chosen.

## Allergies / Warnings
Classic line: standard chemical-sunscreen-filter warnings apply (see [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]). Mineral line: standard mineral-sunscreen precautions apply (see [[mineral-sunscreen-filters]]).

## Symptoms Treated
Sunburn prevention (not a treatment for existing sunburn).
