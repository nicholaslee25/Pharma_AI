## Overview
Confirmed (manufacturer: SC Johnson): STEM For Your Skin Mosquito + Tick Repellent's main active ingredient is **rosemary oil**, in a proprietary essential-oil blend where **soybean oil makes up roughly 95% of total product volume** as the carrier. Independent reporting (The Manual) explicitly confirms **STEM is exempt from EPA oversight** — it "does not contain any of the EPA-approved active ingredients such as DEET, picaridin, or oil of lemon eucalyptus," the same FIFRA 25(b) minimum-risk exemption category as [[Babyganics]] and (plausibly) [[Hello Bello]] elsewhere in this category. This is the **third brand in this category confirmed to use a soybean-oil carrier base** (alongside Babyganics and Hello Bello) — a real, recurring top-9 allergen consideration across roughly a fifth of this category's combined recommendation share once these three "natural" brands are added together.

**Rosemary oil's own evidence base is thin and mixed:** small published studies report meaningful short-term protection (one reporting 100% protection for 8 hours at 20% rosemary oil concentration in a lab setting; another reporting complete repellency for only 90 minutes; a rosemary-oil gel at 24% showing 92% protection against Aedes aegypti for 4 hours) — but the overall literature is characterized by few rigorous studies and inconsistent concentrations/methodologies, and none of these lab studies were confirmed to match STEM's actual real-world formulation or concentration. This is a genuinely different (and thinner) evidence situation than DEET/picaridin's 140+-study comparative base (see [[insect-repellents-deet-picaridin]]), though notably more study-level support exists for rosemary specifically than for some of the other essential oils used by category-mates.

## Pros
- Manufactured by SC Johnson, a large, established consumer-products company with real quality-control infrastructure, unlike some smaller "natural" repellent brands
- Rosemary oil has more published lab-study support (several small trials showing meaningful short-duration protection) than some other essential oils used in this category's competing "natural" brands
- Avoids DEET's documented pediatric seizure case-report history entirely

## Cons
- **Confirmed EPA-exempt (minimum-risk pesticide) status** — no EPA efficacy or safety review of this specific product, consistent with the broader Consumer Reports finding (cited on [[insect-repellents-deet-picaridin]]) that "natural" repellents generally underperform EPA-registered actives
- **Soybean oil constitutes ~95% of total product volume** — the highest confirmed proportion of this top-9 allergen among the three "natural" brands in this category, a real and substantial allergen exposure for soy-sensitive users, not just a minor inactive-ingredient footnote
- Rosemary oil's own lab-study evidence, while more substantial than some essential oils, comes from small, inconsistent studies not confirmed to match STEM's actual concentration or real-world application conditions
- Smallest recommendation share (3%) in this category

## Use Cases
Prevention of insect bites (mosquitoes, ticks) via rosemary-oil-based essential-oil scent-masking, per manufacturer claims not independently re-tested against this specific formulation in this pass.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard essential-oil-product caution: external use, avoid eyes, discontinue if irritation occurs. Given soybean oil's ~95% volume share, this product carries the most substantial soy-allergen exposure of the three essential-oil-based brands in this category — a meaningful consideration for soy-allergic users or caregivers of soy-allergic children.

## Symptoms Treated
None — a preventive repellent, not a symptom-treatment product.
