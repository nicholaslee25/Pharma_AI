## Appears In
Toothpaste, General Use - 2%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: the core **hello®** toothpaste line lists **sodium fluoride 0.24% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)** as its active ingredient, purpose "anticavity" — a standard [[fluoride-toothpaste]] formulation, consistent across most hello SKUs (Penguin Chill, Vitamin C Whitening, Magical Mermaid, Unicorn Sparkle, Dragon Dazzle). One sub-line, **Hello Mighty Multi-Tasker ("8 in 1")**, is instead confirmed **stannous fluoride**-based for anticavity + antigingivitis + antisensitivity — the same multi-formulation-under-one-brand pattern already documented for Crest, Colgate, and Sensodyne. hello is owned by Colgate-Palmolive and markets itself as vegan, Leaping Bunny cruelty-free certified, and "naturally friendly."

**Two real, brand-specific safety issues found in this pass, distinct from any ingredient-level concern:**
1. **Confirmed FDA-reported recall (2023):** on 2023-08-10, Colgate-Palmolive issued a voluntary recall of six lots (182,046 tubes) of hello® Wild Strawberry Fluoride Toothpaste because the tubes were mislabeled as hello® Fresh Watermelon Fluoride-**Free** Toothpaste — meaning fluoride-containing product was sold without disclosing fluoride's presence and without the FDA-required directions/warnings on the tube itself. Per FDA reporting, the underlying formula met safety/quality specs and no adverse events were reported; the defect was labeling, not contamination.
2. **Unresolved, disputed allegation (active litigation, not an FDA finding):** a federal class-action lawsuit filed 2025-07-11 (Damany Browne v. Hello Products, S.D.N.Y.) alleges third-party testing found "alarmingly high" lead and mercury in two Hello Kids products — Dragon Dazzle (428.4 ppb lead, ~28x the EPA action level; 11.8 ppb mercury, ~5x the EPA MCL) and Fresh Watermelon Fluoride-Free (493 ppb lead, ~32x the EPA level; 19 ppb mercury, ~9x the EPA MCL). As of the most recent reporting found in this pass, **neither the FDA nor Hello Products has issued a recall or safety advisory confirming these figures** — this is a pending legal allegation with cited but not independently-verified testing, not a settled fact, and should not be treated as equivalent to a confirmed recall.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established anticavity active ingredient (sodium fluoride) for the core adult line
- The Mighty Multi-Tasker sub-line's stannous-fluoride triple-purpose formulation (anticavity + antigingivitis + antisensitivity) carries the same mechanism/evidence base already documented on [[stannous-fluoride]]
- The 2023 recall was handled as a straightforward voluntary labeling-defect correction with no reported adverse events and a formula that met safety specs — a relatively low-severity, well-resolved incident as recalls go

## Cons
- **Pending, serious allegation:** the 2025 lawsuit's cited lead/mercury figures for two children's products, if accurate, would represent a meaningful heavy-metal exposure concern for a pediatric product — but this database cannot independently verify third-party lawsuit testing data, and no confirming FDA action was found as of this pass. Flagged explicitly rather than either dismissed or treated as proven.
- "hello" is not one fixed formulation — most SKUs are sodium fluoride, but Mighty Multi-Tasker is stannous fluoride — a consumer should check the specific product
- The 2023 mislabeling incident (fluoride product mislabeled as fluoride-free) is exactly the kind of real-world labeling failure that matters most for a family managing a young child's fluoride exposure/allergy status, since a caregiver relying on the tube label alone would have been misled in either direction

## Use Cases
Cavity prevention via daily brushing (sodium fluoride, most SKUs); combined anticavity/antigingivitis/antisensitivity via the separate stannous-fluoride Mighty Multi-Tasker sub-line.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: keep away from children under 6 years; if swallowed in excess, seek medical help or contact Poison Control immediately; check that the safety seal is intact before use. Standard fluoride precautions per [[fluoride-toothpaste]] otherwise apply. See Overview above for the 2023 recall and 2025 pending lawsuit — neither is a standard ingredient-level warning, both are brand-specific safety events worth a caregiver's awareness.

## Symptoms Treated
