## Appears In
Lip Balms - 12%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed (Vaseline Lip Therapy Advanced Healing, Cherry, and Advanced Formula, labeler Unilever): **White Petrolatum, USP 100%** — the single active ingredient, purpose "skin protectant," across all checked SKUs. This is the simplest, most consistent formulation found so far in this subcategory — unlike [[Aquaphor Lip Repair]] and [[ChapStick]], no sunscreen-active or multi-mechanism variant was located for this brand. See [[petrolatum]] for the full ingredient evidence page.

No Vaseline Lip Therapy-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass; the relevant evidence is the general petrolatum literature.

## Pros
- Single, consistent active ingredient (100% white petrolatum) across all checked SKUs — no cross-SKU formulation confusion
- Strong general evidence base for petrolatum as an occlusive skin protectant (see [[petrolatum]] — ~99% reduction in transepidermal water loss, RCT-level comparability to topical antibiotic ointment for minor wound care)
- Simple, low-allergen-risk formulation (no fragrance-heavy actives, no chemical sunscreen filters)
- 12% share, the third-largest in this subcategory

## Cons
- No sunscreen protection — unlike several competitors in this category ([[Aquaphor Lip Repair]], parts of [[Nivea]], [[Palmer's]], [[Sun Bum]]), this product offers no UV protection for lips, a real limitation for sun-exposed use
- No Vaseline Lip Therapy-brand-specific clinical trial was located
- Petrolatum's general evidence base is strongest for wound care and moisture-barrier function; no dedicated trial was found evaluating petrolatum specifically for chapped-lip symptom relief as an endpoint

## Use Cases
Skin protectant for temporarily protecting and relieving chapped or cracked lips, and protecting against the drying effects of wind and cold weather.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: external use only; avoid eye contact; see a doctor if condition lasts more than 7 days; not for deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns; keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Symptoms Treated
Chapped, cracked, or dry lips.
