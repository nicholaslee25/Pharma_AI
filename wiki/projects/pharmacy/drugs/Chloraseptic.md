## Overview
Chloraseptic is a multi-SKU line, confirmed via DailyMed: base lozenges = **menthol + benzocaine**; Warming Sore Throat = benzocaine alone; Total Sore Throat and Cough (Sugar Free) adds **dextromethorphan HBr** to the menthol/benzocaine base. Benzocaine here touches [[topical-anesthetics]] directly — Chloraseptic should be added as a third confirmed benzocaine brand alongside Orajel and Cepacol (already added during earlier brand research in this batch). Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/chloraseptic/dailymed-chloraseptic.md`.

## Pros
- Combines a genuine local anesthetic (benzocaine) with menthol's cooling/soothing sensation and, in some SKUs, dextromethorphan for cough — broader symptom coverage than plain menthol lozenges
- Highest recommendation share (28%) in the Sore Throat Products category in this database, consistent with benzocaine's genuine local-anesthetic pain relief for throat pain specifically (a more targeted mechanism than menthol alone)

## Cons
- **Carries the full benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk profile already documented on [[topical-anesthetics]]** — same real, disproportionate risk (benzocaine ~66% of cases in the cited 242-case review) and the ~20-fold re-exposure risk multiplier apply here as much as to Cepacol or Orajel
- Multiple SKUs with different ingredient combinations (some without benzocaine at all, e.g. potential future plain-menthol variants) mean a consumer should check the specific product rather than assume all Chloraseptic products contain benzocaine
- Dextromethorphan-containing SKU adds the standard MAOI interaction warning on top of the benzocaine risk

## Use Cases
Sore throat pain relief (benzocaine); throat/mouth cooling and minor cough relief (menthol); cough suppression (Total Sore Throat and Cough SKU only).

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use if allergic to local anesthetics. Discontinue and seek medical care for signs of methemoglobinemia (pale/gray/blue skin, lips, nail beds; shortness of breath; fatigue; rapid heart rate).

## Symptoms Treated
Sore throat pain, minor throat irritation, cough (select SKU).
