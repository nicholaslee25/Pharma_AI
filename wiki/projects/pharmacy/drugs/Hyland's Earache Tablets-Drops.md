## Overview

Second-largest share in the category (33%). Per [[homeopathic-ear-products]], the broader evidence base for homeopathic earache products is small-scale and does not establish a clear treatment effect beyond placebo. No Hyland's-brand-specific clinical trial was identified; Standard Homeopathic Company (Hyland's parent) has received an FDA warning letter historically for unrelated products (teething tablets, belladonna-content concerns), not for the Earache line specifically — noted here for completeness but not treated as a defect of this particular product.
