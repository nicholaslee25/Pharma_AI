## Overview
A "boil-and-bite" moldable dental night guard (BPA-free thermoplastic material, per manufacturer/retailer sourcing) intended to protect teeth from nocturnal grinding/clenching (bruxism). Molds to the wearer's bite in under 3 minutes using hot water, is reusable for approximately 6 months, and includes a Scope mint flavor coating. As a physical protective device rather than a pharmaceutical product, no active ingredient, dosing, or drug-safety evidence applies — this page's research consists of confirming its material/device characteristics rather than pharmacological ones, consistent with this category's expected profile.

## Pros
- BPA-free material, addressing a common consumer safety concern for oral appliances worn for extended periods
- Fast, at-home custom-molding process (under 3 minutes) without requiring a dental office visit or custom impression
- Category leader by a wide margin (47%), reflecting broad consumer/pharmacist familiarity with an established, well-known oral-care brand

## Cons
- No clinical trial evidence was sought or is applicable for a moldable protective device of this kind in the way it would be for a drug — efficacy for bruxism-related tooth protection is generally accepted for well-fitted night guards as a device class, but this specific product's protective performance relative to a dentist-fitted custom guard was not independently compared in this pass
- Boil-and-bite guards are generally understood (dental literature broadly) to fit less precisely than a professionally fitted custom guard, which may affect comfort and long-term retention compared to premium/custom options
- Reusable lifespan (~6 months) means recurring replacement cost versus a more durable custom dental-office guard

## Use Cases
Protecting teeth from wear/damage caused by nocturnal teeth grinding or clenching (bruxism); does not treat the underlying cause of bruxism (stress, sleep disorders, malocclusion) itself.

## Allergies / Warnings
No pharmacological allergy profile; standard device precautions apply (discontinue and consult a dentist if the guard causes pain, jaw discomfort, or does not fit properly; replace if damaged or worn).

## Symptoms Treated
