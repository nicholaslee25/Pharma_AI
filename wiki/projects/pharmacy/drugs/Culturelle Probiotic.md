## Appears In
Urinary Health - 10%

## Overview
Culturelle's core Digestive Daily Probiotic is confirmed via manufacturer supplement-facts data as Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG (LGG) — 10 billion CFU per capsule in the standard formulation, with Pro Strength (12 billion), Health & Wellness (15 billion), and Ultimate Strength (20 billion) sub-lines also sold under the same brand name at different potencies. LGG has real, cited antibiotic-associated-diarrhea (AAD) prevention evidence per this database's [[probiotics]] page — but that is a GI-health use case, genuinely distinct from the vaginal/urinary-health use this brand is tracked under in this category (10% share of Urinary Health). Per [[probiotics]]' Vaginal/Urinary Health Use section, the specifically well-studied vaginal/urinary probiotic combination is Lactobacillus rhamnosus GR-1 + Limosilactobacillus reuteri RC-14 — LGG is not that combination.

## Pros
- LGG is a real, well-characterized, extensively studied probiotic strain with genuine AAD-prevention trial support (see [[probiotics]])
- Manufacturer has been transparent and consistent about strain identity (LGG) across its product line, including the separately-confirmed Culturelle Kids line elsewhere in this database
- Shelf-stable formulation (no refrigeration required per manufacturer), a practical usability advantage over some competing probiotic products

## Cons
- **No LGG-specific trial for vaginal/urinary-health outcomes was located in this pass** — the strain's strongest evidence base (AAD prevention) is a different clinical use case from what this category (Urinary Health) implies; this is the general "evidence for one strain/use does not automatically transfer to a different use" caution already stated on [[probiotics]], made concrete here
- Multiple CFU-potency sub-lines (10/12/15/20 billion) under related but not identical naming could create consumer confusion about which specific product corresponds to which marketed benefit
- General probiotic-class caution applies: rare but real risk of bacteremia/fungemia in severely immunocompromised or critically ill patients (a known, if uncommon, probiotic-class safety signal, not specific to Culturelle)

## Use Cases
Marketed general "digestive health" and (per this category's placement) urinary-tract/vaginal-health support; the strongest independent trial evidence specifically supports antibiotic-associated-diarrhea prevention, a different use case.

## Allergies / Warnings
No specific brand recall or lawsuit was located in this pass. General probiotic-class caution: immunocompromised individuals should consult a physician before use given the rare bacteremia/fungemia signal noted above.

## Symptoms Treated
Marketed for digestive/gut health and (in this category) urinary tract/vaginal health support — the urinary/vaginal-health application is not supported by the same direct trial evidence as the strain's AAD-prevention use.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Real evidence-gap finding:** Culturelle's placement in the Urinary Health category (10% share) rests on general "a probiotic should help vaginal/urinary flora" reasoning rather than an LGG-specific vaginal/urinary RCT — a genuine, worth-flagging gap between category placement and the strain's actual best-evidenced use case (GI/AAD prevention). This is not a claim that Culturelle doesn't work for urinary health, only that this pass did not find direct trial support for that specific claim with this specific strain.
