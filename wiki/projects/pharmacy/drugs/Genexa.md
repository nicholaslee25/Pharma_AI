## Overview
Confirmed: Genexa Children's Acetaminophen is **acetaminophen 160 mg per 5 mL** — the identical active ingredient and standardized concentration as Children's Tylenol, matching [[acetaminophen]]. Genexa also sells 80mg chewable acetaminophen tablets. Genexa's differentiation is entirely in its INACTIVE ingredients and marketing positioning ("Made Clean," "Pediatrician Preferred Ingredients," 0% artificial additives, dye-free, vegan, non-GMO, gluten-free) — the liquid formulation uses organic agave syrup and organic flavor rather than the sweeteners/dyes used in some conventional formulations, but the active pharmaceutical ingredient, dose, and clinical evidence base are identical to Children's Tylenol/DrKids/Little Remedies. This is a real, worth-flagging distinction: "clean"/organic marketing applies to inactive ingredients only, not to a different or "gentler" active drug.

## Pros
- Confirmed identical active ingredient/dose to Children's Tylenol — carries the same well-established acetaminophen evidence base per [[acetaminophen]]
- Dye-free formulation may be preferable for caregivers seeking to avoid artificial dyes for a child with a specific sensitivity or family preference, independent of the drug's own efficacy/safety profile
- Certified vegan, non-GMO, gluten-free, free from common allergens in its inactive-ingredient list — genuine, verifiable manufacturing claims distinct from (and not a substitute for) drug safety claims

## Cons
- **The "clean"/organic marketing could be misread as implying a different, gentler, or safer active drug — it is not.** The active ingredient (acetaminophen 160mg/5mL) and its full hepatotoxicity risk profile, dosing limits, and the currently-unresolved FDA pregnancy-related regulatory controversy (per [[acetaminophen]] — though not directly relevant to this pediatric-use product) are identical to any other 160mg/5mL acetaminophen product
- No brand-specific clinical trial evidence exists (or would be expected) beyond the general acetaminophen ingredient-class evidence, since this is the same drug as conventional competitors
- Smallest share (2%) of the researched Children's Analgesics brands in this database, likely reflecting a smaller market presence/higher price point relative to mainstream brands rather than any confirmed efficacy difference

## Use Cases
Pain relief and fever reduction in children ages 2-11, identical use case to Children's Tylenol.

## Allergies / Warnings
Identical to standard acetaminophen 160mg/5mL products per [[acetaminophen]]: severe liver damage risk if exceeding labeled maximum doses or combined with other acetaminophen-containing products; use only the enclosed dosing device.
