## Appears In
Dandruff Shampoo - 28%

## Overview
Head & Shoulders (Procter & Gamble) is confirmed via multiple DailyMed labels as **pyrithione zinc 1%**-based across its major product lines — Classic Clean, Classic Clean Dandruff, Deep Moisture, Itchy Scalp Care, Dry Scalp Care, Scalp Restore, and Bare Minimal Ingredients (both 2-in-1 and shampoo-only formats), per [[pyrithione-zinc]]. Purpose: "anti-dandruff"; uses: helps prevent recurrence of flaking and itching associated with dandruff. **This is the identical active ingredient and concentration confirmed for [[Dove DermaCare Scalp]]** elsewhere in this category — a genuine finding that two of the category's top-five brands by recommendation share (28% + 6% = 34% combined) are pharmacologically identical products sold under entirely separate P&G/Unilever brand identities.

## Pros
- Confirmed 1% pyrithione zinc concentration matches the standard OTC-monograph strength with real RCT support (see [[pyrithione-zinc]])
- Consistent formulation across the many scent/moisturizer sub-variants checked this pass — no dual-mechanism surprise within the Head & Shoulders line itself (unlike [[Selsun Blue]] elsewhere in this category)
- Large product-line variety (Deep Moisture, Itchy Scalp Care, Dry Scalp Care, etc.) lets a consumer address secondary scalp concerns (dryness, itching) while keeping the same core active ingredient

## Cons
- Head-to-head trial data (see [[pyrithione-zinc]] and [[ketoconazole]]) shows ketoconazole 2% modestly outperforming zinc pyrithione 1% (73% vs. 67% improvement, lower recurrence) — though that comparison is not a fair 1%-vs-1% test since ketoconazole's OTC US strength is also only 1%
- No Head & Shoulders-brand-specific clinical trial (as opposed to generic zinc-pyrithione evidence) was located in this pass
- A consumer switching between Head & Shoulders and Dove DermaCare Scalp for variety would be using the identical active ingredient and concentration without realizing it — not a safety issue, but a real "different brand, same drug" finding worth knowing
- Many sub-variant SKUs (moisturizers, fragrances) were not each individually re-verified against a separate DailyMed label in this pass — the core active/concentration is treated as consistent based on the multiple SKUs actually checked, not an exhaustive re-check of every scent/format combination P&G sells

## Use Cases
Dandruff treatment and recurrence prevention via pyrithione zinc's antifungal action against *Malassezia* yeast; secondary scalp-condition sub-lines (dry scalp, itchy scalp) address associated symptoms within the same core formulation.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; avoid contact with eyes (rinse thoroughly with water if contact occurs); stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or does not improve after regular use as directed.

## Symptoms Treated
Dandruff flaking and itching, and prevention of their recurrence, via pyrithione zinc.
