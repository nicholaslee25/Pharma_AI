## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: both "Balmex Diaper Rash Cream" and "Balmex Adult Care Rash" are **zinc oxide 11.3%** — a consistent concentration across both this brand's pediatric and adult-incontinence product lines, in contrast to Desitin (13% vs. 40% split) and Boudreaux's Butt Paste (16% vs. 40% split) both confirmed elsewhere in this same category to vary concentration significantly by SKU. Balmex's 11.3% is also the lowest zinc-oxide concentration confirmed among this database's Diaper Rash Products brands so far. A separate "Balmex Complete Protection" formula is marketed as addressing "all 3 leading causes of diaper rash" with claimed faster results, but was not independently confirmed to differ in zinc-oxide concentration from the standard Diaper Rash Cream.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts labels for both Diaper Rash Cream and Adult Care Rash, matching [[zinc-oxide]]'s barrier-protectant evidence base
- **Consistent 11.3% zinc-oxide concentration across both its pediatric and adult product lines** — a genuine positive counter-example to the concentration-ambiguity problem just found for Desitin and Boudreaux's Butt Paste in this same category, since a caregiver or adult-incontinence user can expect the same active-ingredient strength regardless of which Balmex line they choose
- Long-established brand with real market presence in both listed categories

## Cons
- Lowest confirmed zinc-oxide concentration among Diaper Rash Products brands checked in this pass (11.3% vs. Desitin's 13-40% and Boudreaux's 16-40%) — not necessarily inferior (per [[zinc-oxide]]'s own evidence review, concentration-dependent efficacy comparisons were not established across brands), but a real quantified difference a caregiver comparing products by potency should be aware of
- Per [[zinc-oxide]]'s own evidence review: overall evidence quality for zinc-oxide barrier preparations is graded low-level (Grade C) regardless of brand or concentration
- No Balmex-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general zinc-oxide evidence base
- "Complete Protection" marketing claims (addressing "all 3 leading causes" of diaper rash, faster results) were not independently verified against a specific clinical trial in this pass

## Use Cases
Prevention and treatment of diaper rash (irritant diaper dermatitis) in infants (Diaper Rash Cream); prevention/relief of incontinence-associated skin irritation in adults (Adult Care Rash) — both via the same 11.3% zinc-oxide barrier-protectant mechanism.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: for external use only; avoid eye contact; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or persists beyond 7 days. Keep away from children; seek immediate medical attention if swallowed.
