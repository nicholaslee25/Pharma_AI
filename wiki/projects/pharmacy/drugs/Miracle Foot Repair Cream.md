## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Miracle Foot Repair Cream (Winning Solutions) carries an actual OTC Drug Facts label — active ingredient **Menthol 0.1%**, purpose "external analgesic." No dedicated menthol ingredient page exists yet in this database (a known gap — menthol spans many OTC categories in this vault and has not yet been built out as its own evidence page). The product is built on a 60%-aloe-vera-gel cosmetic base with the menthol added at a low (0.1%) concentration, consistent with a mild cooling/soothing sensory effect rather than a primary analgesic mechanism at that dose. Note: salicylic acid also appears in this formula, but only as an INACTIVE ingredient with no percentage or drug-facts claim — this is a meaningfully different regulatory role than [[Kerasal]]'s active-ingredient use of salicylic acid, and the two should not be conflated despite both appearing in the Diabetic Foot Cream category. Label saved in full at `raw/primary-sources/brands/miracle-foot-repair-cream/dailymed-miracle-foot-repair-cream.md`. No brand-specific clinical trial was found.
