## Appears In
Decongestants, Nasal Spray - 62%

## Overview
Already fully confirmed on [[oxymetazoline]] — that page's dosing table and sourcing are built directly from Afrin Original's own FDA OTC label: **oxymetazoline HCl 0.05%**, 2-3 sprays per nostril every 10-12h, max 2 doses/24h, **not for more than 3 consecutive days**. This upgrades the earlier hedge ("best known as... not independently confirmed") to a full confirmation; the ingredient page was already using Afrin as its representative labeled product.

## Pros
- Longest duration of action (10-12h) among nasal decongestant classes — see [[oxymetazoline]]'s mechanism section for why (imidazoline-class, acts on both venous and arteriolar vessels, more potent/longer-acting than phenylephrine)
- Effective, fast-acting short-term nasal decongestant per the ingredient page's evidence grade ("moderate-to-high for short-term... the major limitation is rebound congestion, not lack of initial efficacy")
- Largest recommendation share (62%) of any product in its category in this database

## Cons
- **The 3-day rebound congestion (rhinitis medicamentosa) limit is the central safety issue for this entire ingredient class**, already thoroughly documented on [[oxymetazoline]] — StatPearls-cited data shows ~49% of self-medicating patients with persistent rhinitis overuse intranasal decongestants despite awareness of duration limits, meaning this is a common real-world failure mode, not a rare edge case
- Benzalkonium chloride (a preservative in the Afrin formulation) is flagged by StatPearls as a possible contributing factor to rebound congestion
- Ask a doctor before use with heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease, or enlarged prostate

## Use Cases
Short-term (≤3 days) relief of nasal/sinus congestion from a cold, hay fever, or upper respiratory allergies.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not exceed 3 consecutive days of use. Children <6y: ask a doctor. Ask a doctor before use with heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease, or trouble urinating due to enlarged prostate. Seek immediate medical help if swallowed.

## Symptoms Treated
Nasal congestion, sinus congestion/pressure.
