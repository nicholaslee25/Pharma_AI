## Overview
Summer's Eve (Prestige Consumer Healthcare) is a feminine-hygiene wash/spray/wipe line with **no FDA Drug Facts panel** on its core cleansing products — a cosmetic product category, consistent with this database's recurring finding that many "feminine hygiene" brands are cosmetics, not FDA-monograph drugs. **A real, current, and serious safety finding was located in this pass:** a class-action lawsuit alleges certain Summer's Eve body sprays contain benzene, a known human carcinogen, following an independent Valisure laboratory study that found benzene contamination across a range of cosmetic/health spray products. This is separate from, but adds to, longer-standing independent (non-lawsuit) criticism from Women's Voices for the Earth, whose testing found a number of Summer's Eve products, including best-selling washes and wipes, containing fragrance/other ingredients the organization characterizes as concerning for women's health.

## Cons
- **Real, active class-action lawsuit alleging benzene contamination** in certain Summer's Eve body sprays, following an independent Valisure study — a serious, current legal and safety matter, not a resolved or historical issue
- Independent testing by Women's Voices for the Earth found concerning ingredients (their characterization) in several best-selling Summer's Eve washes/wipes, a separate, non-lawsuit criticism worth a user's awareness
- **As with most cosmetic-category feminine hygiene products in this database, no FDA Drug Facts panel exists** — meaning no FDA premarket efficacy/safety review applies to these products the way it would for an OTC monograph drug
- The vaginal microbiome is a delicate ecosystem; general gynecological guidance (not independently re-sourced with a Summer's-Eve-specific citation in this pass) generally advises against internal douching/washing products for routine hygiene given disruption-of-normal-flora risk — a real, broader category caution worth noting alongside the brand-specific benzene finding
