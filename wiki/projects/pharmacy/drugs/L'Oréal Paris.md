## Overview
L'Oréal Paris's Revitalift skincare line (Cream Cleanser, Daily Moisture Lotion) — the most plausible drivers of both tracked shares — are **cosmetics**, built around ingredients like retinol, hyaluronic acid, glycolic acid, and vitamin C marketed for anti-aging/exfoliating benefits, but with **no confirmed FDA Drug Facts panel or declared active ingredient** for the core cleanser/moisturizer SKUs. A separate, genuinely FDA-regulated product exists under the same brand — **L'Oréal Paris Dermo Expertise SPF 15 Active Daily Moisture**, confirmed via DailyMed with **ensulizole and octinoxate** as declared active ingredients (a chemical sunscreen combination, see [[chemical-sunscreen-filters]]) — but this specific SPF-labeled SKU was not confirmed to be the product driving either the Cleansers or Moisturizers tracked share in this pass, and is a different, older product line (Dermo Expertise) from the Revitalift line most plausibly responsible for current share.

No independent L'Oréal Paris-brand-specific clinical trial was located in this pass for the cosmetic Revitalift line.
