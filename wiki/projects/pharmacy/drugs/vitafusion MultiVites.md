## Appears In

## Overview
This is [[vitafusion]]'s flagship gummy multivitamin product. See [[vitafusion]] for the parent brand's own confirmed profile: manufactured by Church & Dwight under FDA cGMP oversight, but **not participating in the USP Dietary Supplement Verification Program** (a real, confirmed gap versus [[Centrum]]'s manufacturer Haleon, which supplied the actual tested product in the large COSMOS multivitamin RCT). MultiVites contains ~3g added sugar per 2-gummy serving, per [[vitafusion]]'s own research. No MultiVites-specific clinical trial was located; efficacy evidence is at the general multivitamin-ingredient level per [[multivitamins]], which found no cancer/cardiovascular/mortality benefit from daily multivitamin use in a large RCT (using Centrum Silver, not vitafusion, as the tested product) alongside a real cognitive benefit in that same trial's older-adult population. Multivitamins (4% share) is covered by [[multivitamins]].

## Pros
- Gummy format may improve adherence for users who dislike swallowing tablets/capsules
- Manufactured by a large, FDA-cGMP-audited company (Church & Dwight) — see [[vitafusion]]

## Cons
- **Not USP Dietary Supplement Verified** — a real, confirmed gap versus [[Centrum]] in this same category, per [[vitafusion]]'s own brand profile
- Contains added sugar (~3g per 2-gummy serving) — a real, if modest, consideration for diabetic or sugar-restricted users, and a format-inherent difference from tablet/capsule competitors
- No vitafusion-MultiVites-specific clinical trial was located — efficacy evidence is entirely at the general multivitamin-ingredient level, and the specific trial anchoring [[multivitamins]]'s evidence page (COSMOS) used a different product (Centrum Silver), not vitafusion
- Gummy vitamins generally deliver a narrower nutrient range and lower doses of some minerals (e.g., iron, calcium) than tablet multivitamins due to formulation/taste constraints — this pass did not independently verify vitafusion MultiVites' specific nutrient panel against a full-spectrum tablet multivitamin for completeness

## Use Cases
General nutrient-deficiency correction via a gummy format — see [[multivitamins]] for the general evidence base and its important outcome-dependent nuance (no cancer/CVD/mortality benefit shown; real cognitive benefit shown in a different tested product's trial).
