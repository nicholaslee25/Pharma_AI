## Overview
Florastor is confirmed to use **Saccharomyces boulardii CNCM I-745**, a yeast-based probiotic (not a bacterial strain like most competitors) with a genuinely exceptional, brand-specific evidence base — sourcing cites over **1,000 publications, 130+ clinical trials, and 13+ meta-analyses** specific to this exact strain, and a systematic review/meta-analysis of 21 RCTs (4,780 participants, adults and children) found a **53% reduction in antibiotic-associated diarrhea risk** versus placebo. This strain has a real, distinctive pharmacological property directly relevant to its indication: being a yeast (not a bacterium), it is inherently **resistant to antibiotics**, allowing it to be taken concurrently with an antibiotic course without being killed by it — a genuine, mechanistically important advantage over bacterial probiotics for this specific use case. Florastor is also documented for reducing C. difficile-associated disease risk in hospital/long-term-care settings. This is one of the most extensively, specifically evidenced probiotic brands found across this entire research pass. ## Cons
