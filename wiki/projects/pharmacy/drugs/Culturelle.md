## Overview
Culturelle is category-leading in this database's Probiotic Dietary Supplements listing (32% share) and is confirmed to use a single, specific, well-identified strain: **Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG (LGG)**, described as "the #1 clinically studied probiotic" and one of the most extensively researched probiotic strains in clinical trials generally, including a genuinely distinctive mechanistic detail — LGG has specific surface pili (not present on other Lactobacillus species) that facilitate adhesion to intestinal mucosa. LGG does not require refrigeration, a real practical/shelf-stability advantage over refrigerated competitors like [[Florajen]]. Per [[probiotics]]'s own evidence page, Lactobacillus (the genus LGG belongs to) is one of the two genera (with Bifidobacterium) shown to reduce antibiotic-associated diarrhea risk in a large systematic review — real, quantified, genus-consistent evidence, though this pass did not confirm the specific LGG-strain effect size independently from the genus-level Goodman 2021 review already cited in [[probiotics]]. Probiotic Dietary Supplements (32%) is covered by [[probiotics]] — this brand is a genuinely strong example of the strain-specific identification [[probiotics]] itself notes is necessary for proper evidence-grading.

## Pros
- **Specific, named, extensively-researched strain (LGG)** — one of the best-identified and most heavily studied probiotic strains available, a real positive relative to many competitors that use undisclosed or less-studied strains
- Belongs to the Lactobacillus genus, one of the two genera [[probiotics]]'s own cited systematic review found effective for antibiotic-associated diarrhea prevention
- Shelf-stable (no refrigeration required) — a real practical advantage for storage/travel versus refrigerated competitors

## Cons
- The extensive LGG research base spans many different indications (digestive health, antibiotic-associated diarrhea, general gut health) — this pass did not isolate a single, definitive Culturelle-brand-specific (as opposed to general-LGG-strain) RCT for the specific "digestive health" claim on this exact product's label
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located specifically for Culturelle in this pass
