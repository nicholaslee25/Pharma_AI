## Overview
Confirmed: Systane's (Alcon) own FDA OTC Drug Facts label (DailyMed) lists **polyethylene glycol 400 and propylene glycol** as active ingredients (Systane Ultra formula) — a combination lubricant formulation. Per [[artificial-tears]]'s own Semp et al. 2023 systematic-review finding, combination-ingredient formulations and PEG-containing tears specifically outperformed single-ingredient and CMC/HPMC-based tears in the underlying comparative evidence — meaning Systane's specific formulation choice sits on the more favorably-evidenced end of the artificial-tears spectrum per that review, not merely "an artificial tear" generically. Systane also sells other sub-formulations (Systane Complete, Systane Gel Drops, Systane Ultra Preservative Free) with different active-ingredient combinations not individually re-verified here beyond the flagship Ultra formula. Systane is the category leader by a wide margin (34% share, more than the next four brands combined). No Systane-brand-specific clinical trial beyond the general PEG/combination-formulation evidence already on [[artificial-tears]] was identified in a targeted search.

## Pros
- PEG 400 + propylene glycol combination formulation aligns with the specific formulation types (combination, PEG-containing) that the Semp et al. 2023 systematic review found to outperform single-ingredient and CMC/HPMC-only tears
- Largest recommendation share in the category (34%), with a broad sub-product line (Ultra, Complete, Gel Drops, preservative-free options) covering different severity/preference needs under one brand
- Preservative-free options available (Systane Ultra PF) for patients needing to avoid preservative-related ocular surface irritation, a real consideration flagged on [[artificial-tears]]

## Cons
- Formulation varies meaningfully across the Systane sub-line (Ultra vs. Complete vs. Gel Drops) — a consumer cannot assume one specific active-ingredient profile applies to "Systane" generically without checking the specific SKU
- Per [[artificial-tears]], symptom improvement typically requires about a month of regular use (roughly 4x/daily) — not an instant-relief product, and objective sign improvement takes longer still
- No brand-specific outcome trial beyond the ingredient-class-level PEG/combination-formulation evidence exists to confirm Systane's specific formulation outperforms competing PEG-based products

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of dry-eye discomfort, burning, irritation from wind/sun exposure, and general dry-eye disease symptoms; combination PEG-based lubrication per label indication.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: discontinue and see a doctor for eye pain, vision changes, continued redness/irritation, or symptoms persisting beyond 72 hours. No specific contraindication beyond general external-use-only precautions identified.

## Symptoms Treated
