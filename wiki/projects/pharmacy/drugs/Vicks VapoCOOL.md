## Appears In
Topical Cough Suppressants, Lozenges - 2%

## Overview
VapoCOOL is a multi-SKU line, confirmed via DailyMed: **Severe/Severe Winterfrost/Severe Sugar-Free** lozenges/drops = **menthol** alone (oral anesthetic); **Severe Plus Intense Pain Relief** adds **benzocaine**. Same recurring menthol-ingredient-page gap as other brands in this category; the Plus Intense Pain Relief SKU additionally touches [[topical-anesthetics]] directly via its benzocaine content — should be added as a fourth confirmed benzocaine brand alongside Orajel, Cepacol, and Chloraseptic. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/vicks-vapocool\dailymed-vicks-vapocool.md`.

## Pros
- Plain menthol SKUs offer the same simple, well-tolerated cooling/anesthetic action as other menthol lozenges in this category
- Plus Intense Pain Relief SKU adds genuine local anesthesia (benzocaine) for stronger sore-throat pain relief than menthol alone provides

## Cons
- Same menthol ingredient-page gap as Halls/Ricola/Fisherman's Friend/Honees/Burt's Bees
- The Plus Intense Pain Relief SKU carries the full benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk profile from [[topical-anesthetics]] — a meaningfully different risk profile from the plain-menthol SKUs under the same brand name, so a consumer should check which specific VapoCOOL product they're using
- Smallest recommendation share (2%) of any brand in this category in this database

## Use Cases
Menthol SKUs: cough/throat irritation relief. Plus Intense Pain Relief: stronger sore throat pain relief via benzocaine.

## Allergies / Warnings
Plus Intense Pain Relief: watch for methemoglobinemia signs. Plain menthol SKUs: standard OTC lozenge warnings only.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, sore throat, minor throat irritation (intensity/mechanism varies by SKU).
