## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tylenol 8 HR Arthritis Pain is a genuinely distinct formulation from standard Tylenol — **acetaminophen 650 mg in an extended-release, bi-layer tablet** (an immediate-release layer plus an extended-release layer, per the product's design), not merely a repackaged version of the standard 500mg Extra Strength caplet. This resolves this page's prior hedge ("exact acetaminophen dose not verified against label") into a confirmed finding, and matches [[acetaminophen]]. **Contrast worth noting directly:** this is a genuinely different drug-delivery formulation engineered for longer-lasting arthritis pain control — a real distinction from `drugs/Advil Easy Open Arthritis Cap.md`, where "Arthritis" in the product name refers only to bottle-cap accessibility design, not a different ibuprofen formulation or dose. Two products in the same "Oral Arthritis Pain Relievers" category use the word "Arthritis" in their name for two entirely different reasons — one a real pharmacokinetic reformulation, one a packaging feature.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a genuinely engineered extended-release delivery system (650mg bi-layer), providing longer symptom coverage per dose than standard immediate-release acetaminophen — a real formulation advantage for chronic arthritis pain specifically, not just a marketing label
- By far the largest single-brand share of Oral Arthritis Pain Relievers (38%) in this database
- Acetaminophen's non-NSAID mechanism avoids the GI-bleed/cardiovascular risk NSAID-based arthritis competitors (Motrin, Aleve, Advil) carry, per [[acetaminophen]] vs. [[ibuprofen]]/[[naproxen]]

## Cons
- **Combination-product overlap risk is especially acute here:** at 650mg per extended-release tablet, a patient combining this with any other acetaminophen-containing product (a real, documented leading cause of unintentional acetaminophen overdose per [[acetaminophen]]) reaches the 4000mg/day ceiling faster than with standard 500mg tablets, with less intuitive tracking since the total daily tablet count differs from familiar Extra Strength dosing
- Extended-release formulations can complicate overdose management (altered absorption kinetics relative to the Rumack-Matthew nomogram, which was developed primarily for immediate-release ingestion) — a real clinical nuance for extended-release acetaminophen not elaborated in the general [[acetaminophen]] overdose section
- Carries the same real, unresolved FDA pregnancy label-change controversy and hepatotoxicity risk profile documented on `drugs/Tylenol.md` and [[acetaminophen]] — same active ingredient, same open regulatory questions
- No independent brand-specific RCT (beyond the general acetaminophen ingredient-class evidence) was identified for the extended-release arthritis formulation specifically in this pass

## Use Cases
Extended, longer-duration relief of minor arthritis pain via a bi-layer extended-release acetaminophen tablet — designed for sustained rather than peak-and-trough symptom control.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: severe liver damage may occur if more than 6 caplets are taken in 24 hours (the labeled maximum); acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions; in case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
