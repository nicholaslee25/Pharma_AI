## Overview
**Non-drug category-placement discrepancy, consistent with Zarbee's Naturals and Burt's Bees above:** HoneyWorks Kids Organic Honey Cough Syrup is a **dietary supplement, not an FDA-regulated OTC drug**. Daytime formula: USDA-organic dark honey + English ivy leaf extract + vitamin C + zinc. Nighttime formula: USDA-organic dark honey + English ivy leaf extract + **melatonin** + vitamin C + zinc + grapefruit seed extract. **Genuinely notable finding: the nighttime SKU uses melatonin — a hormone supplement — as its sleep-support mechanism**, a third distinct approach (after antihistamine-based NyQuil Kids and herbal-sedative-based Burt's Bees Nighttime) to the "nighttime cough syrup" sleep-support problem found in this single research batch. No dedicated pediatric-melatonin-in-a-cough-syrup evidence was located; this database's [[melatonin]] ingredient page (if scoped to sleep-aid use generally) was not independently cross-checked against this specific pediatric cough-syrup application in this pass.

## Pros
- No dextromethorphan, antihistamine, or decongestant — avoids those side-effect profiles
- Honey base draws on [[honey-pediatric-cough]]'s real Cochrane-level evidence
- Free of common allergens (cereals, soy, milk, tree nuts, peanuts) per manufacturer labeling — a genuine formulation positive for allergen-sensitive families

## Cons
- **Not an FDA-regulated drug** — same real category-placement issue already flagged for Zarbee's Naturals and Burt's Bees in this batch
- **Melatonin in a children's nighttime cough product is a genuinely distinct and under-scrutinized use case** — melatonin is a hormone, not a benign "natural" sleep aid by default; pediatric melatonin use (for sleep generally) has drawn increasing clinical attention for unregulated dosing consistency and unclear long-term effects of chronic/frequent pediatric use, though this database did not independently pull dedicated pediatric-melatonin safety data cross-referenced to this specific product's dose in this pass — flagged as a real gap
- English ivy (Hedera helix) leaf extract's evidence base was not independently reviewed in this pass — commonly marketed as an expectorant/antitussive botanical but no dedicated ingredient page exists in this database
- Under-12-months honey contraindication applies
- No brand-specific clinical trial located for either formulation as a combination product

## Use Cases
Daytime: soothing cough/throat-irritation relief via honey/ivy-leaf demulcent-type mechanism. Nighttime: same plus melatonin-based sleep support, ages not independently confirmed in this pass (verify against the specific product label).

## Allergies / Warnings
Not for infants under 12 months (honey-botulism risk). Not FDA-evaluated for safety or efficacy as a drug. Melatonin-containing nighttime SKU warrants doctor consultation before regular/nightly pediatric use given the general clinical caution around unregulated pediatric melatonin dosing, though this is a general caution rather than a documented incident specific to this product.

## Symptoms Treated
