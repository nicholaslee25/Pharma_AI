## Overview
**Likely duplicate-brand-under-two-names discrepancy, flagged explicitly:** this file ("Hyland's 4 Kids Cough Syrup," apostrophe-s, Children's Cough category, 3% share) and `drugs/Hyland 4 Kids Cough Syrup.md` (no apostrophe, Children's Homeopathic Cough Products category, 40% share) refer to what is almost certainly the **same real-world product** — Hyland's 4 Kids Cough Syrup with Honey — appearing under two near-identical brand-name spellings across two different Pharmacy Times category source files in this database. Confirmed via manufacturer/DailyMed labeling: a **homeopathic** combination (Aconitum Napellus 6X HPUS for hoarse/dry/croupy cough, Antimonium Tartaricum 6X HPUS for moist cough, Ipecacuanha 6X HPUS for moist/gagging cough, Spongia Tosta 6X HPUS for dry/barking cough), with real honey among the inactive ingredients (alongside cane sugar, citric acid, purified water, sodium benzoate, vegetable glycerine). This is a distinct product from `drugs/Hyland's 4 Kids Cold 'n Cough.md` (a different 7-constituent homeopathic combination, no honey listed) — do not conflate the two despite similar naming.

## Pros
- No conventional drug active ingredient, avoiding dextromethorphan/antihistamine/decongestant side-effect profiles
- Contains real honey, drawing partial support from [[honey-pediatric-cough]]'s moderate-certainty Cochrane evidence, in addition to (not instead of) its homeopathic actives
- No serious adverse events reported for general homeopathic cold/cough products per the Cochrane review documented on [[homeopathic-cold-products]]

## Cons
- **Likely double-counted in this database's Pharmacy Times category data** — if this and "Hyland 4 Kids Cough Syrup" (Children's Homeopathic Cough Products, 40%) are the same product, its true combined pharmacist-recommendation footprint spans two categories under two spellings, which could distort any cross-category aggregate share calculation drawn from this database without accounting for the overlap. Flagged transparently rather than silently merged or left unremarked.
- Per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s Cochrane-level review: no convincing evidence of benefit beyond placebo for general homeopathic cold/cough products at these dilutions; better-designed trials in that review found no effect, while weaker trials reported one
- **Under-12-months honey contraindication applies** given the confirmed honey content
- No product-specific RCT for this exact 4-constituent-plus-honey combination was located

## Use Cases
Marketed for hoarse/dry/croupy cough, moist/gagging cough, and dry/barking cough via homeopathic mechanism, plus honey's demulcent/Cochrane-reviewed cough-relief property.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not for infants under 12 months (honey-botulism risk). Standard homeopathic-product FDA disclaimer applies. Consult a doctor if symptoms persist or worsen.

## Symptoms Treated
Hoarse, dry, croupy, moist, gagging, or barking cough (unproven beyond placebo for the homeopathic component per current evidence; honey component has real Cochrane-level support).
