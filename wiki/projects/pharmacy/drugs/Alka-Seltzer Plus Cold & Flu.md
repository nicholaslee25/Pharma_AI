## Cons

## Use Cases
Multi-symptom flu relief: fever, body aches, cough, allergy-type symptoms via chlorpheniramine.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. May cause drowsiness (chlorpheniramine). Do not use with MAOIs (dextromethorphan interaction).

## Symptoms Treated
