## Overview
Nature Made St. John's Wort is one of three St. John's Wort (Hypericum perforatum) products tracked in this database's Mood Health Supplements category (alongside [[Nature's Way St. John's Wort]] and [[Spring Valley St. John's Wort]]). See [[mood-health-supplements]] for the full ingredient evidence: a real, substantial Cochrane review (29 trials, 5,489 patients) found St. John's Wort more effective than placebo and roughly comparable to standard antidepressants for **mild-to-moderate** major depression, with a significantly lower discontinuation rate than SSRIs — genuinely one of the better-evidenced herbal supplements in this database. However, this comes with a **real, serious drug-interaction risk**: St. John's Wort strongly induces CYP3A4/P-glycoprotein, accelerating clearance of hormonal contraceptives, anticoagulants, immunosuppressants, HIV antiretrovirals, and risking serotonin syndrome if combined with other antidepressants. A real, additional caveat per [[mood-health-supplements]]: hypericum extract composition varies by raw material/extraction/solvent, meaning this specific product's extract standardization was not independently confirmed to match the trial-tested formulations. Mood Health Supplements (6%) is covered by [[mood-health-supplements]].

## Pros
- Backed by real, substantial (29-trial, 5,489-patient) Cochrane-level evidence for mild-to-moderate depression — one of the stronger evidence bases in this entire Mood Health Supplements category
- Real, documented lower discontinuation/dropout rate than SSRIs (fewer side effects driving treatment cessation)
- Backed by [[Nature Made]]'s broader USP-Verified quality infrastructure, with the caveat that per-SKU coverage was not confirmed

## Cons
- **Real, serious drug-interaction risk (CYP3A4/P-glycoprotein induction)** — a genuine, well-documented concern for anyone on hormonal contraceptives, anticoagulants, immunosuppressants, HIV medications, or other antidepressants; this is one of the more consequential herbal-drug interaction profiles in this entire database
- Evidence for **severe** major depression remains insufficient per the cited review — this product should not be assumed effective for more serious depression
- This pass did not confirm this specific product's extract standardization matches the trial-tested formulations, given documented hypericum-extract composition variability
- Self-treating depression with an herbal supplement, without ruling out interactions with existing medications or confirming diagnosis severity, carries real risk
