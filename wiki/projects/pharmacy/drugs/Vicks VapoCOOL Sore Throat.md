## Overview
**Naming/formulation note:** Vicks VapoCOOL Sore Throat is a distinct product line from the plain "[[Vicks VapoCOOL]]" cough-lozenge line (tracked separately under Topical Cough Suppressants, Lozenges). Confirmed via DailyMed: both the spray (**benzocaine 5% + menthol 1%**) and lozenge (**benzocaine 15mg + menthol 20mg**) SKUs use benzocaine as their **base** formula — unlike plain Vicks VapoCOOL, where benzocaine only appears in a separate "Plus Intense Pain Relief" upsell SKU and the base/Severe lozenges are menthol-only. This is a real, consumer-relevant naming distinction: two Vicks VapoCOOL product families exist with different default active-ingredient profiles. Per [[topical-anesthetics]], benzocaine carries a well-documented, disproportionate methemoglobinemia risk relative to lidocaine — the product's own label carries this warning verbatim. This brand joins Orajel, Cepacol, and Chloraseptic as a confirmed benzocaine-containing brand in this database.

## Pros
- Combines genuine local anesthesia (benzocaine) with menthol's cooling sensation for meaningful sore-throat/sore-mouth pain relief, a more targeted mechanism than menthol-only competitors
- Available in both spray and lozenge format, offering delivery-method choice
- Label carries the methemoglobinemia warning explicitly and verbatim, a real (if standard) safety transparency point

## Cons
- **Carries the full benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk profile documented on [[topical-anesthetics]]** — the same disproportionate risk (benzocaine ~66% of cases in the cited 242-case review) and ~20-fold re-exposure risk multiplier apply here as with Orajel, Cepacol, and Chloraseptic
- **Naming-family confusion risk:** consumers may not realize "Vicks VapoCOOL Sore Throat" (benzocaine-based by default) and plain "Vicks VapoCOOL" (menthol-only by default, benzocaine only in a separate upsell SKU) are different formulation defaults under very similar names
- No brand-specific clinical trial beyond general benzocaine/menthol pharmacology was identified

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of occasional minor sore throat and sore mouth irritation/pain in adults and children 12+.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label (verbatim): "Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that must be treated promptly because it reduces the amount of oxygen carried in blood." Do not use if allergic to local anesthetics; discontinue and seek medical care for signs of methemoglobinemia (pale/gray/blue skin, lips, nail beds; shortness of breath; fatigue; rapid heart rate).

## Symptoms Treated
Sore throat and sore mouth pain/irritation.
