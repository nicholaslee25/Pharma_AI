## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: this specific named SKU ("Sambucol Black Elderberry Homeopathic Cold & Flu Relief Quick Dissolve Tablets") is an actual **homeopathic combination product**, not a concentrated elderberry extract. Active ingredients (all at homeopathic dilution): Bryonia 6X, Gelsemium Sempervirens 6X/12X/30X, Sambucus Nigra 3X/6X, Sulphur 12X, Zincum Gluconicum 2X/6X. This is materially different from [[elderberry]]'s framing of Sambucol as "a pharmacologically active herbal extract, not a homeopathic dilution" — that framing describes the separate Sambucol Original Syrup product (see `drugs/Sambucol.md`), not this SKU. This page's own name honestly includes "Homeopathic" in the full product title per the manufacturer, so this is the one Sambucol-family SKU in this database where the brand's own labeling doesn't contradict its categorization here.

## Pros
- Manufacturer's own labeling is internally consistent — sold and labeled as homeopathic, filed here under Homeopathic Flu Products, unlike the ambiguity affecting plain "Sambucol"
- Combines five traditional homeopathic remedies targeting different cold/flu symptom clusters (Bryonia for aches, Gelsemium for sneezing, Sambucus for congestion/cough, Sulphur for congestion/headache, Zincum Gluconicum for fatigue/sore throat) per traditional homeopathic-practice indications listed on the label

## Cons
- At 3X-30X dilutions, none of the five ingredients is present at a pharmacologically meaningful concentration by conventional pharmacology — the label itself discloses "claims are based on traditional homeopathic practice, not accepted medical evidence" and the product "has not been FDA evaluated" for safety or efficacy
- Because only one of five ingredients (Sambucus Nigra) is elderberry, and even that is at homeopathic dilution, [[elderberry]]'s concentrated-extract RCT evidence (Hawkins 2019, Wieland 2021) does **not** meaningfully apply to this specific product — a reader should not assume this tablet delivers elderberry's studied effect
- Contains lactose and sucrose (inactive ingredients) — relevant for lactose-intolerant users despite being a "cold remedy"

## Use Cases
Marketed for common cold/flu symptom relief per traditional homeopathic indications; no conventional pharmacological evidence base specific to this exact combination was located.

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard FDA homeopathic-product disclaimer applies. Contains lactose and sucrose.

## Symptoms Treated
Per traditional homeopathic indication only: muscle ache, headache, dry throat, cough, sneezing, nasal congestion, sore throat, fatigue, nausea (label-claimed, not clinically established for this combination).
