## Overview
This brand-file row is the same product line already fully researched at `drugs/Boiron Arnicare Gel-Cream.md` (Homeopathic Topical Analgesics category, 31% share) — that page confirmed via DailyMed that Boiron Arnicare Gel and Cream both use **Arnica montana 1X HPUS 7%** as the sole active ingredient. This row simply reflects the SAME product's separate appearance in Pharmacy Times' broader "Topical Analgesics" category listing (2% share) alongside diclofenac, trolamine salicylate, and histamine-dihydrochloride-based competitors, rather than a distinct SKU requiring independent research. See [[homeopathic-arnica]] for the full ingredient evidence page.

**Carried over from [[homeopathic-arnica]]'s dedicated research:** a systematic review found "the efficacy of Arnica in doses of 10% and below is not supported by the available evidence," and this product's 1X HPUS 7% dilution falls within that unsupported range. The one positive controlled trial located for topical arnica (Knuesel et al., knee osteoarthritis) used a fresh-plant, non-diluted preparation, not this product's homeopathic dilution. Boiron's own DailyMed label carries the standard FDA homeopathic disclaimer ("FDA has not evaluated this homeopathic product for safety or efficacy").

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of muscle pain, stiffness, and discomfort from minor injuries, overexertion, or falls; reduces bruising-related symptoms (pain, swelling, discoloration).

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: external use only; avoid contact with eyes, mucous membranes, and damaged skin; do not wrap tightly or apply heat/ice immediately before or after use; discontinue if condition persists beyond 3 days or worsens.
