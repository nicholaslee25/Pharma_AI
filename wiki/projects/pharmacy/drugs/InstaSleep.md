## Overview
InstaSleep is confirmed to use a **proprietary 48mg blend of 5-hydroxytryptophan (5-HTP), GABA, and melatonin** in a fast-dissolving "mint melt" format — a real instance of the proprietary-blend pattern this project's protocol specifically watches for: the total combined dose (48mg) is disclosed, but the individual per-ingredient breakdown (how much is actually melatonin versus 5-HTP versus GABA) is not, making it impossible to know the actual melatonin dose relative to [[melatonin]]'s own evidence-optimized 4mg/day finding. 5-HTP and GABA are both real, biologically plausible sleep/mood-related compounds with their own separate literature bases, but neither has a dedicated ingredient evidence page in this database, and their combination with melatonin at undisclosed individual doses was not independently evidence-graded in this pass. Melatonin Sleep Aids (2% share) is covered by [[melatonin]] for the melatonin component specifically.
