## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: MagniLife Leg Cramp Relief tablets are homeopathic — Magnesia Phosphorica, Cuprum Metallicum, Colocynthis, Phytolacca Decandra, Veratrum Album, Causticum, Stannum Metallicum, Rhus Toxicodendron (each at 12X/30X dilutions) — see [[homeopathic-leg-cramp-relief]]. The product is explicitly marketed as **"Quinine-Free"** on-label — a direct competitive positioning against [[Hyland's Leg Cramps]], which contains Cinchona (a quinine source) as its least-diluted ingredient. A separate topical "Relaxing Leg Cream PM" product exists under the same brand with a different formula (Gnaphalium Polycephalum 6X + Causticum 8X).

## Pros
- Explicit "Quinine-Free" marketing directly and transparently differentiates this product from its category-leading competitor's Cinchona-containing formula — a genuine, verifiable point of formulation transparency
- No FDA-flagged ingredient (quinine/Cinchona) at any dilution, unlike Hyland's Leg Cramps

## Cons
- **No FDA evaluation of safety or efficacy** — same standing homeopathic-product finding as every other product in this category; "quinine-free" addresses one specific historical-safety-signal concern but does not itself establish efficacy for any of this formula's eight other ingredients
- No product-specific clinical trial evidence was located in this pass for this exact 8-ingredient combination
- Two different MagniLife products (oral tablets vs. topical cream) exist under closely related "Relaxing Legs" naming with genuinely different formulas — a consumer should confirm which specific SKU/format they are using
