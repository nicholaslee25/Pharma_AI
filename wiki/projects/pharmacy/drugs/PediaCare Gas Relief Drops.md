## Appears In
Infant Gas Products - 3%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: PediaCare Gas Relief Drops = **simethicone 20mg per 0.3mL** — the fourth brand in this category (after Mylicon, Little Remedies, Mommy's Bliss) confirmed at this identical dose and dosing schedule. Label correctly addresses gas from air-swallowing/diet only, with **no colic mention**, maintaining the same correctly-scoped indication as the category's other simethicone brands. A separate "Dye Free" DailyMed listing exists alongside the standard formulation, which itself contains D&C Red 22 and D&C Red 28 synthetic dyes — meaning, unlike Little Remedies and Mommy's Bliss (both confirmed dye-free/free of specific dyes), PediaCare's STANDARD formulation does contain synthetic dyes, with dye-free being a separate SKU rather than the default.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label matching the category's other three simethicone brands' dose and dosing schedule essentially exactly
- Correctly-scoped "gas only" labeling with no colic claim
- Dye-free variant available for caregivers seeking to avoid synthetic dyes, alongside the standard (dyed) formulation

## Cons
- **Standard (non-dye-free) formulation contains D&C Red 22 and D&C Red 28 synthetic dyes** as inactive ingredients — a real formulation difference from Little Remedies (dye-free by default) and Mommy's Bliss, relevant for caregivers avoiding synthetic dyes who might not realize the "Dye Free" version is a separate SKU rather than PediaCare's standard product
- Per [[simethicone]]'s general evidence limitations: adult-population supporting trial, not established for colic, does not reduce actual gas production — same limitations as the category's other simethicone brands
- No PediaCare-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass beyond the general simethicone evidence base
- Essentially a therapeutic equivalent to Mylicon, Little Remedies, and Mommy's Bliss at the same simethicone dose — this database found no differentiating clinical advantage among any of the four simethicone-based brands in this category

## Use Cases
Relief of infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or certain formulas/foods, ages under 2 (0.3mL) and over 2 (0.6mL), up to 12 doses daily.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: do not exceed 12 doses daily; keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) for accidental overdose. Standard formulation contains D&C Red 22 and D&C Red 28 synthetic dyes — check for the "Dye Free" variant specifically if avoiding synthetic dyes. Per [[simethicone]]'s Contraindications: simethicone allergy.

## Symptoms Treated
Infant gas discomfort due to air swallowing or diet — NOT labeled for colic.
