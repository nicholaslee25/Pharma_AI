## Appears In

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: this is a distinct combination-line product from plain [[Rolaids]] — **calcium carbonate 1000mg + magnesium hydroxide 200mg + simethicone 40mg** per chewable tablet, matching the same three-active-ingredient antacid-plus-antigas pattern already confirmed for Mylanta and Gelusil in this same research pass. Unlike those two brands (both aluminum/magnesium-hydroxide based, no calcium), this Rolaids line keeps calcium carbonate as the antacid base, consistent with plain Rolaids' own confirmed formulation, and simply adds simethicone on top for the antigas indication.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA label; genuinely calcium-carbonate-based (like plain Rolaids), plus simethicone for the antigas indication — a real dual-mechanism product rather than a mismatched-category placement
- Available in multiple flavor/format SKUs (Wild Berry, Mixed Berry, Soft Chews) at the same confirmed active-ingredient doses per DailyMed, reducing (though not eliminating) the formulation-ambiguity concern flagged for other multi-SKU brands

## Cons
- Per [[antacids]]: calcium carbonate carries milk-alkali syndrome risk with excessive/chronic use; magnesium hydroxide can cause diarrhea and bind tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotics
- Per [[simethicone]]: the antigas component's evidence base is a single old, small positive trial, with no strong efficacy evidence beyond that
- Shares plain Rolaids' calcium-carbonate dose (1000mg, matching the "Ultra Strength 1200" tier) — a consumer switching between plain Rolaids and this Advanced/Plus Anti-Gas line should know the calcium-carbonate dose is comparable at this specific tier, though plain Rolaids also offers lower (675mg) and higher (1250mg) tiers this combination product does not
- No brand-specific RCT beyond the general antacid/simethicone evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of heartburn, sour stomach, acid indigestion, upset stomach, plus gas/bloating pressure — the same dual-mechanism use case as Mylanta/Gelusil, via a calcium-carbonate-based (rather than aluminum/magnesium-based) antacid component.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: chew 2-3 tablets as symptoms occur; do not use maximum dosage for more than 2 weeks without doctor supervision. Per [[antacids]]: caution in renal failure, hyperparathyroidism; magnesium content relevant to tetracycline/fluoroquinolone antibiotic absorption.

## Symptoms Treated
