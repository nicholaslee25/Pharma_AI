# One A Day Women's Menopause Formula

## Appears In

## Overview
**Confirmed 2026-08-28 via manufacturer ingredient disclosure:** One A Day Women's Menopause Formula is fundamentally a standard women's multivitamin (vitamins A, B6, B12, C, D, E, calcium, magnesium, zinc, copper, manganese, molybdenum, biotin, iodine, selenium, chromium) PLUS soy isoflavones as its one menopause-specific addition. **It does NOT contain black cohosh** — a genuine, confirmed formulation difference from [[Estroven]]'s core product (black cohosh + soy isoflavones + rhapontic rhubarb), despite both brands holding the two largest shares (35% and 50%) in the same category and being marketed as directly competing menopause-relief products. See [[menopause-herbal-supplements]] for the soy-isoflavone evidence base.

## Pros
- Provides genuine baseline multivitamin/mineral nutritional value alongside its menopause-specific claim — a real dual-purpose rationale distinct from Estroven's more herb-concentrated approach
- Soy isoflavones carry the same real, if modest, hot-flash-reduction evidence documented on [[menopause-herbal-supplements]]
- Simpler, single-fixed-formula product (unlike Estroven's multiple sub-SKU variants) — a real navigational advantage for a consumer wanting one consistent product

## Cons
- **Confirmed to lack black cohosh entirely** — meaning this product's menopause-specific evidence rests on soy isoflavones alone, a narrower phytoestrogen exposure than Estroven's three-ingredient (black cohosh + soy + rhapontic rhubarb) approach; this database did not find evidence establishing which specific formulation approach is more effective, only that they are genuinely different
- Soy isoflavones alone carry the same modest/inconsistent efficacy evidence tier documented on [[menopause-herbal-supplements]] — no Cochrane-level high-certainty support located
- As a multivitamin base product, contains multiple micronutrients at doses that could contribute to exceeding tolerable upper intake limits if combined with other supplements/fortified foods — a real, general multivitamin-class caution (not independently re-verified against specific UL thresholds for this exact formulation in this pass)
- No brand-specific (One A Day-specific) RCT distinct from the general soy-isoflavone literature was located in this pass

## Use Cases
General multivitamin/mineral nutritional support plus soy-isoflavone-based hot-flash/menopause-symptom relief.

## Allergies / Warnings
Contains soy (explicit allergen) and is stated to contain a bioengineered food ingredient per manufacturer disclosure — relevant for soy-allergic individuals or those specifically avoiding bioengineered ingredients. Soy isoflavones carry the same theoretical hormone-sensitive-cancer caution documented on [[menopause-herbal-supplements]] — discuss with a physician if personal/family history applies.

## Symptoms Treated
Hot flashes (via soy isoflavones); general nutritional support (via multivitamin base) — no black-cohosh-specific mechanism, unlike its main category competitor.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Real, confirmed, database-worthy finding:** despite both being marketed as leading menopause-relief supplements in the same category, One A Day Women's Menopause Formula and [[Estroven]] use genuinely different core formulations — this product is a multivitamin-plus-soy-isoflavone product, while Estroven's core line is a three-ingredient herbal-phytoestrogen blend without a multivitamin base. A consumer choosing between the two is not choosing between differently-dosed versions of the same product, but between two different formulation philosophies.
