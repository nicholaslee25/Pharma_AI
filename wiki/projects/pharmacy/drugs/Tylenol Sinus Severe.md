## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Tylenol Sinus Severe = **acetaminophen 325mg + guaifenesin 200mg + phenylephrine HCl 5mg** per caplet, max 10 caplets (3,250mg acetaminophen)/24h. This completes the earlier partial hedge ("built around acetaminophen... other actives not verified"). Touches [[acetaminophen]], [[guaifenesin]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]]. ## Pros
- Addresses pain/fever, mucus, and congestion in one product for sinus-specific symptom combinations
- Labeled max daily dose (3,250mg acetaminophen from 10 caplets) sits below the full 4,000mg/day ceiling with some margin, unlike Theraflu's higher per-dose acetaminophen amount discussed elsewhere in this batch

## Cons
- Contains phenylephrine, whose oral efficacy at standard doses has been questioned by an FDA advisory panel (2023) — see [[oral-phenylephrine]] and the Sudafed PE brand page for the full evidence
- Guaifenesin dose (200mg) is well below the 1200mg dose tested in the Bennett 2015 RCT on [[guaifenesin]], so that specific trial's evidence doesn't directly transfer to this product
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication risk applies — same caution as every other Tylenol product in this batch

## Use Cases
Sinus congestion/pressure, headache, nasal congestion, minor aches/pains, fever, and cough due to chest congestion associated with a cold or allergies.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products. Ask a doctor before use with liver disease, high blood pressure, heart/thyroid disease, or diabetes (phenylephrine cautions).

## Symptoms Treated
Sinus congestion/pressure, headache, nasal congestion, minor aches/pains, fever, chest congestion/cough.
