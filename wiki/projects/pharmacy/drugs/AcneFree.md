## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: AcneFree follows the same **benzoyl-peroxide + salicylic-acid dual-mechanism** pattern already documented on [[Cetaphil]] and [[Clearasil]] in this pass. Its flagship "24 HR Acne Clearing System" (including a Sensitive Skin variant) is a **combination kit containing both salicylic acid and benzoyl peroxide** as co-packaged, separately-applied steps, and a standalone "Oil Free Acne Cleanser" SKU is confirmed at benzoyl peroxide 2.5% specifically. Both actives fall within the FDA's approved OTC monograph ranges (benzoyl peroxide 2.5-10%; salicylic acid 0.5-2%, per [[salicylic-acid]] and [[benzoyl-peroxide]]).

## Pros
- Confirmed dual-mechanism system pairs BPO's bactericidal/sebostatic action with salicylic acid's keratolytic exfoliation — a rational combination reflected in dermatology practice
- 2.5% benzoyl-peroxide cleanser sits at the gentlest end of the FDA-approved concentration range, a genuine option for BPO-sensitive users versus PanOxyl's up-to-10% tier
- "Sensitive Skin" variant offers a lower-irritation entry point into the same dual-mechanism system

## Cons
- Same brand-name-spans-two-mechanisms pattern as [[Cetaphil]] and [[Clearasil]] — a consumer must check the specific SKU (system kit vs. standalone cleanser) to know which or both actives are present
- Per [[benzoyl-peroxide]]'s Cochrane evidence, withdrawal due to adverse effects is significantly higher with BPO vs. placebo (RR 2.13) — applies to the BPO-containing components
- Per [[salicylic-acid]]'s Evidence Grade note, evidence for salicylic acid is comparatively thinner and less specifically documented for acne than for its stronger-evidenced wart-removal indication — a caveat equally applicable here
- No AcneFree-brand-specific RCT beyond the general benzoyl-peroxide/salicylic-acid evidence base was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Treatment of mild-to-moderate acne vulgaris via a combined benzoyl-peroxide/salicylic-acid system, or standalone benzoyl-peroxide cleansing (2.5%) for milder use.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per [[benzoyl-peroxide]]: external use only, avoid eyes/lips/mouth, may bleach fabric/hair, discontinue if excessive irritation. Per [[salicylic-acid]]: avoid eyes/mucous membranes, do not combine with other topical acne medications without doctor's advice. System-kit products: follow labeled step sequencing to avoid compounding irritation from two independently irritating actives applied together.

## Symptoms Treated
Acne vulgaris — via combined or standalone benzoyl-peroxide/salicylic-acid mechanisms depending on SKU.
