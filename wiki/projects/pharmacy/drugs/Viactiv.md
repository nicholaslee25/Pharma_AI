## Appears In

## Overview
Viactiv is a soft-chew calcium format (650mg calcium per chew, per manufacturer) that adds both vitamin D and vitamin K to its formulation — a real, meaningful formulation detail, since vitamin K (specifically K1) is not included in most competing calcium products in this category and has its own literature-documented role in bone mineralization/calcium utilization, though this pass did not verify a Viactiv-specific clinical trial confirming a bone-health outcome from the combination. The brand markets itself as lower in sugar than "leading calcium gummies" (2g per serving standard flavor, 1g for milk chocolate) — a real, checkable product-form advantage over gummy-format competitors, though this comparison does not apply to tablet/capsule calcium formats which typically contain no sugar at all. No recall or lawsuit history was located for this brand in this pass. Calcium Supplements (2% share) is covered by [[calcium]].

## Pros
- Adds vitamin K (K1) alongside vitamin D to its calcium formulation — a real, less-common formulation choice among calcium brands in this category, with a plausible (though not independently trial-confirmed for this brand) mechanistic role in calcium utilization/bone mineralization
- Confirmed lower sugar content than competing gummy-format calcium products (2g/serving, or 1g for chocolate) — a real, checkable claim relevant to diabetic or sugar-conscious users choosing between chewable calcium formats
- Soft-chew format may improve adherence for users who dislike swallowing tablets, a genuine practical (not clinical) advantage

## Cons
- No Viactiv-brand-specific clinical trial was located confirming a bone-health outcome from its vitamin D + K combination — the vitamin K addition is a plausible, literature-consistent formulation choice, not an independently proven brand-specific benefit
- Still a chewable/candy-like product format containing some sugar (1–2g/serving) — a real, if modest, consideration for users on very strict sugar restriction, even though it is confirmed lower than gummy competitors
- Smallest tracked share (2%) in this category, alongside [[vitafusion]]'s calcium line
- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located for Viactiv in this pass
- The specific calcium salt used (carbonate vs. citrate) was not confirmed against a label in this pass, unlike [[Citracal]] and [[Caltrate]]

## Use Cases
