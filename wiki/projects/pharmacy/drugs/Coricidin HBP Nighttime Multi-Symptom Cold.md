## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: this specific liquid SKU = **acetaminophen 325mg + dextromethorphan HBr 15mg + doxylamine succinate 6.25mg per 15mL**. Consistent with the base Coricidin HBP brand's confirmed decongestant-free positioning (see `drugs/Coricidin HBP.md`) — this nighttime variant likewise contains no pseudoephedrine or phenylephrine, using doxylamine's sedating/drying antihistamine effect instead of a true decongestant, same pattern as the daytime/base products. New ingredient confirmation touching [[acetaminophen]] and [[dextromethorphan]]; doxylamine again has no dedicated ingredient page (same recurring gap). ## Pros
- Maintains the brand's core decongestant-free positioning into its nighttime line — genuinely useful for hypertensive patients needing nighttime cold-symptom relief without a decongestant's BP-raising effect
- Combines pain/fever relief, cough suppression, and sleep support in one nighttime dose

## Cons
- Same trade-off flagged on the base Coricidin HBP page: avoiding a decongestant doesn't mean avoiding all cardiovascular-relevant risk — doxylamine still carries anticholinergic effects worth considering in older adults or those with other relevant comorbidities
- Standard acetaminophen-duplication and dextromethorphan-MAOI risks apply
- Dosing (30mL every 6h, max 120mL/24h per the label) must not be confused with other Coricidin HBP SKUs' different dosing intervals given the varying ingredient concentrations across the line

## Use Cases
Nighttime relief of fever, body aches, cough, and sleeplessness during a cold — specifically for patients avoiding decongestants due to high blood pressure.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use with other acetaminophen-containing products (max 120mL/24h per label). Do not use with MAOIs. May cause marked drowsiness — avoid alcohol, do not drive. Ask a doctor before use with glaucoma, breathing problems, or enlarged prostate.

## Symptoms Treated
Fever, body aches, cough, sleeplessness during illness.
