## Overview
**Missing-ingredient-page finding resolved, with a real, serious safety history attached.** Confirmed via DailyMed: Fleet Enema is sodium phosphate (monobasic 19g + dibasic 7g per 118mL adult dose) — a rectal saline-laxative mechanism with no prior dedicated ingredient page in this database. A new [[sodium-phosphate]] page was created in this pass specifically because this ingredient carries a real, FDA-documented acute kidney injury risk ("acute phosphate nephropathy") serious enough to prompt two FDA safety communications (2006, 2014) and a 2008 boxed warning on related prescription bowel-prep products — a materially more serious and more specifically FDA-flagged safety history than any other laxative mechanism in this database's Gastrointestinal section.

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA OTC label; by far the fastest onset of any laxative mechanism in this database (bowel movement in 1-5 minutes per label, vs. hours for oral saline/stimulant laxatives and days for PEG-3350/fiber) — genuinely useful for bowel-prep-before-procedure use cases where rapid, predictable evacuation matters
- Rectal (not systemic oral) route limits some systemic drug-interaction concerns relevant to oral laxatives, though the phosphate/fluid-shift risk below is a real exception to this general logic

## Cons
- **Real, FDA-documented acute kidney injury risk with excessive/repeated use** — the FDA explicitly warned that using more than one enema in 24 hours can cause "rare but serious harm to the kidneys and heart, and even death," per two separate safety communications (2006, 2014) and a related 2008 boxed warning on prescription-strength sodium phosphate bowel-prep products
- **Real, quantified risk-factor list from the FDA**: diabetes, age over 65, dehydration, and concurrent NSAID/ACE-inhibitor/ARB/diuretic use all independently raise this kidney-injury risk — a consumer on any of these common medications should know this before self-administering a Fleet enema, a consideration not surfaced by any generic "ask a doctor if on prescription drugs" boilerplate warning
- Contraindicated in kidney disease and on sodium-restricted diets, given the significant sodium load per dose
- Children age 5 and under should not receive sodium phosphate products without professional guidance, per general drug-safety literature reviewed in this pass
- No Fleet-brand-specific RCT beyond the general sodium-phosphate safety/mechanism literature was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Relief of occasional constipation; bowel cleansing before rectal examinations or procedures — via a fast-acting rectal saline mechanism genuinely distinct from every oral laxative in this database.

## Allergies / Warnings
**Real, serious, FDA-documented warning not shared by any other brand in this database's Gastrointestinal section: do not use more than one enema in 24 hours — risk of rare but serious kidney/heart harm, including death, per FDA safety communications.** Real risk-factor list: diabetes, age >65, dehydration, concurrent NSAID/ACE-inhibitor/ARB/diuretic use. Contraindicated in kidney disease and sodium-restricted diets. Children ≤5: consult a health professional first.
