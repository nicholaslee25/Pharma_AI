## Appears In
Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief - 4%

## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: Cortizone 10 Feminine Itch Relief is hydrocortisone 1% cream — see [[hydrocortisone]] for the full ingredient evidence base. **This is genuinely NOT an antifungal treatment** — hydrocortisone is a corticosteroid that reduces inflammation/itching but has no activity against Candida (the fungus causing yeast infections). This is the same real formulation pattern already flagged on [[Monistat Complete Care]]'s "Instant Itch Relief" sub-product elsewhere in this database.

## Pros
- Hydrocortisone 1% is a well-established, real, FDA-monograph anti-itch active ingredient with a long safety record for external itching of various causes
- Clearly positioned as "itch relief" rather than implying antifungal treatment in its product naming, a real, if modest, labeling-clarity advantage over some other brands in this category

## Cons
- **Provides symptom relief only — no antifungal action whatsoever.** A consumer using this product alone for a diagnosed or suspected yeast infection would have their itching masked while the underlying fungal infection remains untreated, potentially delaying appropriate antifungal treatment (e.g., [[Monistat]], [[Lagicam]], [[Vagistat]])
- Topical corticosteroids used on already-irritated or infected tissue carry a standard, real caution: prolonged or excessive use can cause local skin thinning, and masking symptoms can obscure a worsening or unresolved underlying infection
- No brand-specific (Cortizone-10-Feminine-specific) clinical trial was located in this pass beyond hydrocortisone's general anti-itch evidence

## Use Cases
Temporary relief of external feminine itching — symptom relief only, not a yeast-infection treatment.

## Allergies / Warnings
Discontinue and consult a doctor if irritation persists beyond 7 days or worsens. For external use only. Standard topical-corticosteroid cautions apply per [[hydrocortisone]].

## Symptoms Treated
External itching (symptom only) — does not treat the underlying cause, whether that cause is a yeast infection, irritation, or another condition.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Real, worth-flagging finding, consistent with the pattern already documented for [[Monistat Complete Care]]'s Instant Itch Relief sub-product:** this product's placement in the "Yeast Infection Prevention and Relief" category could reasonably lead a consumer to expect antifungal treatment, when it in fact contains only a corticosteroid with no antifungal activity. Its own product name ("Feminine Itch Relief," not "Yeast Infection Treatment") is more accurate than the category placement might imply — a genuine point in the brand's favor relative to less clearly-named competitors.
