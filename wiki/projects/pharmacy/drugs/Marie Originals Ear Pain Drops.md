## Overview
Marie Originals Ear Pain Drops lists **Echinacea 30C and Pulsatilla 30C** as active ingredients — homeopathic dilutions — per manufacturer/retail product listings (no FDA/DailyMed label was located for this brand, a lower-confidence source tier than the DailyMed-confirmed brands in this category). **Discrepancy note:** despite this homeopathic-dilution active-ingredient labeling, the product is marketed with "natural herbal" language and includes apple cider vinegar, essential oil blend, and goldenseal root extract as inactive ingredients — a formulation that blends homeopathic notation with herbal-remedy marketing framing, which can read as more pharmacologically active than the 30C dilution actually is. A separate Marie Originals SKU ("Ear Oil Drops," mullein/garlic oil) is a genuinely different, non-homeopathic herbal-oil product not tracked in this database's category — consumers should not assume the two Marie Originals ear products are interchangeable. Per [[homeopathic-ear-products]], no brand-specific clinical trial exists.

## Cons

## Use Cases

## Allergies / Warnings
Contains alcohol and goldenseal root extract (inactive ingredients) — consider for users avoiding alcohol-based drops or with ragweed-family plant sensitivities (goldenseal, echinacea are both Asteraceae-related). No FDA-reviewed warning label was located; general homeopathic-ear-drop cautions (discontinue if pain worsens, do not use with perforated eardrum) likely apply but are not independently confirmed for this specific brand.

## Symptoms Treated

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Discrepancy flagged on both pages:** Marie Originals blends homeopathic-dilution active-ingredient notation (30C) with herbal/natural marketing language (apple cider vinegar, essential oils, goldenseal) in a way the other three homeopathic brands in this category (Similasan, Hyland's, Ring Relief) do not — those use plain HPUS-style labeling without added "natural herbal" branding.
