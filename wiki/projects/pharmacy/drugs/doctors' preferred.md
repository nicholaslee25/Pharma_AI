## Overview
Doctors' Preferred is a smaller, lower-profile brand; its flagship product in this category is a High Potency Magnesium Glycinate Complex (420mg/serving), marketed as "doctor-developed" and gentler on the stomach than other magnesium salts. The brand states its product is triple-tested for purity/potency and made in an American cGMP-certified facility, is vegan-friendly and gluten-free, and free of synthetic additives — all company-stated claims. This pass found **no independent third-party certification** (no USP Verified Mark, no NSF listing, no ConsumerLab report) and the brand does not appear among major professionally-reviewed magnesium-supplement rankings (ConsumerLab, Forbes Health, U.S. News) alongside better-known competitors like Doctor's Best, Thorne, or Pure Encapsulations.
