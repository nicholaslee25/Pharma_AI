## Overview
Trojan (Church & Dwight) is primarily known as a condom brand (an FDA-regulated Class II medical device, not covered further here) that also sells personal lubricants under the same name — confirmed via manufacturer ingredient disclosure, the Trojan H2O Closer line is a water-based formulation (deionized water, glycerin, hydroxyethylcellulose, sodium hyaluronate, sodium citrate, methylparaben) marketed as latex-condom-compatible. No independent osmolality testing of this specific lubricant line was located in this pass, unlike K-Y/Replens/Astroglide/Good Clean Love.

## Pros
- Explicitly marketed and formulated for compatibility with latex condoms — a real, relevant practical consideration given this database's standing caution that oil-based lubricants can damage latex condoms (per [[vaginal-lubricants-moisturizers]]); Trojan's own water-based lubricant avoids this specific risk by design
- Contains sodium hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid), a genuine humectant/moisturizing ingredient with a real, if not independently re-verified here, general moisture-retention mechanism
- Long brand history and wide availability, primarily built on the company's core condom business

## Cons
- **No independent (non-manufacturer) osmolality testing or dedicated clinical trial was located for this specific lubricant line in this pass** — unlike its top three category-mates (K-Y, Replens, Astroglide), all of which have been independently tested and found hyperosmolar; this is a genuine data gap, not a confirmed favorable or unfavorable result
- Contains methylparaben (a preservative), relevant for anyone specifically avoiding parabens, a consideration also raised for [[Astroglide]]
- Smallest independently-tracked share (1%, tied with Swiss Navy) in this category

## Use Cases
Personal lubrication for sexual activity, marketed with specific latex-condom compatibility.
