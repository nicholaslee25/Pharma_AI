## Overview
DripDrop is confirmed to be a real, physician-developed (Dr. Eduardo Dolhun, Mayo Clinic-trained) oral rehydration solution built specifically on WHO ORS ratio science — sodium paired with glucose to drive sodium-glucose cotransport-mediated water absorption. This pass located genuinely strong, independent, peer-reviewed brand-specific evidence: **Fan et al. 2020**, a randomized crossover trial, found DripDrop produced 34 percentage points more fluid retention than plain water and ~20% more than a standard sports drink — a real, quantified, head-to-head demonstration; and **Sollanek et al. 2019** independently measured DripDrop's osmolarity and confirmed WHO-standard compliance. This is one of the strongest, most directly brand-specific (not merely ingredient-class-level) evidence bases found across this entire Vitamins/Supplements research pass. DripDrop also has a documented military/occupational use case (heat-stress/exertional heat injury prevention). Hydration Support (4% share) is covered by [[oral-rehydration-electrolytes]].

## Pros
- **Genuinely strong, independent, peer-reviewed, brand-specific clinical evidence**: a randomized crossover trial directly comparing DripDrop against both water and a standard sports drink, with real, quantified superior fluid-retention results — a rarer, more direct evidence standard than most brands in this database's Hydration Support category
- Independently confirmed WHO ORS osmolarity compliance (not merely a brand claim)
- Physician-developed with a stated clinical rehydration-science rationale, and real documented adoption in a demanding, high-stakes use case (military heat-stress prevention)

## Cons

- No independent third-party quality certification (USP Verified, NSF) was located specifically for DripDrop in this pass
- As an ORS-ratio product, likely has a higher sodium content than standard sports drinks — appropriate for actual rehydration needs, but a consideration for anyone on a sodium-restricted diet
