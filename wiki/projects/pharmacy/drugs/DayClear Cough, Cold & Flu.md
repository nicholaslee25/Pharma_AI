## Overview
Confirmed via DailyMed: DayClear Cough Cold and Flu (per 30mL dose) = **choline salicylate 870mg + dextromethorphan HBr 20mg + guaifenesin 200mg + phenylephrine HCl 10mg**. This is a genuinely distinctive and previously-unlinked finding: **choline salicylate is a salicylate (aspirin-family) pain reliever, not acetaminophen** — the only product identified in this entire batch that uses a salicylate rather than acetaminophen as its pain/fever component (aside from Alka-Seltzer Plus's separate aspirin-based Cold Medicine Sparkling Original variant). No ingredient page exists for choline salicylate specifically in this database ([[aspirin]] covers acetylsalicylic acid, a related but chemically distinct salicylate ester) — flagged as a data gap. Touches [[dextromethorphan]], [[guaifenesin]], and [[oral-phenylephrine]] directly; choline salicylate itself has no dedicated page. Label saved at `raw/primary-sources/brands/dayclear/dailymed-dayclear.md`.

## Pros
- Provides an acetaminophen-free option for pain/fever relief within a multi-symptom cold/flu product — potentially useful for someone specifically wanting to avoid acetaminophen (e.g., someone already at their acetaminophen limit from another medication) while still getting a pain reliever

## Cons
- **Carries the full salicylate risk profile, not the acetaminophen risk profile a reader might assume from this being a generic "cold and flu" product**: Reye's syndrome risk in children/teenagers with chickenpox or flu-like illness, potential severe allergic reactions to salicylates, and an explicit "contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding" warning on its own label — meaningfully different safety considerations than the acetaminophen-based products dominating this category (DayQuil, NyQuil, Theraflu, Tylenol variants)
- Explicitly **not for children under 12** — a stricter age restriction than some acetaminophen-based competitors, consistent with the Reye's syndrome/salicylate risk
- Do not use with MAOIs (dextromethorphan interaction) or if allergic to salicylates
- No dedicated ingredient page exists for choline salicylate to provide a fuller evidence/safety comparison — a genuine research gap

## Use Cases
Cough, nasal congestion, minor aches and pains, sore throat, headache, and fever relief for adults and children ≥12y specifically wanting or needing a non-acetaminophen pain reliever.

## Allergies / Warnings
Do not use in children/teenagers with chickenpox or flu-like symptoms (Reye's syndrome risk). Do not use if allergic to salicylates (aspirin) or taking other NSAIDs. Do not use with MAOIs. Not for children under 12.

## Symptoms Treated
Cough, nasal congestion, minor aches/pains, sore throat, headache, fever.
