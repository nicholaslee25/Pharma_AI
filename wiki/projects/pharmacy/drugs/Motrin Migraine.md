## Overview

See `drugs/Advil Migraine.md` for the same-formulation comparison and the flagged discrepancy regarding migraine-specific FDA claims across brands (Excedrin Migraine's "only FDA-approved OTC migraine medication" marketing vs. ibuprofen-based products' own "Treats migraine" label Use).

## Pros
- Confirmed FDA Drug Facts label with a well-established NSAID active ingredient (ibuprofen), backed by [[ibuprofen]]'s large-scale Cochrane meta-analytic evidence
- Solubilized/liquid-gel ibuprofen formulation, same category flagged favorably in the Derry 2009 Cochrane review's "soluble ibuprofen salts produced better efficacy estimates" finding
- Functionally identical formulation to Advil Migraine, giving consumers a second brand option for the same active-ingredient approach to OTC migraine treatment

## Cons
- Same regulatory-claim tension flagged for Advil Migraine applies here — not independently resolved in this pass
- Carries the full ibuprofen risk profile documented on [[ibuprofen]]: boxed warnings for cardiovascular thrombotic events and GI bleeding, contraindicated perioperatively for CABG, aspirin-interaction timing issue
- No independent, Motrin-specific migraine RCT was identified in this pass — relies on the general ibuprofen evidence base and its own label's regulatory standing
- Smallest share (3%) of the confirmed-formulation migraine brands in this database's data, despite being chemically identical to the much larger-share Advil Migraine (13%) — a real-world example of brand recognition mattering more than formulation for market share, not a clinical difference
