## Overview
This is [[Nature's Bounty]]'s dedicated fish-oil/omega-3 product line, tracked here in the Cholesterol Management category (16% share). **This is the specific product line directly named in a real, confirmed class-action lawsuit already documented on [[Nature's Bounty]]'s own page**: a suit alleging a Nature's Bounty fish oil product contained "not a single milligram" of the omega-3 fatty acids (EPA/DHA) claimed on its label — a direct, brand-specific label-accuracy allegation, not a generic industry criticism. For the underlying omega-3/cholesterol mechanism, see [[omega-3-fish-oil]]: standard OTC-dose fish oil shows a modest (~15%) triglyceride reduction per a 2020 Cochrane review of 86 RCTs, with no confirmed effect on hard cardiovascular outcomes at that dose — the strongest cardiovascular evidence (REDUCE-IT) applies only to a high-dose prescription formulation. If the mislabeling allegation is accurate, this product would deliver neither the modest standard-dose benefit nor any of the prescription-dose benefit, since it would not be reliably delivering omega-3s at all.
