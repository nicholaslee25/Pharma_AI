## Appears In

## Overview
Swiss Navy (MD Science Lab) sells both a silicone-based line (Premium Silicone Lubricant: cyclopentasiloxane, dimethicone, tocopheryl acetate/vitamin E) and a separate water-based "All Natural" line, confirmed via manufacturer ingredient disclosure. This is a real, confirmed formulation-category split under one brand name: silicone-based lubricants contain no water and are, by definition, not subject to the aqueous-osmolality framework (WHO 380 mOsm/kg guidance, per [[vaginal-lubricants-moisturizers]]) used to evaluate K-Y/Replens/Astroglide/Good Clean Love — a genuinely different safety-evaluation category, not a "safer" or "less safe" one on the same scale.

## Pros
- Silicone-based formulation is naturally hypoallergenic per manufacturer claim, waterproof, pH-neutral, and (since it contains no water) cannot be evaluated as hyperosmotic by the aqueous-osmolality metric that flags several other top brands in this category
- Long-lasting formulation requiring less reapplication, a real practical (not safety) advantage of the silicone-based chemistry generally
- Water-based "All Natural" alternative exists for users preferring that formulation type, free of parabens and glycerin per manufacturer claim

## Cons
- **No independent (non-manufacturer) safety testing, dedicated clinical trial, or third-party review was located for this specific brand in this pass** — evaluation rests on manufacturer ingredient disclosure alone
- Silicone-based lubricants generally (a class-wide consideration, not Swiss-Navy-specific) can degrade silicone-based sex toys on contact — a real, practical material-compatibility caution common to this lubricant type
- Smallest independently-tracked share (1%, tied with Trojan) in this category, consistent with less available independent coverage

## Use Cases
Personal lubrication for sexual activity — silicone-based line for long-lasting, water-resistant use; water-based line for a more traditional, easily-rinsed formulation.

## Allergies / Warnings
No brand-specific recall or lawsuit located in this pass. Silicone-based formulation: avoid use with silicone-based sex toys due to material degradation risk (a general category caution, not brand-specific).

## Symptoms Treated
Vaginal dryness/friction during sexual activity (comfort use only).
