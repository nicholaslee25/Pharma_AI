## Overview
The **Ellume COVID-19 Home Test** was the first fully at-home SARS-CoV-2 test to receive FDA EUA for over-the-counter sale (December 15, 2020), with strong initial clinical performance — **96% sensitivity / 100% specificity in symptomatic individuals**, and **91% sensitivity / 96% specificity in asymptomatic individuals** — genuinely among the best brand-specific figures found in this category, exceeding even the top of the Cochrane-reviewed brand range on [[at-home-covid-tests]] (34.3–91.3% symptomatic). **However, this is materially offset by a real, serious safety event:** the FDA classified an Ellume recall as a **Class I recall (the most serious FDA recall category)** after the company identified an "abnormal rate of false positives" traced to a manufacturing issue. The recall began at 200,000 test kits (April–August shipments) and was later expanded to approximately 2 million affected tests. This is the only brand in this category with a documented Class I recall found in this pass, and it directly contradicts the initially reported 96–100% specificity figures for the affected lots — a genuine, serious discrepancy between pre-market clinical validation and post-market real-world manufacturing quality that this database's other diagnostic brand pages did not encounter.

## Pros
- Historically the strongest brand-specific sensitivity/specificity combination found in this category (96%/100% symptomatic; 91%/96% asymptomatic), when performing to specification
- First fully at-home authorized COVID test — an early, technically significant regulatory milestone
- FDA and manufacturer transparency about the recall (public Class I recall notice, safety communication, and expanding-scope updates) is itself a documented, traceable safety response rather than a quietly-handled issue

## Cons
- **Class I FDA recall for false positives affecting up to ~2 million test kits** — this is the single most serious safety finding among any diagnostic brand researched in this pass and should not be minimized or omitted
- The recall was caused by a manufacturing issue, meaning the pre-recall clinical performance data (96–100% specificity) does not describe the actual real-world performance of affected lots — a stark illustration of why post-market surveillance matters even after strong clinical-trial results
- Smallest share (4%) of this category, which may itself partly reflect market response to the recall (though the recall's exact timing relative to current market share was not independently confirmed in this pass)
- No subsequent independent re-validation study of corrected/current-production lots was located in this pass to confirm the false-positive issue has been fully resolved

## Use Cases
Rapid (~20 minute) fully at-home qualitative detection of SARS-CoV-2 antigen, originally authorized for both symptomatic and asymptomatic use.

## Allergies / Warnings
Not a drug — no allergy profile. **Real, specific device-safety warning:** certain lots (April–August shipment window, later-expanded scope) were recalled for an elevated false-positive rate due to a manufacturing defect — consumers should verify their specific lot against the FDA recall notice before relying on a result, and treat an unexpected positive result from an affected lot with appropriate skepticism pending confirmatory testing.

Not applicable — a diagnostic test, not a treatment.
