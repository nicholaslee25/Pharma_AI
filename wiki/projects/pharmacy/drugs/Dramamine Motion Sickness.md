## Overview
**Confirmed 2026-08-28 via DailyMed:** Dramamine Motion Sickness (Original Formula) is dimenhydrinate 50mg per tablet, resolving this database's prior hedge on [[dimenhydrinate]]. This is the category-leading product (62% share) in Motion Sickness Remedies. Per [[dimenhydrinate]]'s established brand-family finding, "Dramamine" spans at least THREE mechanistically distinct products under one name — dimenhydrinate (this product and Dramamine for Kids), meclizine (Dramamine Nausea, see [[meclizine]]), and ginger root extract (Dramamine Advanced Herbals, a non-drug herbal line) — a consumer should confirm which specific Dramamine product they are using.

## Pros
- Confirmed genuine, established antihistamine mechanism (H1-receptor blockade at the vestibular nucleus/area postrema) for motion-sickness prevention
- Category-leading share reflects extensive real-world use history
- Dosing is age-tiered down to age 2 (per [[dimenhydrinate]]'s pediatric dosing table), giving it broader labeled pediatric applicability than meclizine-based competitors (not labeled under 12)

## Cons
- Shares dimenhydrinate's full documented safety profile per [[dimenhydrinate]]: sedation (a real, common side effect, more pronounced than meclizine's per general first-generation-antihistamine comparison), paradoxical excitation reported in some children, anticholinergic effects (dry mouth, blurred vision, constipation)
- No dedicated adult motion-sickness pediatric-caliber RCT with hard cure/prevention-rate numbers was located for the adult formulation specifically in this pass — the strongest pediatric RCT located on [[dimenhydrinate]] studied gastroenteritis-related vomiting, a different indication, not motion sickness directly
- "Dramamine" brand-name spans three different active-ingredient mechanisms (see Overview) — a real navigation burden already documented on [[dimenhydrinate]] and [[meclizine]]

## Use Cases
Prevention and treatment of motion sickness symptoms: nausea, vomiting, dizziness (per label).

## Allergies / Warnings
Standard first-generation-antihistamine cautions apply per [[dimenhydrinate]]: caution with respiratory disease (asthma) especially under age 2; synergistic CNS depression with alcohol/sedatives; caution with narrow-angle glaucoma.

## Symptoms Treated
Motion-sickness-related nausea, vomiting, dizziness.
