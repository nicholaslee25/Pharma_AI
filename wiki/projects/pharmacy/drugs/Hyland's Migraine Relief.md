## Overview
**Cross-category finding, consistent with [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s established framing:** Hyland's Migraine Relief is confirmed via DailyMed/manufacturer labeling to be a **homeopathic** product — active ingredients Belladonna 6X HPUS, gelsemium 6X HPUS, glonoinum 12X HPUS, iris versicolor 6X HPUS, nux vomica 6X HPUS, and sanguinaria canadensis 6X HPUS (a related DailyMed filing under "Migraine Headache Relief" lists a similar but not identical combination including nitroglycerin, atropa belladonna, gelsemium sempervirens, strychnos nux-vomica, iris versicolor, and sanguinaria canadensis — SKU-to-SKU ingredient variance was not fully reconciled in this pass). The product's own label carries the standard FDA homeopathic disclaimer: **"This product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy,"** and the FDA is not aware of scientific evidence supporting homeopathy as effective. This is the same evidence framing already established on [[homeopathic-cold-products]] (Cochrane review showing better-designed trials find no benefit, weaker trials report an effect) — no dedicated migraine-specific homeopathic RCT was identified in this pass, so that page's general "no convincing evidence of clinically meaningful benefit at claimed dilutions" characterization is the most honest applicable evidence framing here, not a migraine-specific study.

## Pros
- Free of aspirin, acetaminophen, ibuprofen, and naproxen — a genuine option for someone specifically wishing to avoid those active ingredients (e.g., due to a contraindication), regardless of the product's own efficacy question
- Non-drowsy, non-habit-forming per manufacturer labeling
- At the extremely high dilutions used (6X-12X, HPUS), the product is very unlikely to cause direct pharmacological toxicity — the risk profile here is fundamentally different from the NSAID/acetaminophen-based products dominating this category

## Cons
- **The product's own FDA-mandated label states it has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy, and the FDA is not aware of scientific evidence supporting homeopathy as effective** — this is not this database's interpretation, it is the disclaimer language homeopathic products themselves are required to carry
- Per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]'s Cochrane-level evidence for the broader homeopathic-product category, the strongest available systematic review found an inverse relationship between trial quality and reported benefit — better-designed trials found nothing, weaker/higher-bias trials reported an effect — a pattern consistent with a null true effect rather than a real, undetected benefit
- **The primary documented risk with homeopathic products generally (per [[homeopathic-cold-products]]) is not direct toxicity but delaying effective evaluation/treatment** — relevant here specifically because the label itself flags that new/atypical/undiagnosed migraine presentations warrant a doctor's evaluation, a caution that applies doubly to a product without established efficacy
- Ingredient lists appear to vary somewhat across different Hyland's migraine-product SKUs found in this pass (a 6-ingredient list vs. a DailyMed filing listing nitroglycerin among other ingredients) — not fully reconciled here, flagged as an open question rather than resolved

## Use Cases
Marketed for temporary relief of adult migraine headache symptoms — throbbing/pounding/dull headache, dizziness, light sensitivity, nausea, noise sensitivity, blurred vision — without established efficacy evidence per the product's own required FDA disclaimer.

## Allergies / Warnings
Per label: this homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy. As a low-toxicity-risk product at these dilutions, no drug-specific interaction/overdose warnings comparable to the NSAID/acetaminophen products in this category apply — but a consumer with a genuine, undiagnosed, or worsening migraine pattern should still seek medical evaluation rather than relying on this product alone.

## Symptoms Treated
Claimed (not FDA-established) relief of migraine-associated throbbing headache, dizziness, light/noise sensitivity, nausea, and blurred vision.
