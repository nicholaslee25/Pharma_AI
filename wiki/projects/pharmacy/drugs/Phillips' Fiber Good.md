## Appears In
Laxatives, Bulk/Fiber - 1%

## Overview
Confirmed: Phillips' Fiber Good Gummies use **inulin** (inulin syrup + inulin powder) as the fiber source, 4g prebiotic fiber per serving — the same fiber type confirmed for MiraFIBER in this same research pass, but under the Phillips' brand family (which also markets Phillips'/Phillips' Stool Softener, separately researched in this database with a genuinely different mechanism, PEG-3350/docusate respectively). Inulin is not one of the two fiber types ([[fiber]]'s cited meta-analysis: psyllium, pectin) shown to produce significant treatment-response improvement for chronic constipation. A dietary supplement gummy, not an FDA-monograph OTC drug.

## Pros
- Gummy format improves compliance for users who dislike mixing powders or swallowing capsules; labeled for both adults and children (4 years+)
- Inulin has a genuine prebiotic mechanism supporting gut bacterial fermentation, distinct from (though not necessarily superior or inferior to) psyllium's bulk-forming mechanism

## Cons
- **Not psyllium or pectin** — per [[fiber]]'s own cited meta-analysis, inulin was not one of the two fiber types shown to produce significant treatment-response improvement, the same pattern found for most other bulk-fiber brands in this database's research pass
- **Not an FDA-approved OTC drug** — a dietary supplement gummy carrying the standard "not evaluated by the FDA" disclaimer, unlike the plain [[Phillips'|Phillips]] (PEG-3350) and [[Phillips' Stool Softener]] (docusate) brand-family products, which ARE FDA OTC monograph drugs — a real, brand-family-internal regulatory-status split worth flagging, since "Phillips'" spans both FDA-drug and dietary-supplement products under one family name
- A separate "Phillips' Fiber Good Gummies Plus Energy Support" and "Plus Metabolism Support" SKU exist in the real market with added B vitamins/chromium/other ingredients beyond plain inulin — a real multi-formulation spread this database's 1% share does not specify a SKU for
- Smallest recommendation share (1%) of any brand in this category
- No Phillips'-Fiber-Good-brand-specific RCT was independently located in this pass

## Use Cases
Marketed for digestive regularity/prebiotic fiber support via inulin — a gummy-format alternative distinct from the same brand family's FDA-drug laxative products.

## Allergies / Warnings
No FDA Drug Facts label located (dietary supplement); manufacturer's own label carries the standard "not evaluated by FDA" disclaimer. General fiber-supplement gas/bloating tradeoff applies per [[fiber]].

## Symptoms Treated
Marketed for digestive regularity/gut-health support via inulin fiber — a different specific fiber type than Metamucil's psyllium, per [[fiber]], and mechanistically unrelated to the same brand family's PEG-3350/docusate-based laxative drug products.

## Brand-Specific Alignment Check
**Real finding, added to both this page and [[fiber]]:** the "Phillips'" brand family spans genuinely different regulatory categories (FDA OTC monograph drugs for plain Phillips'/Phillips' Stool Softener, vs. a dietary-supplement gummy for Fiber Good) AND genuinely different mechanisms (PEG-3350 osmotic laxative, docusate stool softener, inulin prebiotic fiber) all under one family brand name — a more extensive version of the same-brand-different-mechanism pattern already flagged for MiraLAX/MiraFIBER in this same research pass.
