## Active Ingredient
Guaifenesin 600 mg (extended-release tablet)

## Purpose
Expectorant

## Uses
Helps loosen phlegm (mucus) and thin bronchial secretions to rid the bronchial passageways of bothersome mucus, making coughs more productive.

## Warnings

**Do not use:** for children under 12 years of age.

**Ask a doctor before use if:** persistent/chronic cough occurs with smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, or emphysema; or cough is accompanied by too much phlegm (mucus).

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** cough lasts more than 7 days, comes back, or occurs with fever, rash, or persistent headache — these could be signs of a serious illness.

**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** ask a health professional before use.

## Directions
Do not crush or break the tablet. Take with a full glass of water; may be taken without regard to meal timing. Adults and children ≥12 years: 1–2 extended-release tablets every 12 hours. Do not exceed 4 tablets in 24 hours.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carbomer homopolymer type B, FD&C blue no. 1 aluminum lake, hypromellose USP, magnesium stearate NF, microcrystalline cellulose NF, sodium starch glycolate NF.
