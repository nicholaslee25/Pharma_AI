## Active Ingredient
Benzoyl peroxide 10%

## Purpose
Acne medication

## Uses
For the treatment of acne

## Warnings
**Do not use if:** you have very sensitive skin or sensitivity to benzoyl peroxide.
**When using:** skin irritation may develop (redness, burning, itching, peeling, possibly swelling); avoid combining with other topical acne medications; avoid unnecessary sun exposure, use sunscreen; keep away from eyes, lips, mouth, hair, and dyed fabrics (may bleach).
**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** irritation becomes severe.
**Pregnancy/nursing:** consult a health professional before use.
**Keep out of reach of children;** contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Clean skin thoroughly, work into lather, apply thin layer to affected areas 1-3 times daily, rinse thoroughly and pat dry. Begin with once-daily application, gradually increase frequency as needed. Reduce frequency if excessive dryness occurs. Apply sunscreen when going outside.

## Inactive Ingredients
Water, cetearyl alcohol, decyl glucoside, dextrin, glycerin, sodium chloride, sodium cocoyl isethionate, tetrasodium glutamate diacetate
