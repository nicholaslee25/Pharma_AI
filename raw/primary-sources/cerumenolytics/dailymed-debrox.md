## Active Ingredient
Carbamide peroxide 6.5% (65 mg per mL), non-USP

## Purpose
Earwax removal aid

## Uses
Occasional use as an aid to soften, loosen, and remove excessive earwax.

## Warnings — Do Not Use If
Ear drainage/discharge; ear pain, irritation, or rash in the ear; injury or perforation of the eardrum; after ear surgery (unless directed by a doctor); for more than 4 consecutive days.

## Directions
Adults/children >12y: tilt head sideways, place 10 drops into the ear (applicator tip should not enter the ear canal), keep drops in ear for several minutes (e.g., by placing cotton in the ear). May be used twice daily for up to 4 days if needed. Product foams on contact with earwax due to oxygen release.

## Inactive Ingredients
Citric acid monohydrate, glycerin, propylene glycol, sodium lauroyl sarcosinate, water.
