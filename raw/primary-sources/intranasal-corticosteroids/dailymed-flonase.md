## Active Ingredient
Fluticasone propionate (glucocorticoid) 50 mcg per spray

## Purpose
Allergy symptom reliever

## Uses
Temporarily relieves hay fever/upper respiratory allergy symptoms: nasal congestion, itchy nose, runny nose, itchy watery eyes, sneezing.

## Warnings

**Do not use:** in children under 4 years; to treat asthma; with an unhealed nose injury or nasal surgery; if allergic to the product/ingredients.

**Ask a doctor before use if:** you have or had glaucoma or cataracts.

**Ask a doctor/pharmacist before use if taking:** HIV medicines (e.g. ritonavir), steroid medicines for asthma/allergies/skin rash, or ketoconazole pills for fungal infection.

**When using this product:** child growth rate may slow; brief stinging/sneezing may occur; do not share the bottle; tell your doctor about all medicines you take.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** exposed to chickenpox/measles/tuberculosis; symptoms don't improve within 7 days or new symptoms develop; a constant whistling sound from the nose occurs; allergic reaction develops; vision changes occur; severe or frequent nosebleeds develop.

**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** ask a health professional before use.

## Directions
Shake gently before each use; once daily only.

- **Adults/children ≥12y:** Week 1 — 2 sprays/nostril once daily; Weeks 2 through 6 months — 1–2 sprays/nostril once daily as needed; beyond 6 months — ask a doctor
- **Children 4–11y:** adult-supervised use, 1 spray/nostril once daily; growth monitoring recommended if used beyond 2 months/year
- **Children <4y:** do not use

Relief may begin day 1; full effect after several days. Store 4–30°C (39–86°F).

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzalkonium chloride, dextrose, microcrystalline cellulose, phenylethyl alcohol, polysorbate 80, purified water, sodium carboxymethylcellulose.
