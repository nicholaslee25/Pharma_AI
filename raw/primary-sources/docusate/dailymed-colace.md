## Active Ingredient
Docusate sodium 100 mg per capsule

## Purpose
Stool softener

## Uses
Relieves occasional constipation (irregularity); generally produces a bowel movement in 12 to 72 hours.

## Warnings

**Do not use if:** currently taking mineral oil, unless directed by a doctor.

**Ask a doctor before use if:** stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, or a sudden change in bowel movements persisting over 2 weeks.

**Stop use if:** rectal bleeding occurs, or no bowel movement results after use of a laxative; or if a stool softener is needed for more than 1 week.

**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** ask a health professional before use.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 1–3 capsules daily. Children 2–<12y: 1 capsule daily. Children <2y: consult a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
D&C Red No. 33, FD&C Red No. 40, FD&C Yellow No. 6, gelatin, glycerin, PEG 400, propylene glycol, sorbitol, titanium dioxide.
