## Active Ingredient
Acetaminophen 500 mg per caplet

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer

## Uses
Temporary relief of minor aches/pains: common cold, headache, backache, minor arthritis pain, toothache, muscular aches, menstrual cramps; temporarily reduces fever.

## Warnings

**Liver warning:** this product contains acetaminophen. Severe liver damage may occur if taking more than 4,000 mg of acetaminophen in 24 hours, with other drugs containing acetaminophen, or while consuming 3+ alcoholic drinks every day during use.

**Allergy alert:** acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions (skin reddening, blisters, rash) — stop use and seek medical help if these occur.

**Do not use:** with any other drug containing acetaminophen; if allergic to acetaminophen or any inactive ingredient.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12 years: 2 caplets every 6 hours while symptoms last; do not take more than 6 caplets in 24 hours without doctor approval; do not use beyond 10 days without medical direction.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carnauba wax, corn starch, FD&C red no. 40 aluminum lake, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, modified starch, polyethylene glycol, powdered cellulose, pregelatinized starch, propylene glycol, shellac, sodium starch glycolate, titanium dioxide.
