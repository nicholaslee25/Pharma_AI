# PubMed Abstract — Lancet Puncture Site Pain RCT (condensed facts, not full text)

**Source:** Loveland ME, Carley SD, Cranfield N, Hillier VF, Mackway-Jones K. Assessment of the pain of blood-sugar testing: a randomised controlled trial. Lancet (journal). 1999. PMID: 10489959.
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26 (abstract viewed via PubMed, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10489959/)

## Condensed facts
- Randomized controlled trial (England), publication type Clinical Trial/Letter, comparing pain of blood glucose sampling by different puncture site/method.
- Finding: lancet puncture to the **side of the thumb resulted in less pain** than lancet puncture to the finger or venepuncture at the elbow.
- Success rates (ability to obtain adequate sample) were the same across methods.
- Related follow-up trial (Carley et al., BMJ 2000, PMID 10875827): open prospective RCT comparing ear vs. thumb puncture for blood glucose testing pain (not independently re-verified here beyond citation).
