## Condensed facts
- Systematic review/meta-analysis of new-generation infrared tympanic thermometer (IRTT) diagnostic accuracy in children, searched PubMed/Web of Science/Embase, quality assessed via QUADAS-2, synthesized with MetaDisc 1.4.
- 12 diagnostic studies, 4,639 pediatric patients. Fever cutoffs across included studies ranged 37.0–38.0°C.
- **Optimal cutoff identified: 37.8°C**, with the highest sROC AUC (**0.97**) and Youden Index (**0.83**) among tested cutoffs.
- Conclusion: new-generation tympanic thermometry has high diagnostic accuracy in pediatric patients at this cutoff and can be an alternative for fever screening in children.
