## Active Ingredient
Polyethylene glycol 3350, 17g per cap-fill/packet

## Purpose
Osmotic laxative

## Uses
Relieves occasional constipation (irregularity); generally produces a bowel movement in 1 to 3 days.

## Warnings
Do not use if allergic to PEG, or if kidney disease is present without medical supervision. Consult a doctor before use if nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, sudden bowel habit changes >2 weeks, or IBS. May cause loose/watery/more frequent stools during use. Stop and seek medical advice if rectal bleeding, worsening nausea/bloating/cramping/diarrhea occurs, or if use is needed beyond 1 week. **Do not combine with starch-based thickeners used for swallowing difficulty.**

## Directions
Adults ≥17y: use once a day, dissolve powder in 4–8 oz of any beverage and drink. Do not use for more than 7 days.

## Inactive Ingredients
None.
