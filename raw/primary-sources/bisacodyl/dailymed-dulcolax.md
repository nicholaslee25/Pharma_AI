## Active Ingredient
Bisacodyl USP 5 mg per tablet

## Purpose
Stimulant laxative

## Uses
Temporary relief of occasional constipation/irregularity; generally produces a bowel movement in 6 to 12 hours.

## Warnings
Do not use if unable to swallow tablets whole. Consult a doctor before use if stomach pain, nausea, vomiting, or bowel-habit changes lasting >2 weeks. **Do not chew or crush tablets** (would destroy the enteric coating that protects the stomach and delays release until the colon). **Avoid taking within 1 hour of antacids or milk** (can dissolve the enteric coating prematurely). May cause stomach discomfort, faintness, cramps. Stop use and consult a doctor if rectal bleeding occurs, no bowel movement follows use, or use exceeds 1 week. Pregnant/nursing: consult a healthcare provider.

## Directions
Take with water. Adults/children ≥12y: 1–3 tablets daily. Children 6–11y: 1 tablet daily. Children <6y: consult a doctor.
