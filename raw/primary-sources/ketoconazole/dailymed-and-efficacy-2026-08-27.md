## Nizoral 2% (Rx) — full Drug Facts/label panel
(setid=efbc6388-90ce-11de-b92c-8b7156d89593)

**Active Ingredient:** Ketoconazole 2% (20mg in 1mL)
**Purpose:** Antifungal agent for tinea (pityriasis) versicolor
**Uses:** Treatment of tinea (pityriasis) versicolor caused by or presumed to be caused by Pityrosporum orbiculare
**Warnings:** Severe hypersensitivity reactions including anaphylaxis have been reported. May irritate eye mucous membranes; avoid contact. Adverse effects: hair discoloration, abnormal texture, itching, burning, contact dermatitis, rash, urticaria. Discontinue and seek medical care for rash, swelling, angioedema, or breathing difficulty. Pediatric safety not established.
**Directions:** Apply to damp skin of affected area and a wide margin surrounding it. Lather, leave in place 5 minutes, rinse. One application typically sufficient.
**Manufacturer:** Manufactured for Janssen Pharmaceuticals, Inc., Titusville, NJ; manufactured by Janssen Pharmaceutica NV, Beerse, Belgium.

## Nizoral A-D (OTC, 1%) — search-summarized, not independently pulled as full label
1% ketoconazole, ages 12+, twice weekly use (at least 3 days between applications), not for daily use. Indication: dandruff (flaking/itching).

## Efficacy Literature (search-summarized, not full-text independently pulled)
- Pierard-Franchimont et al., multicenter RCT, PMID 12476017 — ketoconazole 2% vs zinc pyrithione 1% shampoos in severe dandruff/seborrheic dermatitis: 73% improvement in total dandruff severity score at week 4 (ketoconazole 2%) vs 67% (zinc pyrithione 1%); ketoconazole also had lower recurrence rate.
- Dose-response study, PMID 11306850 — ketoconazole 1% vs 2% shampoos, clinical/squamometric/mycological assessments: 2% significantly superior to 1% for flakiness and Malassezia density reduction at 2 and 4 weeks.
- General pooled literature: dose-response order 2% > 1% > 0.5% > placebo/vehicle.
