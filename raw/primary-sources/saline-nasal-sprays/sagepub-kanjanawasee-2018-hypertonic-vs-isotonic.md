# Hypertonic vs. Isotonic Saline Nasal Irrigation — Systematic Review/Meta-analysis

Source: Kanjanawasee et al. 2018 (SAGE journals, doi 10.1177/1945892418773566); additional 2024 meta-analysis (9 trials, 645 patients, allergic rhinitis) and separate 4-trial/351-patient meta-analysis
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Findings
Hypertonic saline showed greater improvement in nasal congestion and nasal secretion reduction vs. isotonic saline. Separate meta-analysis: hypertonic saline improved nasal symptom scores and reduced rescue antihistamine use rates vs. isotonic.

## Mechanism
Hypertonic solution draws water out of nasal mucosa cells via osmosis, reducing mucosal swelling/congestion — distinct from isotonic saline's pure moisturizing/rinsing action.
