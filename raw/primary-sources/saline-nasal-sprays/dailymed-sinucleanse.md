# SinuCleanse Neti-Pot — DailyMed Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ba953a99-4885-4823-9905-75ce3eae160c
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredients (per packet)
Sodium Bicarbonate USP 703 mg, Sodium Chloride USP 2300 mg

## Use
Temporarily relieves sinusitis/cold/flu/allergy symptoms (sneezing, runny nose, nasal stuffiness, post-nasal drip); removes inhaled irritants; promotes nasal/sinus drainage; reduces nasal membrane swelling.

## Directions
Adults/children 4+: 1-2 packets dissolved per neti-pot use, up to every 2 hours as needed. Under 4: consult physician.

## Warning
Stop use and ask a doctor if washing is uncomfortable or symptoms aren't relieved.
