## Composition
Purified gentle salt (saline) solution — no active drug ingredient in the standard formulation. Ingredients vary by SKU: purified water, sodium chloride 0.65%, disodium phosphate, phenylcarbinol, monosodium phosphate, benzalkonium chloride (some SKUs).

## Use
Stuffy nose, post-nasal drip, dryness, nasal irritation from colds, flu, allergies, or pollutants.
