# Cochrane Reviews — Saline Nasal Irrigation (URI and Allergic Rhinitis)

Pulled: 2026-08-26

## King et al. 2015 (CD006821.pub3) — Acute Upper Respiratory Tract Infections
5 RCTs, 544 children + 205 adults, saline irrigation vs. routine care/other nasal sprays (not placebo in most trials). Some symptom benefit reported; comparator was usual care rather than placebo in most included trials — a real limitation on strength of claim.

## Head et al. 2018 (CD012597.pub2) — Allergic Rhinitis
Saline irrigation may reduce patient-reported disease severity vs. no saline irrigation at up to 3 months, in adults and children, no reported adverse effects.
