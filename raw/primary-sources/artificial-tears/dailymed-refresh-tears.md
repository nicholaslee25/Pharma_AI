## Active Ingredient
Carboxymethylcellulose sodium 0.5%

## Purpose
Eye lubricant

## Uses
Temporary relief of burning, irritation, and discomfort due to dryness of the eye or exposure to wind/sun. May serve as a protectant against further irritation.

## Warnings
External use only. Avoid touching container tip to any surface; replace cap after use. Do not use if solution changes color or becomes cloudy.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** you experience eye pain, changes in vision, continued redness/irritation, or condition worsens/persists >72 hours.

**Keep out of reach of children:** if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control right away.

## Directions
Instill 1 or 2 drops in the affected eye(s) as needed.

## Other Information
Use only if tape seal is intact. Use before expiration date. Discard 90 days after opening. Store at 59–77°F (15–25°C).

## Inactive Ingredients
Boric acid, calcium chloride dihydrate, magnesium chloride hexahydrate, potassium chloride, purified water, PURITE stabilized oxychloro complex (preservative), sodium borate decahydrate, sodium chloride, HCl and/or NaOH for pH adjustment.

**Note:** this is one representative OTC artificial-tears label (single-agent carboxymethylcellulose, PURITE-preserved). This ingredient page covers artificial tears as a class — other marketed products vary by active ingredient (hypromellose, glycerin, PEG, propylene glycol, polyvinyl alcohol, hyaluronic acid), concentration, preservative system, and whether preservative-free — see the Semp et al. 2023 systematic review for why these formulation differences matter clinically.
