## Active Ingredient
Aspirin 325 mg (NSAID) per tablet

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer

## Uses
Temporary relief of minor aches/pains: headache, muscle pain, toothache, menstrual pain, cold symptoms, minor arthritis pain; temporarily reduces fever.

## Warnings

**Reye's syndrome:** children and teenagers who have or are recovering from chicken pox or flu-like symptoms should NOT use this product. Seek medical advice if behavioral changes, nausea, or vomiting occur (early Reye syndrome signs).

**Allergy alert:** aspirin may cause a severe allergic reaction — hives, facial swelling, asthma (wheezing), shock.

**Stomach bleeding warning:** contains an NSAID, may cause severe stomach bleeding. Higher risk: age 60+, ulcer history, blood thinner/steroid use, other NSAID use, ≥3 alcoholic drinks/day, exceeding dosage directions.

**Pregnancy:** especially important not to use in the last 3 months of pregnancy unless definitely directed by a doctor.

## Directions
Drink a full glass of water with each dose. Adults/children ≥12: 1–2 tablets every 4 hours, or 3 tablets every 6 hours; do not exceed 12 tablets in 24 hours. Children <12: ask a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Corn starch, hypromellose, powdered cellulose, triacetin.
