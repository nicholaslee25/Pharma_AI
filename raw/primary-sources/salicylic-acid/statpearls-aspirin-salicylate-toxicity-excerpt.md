## Salicylate Toxicity (relevant to excessive/large-BSA topical salicylic acid use)
- **Therapeutic salicylate range: 150-300 mcg/mL. Toxic level: >300 mcg/mL.**
- **Mild toxicity symptoms:** tinnitus, dizziness, lethargy, nausea, vomiting.
- **Severe toxicity:** hyperthermia, tachypnea leading to respiratory alkalosis, high anion gap metabolic acidosis, hypokalemia, hypoglycemia, seizures, coma, cerebral edema.
- **Management:** urinary alkalinization (bicarbonate drips), activated charcoal/bowel irrigation, airway protection; hemodialysis for severe cases (reduces clearance from days to hours). Hemodialysis indicated for: acute ingestion ≥100 mg/dL, chronic ingestion ≥40 mg/dL, neurotoxicity at any level, renal failure, pulmonary edema, or cardiovascular compromise.
- Dose-dependent elimination kinetics switch from first-order to zero-order at higher concentrations — meaning toxicity can escalate disproportionately once saturation occurs, a real pharmacokinetic hazard relevant to repeated/excessive topical application, not just single large oral ingestions.
