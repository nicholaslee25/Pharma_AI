## Active Ingredient
Salicylic acid 17% w/w

## Purpose
Wart remover

## Uses
Removal of common warts (rough, cauliflower-like appearance) and plantar warts (bottom-of-foot only, characteristically tender).

## Warnings
**Do not use:** on irritated/infected/reddened skin; on moles, birthmarks, hairy warts, genital warts, or facial warts; if you have diabetes or circulatory problems.
**During use:** avoid eye contact (flush 15 minutes with water if exposed); do not inhale vapors; store tightly capped at room temperature away from heat.

## Directions
Wash affected area (optional 5-minute warm-water soak first), dry thoroughly, apply one drop at a time to sufficiently cover each wart using the applicator, allow to dry. Repeat once or twice daily **as needed for up to 12 weeks** — this matches the StatPearls-cited 60-80% cure rate window and the Cochrane review's typical trial duration.

## Inactive Ingredients
Alcohol, camphor, castor oil, collodion, ether, ethylcellulose, hypophosphorous acid, menthol, polysorbate 80.
