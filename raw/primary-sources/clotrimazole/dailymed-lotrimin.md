## Active Ingredient
Clotrimazole 1%

## Purpose
Antifungal

## Uses
Cures most athlete's foot, jock itch, and ringworm; relieves itching, burning, cracking, scaling, and discomfort accompanying these conditions. **Not effective on the scalp or nails.**

## Warnings
For external use only. Not for children <2 years unless directed by a doctor. Avoid eye contact. Discontinue and consult a doctor if irritation develops, or if no improvement within 4 weeks (athlete's foot/ringworm) or 2 weeks (jock itch). Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Wash and dry the affected area; apply a thin layer twice daily (morning and night).
- **Athlete's foot:** pay special attention to spaces between toes; wear well-fitting, ventilated shoes; change socks daily; use for 4 weeks
- **Ringworm:** use daily for 4 weeks
- **Jock itch:** use daily for 2 weeks

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzyl alcohol, cetyl alcohol, cetyl esters wax, octyldodecanol, polysorbate 60, sorbitan monostearate, stearyl alcohol, water.
