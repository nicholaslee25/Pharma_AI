## Active Ingredient
Naproxen sodium 220 mg (naproxen 200 mg) per caplet — NSAID

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer

## Uses
Temporary relief of minor aches: arthritis pain, muscular aches, backache, menstrual cramps, headache, toothache, common cold symptoms; reduces fever.

## Warnings

**Allergy alert:** naproxen sodium may cause a severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin. Symptoms: hives, facial swelling, asthma (wheezing), shock, skin reddening, rash, blisters.

**Stomach bleeding warning:** this product contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding. Higher risk if: age 60+, history of stomach ulcers/bleeding, taking a blood thinner or steroid drug, taking other drugs containing an NSAID (aspirin/ibuprofen/etc.), consuming ≥3 alcoholic drinks/day, taking more than directed or for longer than directed.

**Cardiovascular warning:** NSAIDs, except aspirin, increase the risk of heart attack, heart failure, and stroke. These can be fatal. Risk higher with longer use.

**Other warnings:** do not use right before/after heart surgery. Ask a doctor before use if stomach problems, high blood pressure, heart/kidney disease, taking a diuretic, or if ≥60y. Ask a doctor/pharmacist before use if taking a blood thinner, other NSAID/aspirin product, or 3+ alcoholic drinks/day.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** feels faint, vomit blood/bloody or black stools, stomach pain not relieved, ringing in ears/hearing loss, symptoms worsen or persist beyond 10 days (pain) or 3 days (fever).

**Pregnancy:** especially important not to use during last 3 months of pregnancy unless definitely directed by a doctor (matches StatPearls' ≥30-week contraindication).

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 1 caplet (220mg) every 8–12 hours while symptoms persist; may take 2 caplets within the first hour if needed (max first dose); do not exceed 2 caplets in any 8–12h period; do not exceed 3 caplets in 24 hours. Children <12y: ask a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Colloidal silicon dioxide, FD&C blue #2 aluminum lake, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, mannitol, polyethylene glycol, sodium starch glycolate, stearic acid, titanium dioxide.
