## Design
Systematic review/meta-analysis of RCTs, double-blind, placebo-controlled, single oral dose of naproxen or naproxen sodium in adults with moderate-to-severe acute postoperative pain. Searched Cochrane Library, MEDLINE, EMBASE, Oxford Pain Relief Database through July 2004.

## Scale
10 trials, 996 patients (9 naproxen sodium, 1 combined two small naproxen-alone trials).

## Primary Results (≥50% pain relief over 4–6h vs. placebo)
- **Naproxen sodium 550 mg** (6 trials, 500 patients): RR 4.2 (95% CI 2.9–6.0); NNT **2.6** (95% CI 2.2–3.2)
- **Naproxen 400mg / naproxen sodium 440mg** (3 trials, 334 patients): RR 4.8 (95% CI 2.75–8.38)
- Naproxen 200mg / naproxen sodium 220mg: two small studies suggest effective, insufficient data for pooled NNT

## Secondary Results
- No significant difference in proportion of patients experiencing any adverse event vs. placebo
- Weighted mean time to remedication: naproxen sodium 550mg **7.6h** vs. placebo **2.6h**

## Authors' Conclusion
Naproxen sodium 550mg, naproxen 400mg, and naproxen sodium 440mg are effective single-dose oral analgesics for acute postoperative pain in adults. Low adverse-event incidence, though reporting was inconsistent across included trials.
