## Design (as transcribed from the citation index, not independently re-verified word-for-word this pass)
Randomized controlled trial, N=310 (155 active / 155 placebo), AMC/DCBA lozenges for acute sore throat.

## Results (as transcribed)
Pain reduction detectable as early as 5 minutes post-dose, persisting approximately 2 hours. Also improved sore-throat relief, swallowing difficulty, and functional impairment ratings vs. placebo. No difference in adverse events between groups.
