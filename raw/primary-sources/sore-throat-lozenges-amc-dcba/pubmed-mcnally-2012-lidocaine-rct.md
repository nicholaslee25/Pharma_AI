## Design (as transcribed from the citation index, not independently re-verified word-for-word this pass)
N=190, randomized to AMC/DCBA + lidocaine, hexylresorcinol, or placebo lozenge, single dose, for acute sore throat from URI.

## Results (as transcribed)
AMC/DCBA + lidocaine onset of relief approximately 1–10 minutes; hexylresorcinol onset approximately 1–5 minutes. The AMC/DCBA + lidocaine arm did NOT reach statistical significance on its primary endpoint, though several secondary endpoints favored active treatment over placebo.
