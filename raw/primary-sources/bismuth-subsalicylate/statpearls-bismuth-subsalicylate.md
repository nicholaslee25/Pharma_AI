## Mechanism of Action
Hydrolyzes in the stomach into bismuth and salicylic acid. Salicylate is almost completely absorbed into the bloodstream; bismuth remains largely in the GI tract, forming salts with bactericidal properties that prevent bacterial adhesion to mucosal cells, reducing inflammation and promoting ulcer healing. Antidiarrheal action via three pathways: (1) reduced prostaglandin formation via COX inhibition, (2) stimulated fluid/sodium/chloride reabsorption, (3) inhibited intestinal secretions. For H. pylori specifically: blocks bacterial adhesion to gastric epithelial cells and inhibits phospholipase, protease, and urease enzyme activity.

## Indications
**FDA-approved (OTC):** diarrhea, dyspepsia, heartburn, indigestion, nausea, stomach upset.
**Off-label:** H. pylori eradication as part of quadruple therapy (up to 90% success); traveler's diarrhea prevention (62% prophylactic efficacy vs. 80% for antimicrobial prophylaxis); microscopic colitis in patients unable to tolerate budesonide.

## Dosing
**Adult diarrhea/dyspepsia:** regular strength 524mg q30min–1h PRN; maximum strength 1050mg every 60min; max duration 2 days; max total dose ~4200mg.
**Pediatric:** >12y adult dosing; 9–12y 262mg q30min–1h; 6–9y 175mg q30min–1h; 3–6y 87mg q30min–1h.
**H. pylori (off-label):** adults 300mg four times daily x10–14 days (quadruple therapy); pediatric 4mg/kg twice daily x10–14 days.
**Traveler's diarrhea:** prophylaxis 524mg four times daily with meals/bedtime; treatment 524mg q30min–1h, max 8 doses/24h.

## Pharmacokinetics
Salicylate almost completely absorbed into bloodstream; bismuth salt minimally absorbed. Therapeutic effect appears within 4 hours for most patients. Available forms: suspension (262mg/15mL, 525mg/15mL, 525mg/30mL), chewable tablets (262mg).

## Contraindications
Concurrent anticoagulants, sulfinpyrazone, probenecid, methotrexate, or other high-salicylate medications; GI ulceration or hemophilia; bleeding problems or bloody/black stools; flu-like symptoms in children/adolescents (Reye syndrome risk); salicylate sensitivity/aspirin allergy; concurrent malaria prophylaxis with doxycycline (reduces doxycycline absorption).

## Adverse Effects
**Common:** nausea, bitter taste, diarrhea, dark/black stools (harmless, temporary).
**Uncommon but serious — bismuth toxicity** (from extended overconsumption): blackening of tongue/teeth, fatigue, mood changes, deteriorating mental status; neurotoxicity (potentially fatal in rare cases); hearing loss, tinnitus, muscle spasms/weakness, anxiety, confusion, depression, headaches, slurred speech.

## Drug Interactions
Avoid concurrent use with: anticoagulants, sulfinpyrazone, probenecid, methotrexate, other high-salicylate-content medications, doxycycline (malaria prevention — absorption reduced).

## Special Populations
- **Pregnancy:** CDC recommends against BSS for traveler's diarrhea prevention/treatment
- **Breastfeeding:** alternative treatments preferred (potential infant salicylate absorption)
- **Renal impairment:** use lowest effective dose for shortest period feasible — salicylates may worsen renal function
- **Hepatic impairment:** no manufacturer recommendations given
- **Pediatric:** limited data <12y, other options may be preferable

## Toxicity/Overdose
**Salicylate toxicity threshold:** generally >150 mg/kg salicylates (or >6.5g aspirin equivalent). **Symptoms:** impaired cognition, tremors, lethargy, somnolence, insomnia, delirium, myoclonus, seizures, depressed mood, anxiety. **Bismuth encephalopathy:** rare, from prolonged overconsumption. **Management:** immediate discontinuation; activated charcoal if within 2h of ingestion; supportive care with IV fluids; salicylate level monitoring q1–2h until declining; urine alkalinization if concentration >30 mg/dL; hemodialysis for severe cases with altered mental status/metabolic acidosis; careful intubation management (maintain low PCO2 to avoid worsening acidosis); labs: hepatic panel, INR/PTT, CBC, electrolytes, creatinine, ABGs. No specific antidote exists. Routine therapeutic use does not require salicylate level monitoring.

## Patient Counseling
Discontinue if: symptoms persist beyond 14 days, symptoms worsen, diarrhea unimproved after 2 days, fever develops, or hearing loss/tinnitus occurs. Dark stool/tongue discoloration is temporary and harmless.
