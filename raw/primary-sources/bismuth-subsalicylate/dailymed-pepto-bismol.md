## Active Ingredient
Bismuth subsalicylate 525 mg per 30mL dose

## Purpose
Upset stomach reliever and antidiarrheal

## Uses
Traveler's diarrhea, diarrhea, and upset stomach from overindulgence — heartburn, indigestion, nausea, gas, belching, fullness.

## Warnings

**Reye's syndrome:** children/teenagers who have or are recovering from chicken pox or flu-like symptoms should NOT use this product.

**Allergy alert:** contains salicylate — do not take if allergic to salicylates (including aspirin) or taking another salicylate product.

**Do not use if:** ulcer, bleeding problem, or bloody/black stool present.

**When using:** temporary but harmless darkening of stool and/or tongue may occur.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 30mL (1 dose) every ½ hour, or 60mL (2 doses) every hour as needed for diarrhea. Max 8 doses (240mL)/24h. Children <12y: ask a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzoic acid, D&C Red No. 22, D&C Red No. 28, flavor, gellan gum, magnesium aluminum silicate, methylcellulose, salicylic acid, sodium salicylate, sorbic acid, sucralose, water.
