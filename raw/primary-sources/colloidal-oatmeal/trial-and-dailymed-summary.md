## Trial evidence
- Lisante et al. 2017: double-blind RCT, n=90, ages 6mo-18yr, mild-moderate AD, 1% colloidal oatmeal OTC cream vs. prescription ceramide barrier cream, EASI at week 3 - non-inferior.
- 2023 sub-analysis (Black/African American children): EASI change -2.4 (SD 1.7) oatmeal vs -2.1 (SD 2.3) barrier cream.
- Fowler et al. 2012: colloidal oatmeal formulations as adjunct AD treatment (supportive, effect sizes not extracted this pass).

## Note
No Cochrane review located for colloidal oatmeal specifically. Lisante et al. is a Johnson & Johnson-affiliated (Aveeno's parent/Kenvue predecessor) research team - flagged as manufacturer-affiliated rather than fully independent evidence.
