## Efficacy literature (search-summarized, not full-text independently pulled)
- Cureus 2024 single-center study, PMID (search-found, not independently confirmed) — 2.5% selenium sulfide shampoo, favorable safety profile, effective for dandruff/itching/oiliness/greasiness in Indian adult subjects, applied every 3 days for 4 weeks.
- Karger Skin Appendage Disorders comparative RCT — 1% selenium disulfide shampoo vs 2% ketoconazole shampoo in moderate-severe scalp seborrheic dermatitis (title/existence confirmed, quantitative results not independently pulled).
- Taylor & Francis 2025 combination-shampoo study — selenium sulfide + salicylic acid combination shampoo, significant (p<0.001) improvement in dandruff/redness/itching/greasiness in seborrheic dermatitis patients (combination product, not selenium sulfide monotherapy).
