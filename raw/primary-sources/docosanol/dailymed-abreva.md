## Active Ingredient
Docosanol 10%

## Purpose
Cold sore/fever blister treatment

## Uses
Treats cold sores/fever blisters on the face or lips; shortens duration of symptoms including tingling, pain, burning, and/or itching.

## Warnings
**Allergy alert:** may cause severe reactions (hives, facial swelling, breathing difficulty). External use only; avoid eyes and inside the mouth. Do not share applicator/product to prevent spreading infection. Discontinue and consult a doctor if the sore worsens or does not heal within 10 days. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Wash hands before and after applying. Apply to affected area on face or lips at the first sign of symptoms (tingle, itch, bump). Use 5 times a day until healed, with gentle rubbing. Adults and children 12+; children under 12 require medical consultation first.

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzyl alcohol, light mineral oil, propylene glycol, purified water, sucrose distearate, sucrose stearate.
