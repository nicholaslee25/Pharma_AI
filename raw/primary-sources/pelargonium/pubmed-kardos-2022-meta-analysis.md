## Design
Meta-analysis of randomized, placebo-controlled trials of EPs 7630 (Pelargonium sidoides extract) in acute bronchitis (AB) or common cold (CC). Outcomes: change in cough intensity score, cough remission, disease-related quality of life.

## Scale
11 trials, 2,195 participants (3 pediatric/adolescent AB, 3 adult AB, 5 adult CC).

## Results
- **Children/adolescents, AB:** ≥50% cough intensity reduction at day 7: 79.6% EPs 7630 vs. 41.0% placebo, RR 1.86 (95% CI 1.34–2.95). Complete cough remission: 18.0% vs. 5.5%, RR 2.91 (95% CI 1.26–6.72).
- **Adults, AB:** ≥50% cough response: 88.7% vs. 47.6%, RR 2.13 (95% CI 1.37–3.31). No cough at day 7: 26.0% vs. 6.3%, RR 5.00 (95% CI 3.10–8.07).
- **Adults, CC:** ≥50% cough reduction at day 5: 56.8% vs. 38.8%, RR 1.40 (95% CI 1.19–1.65). Complete remission: 26.1% vs. 18.4%, RR 1.40 (95% CI 1.06–1.84).
- QoL/pain/activity-impairment measures also favored EPs 7630 over placebo in CC.

## Authors' Conclusion
EPs 7630 reduces cough burden and leads to earlier cough remission vs. placebo, with QoL benefits, across both pediatric/adult acute bronchitis and adult common cold.
