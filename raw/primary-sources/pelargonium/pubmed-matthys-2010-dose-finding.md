## Design
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter, adaptive group-sequential dose-finding trial in adults with acute bronchitis (outside strict antibiotic indication). 4 parallel arms, 7-day treatment: EPs 7630 10mg TID (30mg/day), 20mg TID (60mg/day), 30mg TID (90mg/day), or placebo TID. Primary endpoint: change in Bronchitis Symptom Score (BSS) from baseline to day 7.

## Scale
406 patients.

## Results
Mean BSS decrease, day 0→7: placebo 2.7±2.3; 30mg/day group 4.3±1.9; 60mg/day group 6.1±2.1; 90mg/day group 6.3±2.0. All active groups vs. placebo p<0.0001. Adverse events mild-to-moderate, frequency dose-dependent; no serious adverse events.

## Authors' Conclusion
All three tested doses significantly outperformed placebo. Weighing efficacy against tolerability, **20mg three times daily (60mg/day)** was judged the optimal dose (comparable efficacy to 90mg/day with a better safety margin).
