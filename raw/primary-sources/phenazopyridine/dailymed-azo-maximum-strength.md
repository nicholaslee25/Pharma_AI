## Active Ingredient
Phenazopyridine Hydrochloride 99.5mg per tablet

## Purpose
Urinary tract analgesic

## Uses
Relief from urinary pain, burning, urgency, and frequency associated with urinary tract infections.

## Warnings
**Ask a doctor before use if you have:** kidney disease; allergies to foods, preservatives, or dyes; previous hypersensitive reaction to phenazopyridine HCl.
**Do not use if:** you have G6PD deficiency, unless approved by your physician.
**When using:** stomach upset may occur (take with meals); urine will become reddish-orange (not harmful, but avoid staining clothing).
**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** symptoms persist beyond 2 days, or you suspect an adverse reaction.
**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** consult a healthcare professional; a pregnancy test and medical consultation recommended before use.
**Overdose:** get medical help or contact Poison Control immediately.

## Directions
Adults and children 12+: 2 tablets three times daily with meals, for up to 2 days maximum (12 tablets total). Children under 12: do not use without consulting a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carnauba wax, croscarmellose sodium, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol, povidone, pregelatinized corn starch (may contain corn starch).
