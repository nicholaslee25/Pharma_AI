## Efficacy literature (search-summarized)
- Pierard-Franchimont et al. 2002, PMID 12476017 — zinc pyrithione 1% vs ketoconazole 2%: 67% vs 73% improvement in dandruff severity at week 4.
- Schwartz et al. 2013, PMID 23614401 — "potentiated" ZPT formula outperformed ZPT/climbazole dual-active product across all 4 weekly timepoints (flaking, epidermal histamine).
