# FDA Drug Facts Label — Peridex (chlorhexidine gluconate 0.12% oral rinse, raw extraction)

## Indication
Treating gingivitis between dental visits — redness and swelling of the gingivae, including gingival bleeding upon probing. Not tested for acute necrotizing ulcerative gingivitis.

## Dosage and Administration
Rinse with 15mL (marked in cap) of undiluted Peridex twice daily for 30 seconds after toothbrushing. Begin therapy following professional dental cleaning; reevaluate at intervals not exceeding six months. Avoid rinsing with water or eating immediately afterward.

## Contraindications
Hypersensitivity to chlorhexidine gluconate or other formula ingredients.

## Warnings
Supragingival calculus increase observed in clinical testing vs. controls. Professional dental prophylaxis recommended every six months maximum. Anaphylaxis and serious allergic reactions have been reported with chlorhexidine-containing dental products. Effect on periodontitis (as opposed to gingivitis) remains undetermined.

## Adverse Reactions
Tooth staining: **56% of users showed measurable increase; 15% developed heavy stain** (vs. 35% staining rate in the control group after six months, per Precautions section). Calculus formation. Taste alteration. Oral mucosal effects (aphthous ulcers, ulceration) at <1% frequency. Post-marketing: stomatitis, glossitis, salivary gland inflammation.

## Pharmacology
Microbiological plaque sampling showed a general reduction of counts of certain assayed bacteria (aerobic and anaerobic), ranging from **54–97% through six months of use**. ~30% of the active ingredient is retained in the oral cavity following rinsing, then slowly released into oral fluids. Poorly absorbed from the GI tract.

## Precautions
**General:** tooth staining risk (56% Peridex vs. 35% control after six months); taste alteration possible, rare permanent changes reported.
**Pregnancy:** animal studies at high doses showed no fetal harm; adequate human studies not completed.
**Nursing:** excretion in human milk undetermined; rat studies at 100x typical-use doses showed no adverse effects on offspring.
**Pediatric:** safety/effectiveness not established under age 18.
**Carcinogenesis/Mutagenesis:** no carcinogenic effect in rats up to 38mg/kg/day; no mutagenic effects in mammalian studies.
