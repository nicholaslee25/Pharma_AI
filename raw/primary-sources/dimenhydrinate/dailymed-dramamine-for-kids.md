## Active Ingredient
Dimenhydrinate 25mg per chewable tablet

## Purpose
Antiemetic

## Uses
Prevention and treatment of motion sickness symptoms: nausea, dizziness, vomiting.

## Warnings
Do not give to children under 2 years without doctor approval. Ask a doctor before use if child has breathing problems (emphysema, chronic bronchitis) or glaucoma, or if child takes sedatives/tranquilizers. May cause marked drowsiness. Keep out of children's reach. If overdose occurs, contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222).

## Dosing by Age

| Age Group | Dose | Frequency | Daily Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 to under 6 years | 1/2 to 1 tablet | Every 6–8 hours | 3 tablets/24h |
| 6 to 12 years | 1 to 2 tablets | Every 6–8 hours | 6 tablets/24h |

Take first dose 1/2 to 1 hour before activity to prevent motion sickness.

## Inactive Ingredients
Aspartame, citric acid, flavors, magnesium stearate, methacrylic acid copolymer, sorbitol. Contains phenylalanine 0.42mg per tablet. Dye-free formulation.
