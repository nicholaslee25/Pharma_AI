## Active Ingredient
Simethicone 250 mg per softgel

## Purpose
Antigas

## Uses
Relief of pressure, bloating, and fullness commonly referred to as gas.

## Warnings
Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: swallow with water, 1–2 softgels as needed after meals and at bedtime. Do not exceed 2 softgels/24h except under a physician's advice/supervision.

## Inactive Ingredients
D&C red no. 33, FD&C blue no. 1, gelatin, glycerin, lecithin, purified water, titanium dioxide.
