# FDA Drug Facts Label — Cold-Eeze (zinc gluconate lozenge, raw extraction)

**Source:** DailyMed (NIH National Library of Medicine), SET ID c7bb8434-9ed1-46e3-9854-875f9db3a24a: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c7bb8434-9ed1-46e3-9854-875f9db3a24a
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-25
**License note:** FDA drug labeling text is government-mandated regulatory content, treated as public domain / freely reproducible.
**Important regulatory note:** Cold-Eeze is labeled as a **homeopathic preparation per HPUS** (Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States) standards — "Zincum Gluconicum 2X" — NOT a standard FDA OTC monograph drug. Despite the homeopathic labeling convention, "2X" is a low dilution (1:100), so the lozenge still delivers a pharmacologically real dose of elemental zinc (13.3mg) — this is a meaningfully different situation from ultra-high-dilution homeopathic products like Oscillococcinum, where essentially no active molecule remains. Per FDA policy (noted independently by this vault, not stated on this specific label page), homeopathic products are generally marketed without FDA evaluation of safety/efficacy.

## Active Ingredient
Zincum Gluconicum 2X (13.3 mg zinc) per lozenge

## Purpose
Cold Remedy

## Uses
Reduces duration of the common cold; reduces severity of cough, sore throat, stuffy nose, sneezing, post-nasal drip, and/or hoarseness.

## Warnings

**Drug interactions:** ask a doctor before use if taking minocycline, doxycycline, or tetracycline, or if on Coumadin (warfarin) therapy — zinc may inhibit absorption of these medications.

**Diabetic warning:** sugar replacements in the lozenge may affect blood sugar levels.

**Duration:** stop use and ask a healthcare practitioner if symptoms persist beyond 7 days.

**Pregnancy/nursing:** ask a health professional before use.

**Pediatric:** keep out of reach of children; children under 12 should consult a health professional before use.

## Directions
Ages 12+: begin treatment within 24–48 hours of symptom onset; dissolve one lozenge completely in mouth (do not chew), repeat every 2–4 hours as needed; max 6 lozenges/day (adults), 4 lozenges/day (ages 12–17).

## Inactive Ingredients
Corn syrup, honey, glycine, natural flavors, sucrose. No artificial colors or preservatives.
