## Active Ingredient
Anas barbariae, hepatis et cordis extractum (10 mg) 200CK HPUS — i.e., duck heart/liver extract, diluted 200 times by the Korsakovian method (per HPUS convention). Per the Cochrane review (separate raw file), this starts from a 1% solution of wild duck heart/liver extract, serially diluted 200 times with water/alcohol — a dilution factor so extreme that, by standard chemistry (Avogadro's number), no molecule of the original substance is expected to remain in the final product.

## Purpose
"Reduces the duration and severity of flu-like symptoms" (per label's own claim)

## Uses
Temporarily relieves flu-like symptoms: body aches, headache, fever, chills, fatigue.

## Warnings
Do not use if allergic to Oscillococcinum. Stop use and consult a doctor if symptoms persist beyond 3 days, worsen, or occur with persistent dry cough, shortness of breath, rash, vomiting, or diarrhea.

## Pregnancy/Breastfeeding
Ask a health professional before use.

## Directions
Adults and children ≥2 years: dissolve entire contents of 1 tube in the mouth every 6 hours, up to 3 times per day, at symptom onset. Children <2y: consult a physician.

## Inactive Ingredients
Lactose, sucrose.

## FDA Disclaimer (quoted directly from the label)
"This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy. FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective."
