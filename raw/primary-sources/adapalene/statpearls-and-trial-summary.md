# Adapalene — StatPearls + Trial Evidence Summary

Sources: StatPearls NBK482509 (Adapalene); FDA NDA 020380Orig1s010 pharmacology review; network meta-analysis PMC9804728 (BJD); Cochrane-indexed adapalene vs. tazarotene trial
Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Mechanism
Retinoic acid receptor (RAR) modulator; normalizes follicular keratinization and microcomedone formation; direct anti-inflammatory activity independent of comedolytic effect. Photostable (unlike tretinoin).

## Pharmacokinetics
<0.01% systemic absorption from topical application; plasma clearance within 72 hours.

## Efficacy
- Network meta-analysis (40 RCTs): adapalene + benzoyl peroxide ranked most effective topical combination for mild-moderate acne; higher withdrawal rate than monotherapy due to irritation.
- Adapalene 0.3% vs. tazarotene 0.1%: roughly equivalent efficacy; adapalene less irritating.
- Adapalene vs. tretinoin (US/Europe multicenter trials): comparable efficacy, adapalene generally better tolerated.
- Retinoid monotherapy underperforms retinoid+antibiotic or retinoid+BPO combination therapy generally.

## Teratogenicity / Pregnancy
Teratogenic in rats/rabbits at oral doses >=25 mg/kg (32-65x MRHD by mg/m2). Former FDA Pregnancy Category C. Systematic review of first-trimester topical retinoid exposure in humans found no clear increase in retinoid embryopathy risk, but product labeling still advises avoidance in pregnancy given minimal but non-zero systemic absorption and the animal data.

## Note
No single dedicated Cochrane systematic review specific to adapalene monotherapy was located in this pass (unlike benzoyl peroxide/salicylic acid, which have dedicated Cochrane reviews already in this database) — evidence compiled from a network meta-analysis and named head-to-head trials instead.
