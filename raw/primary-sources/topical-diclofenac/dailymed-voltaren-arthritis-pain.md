## Active Ingredient
Diclofenac sodium (NSAID) 1% (equivalent to 0.93% diclofenac)

## Purpose
Arthritis pain reliever

## Uses
Temporary relief of arthritis pain in the hand, wrist, elbow (upper body) and foot, ankle, knee (lower body). **May take up to 7 days to work — not for immediate relief.**

## Warnings

**Allergy alert:** may cause a severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin — hives, asthma, skin reddening, blisters, facial swelling, shock, rash.

**Liver warning:** liver damage may occur if applied more than directed, for longer than directed, or combined with other diclofenac-containing drugs.

**Stomach bleeding warning:** contains an NSAID, may cause severe stomach bleeding — higher risk if age 60+, ulcer history, anticoagulant/steroid use, or ≥3 alcoholic drinks/day.

**Heart attack/stroke warning:** NSAIDs (except aspirin) increase risk of heart attack, heart failure, and stroke.

## Do Not Use
If allergic to pain relievers/fever reducers; for strains, sprains, bruises, or sports injuries (i.e., NOT indicated for acute musculoskeletal injury, only arthritis); before/after heart surgery; on more than 2 body areas at once; in eyes, nose, or mouth.

## Directions
Use 4 times per day, every day, for up to 21 days. **Upper body areas:** 2.25 inches (2 grams) per application. **Lower body areas:** 4.5 inches (4 grams) per application.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carbomer homopolymer Type C, cocoyl caprylocaprate, fragrance, isopropyl alcohol, mineral oil, polyoxyl 20 cetostearyl ether, propylene glycol, purified water, strong ammonia solution.
