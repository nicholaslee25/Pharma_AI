## Design
Randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled study. 204 patients (102/group) with confirmed pharyngitis (onset ≤4 days), randomized to flurbiprofen 8.75mg or placebo lozenges, up to 5 lozenges every 3–6h for 7 days. Outcomes: pain intensity (primary), difficulty swallowing, swollen throat, via validated rating scales.

## Results (over first 24h vs. placebo)
- Sore throat pain: **47% greater reduction** with flurbiprofen (p<0.05)
- Difficulty swallowing: **66% greater reduction** (p<0.05)
- Swollen throat: **40% greater reduction** (p<0.05)

## Authors' Conclusion
Multiple doses of flurbiprofen 8.75mg lozenges provide effective relief of sore throat pain intensity, difficulty swallowing, and swollen throat.
