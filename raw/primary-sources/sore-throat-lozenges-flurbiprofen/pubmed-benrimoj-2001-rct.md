## Design
Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group study. 320 patients with objective/subjective sore throat from URI, randomized to flurbiprofen 8.75mg (n=128), flurbiprofen 12.5mg (n=64), or demulcent placebo lozenges (n=128). Efficacy assessed via subjective rating scales (pain relief, throat soreness, swollen throat) over 6h post single dose; tolerability assessed over a 5-day multi-dose regimen.

## Results
- Flurbiprofen 8.75mg significantly superior to placebo on primary endpoint (total pain relief summed 15–120min, TOTPAR15-120min) and for throat soreness (2h) and swollen throat (2h and 6h), all p<0.05
- Flurbiprofen 12.5mg was NOT significantly better than 8.75mg — i.e., the higher dose did not add benefit
- No significant between-group difference in adverse events once taste perversion (a palatability issue, not a tolerability/safety issue) was excluded from analysis (p=0.776)
- **Onset:** symptomatic relief began within 15 minutes (partly attributed to the lozenge's demulcent/soothing physical effect itself, not just the drug), with statistically significant active-vs-placebo separation from 30 minutes, sustained over 4 hours

## Authors' Conclusion
Flurbiprofen 8.75mg lozenges offer a convenient, rapid-onset, well-tolerated treatment for sore throat; the 12.5mg dose offers no additional benefit over 8.75mg.
