## Active Ingredient
Omeprazole delayed-release tablet 20mg (equivalent to 20.6mg omeprazole magnesium)

## Purpose
Acid reducer

## Uses
Treats frequent heartburn occurring ≥2 days/week. **Not intended for immediate relief — may take 1 to 4 days for full effect.**

## Warnings
Allergy alert for omeprazole sensitivity. May cause severe skin reactions (reddening, blisters, rash). Do not use if: swallowing difficulty, bloody vomit/stools, chest pain with respiratory symptoms, or frequent chest pain. Ask a doctor before use if: heartburn persists beyond 3 months, unexplained weight loss, or frequent wheezing occurs.

## Directions
Adults ≥18y: one tablet daily with water, before eating in the morning. Complete a full 14-day course; do not exceed one tablet/day. Swallow whole — do not chew/crush. May repeat the 14-day course every 4 months at most.

## Inactive Ingredients
Glyceryl monostearate, hydroxypropyl cellulose, hypromellose, iron oxide, magnesium stearate, methacrylic acid copolymer, microcrystalline cellulose, paraffin, polyethylene glycol 6000, polysorbate 80, polyvinylpyrrolidone, sodium stearyl fumarate, starch, sucrose, talc, titanium dioxide, triethyl citrate.
