# Benzalkonium Chloride — FDA Regulatory Status (Consumer Antiseptic Wash)

Sources: FDA Federal Register (Safety and Effectiveness of Consumer Antiseptics; Topical Antimicrobial Drug Products for OTC Human Use), FDA Consumer Antiseptic Wash Final Rule Q&A, general web search summarizing these primary documents. Pulled 2026-08-27.

## 2016 Final Rule
On September 2, 2016, the FDA issued a final rule banning 19 active ingredients (including triclosan and triclocarban, the two most commonly used at the time) from OTC consumer antiseptic wash products (soaps/body washes used with water), on grounds manufacturers had not demonstrated GRASE (Generally Recognized As Safe and Effective) status for long-term daily use.

## Deferred Ingredients
Three active ingredients — **benzalkonium chloride, benzethonium chloride, and chloroxylenol** — were explicitly deferred from the 2016 final rule (not banned, not approved) to give sponsors time to complete safety/effectiveness studies addressing FDA-identified data gaps. Benzalkonium chloride was specifically noted as Category III (additional data needed) in the OTC review process.

## 2017 Renewal
On March 24, 2017, FDA renewed the deferrals for another 1-year period, subject to further renewal.

## Current Status (as found via 2024-2025 search results, presumed unchanged through 2026 absent contrary information)
No final GRASE determination for benzalkonium chloride in consumer antiseptic wash products has been identified in this pass. The ingredient remains marketable under the ongoing OTC review process without a final safety/effectiveness determination — an unresolved regulatory status nearly a decade after the original 2016 deferral.

## Distinct Product Category Note
"Consumer Antiseptic Wash" (rinse-off soap/body wash) is regulated separately from "Consumer Antiseptic Rub" (leave-on hand sanitizer/gel) — efficacy/PK data for benzalkonium-chloride rub/sanitizer products should not be assumed to transfer to the wash-off soap products that are the subject of the Dial/Dove/Softsoap/Cetaphil brand pages in this database.
