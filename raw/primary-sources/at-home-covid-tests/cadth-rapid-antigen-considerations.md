## Extracted content
Rapid antigen tests detect SARS-CoV-2 proteins and return results in under 30 minutes vs. one or more days for lab-based (PCR) testing. Serial testing (multiple tests over several days) is recommended to increase overall detection accuracy given single-test sensitivity limits. Combined nasal + throat sampling produced greater detection rates than nasal sampling alone in cited literature. No product-specific sensitivity/specificity percentages are given in this document — see the Cochrane (Dinnes 2022) and Cai 2024 meta-analysis files in this folder for pooled accuracy figures.

Accessibility/real-world use barriers noted: manual dexterity and vision requirements for self-testing, nasal swab discomfort as the most-cited barrier to use, cost ($19.99–$46/package in the Canadian market cited), temperature storage constraints (2–30°C), and language/distribution access barriers.
