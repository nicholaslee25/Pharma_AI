## Condensed facts
- Systematic review/meta-analysis of studies evaluating fully self-performed (self-tested) SARS-CoV-2 rapid antigen tests against RT-PCR as reference standard. Databases: PubMed, Embase, Cochrane Library.
- 45 eligible studies pooled, 50,897 suspected-COVID-19 patients.
- Pooled results: sensitivity **0.77 (77%)**, specificity **1.00 (~100%)**, diagnostic odds ratio 625.95.
- Subgroup analysis: higher sensitivity with Abbott Panbio assays, self-collected nasal swabs, nasopharyngeal/oropharyngeal swab type, and lower RT-PCR Ct cutoff values (i.e., higher viral load at time of testing associated with higher detected sensitivity).
- Conclusion: fully self-performed rapid antigen tests show overall high accuracy vs. RT-PCR and are reasonable surrogates for standard testing, though performance depends heavily on sampling method and viral load.
