## Active Ingredient
Oxymetazoline hydrochloride 0.05%

## Purpose
Nasal decongestant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves nasal congestion from the common cold, hay fever, or upper respiratory allergies; temporarily relieves sinus congestion/pressure; shrinks swollen nasal membranes for easier breathing.

## Warnings

**Ask a doctor before use if:** heart disease, high blood pressure, diabetes, thyroid disease, or trouble urinating due to enlarged prostate.

**When using this product:** do not exceed directed amounts. **"Do not use for more than 3 days. Use only as directed. Frequent or prolonged use may cause nasal congestion to recur or worsen"** (rebound congestion / rhinitis medicamentosa warning, directly on the label). May cause temporary burning, stinging, sneezing, or increased nasal discharge. Do not share the container (infection spread risk).

**Stop use and consult a doctor if:** symptoms persist. Pregnant/nursing: ask a health professional before use. Keep away from children; seek immediate medical help if swallowed.

## Directions
Adults/children 6–12y: 2–3 sprays per nostril every 10–12 hours, max 2 doses/24h. Squeeze bottle firmly without tilting head back; wipe nozzle after use. Children <6y: ask a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzalkonium chloride solution, edetate disodium, polyethylene glycol, povidone, propylene glycol, purified water, sodium phosphate dibasic, sodium phosphate monobasic.

**Note:** benzalkonium chloride (a preservative in this formulation) is specifically flagged in the StatPearls rhinitis medicamentosa article (separate raw file) as a possible contributing factor to rebound congestion, though clinical-significance evidence for that specific claim is described as mixed.
