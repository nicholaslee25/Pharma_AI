# Lumify — DailyMed FDA Label / Approval Extract

Sources: DailyMed (setid 022de945-0b26-40ae-8e88-d3fe2464d106), FDA approval package (application 208144Orig1s000), lumifyeyes.com
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
Brimonidine tartrate ophthalmic solution 0.025%

## Indication
Relief of eye redness due to minor eye irritation. Approved 2017-12-22.

## Mechanism/Differentiator
Selective alpha-2 adrenergic receptor agonist (vs. non-selective alpha-1/alpha-2 agonists naphazoline/tetrahydrozoline). Manufacturer claims onset within 60 seconds, duration up to 8 hours, lower rebound-redness risk because rebound/tachyphylaxis is attributed to alpha-1 activity specifically.

## Clinical Trial Data
Significantly reduced ocular redness for up to 8 hours; negligible rebound redness at 1 week post-discontinuation (1.28%); no tachyphylaxis or rebound redness observed after month-long use in trials reviewed.
