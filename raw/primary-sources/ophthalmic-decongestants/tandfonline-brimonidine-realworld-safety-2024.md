# Real-World Safety Data for Brimonidine Tartrate: Is There Potential for Overuse?

Source: tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17469899.2024.2435809 (2024)
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Findings
Post-marketing adverse-event data shows few reports of tachyphylaxis/rebound redness for brimonidine (Lumify) 0.025%. Lack of tachyphylaxis/rare rebound redness after month-long use suggests low-dose brimonidine is not limited by the side effects seen with other marketed vasoconstrictors (naphazoline/tetrahydrozoline class).

## Caution
Explicitly notes overuse/abuse potential exists with ANY OTC topical vasoconstrictor, brimonidine included — use should be clinically targeted, with patient education/oversight to prevent overuse or masking of underlying pathology.
