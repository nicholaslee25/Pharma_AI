# Opcon-A — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=2d7cfae7-8af4-4fee-93e1-e5cfe455c5a5
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredients
Pheniramine maleate 0.315% (antihistamine), Naphazoline hydrochloride 0.02675% (redness reliever)

## Indication
Eye allergy relief of itching and redness caused by pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander. Manufacturer: Bausch & Lomb.

## Note
Near-identical formulation to Naphcon-A (marginally different concentrations of the same two actives).
