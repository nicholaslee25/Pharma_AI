# Naphcon-A — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=73fa33b5-606a-d6a8-e053-2991aa0a81c1
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredients
Naphazoline hydrochloride 0.025% (redness reliever), Pheniramine maleate 0.3% (antihistamine)

## Indication
Temporary relief of redness and itching of the eye(s) due to ragweed, pollen, grass, animal dander/hair.

## Directions
1-2 drops in affected eye(s) up to 4 times daily.
