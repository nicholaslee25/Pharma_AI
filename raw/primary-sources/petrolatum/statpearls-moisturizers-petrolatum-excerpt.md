Petrolatum is the classic/prototypical occlusive moisturizer ingredient — occlusives form a physical barrier that reduces water evaporation from the skin. **Petrolatum reduces transepidermal water loss by nearly 99%**, the highest reduction among common occlusive agents named (others include mineral oil, paraffin, squalene, dimethicone). This occlusive mechanism is also the basis for petroleum gauze use in wound dressing/grafting contexts — maintaining a moist wound environment is understood to support healing and reduce scab formation/desiccation.
