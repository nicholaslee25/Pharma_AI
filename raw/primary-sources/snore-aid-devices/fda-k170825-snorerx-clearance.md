# SnoreRx — FDA 510(k) Clearance Summary

Source: accessdata.fda.gov/cdrh_docs/pdf17/K170825.pdf; finance.yahoo.com claims summary
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Classification
FDA-cleared, Class II mandibular advancement device via 510(k) pathway (substantial equivalence, not an independent efficacy trial requirement). Boil-and-bite custom fitting, 1mm adjustable calibration.

## Scope
Cleared for snoring reduction. Clearance does NOT cover sleep apnea (OSA) treatment — an important distinction from the broader MAD literature's OSA-focused RCT evidence, most of which involves dentist-fitted custom devices.
