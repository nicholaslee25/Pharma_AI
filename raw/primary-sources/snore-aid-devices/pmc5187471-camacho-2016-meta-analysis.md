# Nasal Dilators (Breathe Right Strips and NoZovent) for Snoring and OSA — Systematic Review/Meta-Analysis

Source: Camacho et al. 2016, PMC5187471, Pulmonary Medicine (Wiley)
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Design
112 studies screened, 58 reviewed, 14 included in meta-analysis; 147 OSA patients with reported AHI data.

## Results
- AHI: 28.7 +/- 24.0 -> 27.4 +/- 23.3 events/hr, p=0.64 (not significant)
- No significant change in AHI, lowest oxygen saturation, or snoring index for OSA patients
- Subgroup: internal nasal dilators showed apnea-index reduction of 4.87 events/hr; external nasal dilators showed an increase of 0.64 events/hr (essentially no benefit)

## Related smaller trials (referenced)
RCT in chronic-rhinitis patients: Breathe Right reduced snoring frequency. Separate PSG study in habitual snorers with nasal obstruction: significantly improved sleep efficiency, no significant change in arousal index or AHI vs. baseline/placebo; ~37% reduction in median arousal rate in one active-phase analysis.
