# Efficacy of Mandibular Advancement Device in OSA — RCT Crossover

Source: PMC4598931
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Finding
Randomized controlled crossover clinical trial supporting mandibular advancement devices (MADs) as an effective treatment for mild-to-moderate obstructive sleep apnea syndrome — part of the broader established sleep-medicine literature on custom-fitted MADs, generally fitted by a dentist/sleep specialist rather than an OTC boil-and-bite consumer product.
