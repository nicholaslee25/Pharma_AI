# Mute (Rhinomed) — Manufacturer Trial/Claim Data

Sources: Amazon product listings, 1800cpap.com, manufacturer white paper references
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Claims
Clinical trial (Royal Victorian Eye and Ear Hospital, Melbourne) reported ~38% more nasal airflow vs. nasal strips. Manufacturer white paper: 75% of users reported reduced snoring; 73% of bed partners reported reduced snoring severity.

## Note
FDA-cleared OTC internal nasal dilator. These airflow/reduction figures are manufacturer-sponsored/reported, not independently replicated in the peer-reviewed literature reviewed in this pass — lower confidence tier than the Camacho et al. 2016 meta-analysis, though directionally consistent with that meta-analysis's finding that internal nasal dilators outperform external ones on an apnea-index subgroup measure.
