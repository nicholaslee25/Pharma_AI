## Active Ingredient
Hydrocortisone, USP 1%

## Purpose
Antipruritic (anti-itch)

## Uses
Temporary relief of itching from minor skin irritation, inflammation, and rashes due to eczema, insect bites, poison ivy/oak/sumac, soaps, detergents, cosmetics, jewelry, seborrheic dermatitis, and psoriasis. Also for external genital, feminine, and anal itching. Other uses require doctor supervision.

## Warnings
For external use only. Do not use for diaper rash or external feminine itching with vaginal discharge without consulting a doctor. Avoid contact with the eyes. Do not insert into the rectum using fingers or mechanical devices.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond 7 days, or symptoms return within days. Do not start another hydrocortisone product without medical consultation. Seek immediate help if rectal bleeding occurs.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥2y: apply to affected area not more than 3–4 times daily. Children <2y: consult a doctor before use. For anal itching in adults: cleanse and dry the area before application.

## Inactive Ingredients
Aloe vera, shea butter, C12-15 alkyl benzoate, caprylic/capric triglyceride, cetearyl alcohol, cetearyl glucoside, cetyl alcohol, dimethicone, ethylhexylglycerin, glyceryl stearate, avocado oil, phenoxyethanol, propylene glycol, sweet almond oil, purified water, sodium polyacrylate, sodium stearoyl glutamate, vitamin E acetate, xanthan gum.
