## Medicasp (setid=6f83214c-d4a9-44fa-8d8e-634907dfddd8)
Active Ingredient: Coal tar 0.5%.
Purpose: controls symptoms of psoriasis, dandruff, seborrheic dermatitis.
Warnings: avoid eyes; sunburn-sensitivity caution 24hrs; do not use prolonged without doctor; do not combine with UV/other psoriasis therapy without doctor.

## Regulatory context (search-summarized)
FDA OTC monograph: coal tar 0.5-5% safe and effective for dandruff/seborrheic dermatitis/psoriasis; FDA states no evidence OTC coal-tar shampoo/soap/ointment increases skin-cancer risk.
California Prop 65: lists coal tar as a chemical known to cause cancer; requires cancer warning on OTC coal-tar shampoo/lotion/cream above 0.5% concentration. Cited risk estimates: ~3.5-in-10-million lifetime skin cancer risk at 1% coal tar shampoo use; ~1.7-in-1-million at 2.5%.
