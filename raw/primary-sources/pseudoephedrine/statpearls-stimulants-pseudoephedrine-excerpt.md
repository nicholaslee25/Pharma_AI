## Mechanism of Action (as given)
"Pseudoephedrine is a drug used as a nasal decongestant and stimulant. It is a sympathomimetic agent that belongs to the amphetamine and phenethylamine drug classes." Works as a nasal/sinus decongestant via increased norepinephrine activity at adrenergic receptors.

## Common Formulations (as given)
Appears in numerous OTC combination formulations with guaifenesin, antihistamines, acetaminophen, and dextromethorphan.

## Indication (as given)
Nasal and sinus congestion.
