## Active Ingredient
Pseudoephedrine HCl 30 mg (per tablet)

## Purpose
Nasal decongestant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves sinus congestion/pressure and nasal congestion due to the common cold, hay fever, or other upper respiratory allergies.

## Warnings

**Do not use if:** currently taking a prescription monoamine oxidase inhibitor (MAOI), or for 2 weeks after stopping an MAOI (includes some drugs for depression, psychiatric/emotional conditions, or Parkinson's disease) — risk of serious interaction (hypertensive crisis).

**Ask a doctor before use if you have:** heart disease, high blood pressure, thyroid disease, diabetes, or trouble urinating due to an enlarged prostate.

**When using this product:** do not exceed recommended dose.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** nervousness, dizziness, or sleeplessness occur; symptoms do not improve within 7 days; symptoms occur with fever.

**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** ask a health professional before use.

**Keep out of reach of children.**

## Directions
- Adults and children ≥12 years: 2 tablets (60 mg) every 4–6 hours
- Children 6–11 years: 1 tablet (30 mg) every 4–6 hours
- Children <6 years: do not use
- Max: 4 doses in 24 hours (i.e., 8 tablets/day adult, 4 tablets/day pediatric 6–11)

## Inactive Ingredients
Carnauba wax, silicon dioxide, D&C yellow #10, FD&C red #40, FD&C yellow #6, iron oxide, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, polyethylene glycol, polyvinyl alcohol, pregelatinized starch, shellac, sodium starch glycolate, talc, titanium dioxide.

**Note:** Behind-the-counter sale in the US is a regulatory requirement (Combat Methamphetamine Epidemic Act, 2005) due to pseudoephedrine's use as a methamphetamine precursor — this restriction is a federal/state legal control, not itself part of the FDA Drug Facts label text above, and is noted here for completeness.
