**Why this file exists:** saved specifically to document that not every product marketed under "boric acid suppository" branding contains a pharmacological dose of boric acid — this one is a homeopathic dilution. Important for the Formulation Caveat on the ingredient page.

## Active Ingredient
"Boricum Acidum 3X HPUS" — a homeopathic dilution officially monographed in the Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States. (3X = diluted 1:10 three times — a small fraction of a pharmacological dose, consistent with homeopathic preparation convention, not equivalent to the 600mg boric acid dose used in the clinical trials cited on the ingredient page.)

## Purpose
"Restore vaginal pH and relieve vaginal itching, burning and irritation."

## Directions
Adults/children 12+: insert one capsule daily for three days (acute) or use an additional package for chronic infection.

## Inactive Ingredients
Maltodextrin, vitamin E, vitamin C, Lactobacillus species, pullulan capsule material.

## Label statement
"The FDA has not evaluated this homeopathic product for safety or efficacy." (Consistent and accurate for this product, unlike the same boilerplate line appearing on the non-homeopathic "THE KILLER" 600mg product in this same folder.)
