## Active Ingredient
Boric Acid NF 600mg (per suppository)

## Purpose
Antiseptic

## Uses
Relief of symptoms of internal and external itching, irritation, burning, and discharge associated with vaginal yeast infections.

## Warnings
For vaginal use only; not for oral consumption. Not indicated for treating sexually transmitted infections. Consult a doctor before use if pregnant, nursing, or experiencing vaginal bleeding, sores, fever, or pelvic pain. Discontinue if symptoms persist, worsen, or burning/itching intensifies. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical assistance and contact Poison Control (1-800-222-1222) immediately. Avoid sexual intercourse during treatment; latex condoms not recommended (may be compromised).

## Directions
Insert 1 suppository into vagina, preferably at bedtime. Use for 7 days for chronic irritation (up to 14 days), or as a spot treatment when needed.

## Inactive Ingredient
Gelatin (capsule)
