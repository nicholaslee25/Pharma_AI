## Active Ingredient
Azelastine HCl 205.5mcg/spray (equivalent to 187.6mcg azelastine)

## Purpose
Antihistamine

## Uses
Temporarily relieves nasal congestion, runny nose, sneezing, itchy nose due to hay fever or other upper respiratory allergies.

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzalkonium chloride, edetate disodium, hypromellose, purified water, sodium citrate, sorbitol, sucralose.

## Warnings
For nasal use only — do not spray in eyes or mouth.

## Brand-specific notes
Genuinely different drug class (topical antihistamine) from the corticosteroid products (FLONASE, Nasacort, Nasonex) dominating the same Pharmacy Times category. No dedicated ingredient page exists in this database for azelastine.
