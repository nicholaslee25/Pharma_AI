# DailyMed — Cepacol Antibacterial Multi-Protection Mouthwash

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=7c19eba6-3ebd-497f-9066-6152b72eafdd
**Manufacturer:** Reckitt Benckiser LLC

## Active Ingredient
Cetylpyridinium Chloride 0.05% — antigingivitis/antiplaque.

## Uses
Kills germs that cause bad breath, reduces plaque, freshens breath.

## Directions
Adults/children 12+: swish 20mL twice daily for 30 seconds, spit out.

## Warnings
Keep out of reach of children under 6; seek medical help/Poison Control if accidentally swallowed beyond rinsing use.

---
Distinct from Cepacol's separate benzocaine-based lozenge line (see [[topical-anesthetics]]). See `drugs/Cepacol Antibacterial.md`.
