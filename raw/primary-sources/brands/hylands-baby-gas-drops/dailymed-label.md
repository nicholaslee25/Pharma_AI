## Purpose / Uses
"Temporarily relieves the symptoms of gas, stomach pressure and pain, swollen tummy, restlessness, crying, burping, fussiness, groaning, gas discomfort, colic, constipation, disturbed sleep, hard tummy, and bloating."

## Warnings
Stop use and ask a doctor if symptoms persist for more than 7 days or worsen.
