# Scope — Classic (cosmetic) and drug sub-lines

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Scope Classic (Original Formula)
**Sources:** crest.com product page; Amazon; skinsafeproducts.com
**Manufacturer's own claim:** "#1 fresh breath brand among non-drug mouthwashes."
**Ingredients:** water, alcohol (~15 wt%), glycerin, flavor, polysorbate 80, sodium saccharin, sodium benzoate, cetylpyridinium chloride, benzoic acid, D&C/FD&C colorants.
**No FDA Drug Facts / therapeutic claim identified** — cosmetic classification per manufacturer marketing and FDA's own cosmetic-vs-drug mouthwash policy (products claiming only breath/taste benefits are cosmetics).

## Crest Scope Original (separate drug SKU)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=21eed451-9368-146c-e063-6294a90af62b
**Active Ingredient:** Cetylpyridinium chloride 0.07% — antigingivitis/antiplaque.

## Crest Scope Advanced (separate drug SKU)
**Active Ingredient:** Sodium Fluoride 0.02% (0.01% w/v fluoride ion) — anticavity.

---
**Note:** despite similar naming, "Scope Classic/Original" (cosmetic, no drug claim) and "Crest Scope Original" (drug, CPC 0.07%, antigingivitis claim) appear to be different products/labels — naming is genuinely ambiguous across sources; the plain "Scope" entry in this database's Cosmetic Mouthwashes category is treated as most plausibly the manufacturer-declared non-drug Classic formula.
