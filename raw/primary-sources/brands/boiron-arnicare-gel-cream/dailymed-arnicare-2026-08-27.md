## Drug Facts (both Gel and Cream)
**Active Ingredient:** Arnica montana 1X HPUS 7%
**Purpose:** Relieves muscle pain, stiffness, bruise-related symptoms
**Uses:** Temporarily addresses muscle discomfort from minor injuries, overexertion, or falls; reduces bruising symptoms (pain, swelling, discoloration).
**Warnings:** External use only. Avoid eyes, mucous membranes, damaged skin. Do not wrap tightly or apply heat/ice immediately before/after use. Discontinue if condition persists beyond 3 days or worsens.
**Directions (Gel):** Apply thin layer as soon as possible after minor injury, repeat 3x/day or as needed. Wait 5 min after heat/ice.
**Directions (Cream):** Adults/children 1+: apply thin layer, massage gently, as soon as possible after injury, up to 3x/day.
**Cream inactive ingredients:** Arachidyl alcohol, arachidyl glucoside, behenyl alcohol, shea butter, coconut oil, glycerin, lactobacillus ferment, purified water, xanthan gum.
**Disclaimer:** FDA has not evaluated this homeopathic product for safety or efficacy; FDA not aware of scientific evidence supporting homeopathy's effectiveness.

## Arnica evidence base (see ingredients/homeopathic-arnica.md)
Systematic review (DARE/NCBI): 11 controlled trials, high heterogeneity, "efficacy of Arnica in doses of 10% and below is not supported by the available evidence." Knuesel et al. open trial: FRESH PLANT gel (not homeopathic dilution), 79 knee OA patients, significant WOMAC improvement, 7.6% local AE rate.
