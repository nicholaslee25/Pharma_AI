# XyliMelts — sourcing

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Formulation
Oral-adhering disc, xylitol (~500-550mg per disc) + cellulose gum (lubricant/humectant) + mild mint flavor, time-released. Manufacturer: OraHealth Corp. / OraCoat.

## Pivotal Study
**Burgess & Lee, PMID 22040224, International Journal of Dental Hygiene ("XyliMelts time-release adhering discs for night-time oral dryness")** — N=15 subjects with self-identified morning oral dryness, evaluated without treatment then with XyliMelts used overnight (self-controlled, non-placebo, non-randomized design). Perceived oral wetness scores increased more than threefold; morning discomfort significantly improved with use.

## Regulatory Note
Xylitol is FDA GRAS (Generally Recognized As Safe) — a food-safety designation, not necessarily equivalent to full OTC monograph drug review.
