# DailyMed — Red Cross Toothache, Liquid

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=3537e599-65d9-48d6-8a46-d42312f71e84
**Manufacturer:** The Mentholatum Company

## Active Ingredient
Eugenol 85%.

## Purpose
Toothache relief agent.

## Uses
Temporarily relieves throbbing, persistent toothache due to a cavity until a dentist can be seen.

## Warnings
For external use only; do not use if allergic to eugenol (clove oil); contact with lips or mucous membranes can cause burning, swelling, or irritation.

## Directions
Adults and children 12 years of age and over: apply up to 4 times daily or as directed by a dentist or doctor.

## Inactive Ingredient
Sesame oil.
