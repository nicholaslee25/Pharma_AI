# Zeasorb — Multi-Line Summary

Sources: DailyMed search results, pulled 2026-08-27

## Zeasorb AF / Athletes Foot / Jock Itch (antifungal)
Active ingredient: Miconazole Nitrate 2%. Talc-free, paraben-free, aluminum-free, patented odor control. Directions: clean/dry area, apply thin layer twice daily (AM/PM), use daily for 4 weeks. Warnings: external use only, avoid eyes, not for children under 2 without doctor direction. ## Zeasorb Excess Moisture Control / Prevention Powder (cosmetic, no active ingredient)
Ingredients: Zea Mays (Corn) Starch, Tricalcium Phosphate, Maranta Arundinacea Root Extract, Tapioca Starch, Calamine, Water, Fragrance, Sodium Benzoate, Potassium Sorbate. Absorbs "3x more moisture than plain talcum powder." No FDA Drug Facts panel.

## Miconazole nitrate comparative evidence (see ingredients/miconazole-nitrate-topical.md)
- El-Gohary 2014 Cochrane review: 129 trials, 18,086 participants, tinea cruris/corporis — all antifungal classes effective; allylamines generally somewhat more efficacious than azoles per secondary summaries.
- Miconazole vs tolnaftate tinea pedis: 68% vs 50% therapeutic cure at 28 days.
- Miconazole 2% vs placebo: 75.5% clinical clearing.
- Miconazole vs ketoconazole: comparable, ketoconazole somewhat faster/more sustained.
