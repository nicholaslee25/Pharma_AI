# DailyMed — Panama Jack Burn Relief Gel, FDA Drug Facts

**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=7055acd5-1c83-397e-e053-2a91aa0a3852
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient & Strength
Lidocaine Hydrochloride 0.72% — topical analgesic.

## Uses
Temporary relief of pain associated with sunburn, insect bites, and minor skin irritations.

## Warnings
Do not use on damaged or broken skin, or in large quantities over raw/blistered areas. Keep out of eyes; rinse with water if contact occurs. Stop use and consult a doctor if rash/irritation develops, condition worsens, or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur. External use only. Keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Adults/children 2+: apply to affected area not more than 3-4 times daily. Under 2: consult a doctor first.
