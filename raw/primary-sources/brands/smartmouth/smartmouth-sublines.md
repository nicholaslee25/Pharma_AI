## SmartMouth Clinical DDS Formula
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=87057ce9-e3b2-44f5-bac0-951aeecad768
**Active Ingredient:** Cetylpyridinium Chloride 0.05% (when mixed as directed) — antigingivitis/antiplaque.
**Uses:** helps prevent/reduce plaque and gingivitis, controls plaque bacteria contributing to gingivitis and bleeding gums.
**Directions:** adults/children 12+: swish 20mL twice daily for 30 seconds, spit out.
**Warnings:** keep from children under 6; supervise 6-11; do not use under 6; seek medical help if accidentally swallowed beyond rinsing use.
