## Active Ingredient
Oxymetazoline hydrochloride 0.05%

## Directions
Adults/children 6 to <12y (adult supervision): 2-3 sprays each nostril, not more often than every 10-12h, max 2 doses/24h.

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzalkonium chloride, benzyl alcohol, dibasic sodium phosphate, edetate disodium, hypromellose, phosphoric acid, purified water, sodium chloride.
