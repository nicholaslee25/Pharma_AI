# DailyMed & FDA NDA History — Salonpas

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Hisamitsu Pharmaceutical Co.

## Salonpas Pain Relief Patch (and Arthritis Pain Patch) — FDA NDA-approved formulation
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=724bc4d3-0bc1-4275-a23b-4bbc62fbc0d2
**Active Ingredients:** Menthol 3% (31.5mg), Methyl Salicylate 10% (105mg) — labeled explicitly as an NSAID.
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves mild to moderate aches/pains — strains, sprains, simple backache, arthritis, bruises.
**Key Warnings:** Stomach bleeding risk (NSAID) — age 60+, ulcer/bleeding history, blood thinner/steroid/other-NSAID users; do not use on face/rashes/wounds/damaged skin; aspirin/NSAID allergy contraindication; do not use with heating pads; do not use while sweating; do not use from pouches open 14+ days; do not use before/after heart surgery. Stop use if stomach pain worsens, bloody vomit/stools occur, rash develops, symptoms persist beyond 3 days. **Pregnancy: "especially important not to use methyl salicylate at 20 weeks or later in pregnancy unless definitely directed to do so by a doctor."**
**Directions (adults 18+):** One patch at a time, max 2 patches daily, apply to clean dry skin 8-12 hours, max 3 consecutive days. Not for children under 18.

## FDA Regulatory History — New Drug Application (NDA), not OTC monograph
Per Hisamitsu's own press materials (BioSpace) and product marketing: in **February 2008, the FDA approved a New Drug Application (NDA)** covering Salonpas Pain Relief Patch and Salonpas Arthritis Pain — described by the manufacturer as the first OTC topical analgesic patch to be FDA-approved via the full NDA process (a substantially higher regulatory bar involving brand-specific efficacy/safety trials) rather than the OTC monograph system that governs most topical analgesics in this database (including every other Thermal Relief Products competitor).

## Pivotal trial — Higashi et al. 2010 (PMID 20171409, Clinical Therapeutics 32(1):34-43)
Randomized, double-blind, parallel-group, placebo-controlled, multicenter RCT, N=208 (per secondary reporting), adults with mild-to-moderate muscle strain, single 8-hour patch application (10% methyl salicylate + 3% l-menthol vs. placebo patch).
**Results per manufacturer's own clinical-studies summary (salonpaspro.com), not independently re-derived from the full paywalled paper:** "40% additional relief experienced with Salonpas vs. Placebo"; "58% of study patients rating satisfaction from Salonpas as good/very good/excellent." Analgesic effect reported to begin within the first hour and last up to 12 hours.
This trial is the pivotal study that supported the 2008 NDA approval above — a genuine case of brand-specific (not just ingredient-class) RCT evidence directly backing a specific commercial product's specific formulation.

## Other Salonpas SKUs (separate formulations, not covered by the above trial)
- **Salonpas Pain Relieving Jet (aerosol spray):** Menthol 3% + Methyl Salicylate 10% — same concentrations as the Pain Relief Patch, but a different delivery format not itself tested in the Higashi trial.
- **Salonpas Deep Relieving (gel):** Menthol 10% + Methyl Salicylate 15% — HIGHER concentrations, exceeding the FDA's 2012 burn-risk thresholds (>3% menthol, >10% methyl salicylate); not covered by the Higashi RCT.
- **Plain "Salonpas" (patch):** Camphor + Menthol + Methyl Salicylate — a three-ingredient formulation, exact percentages not independently confirmed in this pass.
- **Salonpas Pain Relieving HOT (patch):** Capsaicin + Menthol — NO methyl salicylate at all, a mechanistically different formulation combining capsaicin's TRPV1 mechanism with menthol's TRPM8 mechanism; see [[capsaicin]].

## Summary
Salonpas spans at least 5 distinct formulations across its product line, with meaningfully different concentrations and even different active-ingredient classes (methyl salicylate present in most SKUs but absent from the capsaicin-based "HOT" line) — the same "single brand, multiple formulations" pattern flagged repeatedly elsewhere in this database, though here it's complicated by the fact that ONE specific SKU (Pain Relief Patch) carries genuinely superior regulatory/evidence status (FDA NDA approval, dedicated pivotal RCT) that does NOT automatically extend to its sibling SKUs.
