# DailyMed — Tiger Balm (multiple SKUs), FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Haw Par Healthcare Ltd., Singapore (Haw Par Corporation Ltd. license)

## Tiger Balm Active Muscle Rub — full-strength three-ingredient formula
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=2e7638c6-e1ce-4e2f-bd50-52fbce5a138e&type=display
**Active Ingredients:** Methyl Salicylate 15% + Menthol 5% + Camphor 3% — all Topical Analgesics.
**Uses:** Temporary relief of minor aches/pains of muscles/joints — simple backache, arthritis, bruises, strains, sprains.
**Warnings:** Allergy Alert — "if prone to allergic reaction from aspirin or salicylates, consult doctor before use"; pregnancy/breastfeeding — ask a healthcare professional before use, also if you have sensitive skin; do not apply to wounds/irritated skin; avoid eyes/mucous membranes; do not bandage or use heating pads; stop if condition worsens, severe skin irritation develops, pain persists beyond 7 days, or recurs.
**Directions:** Adults/children over 12: rub on affected area 3-4 times daily. 12 and under: consult a doctor first.

## Tiger Balm Muscle Rub — same three-ingredient class (concentrations not independently re-confirmed)
Methyl salicylate + menthol + camphor (synthetic) — same active-ingredient combination as Active Muscle Rub; exact percentages not independently re-pulled for this specific SKU name in this pass.

## Tiger Balm Pain Relieving Muscle Spray — same three-ingredient class, spray format
Methyl salicylate + menthol + camphor (synthetic), spray delivery.

## Tiger Balm Active Muscle Gel — TWO-ingredient formula, no camphor
Methyl salicylate + menthol only (no camphor) — a different combination than the Rub/Spray SKUs above.

## Tiger Balm Neck and Shoulder Rub — TWO-ingredient formula, no methyl salicylate
Camphor (synthetic) + menthol only — NO methyl salicylate, meaning this specific SKU does NOT carry the NSAID-class systemic risk (stomach bleeding, salicylate allergy, pregnancy warning) that the methyl-salicylate-containing SKUs do.

## Tiger Balm Ultra Strength — TWO-ingredient formula, no methyl salicylate
Camphor (synthetic) + menthol — same two-ingredient pattern as Neck and Shoulder Rub; exact percentages not independently re-pulled in this pass.

## Summary — three genuinely different formulation patterns under one brand
1. **Triple-ingredient (methyl salicylate + menthol + camphor):** Active Muscle Rub, Muscle Rub, Pain Relieving Muscle Spray — carries full NSAID-class risk via methyl salicylate.
2. **Methyl salicylate + menthol only (no camphor):** Active Muscle Gel — still carries NSAID-class risk.
3. **Camphor + menthol only (no methyl salicylate):** Neck and Shoulder Rub, Ultra Strength — does NOT carry the methyl-salicylate-specific NSAID-class risk (stomach bleeding, salicylate allergy, pregnancy warning), a meaningfully different safety profile from the other two groups despite sharing the "Tiger Balm" name.
