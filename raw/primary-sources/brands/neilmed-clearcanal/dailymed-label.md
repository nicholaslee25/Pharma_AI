# NeilMed ClearCanal — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d330552d-07b6-f01e-e053-2a95a90a7ca5
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Kit Components
1. **Ear drops:** Carbamide peroxide 6.5% (active); inactive: glycerin, oxyquinoline (some formulations also list aloe barbadensis leaf juice, chamomile flower extract)
2. **Saline rinse spray:** USP water, USP sodium chloride 9 mg/mL, USP sodium bicarbonate 0.5-1.0 mg/mL (pH buffer); some formulations include xylitol

## Use
Combination kit — carbamide peroxide drops to soften/loosen wax, followed by saline spray irrigation to rinse it out. Occasional use only.
