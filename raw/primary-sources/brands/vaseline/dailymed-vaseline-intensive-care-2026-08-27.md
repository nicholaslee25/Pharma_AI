## Drug Facts
**Active Ingredient:** White petrolatum USP 30%
**Purpose:** Skin protectant
**Uses:** Temporarily protects minor cuts, scrapes, and burns. Temporarily protects and helps relieve chapped or cracked skin; protects from environmental drying.
**Warnings:** External use only; avoid eye contact. Discontinue use if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days. Do not use on deep wounds, animal bites, or serious burns. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.
**Directions:** Apply as needed.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Water, caprylic/capric triglyceride, stearic acid, glycerin, sodium hydroxypropyl starch phosphate, glycol stearate, PEG-100 stearate, glyceryl stearate, cetyl alcohol, phenoxyethanol, methylparaben, isopropyl myristate, propylparaben, stearamide AMP, acrylates/C10-30 alkyl acrylate crosspolymer, disodium EDTA, potassium hydroxide, cedrol, tocopheryl acetate, titanium dioxide.
