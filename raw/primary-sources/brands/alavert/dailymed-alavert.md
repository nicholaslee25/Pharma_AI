## Active Ingredient
Loratadine 10 mg, orally disintegrating tablet (melts on tongue, no water required)

## Purpose
Antihistamine

## Uses
Non-drowsy 24-hour relief of sneezing, runny nose, itchy watery eyes, itching of nose or throat.

## Warnings
Ask a doctor before use if liver or kidney disease. Contact Poison Control for overdose.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥6y: one tablet daily; do not use more than 1 tablet in 24h; let tablet dissolve on tongue, with or without water. Children <6y: ask a doctor.

## Brand-specific notes beyond generic loratadine
- Alavert Allergy and Congestion D-12 Hour (loratadine + pseudoephedrine sulfate, extended release) is a separate combination product.
- No Alavert-brand-specific clinical trial found; one registered trial comparing a different loratadine+pseudoephedrine generic product to Claritin-D (NCT01055756) was withdrawn/not completed and does not involve Alavert.
- Functionally identical active ingredient/dose to Claritin (10mg loratadine); Alavert is positioned as the lower-cost store/generic-adjacent brand in this molecule class.
