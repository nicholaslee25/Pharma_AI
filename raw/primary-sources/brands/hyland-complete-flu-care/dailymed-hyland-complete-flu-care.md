## Active Ingredients (homeopathic combination)
Cairina moschata heart/liver autolysate, Bryonia alba root, Eupatorium perfoliatum flowering top, Euphrasia stricta, Gelsemium sempervirens root, Potassium iodide

## Inactive Ingredients
Acacia Gum, Lactose N.F.

## Uses
Temporarily relieves symptoms of fever, chills, body aches, headache, cough, and congestion from the flu or common cold.

## Warnings
Standard FDA homeopathic disclaimer: not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy; FDA is not aware of scientific evidence supporting homeopathy as effective.

## Brand-specific notes
**Cairina moschata is the Muscovy duck** — the same species/organ basis (heart/liver) as Oscillococcinum's Anas barbariae. This is a genuinely related but distinct product from Boiron Oscillococcinum, combining the duck-extract remedy with four additional homeopathic ingredients rather than as a single-remedy preparation. Should not be assumed to share Oscillococcinum's specific Cochrane review evidence.
