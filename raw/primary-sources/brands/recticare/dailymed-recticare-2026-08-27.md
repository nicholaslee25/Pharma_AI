## RectiCare (base)
**Active Ingredient:** Lidocaine 5% w/w
**Purpose:** Local anesthetic
**Uses:** Relieves pain, itching, and burning associated with hemorrhoids and other anorectal disorders.
**Warnings:** For external use only. Avoid eye contact. Do not exceed recommended dosage without doctor approval. Do NOT put this product into the rectum by using fingers or any mechanical device or applicator. Stop use and consult a doctor if rectal bleeding occurs, symptoms worsen after 7 days, allergic reactions develop, or symptoms don't subside.
**Directions:** Adults/children 12+: apply externally to affected area up to 6 times a day; clean and dry area first; use provided finger cots to smooth cream over area. Children under 12: consult a doctor.

## RectiCare Advanced
Lidocaine 5% w/w + Mineral Oil 17% w/w (protectant) + Phenylephrine HCl 0.25% w/w (vasoconstrictor) + White Petrolatum 39% w/w (protectant).
