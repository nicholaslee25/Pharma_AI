# DIFFERIN GEL — FDA Drug Facts Label

Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Active Ingredient
- Adapalene 0.1% (1 mg/g)

## Inactive Ingredients
Carbomer 940, edetate disodium, methylparaben, poloxamer 182, propylene glycol, purified water, sodium hydroxide; may contain hydrochloric acid for pH adjustment.

## Uses
For the treatment of acne.

## Directions
Apply a thin layer to affected areas of the skin only one time a day. Avoid contact with eyes, lips, and mouth.

## Warnings
For external use only. When using this product: skin irritation and dryness is more likely if used with other topical acne medications at the same time; avoid unprotected sun exposure and use of sunlamps, minimize sun exposure, use sunscreen. Ask a doctor before use if pregnant or breastfeeding.
