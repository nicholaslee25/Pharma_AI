## Active Ingredients (per 15mL)
Acetaminophen 325mg, Dextromethorphan HBr 15mg, Doxylamine succinate 6.25mg

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 30mL every 6 hours while symptoms last; do not exceed 120mL (4 doses)/24h.

## Warnings
Contains acetaminophen — severe liver damage may occur if >120mL (4 doses)/24h.

## Brand-specific notes
No pseudoephedrine or phenylephrine — consistent with the base Coricidin HBP brand's confirmed decongestant-free positioning across its whole product line.
