## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU, NONE contain a nasal decongestant
- Cold and Flu (base tablet): Acetaminophen + Chlorpheniramine maleate
- Maximum Strength Cold, Cough and Flu (liquid): Acetaminophen + Dextromethorphan HBr
- Maximum Strength Multi Symptom Flu (tablet): Acetaminophen + Chlorpheniramine maleate + Dextromethorphan HBr
- Maximum Strength Cold and Flu Day and Night (kit): Acetaminophen + Dextromethorphan HBr + Guaifenesin ± Doxylamine succinate (night)

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer, antihistamine (select SKUs), cough suppressant (select SKUs), expectorant (Day/Night kit) — deliberately NO decongestant in any SKU found.

## Brand-specific notes
The defining, confirmed brand feature: formulated without pseudoephedrine or phenylephrine across every SKU found, consistent with "HBP" (high blood pressure) safety positioning for patients who need to avoid decongestant-driven blood pressure elevation.
