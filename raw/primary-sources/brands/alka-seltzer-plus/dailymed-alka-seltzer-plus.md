## Active Ingredients — varies significantly by SKU (this is a large product family, not one formulation)
- **Severe Cold & Flu (tablet):** Acetaminophen 250mg + Chlorpheniramine maleate 2mg + Dextromethorphan HBr 10mg + Phenylephrine HCl 5mg
- **Maximum Strength Day/Night Cold & Flu:** Acetaminophen + Dextromethorphan HBr + Doxylamine succinate (night only) + Phenylephrine HCl, split day/night dosing kit
- **Cold Medicine Sparkling Original:** Aspirin (NSAID) + Chlorpheniramine maleate (antihistamine) + Phenylephrine bitartrate (decongestant) — an ASPIRIN-based, not acetaminophen-based, variant
- Other SKUs (Cold and Cough Power Max Gels, Severe Cold and Flu PowerFast Fizz, Day Severe Cold Plus Flu powder) combine similar actives in varying doses/forms, some adding guaifenesin

## Purpose
Multi-symptom cold/flu relief: pain reliever/fever reducer, antihistamine, cough suppressant, nasal decongestant (combination varies by SKU).

## Warnings (general pattern across SKUs)
Acetaminophen-containing variants: liver damage risk if combined with other acetaminophen-containing products or with ≥3 alcoholic drinks/day. Aspirin-containing variant: Reye's syndrome risk in children/teens with flu-like symptoms or chickenpox; GI bleeding risk; do not use with other NSAIDs/anticoagulants without medical advice. All variants: do not exceed labeled dose; do not use with other cold/allergy/pain products containing the same actives (duplication risk).

## Directions
Varies by SKU — see individual product label; effervescent tablets typically dissolved in water, taken every 4 hours as needed, not exceeding stated max tablets/24h.

## Brand-specific notes
- This is the most fragmented "brand" encountered so far — at least 8 distinct active-ingredient combinations exist under one brand name, including a genuine aspirin-vs-acetaminophen split that matters clinically (Reye's syndrome risk, anticoagulant interactions).
- No single ingredient page in this database's ingredients/ folder covers the whole combination; individual actives map to acetaminophen.md, aspirin.md, dextromethorphan.md, oral-phenylephrine.md/pseudoephedrine.md as applicable per SKU.
