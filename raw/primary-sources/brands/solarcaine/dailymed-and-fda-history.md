# DailyMed & FDA History — Solarcaine

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Solarcaine Burn Pain Relief Spray (4oz) — current formulation
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=14f23c28-c713-4868-9f46-66363bf94b2c&type=display
**Manufacturer:** WellSpring Pharmaceutical Corporation
**Active Ingredient:** Lidocaine HCl 4% — External analgesic
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves discomfort/itching from sunburn, minor burns, cuts, scrapes, insect bites, minor skin irritations.
**Warnings:** External use only; do not apply large quantities over raw/blistered areas; avoid eyes; intentional inhalation can be harmful or fatal; do not puncture/incinerate pressurized container; avoid storage above 40°C (104°F); discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens, persists beyond 7 days, or recurs; keep from children, contact Poison Control if ingested.
**Directions:** Shake well. Adults/children 2+: apply 3-4 times daily max. Under 2: consult a physician. Facial application: spray into palm first.

## Historical FDA Regulatory Note — "Seriously Misbranded" (1988)
A December 5, 1988 Pink Sheet (FDC Reports) headline reads: "SOLARCAINE SEVERE SUNBURN RELIEF SPRAY 'SERIOUSLY MISBRANDED.'" Per WebSearch secondary summary: the FDA considered a Solarcaine lidocaine/aloe vera combination product "seriously misbranded" because the agency was not aware of substantial scientific evidence establishing that this specific ingredient combination was Generally Recognized As Safe and Effective (GRASE) for treating SEVERE sunburn specifically (as opposed to ordinary/minor sunburn). Full article text, resolution, and whether the product was reformulated/relabeled as a result were NOT independently retrieved in this pass — only the headline and date were confirmed. This is a genuinely old (38-year) regulatory event; flagged for completeness and honesty, not presented as a current/ongoing issue, since no evidence of present-day non-compliance was found and current DailyMed labels (above) do not claim "severe" sunburn treatment specifically.
