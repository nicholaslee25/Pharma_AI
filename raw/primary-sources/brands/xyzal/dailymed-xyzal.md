## Active Ingredient
Levocetirizine dihydrochloride 5 mg

## Purpose
Antihistamine

## Uses
Temporarily relieves runny nose, sneezing, itchy watery eyes, itching of nose or throat due to hay fever/upper respiratory allergies; also relieves itching due to uncomplicated skin hives (urticaria).

## Warnings
Do not use if allergic to levocetirizine, cetirizine, or hydroxyzine. Ask a doctor before use with liver or kidney disease. May cause drowsiness — avoid alcohol; use caution driving/operating machinery. Ask a doctor before use if taking tranquilizers or sedatives.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one 5mg tablet once daily in the evening; do not take more than 1 tablet/24h. Children 6-11y: consult a doctor (pediatric oral solution 2.5mg/5mL exists as a separate labeled product).

## Brand-specific notes beyond generic levocetirizine
- **NCT00375713** — a randomized Phase III trial directly comparing Xyzal (levocetirizine) vs. Zyrtec (cetirizine) in subjects with dermatitis/eczema — a genuine head-to-head brand comparison trial, not just molecule-level evidence. Outcome details were not extracted in this pass (trial registry entry found; full results not pulled).
- Levocetirizine is the active (R)-enantiomer of cetirizine — pharmacologically related to Zyrtec's active ingredient but a distinct FDA-approved molecule with its own label.
