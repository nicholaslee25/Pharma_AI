# DailyMed — Salonpas Lidocaine (Plus/FLEX/Gel-Patch), FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Hisamitsu America, Inc.

## Salonpas Lidocaine Plus Cream/Liquid
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=364924d3-fac3-4f4e-9b40-decd4ec48245
**Active Ingredients:** Benzyl alcohol 10% + Lidocaine HCl 4% — both labeled "Topical anesthetic."
**Uses:** Temporary relief of pain.
**Warnings:** Do not use on wounds/damaged skin, in large quantities, with a heating pad, or if allergic to any ingredient; avoid eyes/mucous membranes/rashes; do not bandage tightly; stop use and ask a doctor if skin reactions occur (rash, itching, redness, irritation, pain, swelling, blistering), condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond 7 days, or recur; ask a health professional if pregnant/breastfeeding; keep from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.
**Directions:** Adults/children 12+: clean/dry area, apply 3-4 times daily max. Under 12: consult a doctor.

## Salonpas Pain Relieving Lidocaine 4% FLEX Patch
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9507f727-c49f-4de4-9d64-b6d977d568ab
Lidocaine 4%, stretchable patch format, wearable up to 8 hours, 7 patches per box.

## Salonpas Lidocaine 4% Pain Relieving Gel-Patch
Lidocaine 4%, unscented gel-patch format for back/neck/shoulders/knees/elbows, 6 patches per box.

## Note
This is a genuinely different Salonpas sub-line from the methyl-salicylate-based "Salonpas" products already documented on `drugs/Salonpas.md` — no methyl salicylate present here at all, so none of the NSAID-class systemic risks (stomach bleeding, salicylate allergy, pregnancy warning at 20+ weeks) apply to this lidocaine line. Instead it carries [[topical-anesthetics]]'s lidocaine-specific (lower methemoglobinemia risk) profile.
