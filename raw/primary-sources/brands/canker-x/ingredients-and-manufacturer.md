# Canker-X — Product Notes (Alliance Pharmaceuticals)

Source: https://cankerx.com/products/canker-x-mouth-sore-gel/ (via WebFetch)

Manufacturer: Alliance Pharmaceuticals Ltd. (per site copyright notice)
Marketing claim: "Benzocaine Free - Alcohol Free"
Mechanism: "coats, hydrates, and soothes with aloe vera"; "promotes healing by forming a protective barrier against irritants"
Ingredients: water, polyvinylpyrrolidone, maltodextrin, aloe barbadensis leaf, sodium hyaluronate, and other components (not fully itemized on the fetched page).

Note: this ingredient list is near-identical to GUM Canker-X's confirmed ingredient list (water, PVP, maltodextrin, propylene glycol, PEG-40 hydrogenated castor oil, xanthan gum, potassium sorbate, sodium benzoate, disodium edetate, benzalkonium chloride, saccharin sodium, sodium hyaluronate, glycyrrhetinic acid, aloe vera) — suggesting a shared underlying formulation, possibly licensed from the same source (GUM Canker-X's own page references the international "Aloclair" mouth-ulcer gel). Corporate/licensing relationship between Alliance Pharmaceuticals and Sunstar (GUM's parent) not independently confirmed in this pass.
