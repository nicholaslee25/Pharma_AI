# Beyond BodiHeat — Regulatory Status and Safety Notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Okamoto USA, Inc.

## Product Type & Mechanism
Air-activated exothermic heat patch — composition per manufacturer product description: activated carbon, iron powder, vermiculite (the same class of chemistry as common air-activated hand warmers). Activates upon opening the package, ready within 15-30 minutes, delivers "up to 12 hours" of heat per manufacturer marketing.

## Regulatory Status
No DailyMed listing exists for Beyond BodiHeat (confirmed by direct search) — consistent with this being a device/heat-generating product rather than an FDA-regulated drug, the same regulatory category as [[ThermaCare]] (though FDA device-classification specifics for this particular manufacturer/product were not independently confirmed against an FDA database record in this pass, unlike ThermaCare's confirmed Class II status).

## Safety Warnings (per manufacturer/retail sources)
- Do not use more than 6 hours in any 24-hour period (despite "up to 12 hours" duration marketing — a real distinction between how long the pad STAYS warm and how long it should be WORN).
- Do not use on infants, frostbitten/desensitized skin, while sleeping, or on bruising/swelling from the last 48 hours.
- **Do NOT adhere the pad directly to skin — designed to be worn OVER CLOTHING.** This is a meaningful mechanistic/usage difference from [[ThermaCare]], whose wraps are designed for direct skin adhesion.
- If the pad becomes too hot or irritation occurs, remove immediately — burns can occur if not used correctly, including from direct skin contact.

## Category-placement note
Consistent with [[ThermaCare]]: Beyond BodiHeat is a heat-generating DEVICE, not a counterirritant chemical drug, despite being tracked in the "Thermal Relief Products" category alongside menthol/methyl salicylate/camphor rubs. See [[topical-counterirritants]].
