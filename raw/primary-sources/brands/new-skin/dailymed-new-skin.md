# DailyMed — New-Skin Liquid/Spray Bandage, FDA Drug Facts

**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=60996119-8367-4e22-b6ec-c36490635fa4 (liquid/brush); https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5846f414-8329-4034-b99f-92721bdb3d14 (spray)
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Advantice Health, LLC

## Active Ingredient & Strength
Benzethonium chloride 0.2% — First Aid Antiseptic (both liquid/brush and spray forms).

## Uses
Aids in preventing bacterial contamination in minor cuts and scrapes during initial wound care.

## Warnings
For external use only. Flammable — keep away from fire/flame. Do not use: in eyes or on mucous membranes; over large body areas or more than 1 week without medical direction; on infected/draining wounds or over sutures; combined with other first aid products (lotions/creams). Ask a doctor first if: deep/puncture wounds, animal bites, serious bleeding, diabetes, circulation problems, severe burns. Do not store above 120°F. **Intentional inhalation misuse can be harmful or fatal.** Stop use and consult a doctor if condition worsens/persists or infection develops. Keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Clean area; apply a small amount 1-3 times daily; allow to dry (forms a flexible film/"liquid bandage"); a second coat may be applied for extra protection; remove by applying more product and wiping quickly.

## Inactive Ingredients (liquid/brush form)
Amyl acetate, butyl acetate, camphor, ethyl acetate, nitrocellulose, SD alcohol 3-C (7.2% v/v).

## Note
Storage/handling instructions warn the product will stain floors/countertops/finished surfaces. The solvent-based film-forming formulation (nitrocellulose + acetates + alcohol) is mechanistically analogous to nail polish/collodion-type liquid bandages — the antiseptic action comes specifically from benzethonium chloride, not the film-forming solvent base itself.
