## Care & Protect Antibacterial Beauty Bar — full Drug Facts panel
(setid=b9e248d7-0df3-649d-e053-2a95a90a0d0f)

**Active Ingredient:** Benzalkonium Chloride 0.13%
**Purpose:** Antibacterial
**Uses:** For washing to decrease bacteria on the skin
**Warnings:** For external use only. Do not use in or near the eyes; in case of contact, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. Stop use and ask a doctor if irritation or redness develop. Keep out of reach of children except under adult supervision. If swallowed, seek immediate medical help or contact Poison Control.
**Directions:** Wet bar with water, lather vigorously and use all over body, rinse clean.
