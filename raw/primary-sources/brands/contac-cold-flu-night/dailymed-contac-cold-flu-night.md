## Active Ingredients (per caplet)
Acetaminophen 500mg + Phenylephrine hydrochloride 5mg + Chlorpheniramine maleate

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer, nasal decongestant, antihistamine.

## Brand-specific notes
Directly parallels the Contac Cold + Flu Day formula (acetaminophen + phenylephrine, same doses) with chlorpheniramine added for nighttime. A separate "Contac Cold and Flu" capsule SKU uses dextromethorphan + doxylamine instead — a genuinely different formulation under a similar name; not the product confirmed here.
