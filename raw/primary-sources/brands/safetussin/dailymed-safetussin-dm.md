# SAFETUSSIN DM — Drug Facts Label (DailyMed)

## Active Ingredients (per 5 mL / 1 teaspoon)
- Dextromethorphan HBr, USP 10 mg — Cough Suppressant
- Guaifenesin, USP 100 mg — Expectorant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation; helps loosen phlegm (mucus) and thin bronchial secretions to clear bronchial passages of phlegm (mucus).

## Warnings
Do not use with prescription MAOIs or within 2 weeks of stopping MAOI drugs.
Ask a doctor before use if experiencing excessive mucus production or chronic cough (smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, emphysema).
Stop use and seek medical advice if cough persists beyond 7 days, returns, or accompanies fever, rash, or headache.
Not for children under 12 years. Avoid if pregnant or nursing without professional consultation.

## Directions
Adults and children 12+ years: 2 teaspoons every 4 hours, max 6 doses in 24 hours. Not intended for children under 12.

## Inactive Ingredients
Aspartame, benzoic acid, citric acid, glycerin, menthol, methylparaben, peppermint flavor, propylene glycol, propylparaben, purified water.

## Storage
Keep at 59-86F; protect from excessive heat and freezing.

## Note on brand line
Safetussin (Kramer Laboratories) is a multi-SKU line: DM (above), Non-Drowsy Cough Plus Chest Congestion (dextromethorphan+guaifenesin tablet/liquid), High Blood Pressure and Diabetes Cough Cold and Flu (acetaminophen+dextromethorphan+guaifenesin), and Max Strength MS Cough Cold and Flu (acetaminophen+dextromethorphan+guaifenesin tablet). Marketed as no decongestant, no sugar/alcohol/sorbitol/fructose/gluten/dyes — positioned for diabetic and hypertensive patients avoiding pseudoephedrine and sugar, a formulation/excipient claim rather than a pharmacological one.
