## Active Ingredients
Loratadine 5mg + Pseudoephedrine sulfate 120mg, extended-release tablet

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one tablet every 12 hours.

## Brand-specific notes
Loratadine dose here (5mg) is half the standalone Alavert/Claritin dose (10mg) — same reduced-antihistamine-dose pattern already observed in Zyrtec-D, suggesting this may be a common "-D" combination design convention across brands rather than a one-off.
