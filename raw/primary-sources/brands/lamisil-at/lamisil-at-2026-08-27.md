# Lamisil AT — Evidence Summary

Sources: WebSearch (PubMed/Wiley/PMC), pulled 2026-08-27

## Budimulja et al. 2001 (Mycoses)
"Once daily treatment with terbinafine 1% cream (Lamisil) for one week is effective in the treatment of tinea corporis and cruris. A placebo-controlled study."
Result: 7-day once-daily terbinafine 1% cream — 84.2% mycological cure vs. 23.3% placebo. Also significantly superior on clinical response and signs/symptoms scores.

## Comparative trial vs ketoconazole
Terbinafine 1% emulsion-gel: ~94% mycological cure vs. ketoconazole 2% cream (specific comparator cure rate not extracted in this pass).

## Head-to-head vs butenafine (PMC3481417)
76 male tinea cruris patients (terbinafine n=37, butenafine n=39), day 42: mycological cure butenafine 94.87% vs terbinafine 62.16%; overall cure 79.49% vs 62.16%; effective treatment 92.31% vs 81.08%. Significant by day 7 (P<0.01). Conclusion: butenafine superior to terbinafine for tinea cruris.
