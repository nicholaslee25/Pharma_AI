# Be Koool — Non-Medicated Cooling Gel Sheets

Manufacturer: Kobayashi Americas
Sources referenced (via WebSearch, not independently fetched in full): kobayashiamericas.com/faqs-bekoool, bekoool.com/faqs, product listings (CVS, Walgreens, Amazon, Kroger, Pasteur Pharmacy)

## Composition
Water-based gel sheet: water, a polymer, menthol, trace preservatives. No active pharmaceutical ingredient. No FDA Drug Facts panel found for this product line.

## Mechanism (per manufacturer FAQ)
Cooling effect from evaporation of the water-based gel plus menthol's cooling sensory effect; effect described as lasting up to 8 hours per sheet. Manufacturer states the product is "non-medicated" and therefore safe to use alongside any medication.
