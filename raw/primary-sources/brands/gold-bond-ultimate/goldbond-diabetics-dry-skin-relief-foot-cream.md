## Active Ingredients
- Dimethicone 3% — Skin protectant
- White Petrolatum 30% — Skin protectant

## Purpose
Skin protectant

## Uses
Temporarily protects and helps relieve cracked skin; helps hydrate and smooth rough patches.

## Warnings
For external use only. Avoid eye contact. Stop use and consult a doctor if the condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond seven days, or reoccur within days. Do not apply to deep wounds, puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns. Keep away from children. If swallowed, seek immediate medical help or contact Poison Control.

## Directions
Appropriate for daily use as directed; product marketing notes optimal results applying before bed.

## Note
Formulated for diabetics' dry skin with additional (non-drug-facts) moisturizers marketed as "7 intensive moisturizers" plus aloe and vitamins. The two listed actives (dimethicone, white petrolatum) are both classic OTC skin-protectant monograph ingredients, not a diabetes-specific pharmacology.
