# Sensodyne — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed), two SKUs

## Sensodyne (flagship)
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9e8e7b9f-3ccd-4935-b886-93f3a5045b67

**Active Ingredients:** Potassium nitrate 5%; Sodium fluoride 0.24% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)
**Purposes:** Antihypersensitivity; Anticavity
**Uses:** Builds increasing protection against painful sensitivity of the teeth to cold, heat, acids, sweets, or contact; aids in the prevention of dental cavities.
**Warnings:** Stop use and ask a dentist if the problem persists or worsens, or if pain/sensitivity persists after 4 weeks of use. Keep out of reach of children; get medical help or contact Poison Control if more than used for brushing is accidentally swallowed.
**Directions:** Adults/children 12+: apply at least a 1-inch strip to a soft-bristle toothbrush, brush thoroughly for at least 1 minute twice daily (morning/evening), not more than 3x/day. Children under 12: consult a dentist or doctor.

## Sensodyne Sensitivity, Gum and Enamel Mint
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=d4e9e5cf-d058-4c3f-ab5e-0b5a20a012e2

**Active Ingredient:** Stannous fluoride 0.454% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)
**Purpose:** Anticavity, Antigingivitis, Antihypersensitivity
**Uses:** Helps prevent dental cavities; helps control bleeding gums; interferes with harmful plaque effects linked to gingivitis; builds protection against tooth sensitivity to cold, heat, acids, sweets, or contact.
**Warnings:** Stop use and ask a dentist if the problem persists/worsens, pain/sensitivity remains after 4 weeks, gingivitis/bleeding/redness lasts beyond 2 weeks, or if painful/swollen gums, pus, loose teeth, or increased tooth spacing occur (possible periodontitis). Keep out of reach of children.
**Directions:** Adults/children 12+: apply a 1-inch strip, brush thoroughly for at least 1 minute twice daily, max 3x/day. Children under 12: consult a dentist or doctor.
