## Active Ingredient
Loratadine 10 mg (tablet/RediTab); Claritin Chewables 5 mg; Claritin Syrup 5mg/5mL

## Purpose
Antihistamine

## Uses
24-hour relief of sneezing, runny nose, itchy watery eyes, itchy throat or nose — indoor/outdoor allergies.

## Warnings
Ask a doctor before use if liver or kidney disease (may need lower dose). Non-drowsy formula at labeled OTC dose. Contact Poison Control for overdose.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥6y: one 10mg tablet once daily; do not use more than 1 tablet in 24h. Children 2–5y (syrup/chewable 5mg): consult a doctor.

## Brand-specific notes beyond generic loratadine
- Claritin-D (loratadine + pseudoephedrine) is a separate combination product, tracked as its own drugs/ page.
- A PNAS-published pair of RCTs (Kaptchuk et al., "Advertisements impact the physiological efficacy of a branded drug") found that direct-to-consumer advertising exposure measurably altered subjects' objective physiological response to Claritin relative to unbranded/generic presentation of the same molecule — a brand-perception effect, not a pharmacological difference in loratadine itself. Included here because it is genuinely Claritin-brand-specific research, distinct from general loratadine efficacy data.
- Claritin-D 24 Hour vs. Claritin-D 12 Hour vs. placebo RCT (multicenter) and a phase 4 crossover trial (NCT03443843) comparing Claritin-D to Flonase nasal spray for nasal airflow both exist but pertain to the -D combination product, not plain Claritin.
