(A separate DailyMed listing also covers Cortizone 10 Soothing Aloe Itch Relief Cream, Water Resistant Ointment, Ultra Soothing Creme, Sensitive Skin Creme, and Easy Relief liquid — all confirmed hydrocortisone 1% as well, per search result titles.)

## Active Ingredient & Strength
Hydrocortisone 1% (10 mg per 1 g), all formulations.

## Purpose
Anti-itch.

## Uses
Temporarily relieves itching associated with minor skin irritations, inflammation, and rashes due to: eczema, psoriasis, poison ivy/oak/sumac, insect bites, detergents, jewelry, cosmetics, soaps; seborrheic dermatitis; external anal and genital itching.

## Warnings
- For external use only.
- Avoid contact with eyes.
- Do not use for the treatment of diaper rash; consult a doctor.
- Do not use in the genital area if there is vaginal discharge.
- When using this product, do not exceed recommended dosage unless directed by a doctor.
- Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens, or symptoms persist for more than 7 days, or clear up and occur again within a few days.

## Directions
- Adults and children 2 years and older: apply to affected area not more than 3-4 times daily.
- Anal/genital itching (adults only): clean affected area, apply externally not more than 3-4 times daily.
- Children under 2 years: do not use, consult a doctor.
