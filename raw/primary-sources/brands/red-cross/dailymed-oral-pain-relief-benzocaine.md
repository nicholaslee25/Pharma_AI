# Red Cross Oral Pain Relief — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=cbd41429-f730-4577-9f06-ef1831cf62e1

Active Ingredient: Benzocaine 20%
Purpose: Oral pain reliever
Uses: Temporarily relieves discomfort from toothaches, canker sores, minor mouth irritation, and denture or brace-related mouth and gum injuries.
Warnings: Methemoglobinemia risk (stop use, seek immediate medical attention if pale/blue skin, headache, rapid heartbeat, breathing difficulty, dizziness, fatigue); avoid if allergic to "caine" anesthetics; not for teething or children under 2; limit to 7 days unless directed by dentist/doctor.
Directions: Adults/children 2+: use up to 4x daily via cotton pellet application; supervise children under 12.

Note: contrast with "Red Cross Toothache Medication" (eugenol 85%, sesame oil base) — already documented in raw/primary-sources for that separate product — same brand, different active ingredient.
