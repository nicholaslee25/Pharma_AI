## Epsom Salt Soaking Solution (magnesium sulfate USP)
Labeler: Advanced Beauty Systems, Inc.
**Purpose:** External soaking solution for pain relief; internal saline laxative (dual use, same product)
**Uses:** External — relieves pain from minor sprains and bruises. Internal — relieves occasional constipation, results in 1/2 to 6 hours.
**Directions:** External soak — 2 cups Epsom Salts under warm running bath water, soak 20 minutes. Internal — adults/children 12+: 2-4 level teaspoons dissolved in 8oz water, do not exceed 2 doses daily.
**Warnings:** Keep away from children, contact Poison Control if ingested outside laxative directions; avoid with kidney disease, prescription meds, or magnesium-restricted diet; stop and consult doctor if redness/irritation/swelling (external) or rectal bleeding (internal); pregnant/nursing consult physician; hot/warm soaks not recommended for diabetics without physician guidance.

## Pre and Post Workout Pain Relief Cream (menthol)
Labeler: PDC Brands
**Active Ingredient:** Menthol 10% (100mg/1g)
**Purpose:** Topical analgesic
**Uses:** Temporary relief of minor aches/pains of muscles/joints (backache, arthritis, strains, bruises, sprains).
**Directions:** Thin layer, up to 3-4x daily, 2-3 hours between applications, ages 12+.
**Warnings:** External use only; avoid wounds/damaged skin, heating pads, eyes/mucous membranes; do not bandage tightly; consult doctor if redness/worsening/persists beyond 7 days; pregnant/nursing consult provider; children under 12 with arthritis-like conditions need doctor approval.

## Transdermal magnesium absorption evidence (contested)
- Waring 2004 (University of Birmingham): n=19, 12-min daily magnesium sulfate baths x7 days, reported rise in plasma/urinary magnesium and sulfate. NOT peer-reviewed, no control group, hosted/promoted by the Epsom Salt Council (epsomsaltcouncil.org) — real conflict-of-interest concern.
- 2017 peer-reviewed review (Nutrients journal): concluded current evidence does not support transdermal magnesium absorption claims generally.
