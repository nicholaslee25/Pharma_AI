# TheraBreath — flagship (cosmetic) and drug sub-lines

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## TheraBreath Fresh Breath (flagship line)
**Sources:** therabreath.com/blogs/oral-health/how-therabreath-works; manufacturer marketing
**Marketing-claimed active component:** OXYD-8, described as a pH-balanced, stabilized formula derived from sodium chlorite (0.1%) — an oxidant said to fight anaerobic, sulfur-compound-producing bacteria. No DailyMed Drug Facts label was located specifically for this flagship "Fresh Breath"-branded formula in this pass.

## TheraBreath Anticavity Oral Rinse (Sparkle Mint)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=feab3d45-ff95-4b32-8258-7d932a7380c9
**Active Ingredient:** Sodium Fluoride — anticavity. Manufactured by Church & Dwight Co., Inc.
**Directions:** vigorously swish 10 mL between teeth for 1 minute, spit out, do not swallow, do not eat/drink for 30 minutes after.

## TheraBreath Deep Clean Fresh Mint
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=19bb1bc2-f4e3-4faa-82a5-656fec4397dd
**Active Ingredient:** Cetylpyridinium chloride — antigingivitis/antiplaque. Manufactured by Church & Dwight Co., Inc.

## TheraBreath Healthy Smile Oral Rinse
**Active Ingredient:** Sodium Fluoride. Manufactured by Dr. Harold Katz, LLC (the original TheraBreath company).

---
**Summary:** the flagship OXYD-8/sodium-chlorite "Fresh Breath" formula (Dr. Harold Katz, LLC) has no located Drug Facts panel — most plausibly cosmetic; separate Church & Dwight-manufactured "Anticavity" (fluoride) and "Deep Clean" (CPC) sub-lines are confirmed drugs. See `drugs/TheraBreath.md`.
