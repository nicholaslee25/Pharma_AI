# hello Toothpaste — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed) + Safety Events

## DailyMed — HELLO Toothpaste (flagship)
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=5e4197ed-2ed5-41cc-817e-8e9213dc780a

**Active Ingredient:** Sodium fluoride 0.24% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)
**Purpose:** Anticavity
**Use:** Aids in the prevention of dental cavities
**Warnings:** Keep away from children under 6 years; if swallowed in excess, seek medical help or contact Poison Control immediately; check that safety seal is intact before use.
**Directions:** Adults/children 2+: brush thoroughly at least twice daily or after meals, as dentist directs. Do not swallow. Children under 6: pea-sized amount, supervised. Children under 2: consult a dentist/physician.
**Flavors noted:** Peach Mango Mint, Bubble Gum, Green Apple, Blue Raspberry.

## Safety Event 1 — Confirmed FDA-reported recall (2023-08-10)
Colgate-Palmolive voluntary recall of 6 lots (182,046 tubes) of hello Wild Strawberry Fluoride Toothpaste (4.2oz, UPC 819156020264). Tubes were incorrectly labeled as hello Fresh Watermelon Fluoride-FREE Toothpaste — the mislabeled tube did not disclose fluoride's presence and lacked FDA-required directions/warnings. Product sold on or after October 2022. Per Colgate-Palmolive/FDA reporting: the formula met safety/quality specs and no adverse events were reported; root cause was a labeling defect, not contamination.
Sources: FDA recall listing referenced via ndclist.com (D-1084-2023); Happi.com; Beauty Packaging; Thrive Market recall notice.

## Safety Event 2 — Unresolved litigation allegation (filed 2025-07-11, NOT an FDA finding)
Federal class action, Damany Browne v. Hello Products LLC (S.D.N.Y.), alleges third-party testing found:
- Hello Kids Dragon Dazzle toothpaste: 428.4 ppb lead (~28x EPA action level), 11.8 ppb mercury (~5x EPA MCL)
- Hello Kids Fresh Watermelon Fluoride-Free toothpaste: 493 ppb lead (~32x EPA action level), 19 ppb mercury (~9x EPA MCL)
As of reporting found in this pass (per classaction.org, topclassactions.com), no FDA recall or safety advisory has confirmed these figures, and Hello Products had not issued a recall. This is a pending, disputed legal allegation — not independently verified by this database.
