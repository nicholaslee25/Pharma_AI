## Active Ingredient
Pectin (oral demulcent)

## Uses
Temporary relief of minor discomfort and protection of irritated areas in sore mouth and sore throat.

## Inactive Ingredients (vary by flavor)
Ascorbic acid, citric acid, corn syrup, malic acid, sodium acetate, sodium chloride, soybean oil, sucrose.

## Brand-specific notes
Genuinely different mechanism (demulcent/coating) from the menthol-based (cough suppressant/anesthetic) and benzocaine-based (anesthetic) lozenges dominating this category — no cough-suppressant or anesthetic claim made.
