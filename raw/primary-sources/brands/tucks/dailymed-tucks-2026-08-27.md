# Tucks — DailyMed Drug Facts

Source: DailyMed setid=a7cb6d78-3048-43b3-afe7-4d862fdcfd3f (Multi-Care Relief Kit, combines both SKUs below), and setid=b3e68008-3d0d-4124-9579-b503e9fb95c9 (Medicated Cooling Pads standalone). Pulled 2026-08-27.

## Tucks Medicated Cooling Pads
**Active Ingredient:** Witch hazel 50% w/w
**Purpose:** Astringent
**Uses:** Cleanses and soothes rectal or vaginal areas; temporarily relieves local itching and discomfort associated with hemorrhoids; aids in protecting irritated areas.
**Warnings:** External use only; do not insert; seek medical attention if bleeding or worsening occurs. DO NOT FLUSH pads after use.
**Directions:** Clean and dry area, apply up to 6 times daily.

## Tucks Hemorrhoidal Cream
**Active Ingredients:** Glycerin 15.0% w/w (protectant) + Lidocaine 5.0% w/w (local anesthetic)
**Purpose:** Numbs and protects painful hemorrhoids
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves local pain, itching, and discomfort associated with hemorrhoids; provides temporary coating relief for anorectal discomfort.
**Warnings:** External use only; do not insert directly into rectum; discontinue if rectal bleeding occurs or condition doesn't improve within 7 days.
**Directions:** Clean area, dry gently, apply externally up to 6 times daily or after each bowel movement.

## Witch hazel evidence context (PMC12128686, "Natural Products in Hemorrhoid Management")
Topical witch hazel preparations "have demonstrated efficacy in reducing anal itching, mild bleeding, burning, and inflammation" in external/Grade I-II internal hemorrhoids; recognized by American Society of Colon and Rectal Surgeons for conservative management. No specific RCT data or quantitative outcomes presented in this review — evidence characterized at clinical-use/guideline level, not RCT level. Contrast: horse chestnut (different botanical, not used by any brand in this database) does have cited RCT evidence per the same review.
