# BC Powder — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed) + Safety Case Reports

## DailyMed
- BC Cherry / BC / BC Arthritis: aspirin (NSAID, pain reliever/fever reducer) + caffeine (pain reliever aid)
- BC Max: acetaminophen 500mg (pain reliever) + aspirin 500mg (NSAID, pain reliever) + caffeine 65mg (pain reliever aid)
Warning (label): limit caffeine-containing medications, foods, or beverages while taking this product — too much caffeine may cause nervousness, irritability, sleeplessness, and occasionally rapid heartbeat.

## Safety literature (via WebSearch, not independently fetched in full)
- Case report: "From Relief to Risk: BC Powder's Impact on Chronic Salicylate Toxicity and Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome" (Iris Publishers, irispublishers.com)
- Case report: kidney failure after ~4 years of BC Powder use combined with other medications (MDedge, mdedge.com/familymedicine)
- General context: BC/Goody's/Stanback powders originated in and remain associated with the American South; broader analgesic-nephropathy literature (historically linked to phenacetin-containing combination analgesics) provides background context for chronic combination-analgesic-powder caution.
