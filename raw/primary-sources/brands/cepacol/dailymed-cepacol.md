## Active Ingredient(s) — varies by SKU
- Extra Strength Sore Throat and Cough: Benzocaine 7.5mg + Dextromethorphan HBr 5mg per lozenge
- Extra Strength Sore Throat (Cherry / Honey Lemon): Benzocaine 15mg + Menthol per lozenge

## Purpose
Oral anesthetic (benzocaine) + cough suppressant (dextromethorphan, cough SKU only)

## Uses
Temporary relief of occasional minor sore throat pain and irritation; cough due to minor throat/bronchial irritation (cough SKU).

## Warnings
Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition requiring prompt treatment (reduces oxygen-carrying capacity of blood). Signs: pale/gray/blue skin, lips, nail beds; shortness of breath; fatigue; rapid heart rate. Stop use and seek medical attention immediately if these occur.

## Directions
Adults and children ≥5y: allow one lozenge to dissolve slowly in mouth; may repeat every 2 hours as needed or as directed by doctor/dentist. Children <5y: ask a doctor.

## Brand-specific notes
- Not all Cepacol SKUs share the same active ingredients — the brand spans plain benzocaine/menthol sore-throat lozenges and a benzocaine+dextromethorphan combination cough-and-sore-throat product.
- The Cosmetic Mouthwash/Oral Rinse Cepacol SKU (separate Pharmacy Times category, 12% share) was not pulled in this pass — historically cetylpyridinium-chloride-based but not independently confirmed here.
