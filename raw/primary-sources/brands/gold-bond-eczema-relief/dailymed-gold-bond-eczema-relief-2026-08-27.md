## Gold Bond Medicated Eczema Relief — full Drug Facts panel
**Active Ingredient:** Colloidal oatmeal 2%
**Purpose:** Skin protectant
**Uses:** Temporarily protects and helps relieve minor skin irritation and itching due to eczema and rashes.
**Warnings:** External use only. Avoid eye contact. Discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur within several days. Keep away from children; seek immediate medical attention if ingested.
**Directions:** Apply to affected areas of skin as needed.
NDC: 84714-0567-1, 5.5 oz (155 g) tube. Marketing start date: 2014-12-22.
