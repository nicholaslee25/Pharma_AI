# DailyMed — Lip Clear Lysine Plus, Ointment

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=21a5b4fb-baed-4766-9820-5c6051c14720
**Manufacturer:** Quantum, Inc. (Quantum Health)

## Active Ingredient
Menthol 0.1% (per DailyMed product title: "menthol, unspecified form ointment").

## Formulation Notes
**Discrepancy:** despite the "Lysine+" brand name and marketing emphasis on L-lysine, lysine is NOT the FDA-recognized active ingredient — it appears only among inactive ingredients, alongside Olea Europaea (Olive) Fruit Oil, beeswax, water, Calendula Officinalis Extract, Echinacea Purpurea Extract, Zinc Oxide, and Propolis Extract. Per third-party reporting (GoodRx), this product is not FDA-approved specifically for cold sore treatment the way Abreva's docosanol is; menthol here functions as a generically-approved external analgesic under the OTC monograph.
