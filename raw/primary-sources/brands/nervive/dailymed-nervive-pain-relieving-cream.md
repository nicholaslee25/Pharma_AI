# Nervive Pain Relieving Cream - DailyMed Drug Facts

Source: https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=1e8bfb1e-8f5a-d0f3-e063-6294a90a752a
Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Active Ingredients
- Lidocaine HCl 4% - Topical anesthetic
- Menthol 1% - Topical analgesic

## Purpose
Temporarily relieves minor pain

## Warnings
For external use only. Flammable; keep away from heat and open flame.
Do not use: if allergic to lidocaine or other local anesthetics; on large areas, cuts, irritated, blistered, or swollen skin; on puncture wounds; for more than one week without consulting a doctor.
When using: use only as directed; avoid eyes and mucous membranes; do not apply to wounds or damaged skin; do not bandage tightly or apply heat/medicated patches; a transient burning sensation may occur upon application; avoid skin folds.
Stop use and ask a doctor if: condition worsens or severe burning/rash develops; symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur; signs of skin injury appear (pain, swelling, blistering).
If pregnant or breast-feeding: consult health professional.
Keep out of reach of children and pets; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Adults and children 12+: apply thin layer every 6-8 hours. Maximum 3 applications per 24 hours. Massage until absorbed. Wash hands after applying. Children under 12: consult doctor.
Storage: keep below 25°C (77°F)

## Related source: Nervive Nerve Relief (oral supplement, separate product)
Per manufacturer (pgsciencebehind.com/en-ca/nervive/painrelief): active ingredient alpha lipoic acid 600mg per tablet, plus B-vitamins (B1/B6/B12), turmeric, ginger. Dosing: 3 tablets/day for first 7 days, then 1 tablet/day. Not FDA-evaluated (dietary supplement, DSHEA framework / Health Canada NPN in Canada).
Cited clinical study: Ziegler D et al., "Oral Treatment With alpha-Lipoic Acid Improves Symptomatic Diabetic Polyneuropathy: The SYDNEY 2 trial," Diabetes Care 2006;29(11):2365-2370.
