## Active Ingredient
Oxymetazoline hydrochloride 0.05% (all three variants checked)

## Brand-specific notes
"Severe" and "VapoCOOL" naming reflects added formulation ingredients (moisturizers, menthol/cooling agents) rather than a different or higher oxymetazoline dose. Identical active ingredient/concentration to Afrin, Dristan, and Mucinex Nasal Spray.
