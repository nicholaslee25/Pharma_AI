# Hawaiian Tropic — Regulatory Status and After-Sun Notes

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## DailyMed search results
Direct search for "Hawaiian Tropic" + "lidocaine" on DailyMed returned ONLY sunscreen products (avobenzone, homosalate, octisalate, octocrylene combinations across lotion/spray/stick/oil formats; also an octocrylene/oxybenzone oil SKU). **No Hawaiian Tropic after-sun or lidocaine-containing product appears in DailyMed.**

## "Burn Relief Gel with Lidocaine, Cool Aloe I.C.E." — claimed but unconfirmed
Retail listings (Instacart) advertise a "Hawaiian Tropic Burn Relief Gel with Lidocaine, Cool Aloe I.C.E." product, and a secondary ingredient-explainer source claims "Active Ingredient: Lidocaine (0.8%)" for a related "After Sun Aloe Gel" product, with an ingredient list also including menthol — but listed there as an INACTIVE ingredient alongside allantoin (comfrey root-derived), vitamin A/E, and various botanical extracts. **Neither the lidocaine claim nor the exact formulation could be independently confirmed against an actual DailyMed/FDA Drug Facts label in this pass.** This is flagged as an unverified retail/marketing claim, not confirmed primary-source data — it may reflect a real product not yet indexed in DailyMed's database (which is not fully comprehensive of every private-label/regional OTC SKU), or a marketing claim not matching an actual FDA-registered drug listing. Treated honestly as unresolved rather than assumed either way.

## Sunscreen line — confirmed via DailyMed
Multiple Hawaiian Tropic sunscreen SKUs confirmed: avobenzone + homosalate + octisalate + octocrylene (lotion, spray, aerosol, stick formats), and a separate octocrylene + oxybenzone oil SKU, plus a "Sensitive Skin" lotion sunscreen (avobenzone + octocrylene + oxybenzone spray).

## Category-placement note
Same pattern as [[Banana Boat]]: Hawaiian Tropic's DailyMed-confirmed identity is entirely as a sunscreen (prevention) brand. Its "Sun Burn Relief" (treatment) category share is most plausibly attributable to an after-sun/aloe gel product line whose exact active-ingredient content could not be independently confirmed against a primary FDA source in this pass.
