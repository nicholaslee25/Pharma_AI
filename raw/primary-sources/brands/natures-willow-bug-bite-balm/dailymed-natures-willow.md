# DailyMed — Nature's Willow Bug Bite Balm, FDA Drug Facts

**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=b9fab18f-32c7-d5bd-e053-2995a90a6d0f
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** WB Ventures LLC

## Active Ingredient & Strength
Menthol 1% (10mg/1g) — External Analgesic

## Uses
Temporary relief of pain and itching associated with insect bites and minor skin irritations.

## Warnings
External use only; avoid eyes; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur shortly after clearing; pregnant/nursing individuals should seek professional medical advice before use; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Adults/children 2+: apply to affected area not more than 3-4 times daily. Children under 2: consult a physician.

## Inactive Ingredients (per label)
Almond Oil, **Camphor**, Caprylyl Glycol, Ceteareth-20, Cetearyl Alcohol, Cetyl Alcohol, Eucalyptus Oil, Geranium Oil, Glyceryl Stearate SE, Helichrysum Gymnocephalum Oil, Isopropyl Palmitate, Jojoba Seed Extract, Lavender Oil, Lemongrass Oil, Palmitic Acid, Phenoxyethanol, Propanediol, Sorbic Acid, Stearic Acid, Stearyl Alcohol, Sunflower Oil, Triethanolamine, Water, and **White Willow Bark Extract**.

## Note — brand-name/active-ingredient mismatch
Despite the brand name "Nature's Willow," white willow bark extract (the plant source of salicin, a natural precursor to salicylic acid) is listed as an INACTIVE ingredient on the FDA label — the only FDA-recognized active ingredient providing the labeled pain/itch relief is menthol 1%. Camphor is also present but likewise listed as inactive (contrast with BiteMD, where camphor 3% is an active ingredient at higher concentration). A search for willow-bark-extract clinical evidence found real dermatologic research (PMID 20883292, topical salicin serum for skin aging — wrinkles, radiance) but this concerns anti-aging/exfoliation, not insect-bite itch/pain relief, and is not the product tested here regardless.
