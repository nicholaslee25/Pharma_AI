# DailyMed — Blistex (multiple SKUs)

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Labeler:** Blistex Inc.

## Blistex Cold Sore Treatment (Docosanol Cream)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=bcdd51a9-210c-4d92-b67e-e3ac16c99812
**Active Ingredient:** Docosanol 10%.
**Uses:** Treats cold sores/fever blisters on the face or lips; shortens healing time and duration of symptoms (tingling, pain, burning, itching). Apply to the affected area at the first sign of a cold sore/fever blister (tingle); rub in gently but completely; use 5 times a day until healed.

## Blistex Cold Sore Fast-Acting Pain Relief (Glycerin and Lidocaine Gel)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c7511322-dd6c-441c-bf7d-ad4f0c14956a
**Active Ingredients:** Glycerin, Lidocaine.
**Uses:** Temporary relief of pain and itching associated with fever blisters/cold sores; relieves dryness and softens cold sores/fever blisters and crusts (scabs); temporarily protects chapped or cracked lips.

## Blistex Medicated (Lip Ointment)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e95439d3-f12b-4c1c-999a-79f2ddba4cfb
**Active Ingredients:** Dimethicone, Camphor (synthetic), Menthol, Phenol.
**Uses:** Temporary relief of pain and itching associated with minor lip irritation or cold sores; temporarily protects and helps relieve chapped/cracked lips.

## Blistex Cold and Allergy Lip Soother
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5db6db29-f6eb-498f-8d80-76e9c36b8874
**Active Ingredients:** Dimethicone, Pramoxine Hydrochloride.
**Uses:** Relieves lip discomfort and dryness due to illness or medication; infused with Vitamins C&E, Green Tea, Elderberry, Chamomile, Honey.

**Summary:** four different formulations/mechanisms under one brand name — docosanol (antiviral), lidocaine (anesthetic), camphor/menthol/phenol (counterirritant/protectant), and dimethicone/pramoxine (protectant/anesthetic). See `drugs/Blistex.md`, [[docosanol]], [[topical-anesthetics]].
