# Excedrin Family — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed) + Migraine Trial

## DailyMed — Excedrin Extra Strength / Excedrin Tension Headache (kit) / Excedrin Migraine / Excedrin Migraine Relief
All confirmed: Acetaminophen 250 mg, Aspirin 250 mg (NSAID), Caffeine 65 mg per caplet.
Warnings: Contains an NSAID (aspirin) — may cause severe stomach bleeding. Severe liver damage may occur with more than 8 caplets in 24 hours, with other acetaminophen-containing products, or with 3+ alcoholic drinks/day. The recommended dose contains about as much caffeine as a cup of coffee.

## DailyMed — Excedrin PM Triple Action Caplets
Different formulation, noted for contrast: Acetaminophen + Aspirin (NSAID) + Diphenhydramine citrate 38 mg (caffeine-free, sleep-aid variant — not part of this Headache Products research pass).

## Excedrin Migraine — FDA approval and trial basis
FDA approved the acetaminophen/aspirin/caffeine combination in 1998 as an acute treatment for mild-to-moderate migraine — the only OTC product with an FDA-approved migraine indication specifically.

**Lipton et al. 1998, Archives of Neurology, PMID 9482363** — "Efficacy and safety of acetaminophen, aspirin, and caffeine in alleviating migraine headache pain: three double-blind, randomized, placebo-controlled trials." Migraineurs meeting IHS diagnostic criteria (with or without aura), moderate/severe headache pain. Significant differences favoring the AAC combination over placebo found in all three trials from approximately 1-6 hours postdose, for headache pain and for associated nausea, photophobia, phonophobia, and functional disability. Conclusion: combination "highly effective," "excellent safety profile," "well tolerated."

Note: a claim that this combination is "significantly more effective than sumatriptan" circulates in secondary/marketing sources (doctronic.ai, migrainetrail.com) referencing a separate trial (NCT01248468, "Efficacy and Safety of Acetaminophen, Aspirin and Caffeine With Sumatriptan in the Acute Treatment of Migraine") — this specific comparative claim was NOT independently verified against the primary trial data in this pass and should be treated as unconfirmed.
