# DIABETIC TUSSIN DM — Drug Facts Label (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=e70bcb6c-6ffb-474f-b388-7a6c8cc8c146
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredients (per 5 mL)
- Dextromethorphan HBr 10 mg — Cough Suppressant
- Guaifenesin 100 mg — Expectorant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves cough; helps loosen phlegm (mucus) and thin bronchial secretions to rid bronchial passageways of bothersome mucus.

## Warnings
Do not use if currently taking an MAOI prescription drug for depression, psychiatric conditions, or Parkinson's disease, or within two weeks of stopping such medication.
Ask a doctor before use if you have a cough with excessive phlegm or a chronic cough associated with smoking, asthma, chronic bronchitis, or emphysema.
Stop use and ask a doctor if cough lasts more than 7 days, comes back, or occurs with fever, rash, or headache that lasts.
Pregnancy/breastfeeding: consult a health professional before use.
Overdose: get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions
Take every 4 hours; do not exceed 6 doses in 24 hours.
- Adults & children 12+ years: 10 mL (2 teaspoonfuls)
- Children 6–under 12 years: 5 mL (1 teaspoonful)
- Children 2–under 6 years: 2.5 mL (½ teaspoonful)
- Children under 2 years: ask a doctor

## Inactive Ingredients
Acesulfame potassium, artificial cherry flavor, artificial vanilla flavor, aspartame, hypromellose, menthol, methylparaben, potassium sorbate, purified water, citric acid (pH adjustment).

## Note on brand line
Diabetic Tussin is a multi-SKU line (per DailyMed search): DM (dextromethorphan+guaifenesin, above), DM Maximum Strength, Expectorant (guaifenesin only, 100mg/5mL), Sore Throat and Cough Cherry lozenges (benzocaine+dextromethorphan), Sore Throat Cherry spray (phenol), and Nighttime Cold and Flu (acetaminophen+dextromethorphan+diphenhydramine). All marketed as sugar/alcohol-free ("safe for diabetics" per product positioning — a formulation/excipient claim, not a pharmacological one).
