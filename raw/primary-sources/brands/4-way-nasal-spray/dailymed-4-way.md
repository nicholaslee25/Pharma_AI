## Active Ingredient
Phenylephrine hydrochloride 1%

## Purpose
Nasal decongestant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves nasal congestion due to common cold, hay fever, and upper respiratory allergies.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 2-3 sprays each nostril, not more often than every 4 hours.

## Brand-specific notes
Same topical-phenylephrine route distinction as Neo-Synephrine — does not fall under the oral-phenylephrine GRASE controversy. No dedicated nasal-phenylephrine ingredient page exists in this database.
