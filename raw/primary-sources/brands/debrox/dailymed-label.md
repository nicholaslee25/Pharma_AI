## Active Ingredient
Carbamide peroxide 6.5% (non-USP)

## Purpose
Earwax removal aid — occasional use to soften, loosen, and remove excessive earwax.

## Directions
Adults and children over 12: 5-10 drops into ear, keep in ear several minutes (use a cotton plug if desired); use twice daily for up to 4 days if needed, or as directed by a doctor. Children under 12: consult a doctor.
