## Active Ingredients (homeopathic combination)
Allium cepa 3C, Apis mellifica 6C, Belladonna 6C, Eupatorium perfoliatum 3C, Gelsemium sempervirens 6C, Kali bichromicum 6C, Nux vomica 3C, Phytolacca decandra 6C, Pulsatilla 6C

## Inactive Ingredients
Croscarmellose sodium, lactose, magnesium stearate.

## Uses
Temporarily relieves cold symptoms: sneezing, runny nose, sinus and nasal congestion, minor sore throat.

## Warnings
Standard FDA homeopathic disclaimer: not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy; FDA is not aware of scientific evidence supporting homeopathy as effective.

## Brand-specific notes
Nine-ingredient homeopathic combination at C-scale (1:100) dilutions — less extreme than Oscillococcinum's 200CK dilution, though this alone does not establish clinical efficacy.
