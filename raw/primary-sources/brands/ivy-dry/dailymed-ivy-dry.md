# Ivy-Dry Product Line - DailyMed Drug Facts

Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Ivy Dry Cream
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=5fe81556-5b5f-4765-9136-796e4974aed0&type=display
Active ingredients: Benzyl Alcohol 10%, Camphor 0.6%, Menthol 0.4%
Purpose: External Analgesic
Uses: Temporary relief of itching associated with insect bites and minor skin irritations.
Directions: External use only; apply to affected areas no more than 3 times daily; not recommended for children under 2 years without physician approval; should not be used on face, genital areas, or blistered/broken skin.

## Ivy-Dry Super (spray)
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=22014fde-2648-4514-99c5-668481fa12c1&type=display
Active ingredients: Benzyl Alcohol 10%, Camphor 0.5%, Menthol 0.25%
Purpose: External Analgesic
Uses: Temporary relief from itching related to insect bites and minor skin irritations.
Warnings: Do not use on face, genital areas, or broken/blistered skin; avoid eye contact; not for children under 6 years; stop use if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days; consult physician if pregnant/nursing.
Directions: Apply to affected area maximum 3 times daily; test on small skin patch before full-body application.
