## Active Ingredients
Capsule: Acetaminophen 325mg + Dextromethorphan HBr 10mg + Phenylephrine HCl 5mg. A related caplet SKU adds Guaifenesin 200mg.

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer, cough suppressant, nasal decongestant, ± expectorant (caplet SKU).

## Brand-specific notes
Part of a larger Tylenol Cold + Flu family that includes Severe, Severe Day/Night combo packs, and other variants with different ingredient sets (including doxylamine in some night/severe SKUs) — always verify the exact product name against its own label.
