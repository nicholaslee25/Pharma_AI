## Drug Facts
**Active Ingredient:** Trolamine salicylate 10%
**Purpose:** Topical analgesic
**Uses:** Temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints (arthritis, backache, strains, sprains, bruises).
**Warnings:** Allergy alert (aspirin/salicylate sensitivity, consult doctor); do not bandage tightly, use with heating pad, or apply to wounds/damaged skin; avoid eyes/mucous membranes; pregnant/nursing ask health professional; discontinue if worsens/persists beyond 7 days/redness or irritation develops. California Prop 65-style trace diethanolamine exposure warning.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Acrylates/C10-30 alkyl acrylate crosspolymer, allantoin, aloe barbadensis leaf juice, cetyl alcohol, dimethicone, disodium EDTA, emu oil, ethylhexylglycerin, FD&C Blue#1, glucosamine HCl, glycerin, glyceryl stearate/PEG-100 stearate, methylsulfonylmethane (MSM), mineral oil, panthenol, phenoxyethanol, polysorbate 80, stearic acid, tocopheryl acetate, triethanolamine, water.

## NOT homeopathic
This is a conventional FDA OTC monograph drug (trolamine salicylate) with no HPUS labeling, decimal/centesimal dilution notation, or homeopathic FDA disclaimer — genuinely different regulatory category from the other three brands (Boiron Arnicare, Theraworx Relief, MagniLife Arnica Gel) in Pharmacy Times' "Homeopathic Topical Analgesics" category (25% share).
