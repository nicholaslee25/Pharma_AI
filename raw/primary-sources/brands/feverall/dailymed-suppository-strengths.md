# FeverAll — DailyMed, Suppository Strengths

- FeverAll Infants: acetaminophen 80mg suppository
- FeverAll Children's: acetaminophen 120mg suppository
- FeverAll Jr. Strength: acetaminophen 325mg suppository

Warnings: severe liver damage may occur if more than 4 doses/24h given for ages 6-11 months, more than 5 doses/24h for ages 12-36 months (Infants strength); more than 5 doses/24h for Children's strength.
