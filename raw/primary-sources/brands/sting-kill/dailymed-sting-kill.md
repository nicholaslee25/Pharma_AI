# DailyMed — Sting-Kill Swabs, FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredients
- Benzocaine USP 20% — topical anesthetic, pain relief
- Menthol USP 1% — antipruritic, anti-itch

## Uses
Temporarily relieves pain and itching of bee stings, insect bites, and minor skin irritations.

## Warnings
External use only; avoid eyes; not for children under 2 without a doctor; seek immediate medical attention for signs of insect-venom sensitivity/serious allergic reaction; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or persists beyond 1 week; if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control immediately.

## Directions
Remove bee stinger first if applicable. Single-use swab: crack the vial to saturate the applicator, apply directly to affected area immediately, dispose after use. Adults/children 2+: not more than 3-4 times a day.
