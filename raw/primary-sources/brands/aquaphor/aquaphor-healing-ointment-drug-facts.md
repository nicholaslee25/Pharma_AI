# AQUAPHOR HEALING OINTMENT — Drug Facts Label

Sources: Kaiser Permanente drug encyclopedia entry ("Aquaphor Healing 41% topical ointment"); manufacturer ingredient disclosure
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
- White Petrolatum 41% — Skin protectant

## Inactive Ingredients (selected)
Panthenol, glycerin, mineral oil, ceresin, bisabolol, lanolin alcohol. Free of added fragrance, preservatives, and dyes per manufacturer.

## Uses
Temporarily protects and helps relieve chapped or cracked skin; helps protect minor cuts, scrapes, and burns; helps protect and help relieve chapped, cracked skin.

## Note
Aquaphor is registered as a topical OTC skin-protectant drug (confirmed via a clinical drug-encyclopedia entry, not a direct DailyMed pull in this pass), at a higher petrolatum concentration (41%) than plain white petrolatum products. It is a petrolatum-containing COMBINATION product (added panthenol, lanolin, bisabolol, mineral oil) — not pure white petrolatum — so the pivotal Smack et al. 1996 JAMA RCT on plain petrolatum (see ingredients/petrolatum.md) was not conducted on this exact formulation.
