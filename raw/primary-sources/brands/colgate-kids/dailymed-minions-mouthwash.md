# Colgate Kids Minions Mouthwash — DailyMed

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=c4a050a6-601e-467b-a0b0-5b21035bdb07&type=display

Active Ingredient: Sodium fluoride 0.02% (0.01% w/v fluoride ion)
Purpose: Anticavity
Use: Aids in the prevention of dental cavities
Warnings: Keep away from children; if more than used for rinsing is accidentally swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
Directions: Ages 6+: rinse twice daily with 10mL for one minute after brushing, spit out. Do not swallow. Avoid eating/drinking for 30 minutes after use. Supervise children under 12. Under 6: consult a dentist or doctor.

Inactive ingredients include: water, glycerin, propylene glycol, sorbitol, poloxamer 407, poloxamer 338, flavor, PEG-40 hydrogenated castor oil, phosphoric acid, sodium saccharin, disodium phosphate, potassium sorbate, cetylpyridinium chloride, sucralose, colorants. Cetylpyridinium chloride confirmed as an INACTIVE ingredient in this formulation despite its real antimicrobial properties.
