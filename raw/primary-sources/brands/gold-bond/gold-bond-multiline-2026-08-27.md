## Key discrepancy
Secondary source (biologyinsights.com) explicitly states: "The classic Gold Bond Medicated Powder contains menthol as its active ingredient, not an antifungal agent... does not have antifungal properties." True antifungals (clotrimazole, miconazole, terbinafine, tolnaftate) are absent from the flagship powder. Confirmed Gold Bond DOES separately sell a genuine miconazole nitrate 2% antifungal spray — so the brand spans both a real antifungal drug and a non-antifungal comfort product within the same Jock Itch/Antifungal Products category.
