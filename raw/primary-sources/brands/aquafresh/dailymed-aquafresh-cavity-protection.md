# Aquafresh Cavity Protection — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=5484999c-8e6f-430d-99a5-6893a2489467

**Active Ingredient:** Sodium fluoride 0.25% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion)

**Purpose:** Anticavity agent

**Use:** Aids in the prevention of dental cavities through twice-daily brushing

**Warnings:** Discontinue use if irritation develops. Keep away from children under 6 years of age. Contact Poison Control or seek medical assistance if more than the brushing amount is swallowed.

**Directions:** Individuals aged 2 and older: brush teeth after meals or minimum twice daily (maximum three times daily), or as dentist recommends. Minimize swallowing, spit out after brushing. Children under 6: use pea-sized amounts with supervised brushing. Children under 2: consult a dentist.
