## Sambucol Cold and Flu Relief — Active Ingredients (homeopathic, per DailyMed)
Bryonia 6X, Gelsemium Sempervirens 6X/12X/30X, Sambucus Nigra 3X/6X, Sulphur 12X, Zincum Gluconicum 2X/6X (orally disintegrating tablet)

## Purpose / Uses (per label, traditional homeopathic indications)
Bryonia: muscle ache, headache, dry throat, cough. Gelsemium Sempervirens: sneezing. Sambucus Nigra: nasal congestion and coughing. Sulphur: nasal congestion and pressure, headache. Zincum Gluconicum: fatigue, nausea, minor sore throat.

## Warnings
Components are officially monographed in the Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States (HPUS); claims based on traditional homeopathic practice, not accepted medical evidence. Product not evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy.

## Inactive Ingredients
Black Carrot Extract (colorant), Croscarmellose Sodium, Elderberry Flavor, Lactose, Magnesium Stearate, Maltodextrin, Microcrystalline Cellulose, Sucrose.

## Sambucol Black Elderberry Original Syrup — Ingredients (dietary supplement, manufacturer listing, not an FDA drug label)
Elderberry Extract (16,720mg black elderberries per max serving), Glucose Syrup, Purified Water, Citric Acid, Potassium Sorbate. Marketed gluten-free, vegan, dairy/soy/nut-free. Standard supplement disclaimer: "not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease."

## Brand-specific notes
- These are genuinely two different products under one brand name with very different evidence implications: the syrup is a real concentrated-extract dietary supplement (the kind studied in Hawkins 2019 and Wieland 2021 RCTs on the elderberry ingredient page); the "Cold and Flu Relief" tablet is an actual homeopathic combination remedy where elderberry is a minor, highly-diluted component among four other unrelated homeopathic ingredients.
