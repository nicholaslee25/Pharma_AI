## Active Ingredients (per 5mL)
Dextromethorphan HBr 10mg (cough suppressant), Guaifenesin 100mg (expectorant)

## Free-from claims (per label/marketing)
No decongestants, acetaminophen, sugar, alcohol, sorbitol, fructose, gluten, or dyes. Marketed as safe for adults with high blood pressure and diabetes.

## Warnings
Do not use with a prescription MAOI or within 2 weeks of stopping one; ask a doctor/pharmacist if unsure whether a prescription drug contains an MAOI.

## Brand-specific notes
This free-from formulation addresses both hypertension (no decongestant) and diabetes (no sugar) simultaneously — broader than single-axis competitors in this batch (Coricidin HBP: decongestant-free only; some Robitussin variants: sugar-free only).
