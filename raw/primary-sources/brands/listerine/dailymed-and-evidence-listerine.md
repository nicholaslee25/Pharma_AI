# DailyMed + evidence sourcing — Listerine

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Listerine Cool Mint / Original Antiseptic
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=edb9e374-4dba-5823-e053-2a95a90a3da5
**Active Ingredients:** Eucalyptol, Menthol, Methyl Salicylate 0.060%, Thymol.
**Purpose:** Antiseptic / Antigingivitis / Antiplaque.

## Listerine Total Care Zero Alcohol Anticavity Mouthwash Fresh Mint
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=4b594a6e-db1d-443b-bfa7-23ee15749f47
**Active Ingredients:** Eucalyptol, Methyl Salicylate, Thymol, Menthol (plus fluoride for anticavity claim, alcohol-free).

## Evidence
Meta-analysis (JADA, published research referenced via search) of essential-oil-containing mouthrinse: 32 long-term RCTs, 5,000+ gingivitis participants — clinically significant additional benefit in plaque/gingivitis reduction vs. brushing/flossing alone. A 6-month study found essential-oil rinse outperformed both a CPC rinse and placebo at all post-baseline timepoints.
