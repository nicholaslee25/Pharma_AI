# MENTHOLATUM CHILDRENS NIGHTTIME VAPORIZING RUB - camphor, eucalyptus oil, menthol ointment

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e18c434b-6d55-44f9-b500-c3a27910f7e3

## Active Ingredients & Concentrations
- Camphor 5.3% (53 mg per 1g) — Cough suppressant / topical analgesic
- Eucalyptus oil 1.3% (13 mg per 1g) — Cough suppressant
- Menthol 2.8% (28 mg per 1g) — Cough suppressant / topical analgesic

## Uses
Temporarily relieves cough due to minor throat and bronchial irritation associated with a cold; helps with cough-related sleep disruption; eases minor muscle and joint aches.

## Warnings
- Children under 2 years: ask a doctor
- External use only
- Do not heat, microwave, or add to hot water
- Do not take by mouth or place in nostrils
- Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin
- Keep out of reach of children; contact Poison Control if swallowed

## Directions
Ages 2+: rub a thick layer on throat and chest for cough, or apply to sore muscles/joints. Use up to 3 times daily; may cover with warm cloth.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carthamus Tinctorius (Safflower) Seed Oil, Chamomilla Recutita (Matricaria) Flower, Fragrance, Lavandula Angustifolia (Lavender) Flower, Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Oil, White Petrolatum.
