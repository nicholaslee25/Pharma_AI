# Eosera Ear Pain MD — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: FDA.report/DailyMed listing (setid 6a36337c-8357-4585-b4e5-879ce2224a40); manufacturer site eosera.com
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
Lidocaine HCl monohydrate 4%

## Inactive Ingredients
Aloe barbadensis (aloe), benzalkonium chloride, benzyl alcohol, hypromellose (HPMC), water; may contain HCl and/or NaOH for pH adjustment

## Purpose
Topical analgesic — temporary relief of minor ear pain/discomfort. Not intended to treat underlying infection.

## Directions
Adults and children 2+: 2-3 drops in affected ear, up to 3-4 times daily. A "Ear Pain MD +PLUS" SKU adds an antibacterial component (infection-protection claim) not present in the base formula.
