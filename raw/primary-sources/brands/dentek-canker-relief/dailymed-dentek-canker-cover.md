# DailyMed — DenTek Canker Cover (marketed as DenTek Canker Relief Canker Sore Patches), Menthol Patch, Extended Release

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=dccdb4ef-203f-4c9b-85b4-a8a061c5656d
**Labeler:** DenTek Oral Care, Inc.

## Active Ingredient
Menthol 2.5 mg per patch — oral pain reliever.

## Purpose
Oral pain reliever.

## Uses
Relief of pain associated with canker sores and mouth sores.

## Directions
Adults and children 5 years of age and older: apply up to 3 times a day as needed. Place the patch on a clean, dry sore with the white side down and hold in place for 20 seconds. Within 30 minutes, the patch forms a clear, gel-like protective bandage over the sore before dissolving.

## Inactive Ingredients (per consumer-facing product description)
Citrus medica fruit, povidone, colloidal silicon dioxide, carbomer 934, xylitol, hydroxypropyl cellulose, sea salt, magnesium stearate.

**Note:** This is a materially different mechanism (menthol + physical adhesive-patch barrier) than the benzocaine-gel products dominating this category (Orajel, Anbesol, Kank-A) — no methemoglobinemia risk applies.
