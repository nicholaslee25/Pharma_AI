## Active Ingredients (per caplet)
Acetaminophen 500mg + Phenylephrine hydrochloride 5mg

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer + nasal decongestant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves common cold/flu symptoms: nasal congestion, headache, minor aches and pains, stuffy nose, sore throat, fever, sinus congestion and pressure.

## Brand-specific notes
A separate "Contac Cold Flu Day Night" kit (acetaminophen + phenylephrine + chlorpheniramine, day/night split) and "Contac Cold and Flu" capsule (acetaminophen + dextromethorphan + doxylamine) are distinct SKUs under the same brand family, not confirmed to be the exact product behind this database's single "Contac Cold + Flu Day" category entry (3% share) — though the "Day" tablet SKU found here (acetaminophen + phenylephrine only) is the most likely match given the exact name match.
