## Active Ingredient
Cromolyn sodium 5.2mg per spray (nasal solution, USP, original prescription strength)

## Purpose
Nasal allergy symptom controller (mast-cell stabilizer)

## Uses
Prevention and relief of nasal allergy symptoms: sneezing, runny nose, itchy nose.

## Ages
Safe for ages 2 and older.

## Brand-specific notes
Genuinely different mechanism from every other nasal spray brand in this database's Decongestants/Intranasal Allergy categories — not a vasoconstrictor (unlike oxymetazoline/phenylephrine products) and not a corticosteroid (unlike FLONASE). No rebound congestion risk since it isn't a vasoconstrictor. No dedicated ingredient page exists in this database for cromolyn sodium — a real research gap.
