# Domeboro - DailyMed Drug Facts

Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Domeboro Powder/Tablets (Burow's Solution)
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=28c39200-2f7c-954b-3ce7-7349b2738812
Active ingredients: Aluminum sulfate tetradecahydrate, Calcium acetate monohydrate (combine in water to form aluminum acetate solution)
Purpose: Astringent
Uses: Temporarily relieves minor skin irritations due to poison ivy, poison oak, poison sumac, insect bites, athlete's foot, and rashes caused by soaps, detergents, cosmetics, or jewelry.

## Domeboro Gel
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=cfd8fc64-79a4-4a49-b3eb-d40e644cde7b
Active ingredient: Aluminum acetate 0.5%
Purpose: Astringent
Directions: Apply liberally to the affected area as needed or as directed by a doctor, repeating three times a day or as directed by a doctor.

## Warnings (both formats)
For external use only; avoid contact with eyes. Stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist for more than 7 days.
