# Children's Tylenol + 2011 Acetaminophen Concentration Standardization

## DailyMed — Children's Tylenol
Active Ingredient: Acetaminophen 160mg/5mL oral suspension. Ages 2-11. Dose every 4 hours while symptoms last, max 5 doses/24h. Use only enclosed dosing cup.

## 2011 Standardization History
Pre-2011: "infant" concentrated drops (80mg/0.8mL) vs. "children's" liquid (160mg/5mL) — two different concentrations caused real dosing errors when caregivers/clinicians conflated them (up to 3-fold overdose or ~66% underdose risk from mix-ups). Following a 2009 FDA advisory committee recommendation, major manufacturers voluntarily standardized to 160mg/5mL for both infant and children's liquid acetaminophen beginning June 2011. Post-standardization, annual NPDS medication-error exposures for single-ingredient liquid acetaminophen fell from ~8,260/year (2007-2011) to ~6,669/year (2012-2016), a 19% reduction.
