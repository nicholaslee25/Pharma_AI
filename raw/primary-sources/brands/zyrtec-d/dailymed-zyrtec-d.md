## Active Ingredients (bilayer tablet)
Cetirizine HCl 5mg (immediate-release layer) + Pseudoephedrine HCl 120mg (extended-release layer)

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one tablet every 12 hours.

## Inactive Ingredients
Colloidal silicon dioxide, croscarmellose sodium, hypromellose, lactose monohydrate, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose.

## Brand-specific notes
Cetirizine dose here (5mg) is half the standalone Zyrtec dose (10mg) — a genuine, worth-noting design difference from the plain antihistamine product.
