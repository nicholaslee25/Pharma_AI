# DailyMed — After Bite (multiple SKUs), FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## After Bite Xtra — diphenhydramine HCl gel
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=af965c5c-072a-569f-e053-2995a90a017e
**Active Ingredient:** Diphenhydramine HCl 2% (20mg/1g)
**Purpose:** Topical Analgesic
**Uses:** Temporary relief of pain and itching from minor burns, cuts, scrapes, insect bites, and skin irritations/rashes from poison ivy/oak/sumac.
**Warnings:** External use only; do not apply to large body areas; do not use with other diphenhydramine products (oral or topical); ask a doctor before use on chicken pox or measles; avoid eyes; stop use and ask a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if ingested.
**Directions:** Adults/children 2+: apply to affected area up to 3-4 times daily. Children under 2: consult a doctor.

## After Bite Advanced — sodium bicarbonate liquid
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=223d1de8-7940-bbdc-e063-6294a90a51d8
**Active Ingredient:** Sodium bicarbonate
**Uses:** Temporarily protects and helps relieve minor skin irritation and itching due to insect bites, poison ivy, oak, and sumac.

## After Bite Wipe — ethyl alcohol / lidocaine HCl liquid
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=3fef81ee-6fe1-50a5-e054-00144ff88e88
**Active Ingredients:** Ethyl alcohol, lidocaine HCl
**Uses:** First aid to help prevent infection in minor scrapes; temporary relief of itching from insect bites.

## Original "After Bite" (the classic pen/roll-on) — ammonium hydroxide
Per secondary sources (Drugs.com listing "After Bite the Itch Eraser," manufacturer safety data sheet), the original/classic After Bite formula is ammonium hydroxide (~3.5%, ~35mg ammonia/mL), distinct from every DailyMed-confirmed SKU above. A DailyMed setid for this specific ammonium-hydroxide formulation was not located/confirmed directly in this pass — flagged as a secondary-source claim, not independently verified against a DailyMed label.

## Summary: at least FIVE distinct active-ingredient classes under one brand name
Ammonium hydroxide (classic, per secondary source only), sodium bicarbonate (Advanced, Kids, X Tech), diphenhydramine HCl (Xtra), lidocaine HCl (Wipe), and benzocaine (Wipe Insect Sting Relief) — the widest formulation spread identified for any single brand in this database so far.
