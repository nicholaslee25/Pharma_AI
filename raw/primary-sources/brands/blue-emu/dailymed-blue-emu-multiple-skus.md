# DailyMed — Blue-Emu (multiple SKUs), FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Blue-Emu Maximum Pain Relief Cream (and "Original Super Strength") — trolamine salicylate
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=c863351a-951b-4f7c-a9c0-4289d702054a&type=display
**Active Ingredient:** Trolamine Salicylate 10% — topical analgesic. A separate spray SKU ("Maximum Strength Blue Emu Pain Relief") also confirmed trolamine salicylate.
**Uses:** Temporary relief of minor aches from arthritis, backache, strains, sprains, bruises.
**Warnings:** Allergy alert — aspirin/salicylate sensitivity, consult a doctor before use; do not bandage tightly, use with heating pads, or apply to wounds/damaged skin; avoid eyes/mucous membranes; pregnant/nursing — ask a health professional before use; discontinue if condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond 7 days, or redness/irritation develops.
**Directions:** Adults/children over 12: apply generously, massage in, repeat up to 4x daily. 12 and under: consult a doctor.
**Notable INACTIVE ingredients:** Emu oil (the brand's namesake — listed as inactive, a carrier oil, NOT the FDA-recognized active ingredient), aloe vera leaf juice, glucosamine HCl, MSM, vitamin E, allantoin.
**California Prop 65-style warning:** product may expose users to trace diethanolamine levels.

## Blue-Emu Lidocaine Pain Relief Cream — separate SKU, different active ingredient
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=592811f2-e21c-6a58-e053-2991aa0a69a9 (PDF fetch was corrupted/unreadable in this pass; ingredient confirmed via DailyMed search-result title and summary, not the full label text)
**Active Ingredient:** Lidocaine HCl 4% — topical anesthetic, "temporarily relieve minor pain." Full warnings/directions text not independently re-extracted in this pass; standard lidocaine OTC label conventions (per [[topical-anesthetics]]) presumed to apply, not independently confirmed word-for-word for this specific SKU.

## Key finding — brand-name/active-ingredient mismatch
"Blue-Emu" is named for emu oil, marketed heavily around emu oil as a "carrier" that helps other ingredients absorb — but emu oil is NOT the FDA-recognized active ingredient in ANY confirmed Blue-Emu SKU. The Original/Maximum Pain Relief line's actual active ingredient is trolamine salicylate 10% (a different salicylate than methyl salicylate, also the active ingredient in Aspercreme's non-lidocaine line); the separate Lidocaine Pain Relief Cream's active ingredient is lidocaine 4%. This is the same "brand name touts an ingredient that is not the actual FDA active ingredient" pattern already flagged for [[Nature's Willow Bug Bite Balm]] (willow bark extract, also inactive).
