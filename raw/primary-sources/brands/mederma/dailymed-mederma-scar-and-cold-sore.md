# DailyMed / manufacturer sourcing — Mederma (multiple SKUs)

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Mederma Advanced Scar (Gel)
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=25bc1eae-3502-4c63-bd8a-43be2dfb0d6d
**Active Ingredient:** Allantoin 0.5% — skin protectant.
**Use:** Scar appearance/texture improvement (Scar Treatments category, 56% share — category leader).

## Mederma Scar Cream
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=f9be5adb-dce3-4b17-939f-d80d486f2cb0
**Active Ingredients:** Avobenzone, Octocrylene, Oxybenzone — sunscreen actives, for scar-area sun protection during healing (not part of the Advanced Scar gel's core mechanism).

## Mederma Cold Sore Discreet Patch
Per manufacturer marketing (Amazon product listing): a hydrocolloid gel patch that "relieves redness & swelling" and "conceals cold sores." **No DailyMed label was located for this product** — no confirmed active pharmaceutical ingredient was identified, consistent with this being a cosmetic/device product (a physical hydrocolloid wound covering, similar in concept to an acne "pimple patch") rather than an FDA OTC monograph drug.

---
**Summary:** the Scar Treatments-category product (allantoin) is a confirmed drug; the Cold Sore Treatments-category product (hydrocolloid patch) is most plausibly NOT a drug at all — a category-placement discrepancy. See `drugs/Mederma.md`.
