# Arm & Hammer Complete Care Whole Mouth Protection — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=d00f9d16-de0e-4ffc-e053-2a95a90a2069&type=display

**Active Ingredient:** Sodium fluoride 0.24% (0.15% w/v fluoride ion) (1.5 mg in 1 g)

**Purpose:** Anticavity toothpaste — aids in the prevention of dental decay

**Use:** Helps prevent cavities

**Warnings:** Do not use if irritation occurs and persists. Keep out of reach of children under 6 years of age. If more than used for brushing is swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

**Directions:** Do not swallow. Adults and children 2+: brush teeth thoroughly after meals or at least twice a day. Children under 6: instruct in proper brushing/rinsing to minimize swallowing. Children under 2: consult a dentist or physician. Rinse away toothpaste residue thoroughly after brushing.

**Sodium Bicarbonate Status:** Sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) appears as an INACTIVE ingredient in this formulation, not as the FDA-recognized active ingredient.
