# Arm & Hammer Sensitive Teeth and Gums — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=935172db-d78f-0c69-e053-2a95a90a0b69

**Active Ingredients:** Potassium Nitrate 5.0%; Sodium Fluoride 0.243%

**Mechanism (per label):** Potassium salts move along the dentinal tubules and, by blocking the action of the intradental nerve fibers, decrease the excitability of the tooth. Potassium salts generally require a period of use (e.g., 14 to 28 days) before their benefit is established.

**Directions:** Brush teeth for at least one minute, preferably after each meal or at least twice a day (morning and evening), or as recommended by a dentist or physician; brush all sensitive areas of the teeth.

**Warnings:** Do not use longer than 4 weeks unless recommended by a dentist or physician. Sensitive teeth may indicate a serious problem that may need prompt care by a dentist.
