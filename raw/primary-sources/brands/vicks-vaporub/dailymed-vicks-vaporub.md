## Active Ingredients
Camphor (synthetic) 4.8%, Eucalyptus oil 1.2%, Menthol 2.63%

## Purpose
Cough suppressant, topical analgesic

## Brand-specific notes
No dedicated ingredient page exists in this database for camphor or this three-ingredient topical combination. Children's VapoRub uses the same active-ingredient concentrations per label. Mentholatum's "Vaporizing Rub" line uses a very similar formula (camphor 5.3%/eucalyptus 1.3%/menthol 2.8%).
