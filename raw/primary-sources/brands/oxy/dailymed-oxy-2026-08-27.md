### OXY Advanced Care Maximum Strength 3-in-1 Acne Pads — full Drug Facts panel
(setid=99874117-b1f5-48bf-a1b1-3a2af9d9ae27)

**Active Ingredient:** Salicylic acid 2%
**Purpose:** Acne treatment
**Uses:** Treats existing acne pimples and helps skin heal; penetrates pores to control blackheads & whiteheads; helps prevent new acne lesions from developing
**Warnings:** For external use only. Keep away from eyes, lips, and mouth; flush with water if contact occurs. Skin irritation and dryness is more likely to occur if you use another topical acne medication at the same time. Do not leave pad on skin. Pregnant or nursing: ask a doctor before use. Keep out of reach of children; if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control right away.
**Directions:** Use wipe to cleanse skin thoroughly. Cover the entire affected area with a thin layer one to three times daily. Because too much of this medication can cause excessive skin irritation, start with one application daily, then gradually increase to two or three times daily if needed or as directed by a doctor. If bothersome dryness or peeling occurs, reduce application to once a day or every other day.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Water, alcohol, PEG-8/SMDI copolymer, isoceteth-20, allantoin, capryloyl glycine, fragrance, jojoba wax PEG-120 esters, phytic acid, triethanolamine, trisodium EDTA
