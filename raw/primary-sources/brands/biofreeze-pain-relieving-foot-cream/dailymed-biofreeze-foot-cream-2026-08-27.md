## Drug Facts
**Active Ingredient:** Menthol USP 10%
**Purpose:** Topical analgesic / counterirritant
**Uses:** Temporary relief from minor aches and pains of sore muscles and joints associated with arthritis, backache, strains, and sprains.
**Directions:** Adults and children 12+: rub a thin film over affected area not more than 4 times daily; massage not necessary. Children under 12: consult a physician.
**Warnings:** Avoid contact with eyes or mucous membranes; do not apply to wounds or damaged skin; do not use with heating pads or other ointments/creams/sprays/liniments.
