# Tylenol 8 HR Arthritis Pain — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: DailyMed setid=3af22fc8-e4e0-4863-8696-6bc1257047cb

Active Ingredient: Acetaminophen 650 mg, extended-release, bi-layer tablet (capsule-shaped)
Purpose: Pain reliever/fever reducer
Use: Temporary relief of minor arthritis pain
Warnings: Severe liver damage may occur if more than 6 caplets are taken in 24 hours (labeled maximum); acetaminophen may cause severe skin reactions; in case of overdose, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.
