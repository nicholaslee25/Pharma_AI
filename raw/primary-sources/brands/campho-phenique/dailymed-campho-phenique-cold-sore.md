## Active Ingredients
Camphor (synthetic), Phenol.

## Formulation Notes
A counterirritant/local-antiseptic combination — no antiviral or anesthetic-class active ingredient beyond phenol's own mild local-anesthetic property. A separate "Campho-Phenique Antiseptic" liquid/gel line uses the same camphor+phenol combination for general first-aid antiseptic use (minor cuts, scrapes, burns) rather than cold-sore-specific marketing.
