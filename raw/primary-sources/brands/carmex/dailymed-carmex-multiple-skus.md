# DailyMed / FDA.report — Carmex (multiple SKUs)

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Carma Laboratories, Inc.

## Carmex Classic Lip Balm Medicated
**Active Ingredients:** Camphor 1.70%, White Petrolatum 45.30%.
**Inactive ingredients (per manufacturer):** lanolin, cetyl esters, theobroma cacao seed butter, paraffin, beeswax, menthol, salicylic acid, phenol, vanillin, flavor.
**Purpose:** Skin protectant / mild counterirritant for chapped/cracked lips.

## Carmex Multi-Symptom 3-in-1 Cold Sore Treatment
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=bbf0abcd-b7d6-b5e4-e053-2995a90a5240
**Active Ingredients:** Benzocaine (external analgesic), White Petrolatum 44.69% (skin protectant).
**Purpose:** Temporary pain relief plus protective barrier for cold sores/fever blisters.

**Summary:** two different active-ingredient sets under one brand — the Classic Lip Balm (camphor+petrolatum, no true anesthetic, no benzocaine methemoglobinemia risk) vs. the dedicated Cold Sore Treatment (benzocaine+petrolatum, full anesthetic + methemoglobinemia risk profile per [[topical-anesthetics]]).
