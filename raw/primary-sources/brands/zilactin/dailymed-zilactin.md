# DailyMed — Zilactin (Early Relief Cold Sore Gel / original bioadhesive gel)

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Sources:**
- https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=42ef8bfa-c4d9-01cf-e063-6394a90ac88a ("Zilactin Early Relief Cold Sore" — benzyl alcohol gel)
- FDA SPL data (accessdata.fda.gov) for the original Zilactin bioadhesive gel formula

## Active Ingredient
Benzyl alcohol 10%.

## Uses
Temporarily relieves pain from cold sores, fever blisters, canker sores, mouth sores, and gum irritations, via a bioadhesive film that also provides a protective barrier (manufacturer-stated up to 6 hours of relief per application).

## Inactive Ingredients
Boric Acid, Hydroxypropylcellulose, Propylene Glycol, Purified Water, Salicylic Acid, SD Alcohol 37, Tannic Acid.

## Warnings (per label)
Do not use for more than 7 days unless directed by a physician or dentist. Avoid contact with the eyes. Seek medical attention if symptoms do not improve within 7 days or if swelling, rash, or fever develops.

**Note:** a separate sibling product, Zilactin-B, uses benzocaine gel instead — a materially different active ingredient/anesthetic class marketed for canker/mouth sores. See `drugs/Zilactin.md`.
