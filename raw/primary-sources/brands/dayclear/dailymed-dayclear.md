## Active Ingredients (per 30mL dose)
Choline Salicylate 870mg (pain reliever/fever reducer), Dextromethorphan HBr 20mg (cough suppressant), Guaifenesin 200mg (expectorant), Phenylephrine Hydrochloride 10mg (nasal decongestant)

## Uses
Temporarily relieves cough, nasal congestion, minor aches and pains, sore throat, headache; reduces fever; helps thin bronchial secretions to make coughs more productive.

## Warnings
- Reye's syndrome risk in children/teenagers with chicken pox or flu
- Potential severe allergic reactions to salicylates
- "This product contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding"
- Not for children under 12 years old
- Avoid if allergic to salicylates or currently taking MAOIs

## Directions
Adults and children ≥12y: 30mL (2 tablespoons) every 4 hours, max 6 doses/24h. Children <12y: do not use.

## Brand-specific notes
**Notable finding**: the only product identified in this entire research batch (cough-cold-allergy category) whose pain reliever is a salicylate (choline salicylate) rather than acetaminophen, aside from Alka-Seltzer Plus's separate aspirin-based variant. Carries the full salicylate risk profile (Reye's syndrome, GI bleeding, salicylate allergy) rather than the acetaminophen-duplication risk that dominates most other products in this category.
