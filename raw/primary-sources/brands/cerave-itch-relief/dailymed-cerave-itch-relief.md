# CeraVe Itch Relief - DailyMed Drug Facts

## Active Ingredient
Pramoxine hydrochloride 1%

## Purpose
External analgesic

## Uses
Temporary relief of itching associated with minor skin irritations.

## Warnings
For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes. If condition worsens, or symptoms persist beyond 7 days, or clear up and recur within a few days, discontinue use and consult a doctor.

## Directions
Adults and children 2 years of age and older: apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 2 years of age: consult a doctor.

Manufacturer: L'Oreal USA Products Inc. Both cream and lotion formats share the identical active ingredient and Drug Facts information.
