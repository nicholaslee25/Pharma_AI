## Active Ingredients
- Severe Cold and Flu (liquid, per 15mL): Acetaminophen 325mg, Dextromethorphan HBr 10mg, Guaifenesin 200mg, Phenylephrine HCl 5mg
- Cold and Flu (capsule/liquid, no guaifenesin): Acetaminophen 325mg, Dextromethorphan HBr 10mg, Phenylephrine HCl 5mg

## Purpose
Pain reliever/fever reducer, cough suppressant, expectorant (Severe SKU only), nasal decongestant.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y per standard OTC dosing interval (specific interval not independently extracted in full this pass).

## Brand-specific notes
Multiple DayQuil SKUs exist with and without guaifenesin — always a non-drowsy (no antihistamine) daytime formulation, contrasted with NyQuil's addition of doxylamine at night.
