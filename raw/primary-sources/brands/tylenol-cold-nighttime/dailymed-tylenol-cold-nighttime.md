## Active Ingredients
- Nighttime Cold and Flu: Acetaminophen 325mg + Dextromethorphan HBr 15mg + Doxylamine succinate 6.25mg
- Nighttime Severe Cold and Flu: adds Phenylephrine HCl

## Warnings
Max 4 doses/24h (acetaminophen liver-damage warning).

## Brand-specific notes
Matched acetaminophen dose (325mg) with the Tylenol Cold Daytime product for straightforward same-day dose tracking — but other Tylenol-branded products (e.g. Extra Strength, 500mg) use a different acetaminophen amount, so total-daily-dose tracking is not automatically safe across the entire Tylenol product range.
