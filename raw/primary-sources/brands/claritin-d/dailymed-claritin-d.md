## Active Ingredients
Loratadine 10mg + Pseudoephedrine sulfate 240mg, extended-release tablet

## Uses
Relief of nasal/sinus congestion due to colds or allergies, sneezing, runny nose, itchy watery eyes, itchy throat or nose.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one tablet once daily.

## Brand-specific notes
Uses the full 10mg loratadine dose (same as standalone Claritin), unlike Zyrtec-D (half-dose cetirizine) and Alavert D-12 (half-dose loratadine) — a genuine brand-design difference, not an inconsistency to resolve. A separate Claritin-D 12 Hour SKU exists; its exact dose split was not independently confirmed in this pass.
