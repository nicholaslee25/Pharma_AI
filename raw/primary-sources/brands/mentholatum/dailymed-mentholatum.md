## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU
- Mentholatum Original: Camphor 9%, Menthol 1.3% (topical analgesic only — no cough-suppressant claim, no eucalyptus)
- Vaporizing Rub line (Cherry, No Mess, Lavender, Nighttime, Vapor Max): Camphor 5.3%, Eucalyptus oil 1.3%, Menthol 2.8% (cough suppressant + topical analgesic)
- Cherry Vaporizing Rub (a distinct sub-variant found): Camphor 4.7%, Eucalyptus oil 1.2%, Menthol 2.6%

## Brand-specific notes
Real, consumer-relevant distinction: "Mentholatum" plain/Original is NOT the same product as "Mentholatum Vaporizing Rub" — different camphor concentration, no eucalyptus, and no cough-suppressant labeled use for the Original formula.
