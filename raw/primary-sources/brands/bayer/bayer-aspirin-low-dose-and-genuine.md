# Bayer Aspirin — Two Formulations

## Bayer Genuine Aspirin (325mg) — already documented in raw/primary-sources/aspirin/dailymed-bayer-genuine-aspirin.md
Active Ingredient: Aspirin 325 mg
Directions: 1-2 tablets (325-650mg) q4h, or 3 tablets (975mg) q6h, max 12 tablets (3900mg)/24h

## Bayer Aspirin Regimen / Bayer Low Dose (81mg)
Source referenced: DailyMed "BAYER LOW DOSE- aspirin tablet" (setid=075b103e-0bb4-4b7a-ac0e-5645bcbd0a07), bayeraspirin.com product page.

Active Ingredient: Aspirin 81 mg (NSAID)
Formulation: Enteric-coated, safety-coated, caffeine-free, sodium-free
Purpose/Use: For adults whose doctors recommend a low-dose aspirin regimen; taking aspirin regularly as directed can help lower risk of another heart attack or clot-related (ischemic) stroke. Manufacturer-cited figure: "can help reduce the chance of a second heart attack by 31%" (not independently re-verified against a primary trial in this pass).
Warning: Aspirin is not appropriate for everyone — talk to a doctor before beginning an aspirin regimen.
