# DailyMed — Colgate Peroxyl / Peroxyl Mouth Sore Rinse

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b1e1b7c9-dea7-4d64-b6a8-5343f4176359

## Active Ingredient
Hydrogen Peroxide 1.5% — oral debriding agent / oral wound cleanser.

## Uses
Temporary use to cleanse canker sores, minor wounds, or minor gum inflammation.

## Directions
Apply up to 4 times daily after meals and at bedtime, or as directed by a dentist or physician; do not use for more than 7 days unless directed.
