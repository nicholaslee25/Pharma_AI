## Active Ingredient
Phenylephrine hydrochloride (concentration varies: Mild/Regular/Maximum strength variants)

## Directions
Typically 2-3 sprays every 4 hours, not more than 3 days.

## Warnings
Frequent or prolonged use may cause nasal congestion to recur or worsen (rebound congestion, class-wide warning for topical decongestants).

## Brand-specific notes
**Important route distinction**: this is TOPICAL/nasal phenylephrine, not oral. The FDA's 2023 finding that oral phenylephrine (10mg tablets, e.g. Sudafed PE) is not GRASE as a decongestant does NOT apply to this product — oral phenylephrine's poor efficacy stems from extensive first-pass hepatic/gut-wall metabolism when swallowed, a problem topical nasal application bypasses entirely by delivering the drug directly to the target tissue. The [[oral-phenylephrine]] ingredient page itself already states this route distinction explicitly (line 29 of that page), so this is not a discovered discrepancy — just an important brand-page clarification to prevent a reader from incorrectly conflating this product with Sudafed PE's efficacy controversy.
