## Active Ingredients & Purposes
- Aconitum napellus 3X HPUS — Minor Pain Reliever
- Bryonia alba 3X HPUS — Anti-inflammatory
- Hypericum perforatum 3X HPUS — Topical Analgesic

(HPUS = Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States)

## Uses
Temporary relief of neuropathy symptoms including pain, numbness, tingling, and weakness, per Homeopathic Materia Medica.

## Warnings
For external use only. Do not apply to wounds, broken skin, or large body areas. Avoid eyes, mouth, nose, and mucous membranes. Seek medical care if experiencing diabetic neuropathy symptoms. Stop use if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Adults and children 12+: apply 10–30 drops to affected areas up to 4 times daily. Children under 12 require physician consultation.

## Note — brand-line distinction
This exact SKU ("Frankincense and Myrrh Neuropathy") is a homeopathic product (3X-potency Aconitum napellus, Bryonia alba, Hypericum perforatum) — NOT the same formulation as the separately-sold "Frankincense and Myrrh Foot Pain Relief" and "Frankincense and Myrrh Arthritis Pain Relief" SKUs, which use Menthol 2% as their active ingredient instead. Both product lines share the same frankincense/myrrh/essential-oil-blend inactive-ingredient base and near-identical branding, creating real potential for consumer confusion between a homeopathic-dilution product and a conventional monograph menthol product sold under the same family name.
