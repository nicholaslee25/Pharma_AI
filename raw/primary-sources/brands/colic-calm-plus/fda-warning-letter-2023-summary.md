# FDA Warning Letter Summary — CalmCo LLC (Colic Calm / Colic Calm +)

Source: https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/calmco-llc-previously-named-ketomi-llc-663668-08092023
(condensed facts only, per project convention — no full-text reproduction)

- Date: 2023-08-09
- Recipient: CalmCo LLC (previously named Ketomi LLC)
- Products cited: "Colic Calm" and "Colic Calm +"
- Finding 1: Company website statements were determined to be illegal drug claims (e.g., "Colic Calm eases baby's discomfort often associated with gas, colic, hiccups and teething") because they claim to diagnose, cure, mitigate, treat, or prevent disease / affect body structure or function — making these unapproved new drugs under FD&C Act sections 505(a)/301(d).
- Finding 2: References a separate CGMP warning letter to Denison Pharmaceuticals, LLC (MARCS-CMS 654226, dated 2023-07-26) — the actual manufacturer — which found the firm failed to adequately investigate unexplained discrepancies or batch/component failures to meet specifications.
- FDA's own characterization: the products were "particularly concerning from a public health perspective" given the CGMP violations combined with their being directed for use in infants.

## Related product identification note
"Colic Calm Plus" (as listed in the Pharmacy Times 2026 OTC Guide, Infant Gas Products category, 3% share) is identified with high confidence as the same product as "Colic Calm +" named in this warning letter, though the exact naming match was not independently re-verified against the primary warning letter document text.
