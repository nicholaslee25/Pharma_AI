# Lubricity — sourcing

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Sources:** lubricityinnovations.com; Amazon product listings

## Formulation
Water, sodium hyaluronate (hyaluronic acid), xylitol, sodium benzoate, potassium sorbate — 5 total ingredients.

## Regulatory Status
Per manufacturer's own marketing: "FDA cleared and is a medical device registered with the United States Food and Drug Administration," manufactured under current Good Manufacturing Practices — a DEVICE, not a drug. No Drug Facts panel located.

## Mechanism (per manufacturer)
Forms a flavorless gel film coating the oral mucosal tissue for tissue-on-tissue lubrication, lasting up to 4 hours.
