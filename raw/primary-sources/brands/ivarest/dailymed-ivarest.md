# IVAREST - DailyMed Drug Facts

Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Active Ingredients and Concentrations
- Calamine 14.0% (w/w) - Skin protectant
- Benzyl Alcohol 10.5% (w/w) - External analgesic
- Diphenhydramine Hydrochloride 2.0% (w/w) - External analgesic

## Uses
Temporary relief of pain and itching associated with poison ivy, poison oak, poison sumac, insect bites or minor skin irritations. Dries the oozing and weeping of poison ivy, oak, and sumac.

## Warnings
For external use only. Do not use: on large body areas; with other diphenhydramine products (oral or topical). Ask a doctor before use on chicken pox or measles. Avoid eye contact. Stop use and consult doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur shortly after clearing. Keep away from children; contact poison control if swallowed.

## Directions
Apply as soon as possible after exposure. Adults and children 2 years and older: apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 2 require medical consultation. Apply liberally to form an opaque layer; do not exceed recommended frequency.

Some Ivarest SKUs use zinc oxide rather than calamine as the skin-protectant component per other DailyMed listings; not independently pulled in full in this pass.
