# MIRACLE FOOT REPAIR CREAM (Winning Solutions) — Drug Facts Label

Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
- Menthol 0.1% — External analgesic

## Purpose
External analgesic

## Uses
Temporarily protects and helps relieve chapped or cracked skin.

## Warnings
For external use only. In case of accidental ingestion, get medical help or contact a Poison Control center right away. Keep away from children under 2 years.

## Directions
Apply as needed to clean, dry skin for those 2 years and older. Massage liberally over affected area until absorbed. Children under 2 years require physician approval before use.

## Inactive Ingredients
Aloe vera leaf juice, water, mineral oil, glyceryl stearate, PEG-100 stearate, cetyl alcohol, cetearyl alcohol, polysorbate 60, propylene glycol, stearic acid, hydrogenated polyisobutene, dimethicone, triethanolamine, salicylic acid, imidazolidinyl urea, carrageenan, methylparaben, propylparaben, peppermint oil, chloroxylenol, and DMDM hydantoin.

## Note
Salicylic acid appears here as an INACTIVE ingredient only (not labeled with a percentage or a drug-facts "active" claim) — distinct from Kerasal's use of salicylic acid as an active keratolytic ingredient. Do not conflate the two products' regulatory status for this shared inactive/active ingredient. Manufacturer: Winning Solutions. NDC listed as inactivated in DailyMed at time of pull.
