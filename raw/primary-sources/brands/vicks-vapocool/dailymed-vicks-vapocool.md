## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU
- Severe / Severe Winterfrost / Severe Sugar-Free: Menthol only
- Severe Plus Intense Pain Relief: Menthol + Benzocaine

## Brand-specific notes
The Plus Intense Pain Relief SKU adds Vicks VapoCOOL as a fourth confirmed benzocaine brand alongside Orajel, Cepacol, and Chloraseptic on [[topical-anesthetics]] — but this applies only to that specific SKU, not the plain-menthol VapoCOOL products.
