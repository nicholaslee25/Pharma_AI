# DailyMed — BiteMD External Analgesic First Aid Antiseptic, FDA Drug Facts

**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=4c60db03-6d2b-4ae0-a200-718b3a50eb0f&type=display
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Cutter (bug-repellent brand family)

## Active Ingredients & Purposes
- Benzocaine 5% — Analgesic, Anesthetic, Antipruritic
- Camphor 3% — Topical Analgesic
- Phenol 1.35% — First Aid Antiseptic

## Uses
First aid for temporary relief of pain, itching, and to help prevent infection.

## Warnings
External use only; avoid eyes and large body areas; do not bandage after application; consult a doctor for deep/puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns; discontinue if condition worsens or persists beyond 7 days; do not use longer than 1 week without medical direction; keep away from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed; flush eyes with water for 15 minutes if product enters eyes.

## Directions
Clean affected area; apply by pressing down repeatedly until liquid flows, then dab sparingly; may reapply after 10-15 minutes if itching continues; remove ticks/stingers before treatment; use 1-3 times daily, ages 2+; children under 2 consult a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredient
Propylene glycol.

## Note
A secondary retail source (Cutter's own product page) lists only benzocaine 5% + phenol 1.35% without mentioning camphor — the DailyMed FDA label (the authoritative regulatory source) lists all three actives including camphor 3%. This page trusts the DailyMed label.
