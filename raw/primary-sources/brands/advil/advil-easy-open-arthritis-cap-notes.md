# Advil Easy Open Arthritis Cap — Product Notes

Sources referenced (via WebSearch, not independently fetched in full): advil.com/our-products/advil-pain/advil-arthritis-cap/, arthritis.org/partnership/ease-of-use-products/advil-easy-open-arthritis-cap, retailer listings (Amazon, Walmart, Kroger, Harris Teeter, Hy-Vee).

## Product identity
Advil Coated Tablets, Ibuprofen 200mg — identical active ingredient and dose to standard Advil tablets. The "Easy Open Arthritis Cap" refers to a bottle-cap design ("faucet design") intended to be easier to open for people with arthritis/joint pain affecting hand dexterity. This cap received an "Ease-of-Use Commendation" from the Arthritis Foundation.

## Marketing note
Advil's own marketing states its Easy Open Arthritis Cap is "easier to open than Aleve Easy Open Arthritis Caps" — confirming Aleve also markets a comparable accessible-cap product under a similar naming convention.

## Uses (per label/marketing)
Headaches, muscle aches, backaches, menstrual pain, minor arthritis and other joint pain, aches and pains from the common cold. Dosage: ages 12+, 1 tablet every 4-6 hours — standard ibuprofen OTC dosing, no arthritis-specific dose escalation.
