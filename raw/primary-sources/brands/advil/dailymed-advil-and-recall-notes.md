# Advil — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed) + Recall Notes

## DailyMed — ADVIL (ibuprofen tablet, coated)
Active Ingredient: Ibuprofen 200 mg
Purpose: Pain reliever/fever reducer
Uses: Temporarily relieves minor aches and pains due to headache, toothache, backache, menstrual cramps, the common cold, muscular aches, and minor pain of arthritis; temporarily reduces fever.
Warnings: Contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding; may cause a severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin.

## DailyMed — ADVIL (ibuprofen sodium tablet, coated / Film-Coated)
Manufacturer listed: Haleon US Holdings LLC
Active Ingredient: Ibuprofen sodium 256 mg (providing ibuprofen 200 mg)

## DailyMed — ADVIL LIQUI-GELS (ibuprofen capsule, liquid filled)
Active Ingredient: Solubilized ibuprofen equal to 200 mg ibuprofen (present as the free acid and potassium salt)

## Recall 1 — Class I, label defect (2022-12-06 voluntary / 2023-01-25 FDA Class I designation)
GSK Consumer Healthcare Holdings (Haleon) voluntarily recalled several lots of Advil tablets/capsules due to an adhesive-migration defect causing product labels to tear and become illegible when peeled back. FDA designated this a Class I recall.

## Recall 2 — Children's Advil Suspension Bubble Gum (Pfizer Consumer Healthcare, historical distributor)
Voluntary nationwide recall of one lot due to a mismatch between the dosage cup (marked in teaspoons) and label instructions (in milliliters), creating a potential overdose risk.
Source referenced: fda.gov recalls listing (Pfizer Inc Issues Voluntary Nationwide Recall of One Lot Children's Advil Suspension Bubble Gum).
