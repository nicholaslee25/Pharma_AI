# Advil Migraine — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed)

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e4a6219d-4ad4-0119-4cf8-7fc39b4b5979&audience=consumer

**Active Ingredient:** Solubilized ibuprofen equal to 200 mg ibuprofen (NSAID), present as free acid and potassium salt

**Purpose:** Pain reliever

**Uses:** Treats migraine

**Warnings:** Allergy alert — may cause severe reactions (hives, facial swelling, asthma, shock, rash, blisters); stomach bleeding risk especially for those 60+, with ulcer history, or using blood thinners; NSAIDs increase heart attack and stroke risk; medication overuse headache warning if used 10+ days monthly; do not use if allergic to other pain relievers or before/after heart surgery; consult doctor for undiagnosed migraines or headaches differing from usual pattern.

**Directions:** Adults take 2 capsules with water; if symptoms persist/worsen, consult doctor; maximum 2 capsules in 24 hours unless directed otherwise; individuals under 18 should ask a doctor; use smallest effective dose.
