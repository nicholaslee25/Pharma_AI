## Active Ingredients (per caplet)
Acetaminophen 325mg + Guaifenesin 200mg + Phenylephrine HCl 5mg

## Uses
Temporarily relieves sinus congestion/pressure, headache, nasal congestion, minor aches/pains, fever, and helps loosen phlegm/thin bronchial secretions.

## Warnings
Max daily dose: 10 caplets (3,250mg acetaminophen)/24h. Severe liver damage may occur if >4,000mg/24h total acetaminophen (including other products) or with ≥3 alcoholic drinks/day.
