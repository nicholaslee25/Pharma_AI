## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU
- Sore Throat Plus Pain Relief (spray): Benzocaine 7% + Menthol 1%
- Sore Throat Plus Cough Relief (lozenge): Dextromethorphan HBr + Hexylresorcinol
- Sore Throat Plus Soothing Comfort (lozenge): Hexylresorcinol
- Pain Relief (lozenge): Hexylresorcinol

## Brand-specific notes
Hexylresorcinol is a distinct oral anesthetic with no dedicated ingredient page in this database — a new gap alongside dyclonine (Sucrets). Benzocaine spray SKU adds to [[topical-anesthetics]]' safety data. Three different "Mucinex" product families (oral guaifenesin, nasal oxymetazoline, InstaSoothe benzocaine/hexylresorcinol) share no common active ingredient.
