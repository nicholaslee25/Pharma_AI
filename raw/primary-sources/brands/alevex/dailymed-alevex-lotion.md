# AleveX Lotion - DailyMed Drug Facts

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=bafdf06b-25f5-e29e-e053-2a95a90a5e1b&type=display
Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Active Ingredients
Camphor 5.5%
Menthol 16%

## Purpose
Pain relieving lotion

## Uses
Temporary relief of minor muscle and joint aches associated with arthritis, backache, strains, sprains, and bruises with penetrating relief.

## Warnings
Do not use if allergic to this product or any ingredient. When using: avoid eyes and mucous membranes; don't apply tight bandages, heating pads, or other heat sources; keep away from wounds or irritated skin. Stop use if condition worsens, irritation develops, pain/swelling/blistering occurs, severe burning develops, or symptoms persist beyond 7 days. Pregnant or breast-feeding individuals should consult a health professional first. Keep out of reach of children. For external use only; product is flammable.

## Directions
Adults and children 12+: apply to affected area 3-4 times daily maximum; wash hands afterward. Children under 12: consult a doctor.

## Manufacturer note
Manufacturer FAQ (aleve.com/alevex/faq) explicitly states AleveX does not contain naproxen sodium (the NSAID active ingredient in oral Aleve).
