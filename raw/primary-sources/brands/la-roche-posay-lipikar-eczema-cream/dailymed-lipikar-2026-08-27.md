## Drug Facts (Lipikar Eczema Relief)
**Active Ingredient:** Colloidal oatmeal 1% (10 mg/mL)
**Purpose:** Skin protectant
**Uses:** Temporarily protects and relieves minor skin irritation and itching associated with eczema and rashes.
**Warnings:** External use only. Avoid contact with eyes. Discontinue use if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days. Keep out of reach of children; if swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control right away.
**Directions:** Apply as needed.
Available sizes: 3 mL, 5 mL, 200 mL. OTC Monograph Drug, approved 2017-11-21.

## Regional clinical-claims discrepancy
Search results for "La Roche-Posay Lipikar clinical study" surface: "82% of patients using Lipikar Baume AP+M for one month don't relapse vs only 26% of people using a standard care" and "tested on 14,180 patients in 29 clinical studies under dermatological control" — sourced to laroche-posay-me.com (Middle East site) describing "Lipikar Baume AP+M," a different product from the US "Lipikar Eczema Relief Cream" / "Lipikar Eczema Soothing Relief" actually sold domestically and tracked by Pharmacy Times. The US laroche-posay.us product page for "Lipikar Eczema Relief Cream" makes only general uncited claims ("clinically shown to relieve itchy, irritated skin," "dermatologist and pediatrician tested") without referencing the 29-study/14,180-patient dataset. No confirmation found that the US product and Lipikar Baume AP+M share an identical formulation — flagged as an open discrepancy, not resolved.
