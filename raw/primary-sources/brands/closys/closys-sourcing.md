# CloSYS — Cloralstan marketing vs. FDA active-ingredient labeling

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## CloSYS Healthy Teeth Mouthwash
**Active Ingredient (per FDA Drug Facts convention):** Sodium Fluoride 0.02% — anticavity.
**Inactive ingredient:** Cloralstan® (stabilized chlorine dioxide) — the brand's marketed "technology," per manufacturer sourcing.

## Supporting Evidence for Chlorine Dioxide (independent of FDA active-ingredient labeling)
**Pham et al. 2018, Clinical and Experimental Dental Research** — randomized, double-blind, 2-week crossover trial, N=39 subjects with oral malodor, 0.1% ClO2 mouthwash vs. 0.9% sodium chloride control, 4-week washout between arms. Assessed oral malodor, periodontal/salivary parameters, tongue coating, and gram-negative/positive bacteria in saliva.
