## Drug Facts (Jock Itch)
**Active Ingredient:** Tolnaftate 1%
**Indication:** Cures most jock itch (tinea cruris), effective relief of itching, chafing, burning.
**Warnings:** External use only; not for children under 2 without doctor advice/supervision; avoid eye contact; discontinue if irritation occurs or no improvement within 2 weeks; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Tolnaftate pharmacology (ingredients/tolnaftate.md)
Mechanism: squalene epoxidase inhibition (same target as terbinafine/butenafine, different chemical class — thiocarbamate). Efficacy: tolnaftate 1% cream twice daily x3-4 weeks as effective as clotrimazole 1% for tinea pedis/corporis/cruris in older comparative studies. 93% mycologic eradication, 53-70% clinical cure in small studies, 60-90% general cure rate range. Not effective for onychomycosis (no nail-plate penetration). Clinical use has declined relative to terbinafine/naftifine/butenafine per pharmacology literature.
