## Active Ingredient
Oxymetazoline hydrochloride 0.05%

## Uses
Temporarily relieves nasal congestion due to a cold, hay fever, or other upper respiratory allergies; promotes nasal and/or sinus drainage; temporarily relieves sinus congestion/pressure; helps clear nasal passages; shrinks swollen membranes.

## Warnings
Do not exceed recommended dosage; do not use for more than 3 days. Frequent or prolonged use may cause nasal congestion to recur or worsen.

## Brand-specific notes
Shares no active ingredient with oral Mucinex (guaifenesin) despite the shared brand name — genuinely different drug under the same brand.
