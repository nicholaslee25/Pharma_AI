# DailyMed — Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun, FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun Medicated Relief Gel
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=c95c5b64-b90f-1143-e053-2a95a90aa03c
**Active Ingredient & Strength:** Camphor 0.45% — External analgesic
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves pain and itching associated with sunburn.
**Warnings:** External use only; flammable, keep away from fire/flame; avoid eyes; keep out of reach of children, contact Poison Control if swallowed; discontinue and consult a doctor if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days or recur.
**Directions:** Adults/children 2+: apply to affected area 3-4 times daily max. Under 2: ask a doctor.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Water, Alcohol Denat., Triethanolamine, Carbomer, EDTA.

## Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun Replenishing Lotion — separate, NON-drug SKU
Per third-party ingredient-listing sources (incidecoder, skinsort — not independently confirmed against a DailyMed Drug Facts listing, because none appears to exist for this SKU): this lotion contains water, mineral oil, glycerin, shea butter, sesame seed oil, aloe vera, mint extract, and other cosmetic/moisturizing ingredients — **no active drug ingredient (no camphor, no other Drug-Facts-listed analgesic)**. This is a moisturizer, not a drug, despite sharing the "Sun Rescue After Sun" brand name with the camphor-containing Medicated Relief Gel above.

## Note — brand-name pattern
"Neutrogena Sun Rescue After Sun" spans (at least) one genuine OTC drug SKU (Medicated Relief Gel, camphor 0.45%) and one non-drug cosmetic/moisturizing SKU (Replenishing Lotion) under nearly identical branding — a consumer should check for the word "Medicated" specifically to know whether a given Neutrogena after-sun product contains any active pain-relief ingredient at all.
