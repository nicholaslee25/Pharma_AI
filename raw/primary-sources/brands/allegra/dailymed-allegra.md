## Active Ingredient
Fexofenadine HCl 60 mg (12-hour tablet) or 180 mg (24-hour tablet). Allegra Hives 24HR: fexofenadine HCl 180mg specifically labeled for hives/urticaria itching.

## Purpose
Antihistamine

## Uses
Temporarily relieves runny nose, sneezing, itchy watery eyes, itching of nose or throat due to hay fever/upper respiratory allergies. Allegra Hives 24HR: relieves itching and reduces hives (urticaria).

## Warnings
Ask a doctor before use if kidney disease. Take with water — do not take with fruit juices (apple, orange, grapefruit — reduce absorption per known fexofenadine PK interaction). Contact Poison Control for overdose.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: 180mg once daily or 60mg twice daily; children 2–11y: 30mg twice daily (suspension). Do not exceed 1 dose/24h for the 24HR product.

## Brand-specific notes beyond generic fexofenadine
- Sanofi-sponsored pediatric trials NCT01244230 (atopic dermatitis) and NCT01244217 (perennial allergic rhinitis) evaluated Allegra specifically for safety/efficacy/PK in children — these are brand-sponsored but molecule-level (fexofenadine) trials, not evidence of a formulation difference from generic fexofenadine.
- "Twenty-five years: the fexofenadine clinical experience" (review, PMC11382105) covers the cumulative trial record; FDA safety database cited ~18,361 subjects exposed to fexofenadine across 124 studies — this is ingredient-level, not Allegra-brand-specific.
- No evidence found of a distinct Allegra-brand clinical effect beyond the fexofenadine molecule itself.
