# Calmol 4 — DailyMed Drug Facts

Labeler: ResiCal, Inc.
Source: DailyMed setid=5516f800-d3fa-4b90-a3eb-c0bdf3183b9b, pulled 2026-08-27

## Drug Facts
**Active Ingredients:** Zinc Oxide 10% (270 mg), Cocoa Butter 76% (2052 mg)
**Purpose:** Protectant (both); zinc oxide also described by manufacturer as an astringent/healing agent
**Uses:** Temporary relief of burning, irritation, itching, and discomfort associated with hemorrhoids; temporarily provides a coating for anorectal discomfort; temporarily protects inflamed/irritated anorectal surface to help make bowel movements less painful.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Glyceryl monostearate, hard fat, methylparaben, propylparaben, bismuth subgallate.

No vasoconstrictor, anesthetic, or corticosteroid active — the only protectant-only brand in this category (vs. Preparation H's phenylephrine/pramoxine/hydrocortisone, RectiCare's lidocaine ± phenylephrine, Tucks' witch hazel/lidocaine).
