# Dentemp — Zinc Oxide Eugenol (ZOE) Repair Cement Ingredients

Sources referenced (via WebSearch, not fetched in full): amazon.com Dentemp product listings, dentek.com Temparin Max page.

## Dentemp Maximum Strength (Lost Fillings and Loose Caps Repair)
Ingredients: calcium sulfate, barium sulfate, dimethoxytetraethylene glycol, zinc oxide, eugenol, ethylmethacrylate polymer.

## DenTek Temparin Max Advanced Repair Kit (related product family)
Ingredients: aluminum phosphate, aluminum sulfate, calcium sulfate, eugenol, iron oxides, menthol, paraffin, petrolatum, potassium alum, zinc oxide.

Context: Zinc oxide + eugenol (ZOE) is a classical, long-established dental cement/temporary filling material. Eugenol provides secondary analgesic/antiseptic action (same active compound documented for Red Cross Toothache Medication).
