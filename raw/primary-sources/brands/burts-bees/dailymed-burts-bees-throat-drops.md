## Active Ingredient
Menthol 2.0mg per drop

## Inactive Ingredients
Honey, Sucrose, Maltodextrin, Oryza Sativa (Rice) Extract, Water, Eucalyptus Globulus (Eucalyptus) Leaf Oil

## Brand-specific notes
Lowest confirmed menthol dose among brands checked in this category (2.0mg). This label covers only the Throat Drops product — Burt's Bees' other product lines (toothpaste, acne, lip balm, skin cleansers) are separate, unresearched products in this pass.
