### Full Drug Facts panel — Dial Advanced Clean Deodorant Bar Soap Antibacterial Gold
**Active Ingredient:** Benzalkonium Chloride 0.10%
**Purpose:** Antibacterial
**Uses:** For washing to decrease the bacteria on the skin
**Warnings:** For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes; in case of eye contact, flush with water. Stop using and ask a doctor if irritation or redness develops. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control right away.
**Directions:** Wet bar with water, lather vigorously and wash skin, rinse and dry thoroughly.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Soap (Sodium Palmate, Sodium Cocoate, Sodium Palm Kernelate), Water, Glycerin, Coconut Acid, Palm Acid, Palm Kernel Acid, Stearic Acid, Fragrance, Sorbitol, Sodium Chloride, colorants (Yellow 5, Yellow 8, Red 4)
**Label updated:** November 13, 2025
