# Australian Dream Pain Relieving Arthritis Cream - DailyMed Drug Facts

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=8363f3dd-7ed3-407e-b681-3d388e8651e0&type=display
Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Active Ingredient
Histamine dihydrochloride 0.025%

## Purpose
External analgesic for topical pain relief

## Uses
For the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with arthritis, simple backache, strains & bruises.

## Warnings
- For external use only
- Do not use on wounds or damaged skin or if you are allergic to ingredients
- Avoid eye contact; rinse with water if exposure occurs
- Do not apply under tight bandages or use heating pads
- Stop use if rash appears, symptoms worsen, persist beyond 7 days, or recur within days
- Consult a health professional if pregnant or breastfeeding
- Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if swallowed

## Directions/Dosage
Apply a thin layer to pain site and massage until thoroughly absorbed into skin. Apply no more than 3 to 4 times daily. Intended for adults and children over 12 years; younger children require physician consultation.
