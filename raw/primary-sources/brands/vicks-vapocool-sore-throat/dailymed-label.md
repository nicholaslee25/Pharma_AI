## Lozenge — Active Ingredients
Benzocaine 15 mg, Menthol 20 mg (both oral anesthetics)

Temporary relief of occasional minor irritation/pain due to sore throat and sore mouth. Adults and children 12+.

## Warning (verbatim per label)
"Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that must be treated promptly because it reduces the amount of oxygen carried in blood."

## Note
Distinct product line from "Vicks VapoCOOL" (Topical Cough Suppressants, Lozenges category), where the base/Severe SKUs are menthol-only and benzocaine appears only in the separate "Plus Intense Pain Relief" SKU. In this "Sore Throat"-branded line, benzocaine+menthol is the base formula for both spray and lozenge, not an upsell tier — a real naming/formulation distinction between two similarly-named Vicks VapoCOOL product families.
