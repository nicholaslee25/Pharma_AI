# Tecnu Product Line - Sourcing Notes

Pulled: 2026-08-27

## Tecnu Original Outdoor Skin Cleanser
Source: CVS ingredient listing (cvs.com/shop/ingredients/tecnu-original-outdoor-skin-cleanser-prodid-492741)
No FDA drug active ingredient (cosmetic cleanser). Ingredients: deodorized mineral spirits, water, propylene glycol, octylphenoxy-polyethoxyethanol, mixed fatty acid soap, fragrance.
Intended for use shortly after urushiol (poison ivy/oak/sumac) exposure to mechanically remove the oil before a rash develops.

## Tecnu Rash Relief Spray
Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ffc38ad5-d414-4e68-e053-6294a90aa840

Label: "This product has not been evaluated by the FDA for safety or efficacy. FDA is not aware of scientific evidence to support homeopathy as effective."
Inactive ingredients: disodium EDTA, glycerin, menthol, polyoxyl 35 castor oil, purified water, SD alcohol 40B (14% by weight), tea tree oil, white thyme oil.
