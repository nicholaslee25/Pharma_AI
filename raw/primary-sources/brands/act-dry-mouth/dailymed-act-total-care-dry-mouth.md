# DailyMed — ACT Total Care Dry Mouth Anticavity Mouthwash

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=06684b82-aaa0-4ca6-8d56-4f9866269000

## Active Ingredient
Sodium Fluoride 0.02% (0.009% w/v fluoride ion) — anticavity.

## Discrepancy Note
Despite the "Dry Mouth" product name, the only FDA-recognized active ingredient targets cavity prevention, not xerostomia symptoms. See `drugs/ACT Dry Mouth.md` and [[fluoride-toothpaste]].
