# DailyMed — Lanacane First Aid Spray, FDA Drug Facts

**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=4b965cd3-bfe4-4aad-a52c-b747696baa1c&type=display
**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** RB Health (US) LLC

## Active Ingredients & Purpose
- Benzethonium chloride 0.2% — First-aid antiseptic
- Benzocaine 20% — Pain relieving spray

## Uses
First aid for temporary relief of pain and itching and to help prevent infection in minor cuts, scrapes, and burns.

## Warnings
External use only; flammable (avoid heat/flame/fire/smoking); do not apply in eyes or over large body areas; avoid puncturing/incinerating the pressurized container; do not store above 120°F; intentional inhalation misuse poses serious health risks; consult a doctor for deep wounds, animal bites, or serious burns; discontinue if symptoms worsen, persist beyond 7 days, or recur; keep from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.

## Directions
Ages 2+: clean area, spray a small amount 1-3 times daily. Under 2: consult a doctor. Allow to dry before bandaging.
