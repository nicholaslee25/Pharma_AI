# DailyMed — BENGAY (multiple SKUs), FDA Drug Facts

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Manufacturer:** Kenvue Brands LLC

## BENGAY Ultra Strength Non-Greasy Cream — highest concentration
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=0a41ec65-bd0b-4fc6-807d-74f353341cc7&type=display
**Active Ingredients:** Camphor (synthetic) 4%, Menthol 10%, Methyl Salicylate 30% — all three exceed the FDA's 2012 burn-risk thresholds (>3% menthol, >10% methyl salicylate); BENGAY was explicitly named in that FDA communication's press coverage.
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves minor aches/pains of muscles/joints — simple backache, arthritis, strains, bruises, sprains.
**Warnings:** Do not use on wounds/damaged skin, with heating pads, or on children under 12 with arthritis-like conditions; avoid eyes/mucous membranes; do not bandage tightly; stop use if condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond 7 days, recur, or excessive skin irritation develops; keep from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.
**Directions:** Adults/children 12+: apply 3-4 times daily. Under 12: consult a doctor.

## BENGAY Zero Degrees (Menthol Pain Relieving Gel) — moderate concentration, single ingredient
Active Ingredient: Menthol 5% only — no camphor, no methyl salicylate. Below the FDA's >3% menthol threshold... actually exceeds 3% (5% > 3%) but with no methyl salicylate present, a narrower risk profile than the Ultra Strength triple-ingredient product.

## BENGAY Vanishing Scent — lowest concentration, single ingredient
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=6cc3294b-3b0f-44d0-af71-61db1022d269
Active Ingredient: Menthol 2.5% only — BELOW the FDA's >3% menthol burn-risk threshold, and no methyl salicylate. The mildest formulation in the BENGAY line by a meaningful margin.

## Summary — a wide concentration spread under one brand name
BENGAY spans from a mild, single-ingredient 2.5% menthol gel (Vanishing Scent) to a triple-ingredient, high-concentration cream (camphor 4% + menthol 10% + methyl salicylate 30%, Ultra Strength) that exceeds the FDA's identified burn-risk thresholds on every dimension. A consumer choosing "BENGAY" without checking the specific product name is choosing between meaningfully different risk profiles, not a single fixed formulation.
