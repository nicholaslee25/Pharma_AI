## Lotrimin Ultra
Active ingredient: Butenafine hydrochloride 1%. Ages 12+. DailyMed setid=d11dfdf6-9b36-03bc-e053-2995a90a0bc3 (also 0eadac26-4619-4227-9dda-28e739d77a87, 9dfa08fb-0913-4326-b1eb-1425df6a2c82 Jock Itch, 1b438ca7-fd5f-62cb-e063-6294a90ab3a6 Ringworm).
**Purpose:** Antifungal. **Uses:** Cures most athlete's foot between the toes; effectiveness on bottom/sides of foot unknown; relieves itching, burning, cracking, scaling.
**Warnings:** External use only — not for nails, scalp, mouth, eyes, or vaginal yeast infections.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Stearic acid, trolamine, glycerin, white petrolatum, benzyl alcohol, cetyl alcohol, water, sodium benzoate, ceteth-23, glyceryl monostearate, propylene glycol dicaprylate.

## CORRECTION MADE THIS PASS
Prior database entries (ingredients/terbinafine.md, ingredients/clotrimazole.md) incorrectly stated Lotrimin Ultra uses terbinafine. Confirmed via DailyMed: it is butenafine, not terbinafine. Both ingredient pages corrected 2026-08-27.

## Butenafine efficacy evidence (see ingredients/butenafine.md)
- Placebo-controlled tinea pedis RCT: 88% vs 33% mycologic cure.
- Head-to-head vs clotrimazole (tinea cruris/corporis): 26.5% vs 2.9% clinical cure at 1 week; 61.7% vs 17.6% mycological cure.
- Head-to-head vs terbinafine (76 patients, tinea cruris): 94.87% vs 62.16% mycological cure at day 42.
