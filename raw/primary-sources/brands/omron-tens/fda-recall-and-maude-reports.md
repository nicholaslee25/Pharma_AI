# OMRON TENS — FDA Recall + MAUDE Adverse Event Reports

## FDA Device Recalls (accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm)
"Class 3 Device Recall TENS Device" — records id=53780 and id=53779, associated with OMRON (Dalian) Co., Ltd. manufacturing. Note: FDA device-recall "Class 3" is the LOWEST severity category for device recalls (opposite convention from FDA drug recalls, where Class I is most severe) — reserved for situations unlikely to cause adverse health consequences.

## MAUDE Adverse Event Reports (voluntary, unverified — not confirmed causation)
- OMRON (Dalian) Co., Ltd. (Plant 1), "Max Power Relief TENS Therapy" — mdrfoi_id=17738457: report describes the unit reportedly catching fire on first day of use, "from the box."
- OMRON (Dalian) Co., Ltd. (Plant 2), "Omron Electrotherapy Pain Relief / Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulator T.E.N.S." — mdrfoi_id=11614535: report describes a user experiencing pain in the hip/thigh area during use, in an area where the device had previously been used successfully elsewhere on the body.
- A third similar MAUDE report (mdrfoi_id=6635587) for "Omorn Max Power Relief" TENS device also referenced in search results.

## FDA 510(k) Clearance
Omron Healthcare, Inc. received FDA 510(k) clearance (substantial equivalence to legally marketed predicate devices), per FDA correspondence letters (K182166, K172079) referenced in search results.
