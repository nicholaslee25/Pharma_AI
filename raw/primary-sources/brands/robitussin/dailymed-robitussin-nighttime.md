## Active Ingredients
- Soft Chews (per tablet): Dextromethorphan (equiv. 15mg HBr) + Doxylamine succinate 6.25mg
- Honey Maximum Strength (per 20mL): Dextromethorphan HBr 30mg + Doxylamine succinate 12.5mg

## Purpose
Cough suppressant + antihistamine (nighttime sleep aid).

## Brand-specific notes
No acetaminophen and no guaifenesin in this specific Nighttime Cough DM line — a real formulation distinction from plain Robitussin, Robitussin CF, and most other "Nighttime" combination products from competing brands in this batch, all of which do include acetaminophen. This product does not treat fever.
