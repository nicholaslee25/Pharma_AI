## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU
- Maximum Strength Cough Plus Chest Congestion DM (per 20mL): Dextromethorphan HBr 20mg + Guaifenesin 400mg
- 12 Hour Cough and Mucus Relief (extended-release tablet): Guaifenesin 1200mg + Dextromethorphan HBr 60mg
- CF: adds Pseudoephedrine HCl to the DM combination
- Day and Night kits: DM formula by day, adds Doxylamine succinate at night
- Children's DM: Dextromethorphan HBr + Guaifenesin, pediatric dose, ages 4+

## Purpose
Cough suppressant (dextromethorphan) + expectorant (guaifenesin) ± nasal decongestant (pseudoephedrine, CF only) ± nighttime sleep aid (doxylamine, Day/Night only).

## Warnings
Do not use with MAOIs or within 2 weeks of stopping one (dextromethorphan interaction warning found across labels).

## Brand-specific notes
Robitussin spans plain expectorant-leaning products through complex multi-ingredient combinations — no single "Robitussin formulation" exists; always check the specific SKU. Sugar-free/dye-free variants exist, relevant to its "Diabetic Cough Products" category listing (a formulation, not efficacy, distinction).
