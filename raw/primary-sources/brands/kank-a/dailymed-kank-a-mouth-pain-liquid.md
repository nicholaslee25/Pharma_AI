# DailyMed — Blistex Kank-A Liquid (Kank-A Mouth Pain Liquid)

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=fe1e32e8-ec36-4d1f-b3e4-913dbc852138
**Labeler:** Blistex Inc.

## Active Ingredients
- Benzocaine 20% (20 g/100 mL)
- Benzoin Resin 0.5% (0.5 g/100 mL)

## Purpose
Immediate-acting anesthetic pain relief; forms a protective polymer layer that adheres to oral lesions.

## Uses
Temporary relief of pain due to canker sores, minor irritation of the mouth and gums caused by dentures or orthodontic appliances, and minor injury of the mouth or gums.

## Warnings
Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that must be treated promptly. Not for use during pregnancy or lactation without consulting a doctor. Contraindicated in those with hypersensitivity to formula components. Do not use for more than 7 days. Contains benzocaine, which may cause allergic reactions; patch testing recommended before extensive use. Discontinue if no improvement occurs within 72 hours or if swelling develops. Keep out of reach of children.

## Directions
Apply a small amount to the affected area using the integrated applicator every 4 hours, or as frequently as every 2 hours if necessary.

## Inactive Ingredients
Alcohol, polyethylene glycol 300, propylene glycol, tannic acid, dimethyl isosorbide, benzyl alcohol, oleth-10, castor oil, saccharin, ethylcellulose, cetylpyridinium chloride.
