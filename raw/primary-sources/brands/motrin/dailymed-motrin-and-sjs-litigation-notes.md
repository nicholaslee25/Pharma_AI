# Motrin — FDA Drug Facts (DailyMed) + SJS/TEN Litigation Notes

## DailyMed — MOTRIN IB (ibuprofen tablet, film coated)
Active Ingredient: Ibuprofen 200 mg
Purpose: Pain reliever/fever reducer
Uses: Temporarily relieves minor aches and pains due to headache, muscular aches, minor pain of arthritis, toothache, backache, the common cold, and menstrual cramps; temporarily reduces fever.
Warnings: May cause a severe allergic reaction, especially in people allergic to aspirin. Contains an NSAID, which may cause severe stomach bleeding.

Other confirmed DailyMed SKUs: MOTRIN IB MIGRAINE (ibuprofen liquid-filled capsule); MOTRIN PM (ibuprofen + diphenhydramine citrate); CHILDRENS MOTRIN (ibuprofen suspension).

## SJS/TEN Product-Liability Litigation (McNeil/Johnson & Johnson)
- Samantha Reckis: given Motrin (ibuprofen) at age 7, developed toxic epidermal necrolysis (TEN), lost ~90% of skin, was blinded. Plymouth Superior Court (MA) jury awarded $63M ($109M reported with interest), upheld on appeal.
- Christopher Trejo: developed Stevens-Johnson syndrome (SJS) after taking Motrin as a teenager. Los Angeles jury awarded $48.1M.
- Brianna Maya: 12-year-old given Children's Motrin in 2000, suffered TEN with burns over 84% of body, blinded in one eye. Philadelphia jury awarded $10M.

## 2010 Multistate Consumer-Fraud Settlement
McNeil Consumer Healthcare (J&J subsidiary) paid ~$33M to ~40 US states + DC to settle consumer-fraud allegations tied to manufacturing quality-control lapses that led to recalls of McNeil brands including Tylenol, Motrin, Benadryl, and Sudafed (approx. 2009-2010 timeframe).
