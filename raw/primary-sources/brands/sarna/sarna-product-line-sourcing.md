## Sarna Calm + Cool
Source: DailyMed setid=19c41e75-a654-90cf-e063-6394a90a7e97; manufacturer Crown Laboratories press release (crownlaboratories.com)
Active ingredients: Pramoxine Hydrochloride 1%, Menthol 0.5%
Also enriched with witch hazel extract per 2024 rebrand/reformulation. Steroid free.

## Sarna Original
Source: Kaiser Permanente drug encyclopedia (healthy.kaiserpermanente.org) — secondary reference
Active ingredients: Pramoxine HCl 0.5%, Menthol 0.5% (per secondary sourcing, not independently pulled from a DailyMed label in this pass)

## Sarna Sensitive
Source: DailyMed setid=19b0ce1b-e147-01a1-e063-6394a90af808
Pramoxine hydrochloride only, no menthol (for users sensitive to cooling agents) — full label not independently pulled in this pass.

## Uses (general, per Calm + Cool label)
Temporary relief of pain and itching associated with minor burns, sunburn, minor cuts, scrapes, insect bites, minor skin irritations and rashes due to poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac.

## Directions
Adults and children 2 years of age and older: apply to affected area not more than 3 to 4 times daily. Children under 2 years of age: consult a doctor.

Manufacturer: Crown Laboratories, Inc.
