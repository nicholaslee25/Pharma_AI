# Clinere — Product Line Extract (device + drug SKUs)

Sources: DailyMed (Clinere Ear Wax Remover / Ear Wax Removal Kit, setid d967eac3-0aac-a7ae-e053-2995a90a0e1e), clinere.com, Amazon/Publix product listings
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Two distinct product types under one brand
1. **Clinere Ear Cleaner (reusable/disposable tool)** — a mechanical medical-grade-silicone scoop-and-fin tool, no active ingredient, not a drug. Designed to clean only the entrance to the ear canal and outer ear via a built-in comfort-stop guide, explicitly not meant to enter the canal beyond that guide.
2. **Clinere Ear Wax Removal Kit / Ear Wax Remover (liquid)** — per its own DailyMed label, carbamide peroxide-based liquid (same active-ingredient class as Debrox), sold as a kit alongside the mechanical tool.

## Safety Language (tool)
Do not put drops/liquid in the ear with a perforated eardrum; do not use if there is a history of ear pain, ear surgery, or perforated eardrum; use lightly/softly, especially on first use.
