## Trial context
CASCADE study (Oregon Health & Science University) — ongoing trial letting parents choose among OTC emollients (Vaseline, Vanicream, CeraVe Healing Ointment, CeraVe cream, Cetaphil cream) for infant eczema prevention/management. Relevant to moisturizers-ceramides.md's Kelleher 2021 Cochrane finding (regular infant emollient use probably does not prevent eczema, probably increases local skin infections) — not itself a completed efficacy result for Vanicream specifically.
