# LISTERINE SMART RINSE ANTICAVITY FLUORIDE RINSE - PINK LEMONADE FLAVOR

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=773255d9-aa0c-435e-a1e5-0b9332793831
Packager: Kenvue Brands LLC | NDC: 69968-0691-5 | Type: OTC Drug

## Active Ingredient & Concentration
Sodium fluoride 0.02% (0.01% w/v fluoride ion, 0.1 mg per 1 mL)

## Purpose
Anticavity

## Uses
Aids in the prevention of dental cavities

## Warnings
Keep out of reach of children. If more than used for rinsing is accidentally swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

## Directions
- Ages 6+: Rinse twice daily with 10 mL (2 teaspoonfuls) for 1 minute after brushing teeth; do not swallow; avoid eating/drinking for 30 minutes
- Children under 12: Requires adult supervision and instruction in proper rinsing habits
- Under 6 years: Consult dentist or doctor before use

## Inactive Ingredients
Water, sorbitol, flavor, phosphoric acid, cetylpyridinium chloride, sucralose, sodium saccharin, disodium phosphate, FD&C Red No. 40, and D&C Red No. 33
