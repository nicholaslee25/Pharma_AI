# DailyMed — SunBurnt (two very different SKUs), FDA Labels

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## SunBurnt PLUS — conventional drug
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=12de2559-05b0-4580-84c1-d3bd010b1c99&type=display
**Active Ingredient:** Lidocaine Hydrochloride 4.0% — External analgesic
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves pain and itching from sunburn, minor thermal injuries, insect bites, minor skin irritations, cuts, and scrapes.
**Warnings:** External use only; do not apply large quantities over raw/blistered areas or if allergic to any component; consult a physician before use for severe sunburn or compromised skin integrity; avoid eyes; discontinue and seek medical guidance if condition worsens, symptoms persist beyond 7 days, or recur; keep from children, contact Poison Control if swallowed.
**Directions:** Clean area first. Adults/children 2+: apply 3-4 times daily max. Under 2: consult a physician.

## SunBurnt Advanced After-Sun Gel — HOMEOPATHIC product
**Source:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=dcb2fd62-df8e-4a26-b7ed-274caefeeb76
**Manufacturer:** Welmedix Consumer Healthcare, Princeton, NJ
**FDA Disclaimer (verbatim on label):** "This homeopathic product has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration for safety or efficacy."
**Active Ingredients (homeopathic dilutions, HPUS = Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia of the United States monograph status):**
- Cantharis vesicatoria 3X HPUS (8 parts) — "addresses sunburns and rawness"
- Calendula officinalis TINC HPUS (1 part) — "promotes skin healing"
- Echinacea angustifolia TINC HPUS (1 part) — "relieves itching, burning, redness"
**Uses:** Temporarily relieves mild to moderate sunburn symptoms — overheated skin, redness, rawness, itching, dryness.
**Warnings:** External use only; avoid severe sunburns, broken skin, existing rashes; avoid eyes/mucous membranes; discontinue if symptoms persist beyond 7 days; consult a healthcare provider if pregnant/breastfeeding.
**Directions:** Apply 2-3 times daily (adults/children 2+); consult a doctor for children under 2.

## Cantharis / cantharidin background (general pharmacology, not SunBurnt-specific safety data)
Cantharis vesicatoria (Spanish fly / blister beetle) is the source of cantharidin, a real vesicant compound — at pharmacologically active (non-diluted) concentrations, topical cantharidin weakens epidermal desmosomes, causing blistering, and is used medically in exactly that capacity for wart/molluscum removal (see ClinicalTrials.gov studies on VP-102/cantharidin for molluscum contagiosum). Ingested in toxic doses, cantharidin causes GI burning, vomiting, and can affect the kidneys/urinary tract. **3X dilution is a comparatively LOW homeopathic potency** (a 1:1,000 dilution of the mother tincture) — far less dilute than typical ultra-high-potency homeopathic remedies (e.g., 30C, a 10^-60 dilution where no molecules of the original substance statistically remain). No specific FDA warning or adverse-event report for cantharis 3X preparations specifically was located in this pass. This is flagged as a genuine point of interest (a real vesicant compound, at a comparatively less-dilute potency, applied to already-sun-damaged skin) rather than a confirmed safety incident — no evidence of actual harm from this specific product was found.
