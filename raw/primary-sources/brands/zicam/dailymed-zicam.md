## Active Ingredients — varies significantly by SKU
- **RapidMelts Citrus:** Zinc acetate anhydrous + zinc gluconate (tablet)
- **RapidMelts Elderberry Citrus:** Zinc acetate anhydrous + zinc gluconate + Sambucus nigra (elderberry) flowering top
- **Cold Remedy Soft Chews:** Zincum aceticum, zincum gluconicum, caffeinum (homeopathic-notation labeling)
- **Cold Remedy Nasal Swabs:** Do NOT contain zinc — different homeopathic ingredients (not individually itemized in search results found)

## Purpose
Reduces duration/severity of common cold symptoms; relieves nasal congestion (per label language found).

## Warnings
Standard zinc-lozenge class warnings apply per search results (not independently verified per exact SKU in this pass): avoid citrus around dosing, interaction caution with tetracycline-class antibiotics/warfarin.

## Directions
Varies by SKU/format (RapidMelts dissolve on tongue, soft chews chewed, etc.) — see individual product label.

## Brand-specific notes
- **Historical anosmia concern:** An older Zicam intranasal zinc gel/swab product line (pre-dating the current swab formulations found in this search, which do not list zinc) was associated with reports of permanent smell loss (anosmia) and an FDA warning/product reformulation in the mid-2010s. This history was referenced based on general knowledge and the [[zinc]] ingredient page's existing anosmia note — it was not independently re-verified against a specific FDA safety communication document in this pass, and should be treated as a lead for future verification, not a fully sourced citation.
- Zicam spans genuinely different formulations (zinc lozenges/RapidMelts vs. non-zinc nasal swabs) under one brand — do not assume all Zicam SKUs share the same active ingredient or safety profile.
