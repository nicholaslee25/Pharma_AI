## Rectal phenylephrine general reference (uspharmacist.com "Counseling Patients With Hemorrhoids")
Phenylephrine "the only vasoconstrictor in widespread use," up to 4x daily, contraindicated/avoid in heart disease, hypertension, thyroid disease, diabetes, BPH-related urination difficulty, or on prescription hypertension/depression medications. No RCT evidence for reducing bleeding/prolapse (per Medscape/WebMD/drugs.com phenylephrine-rectal monograph summaries).
