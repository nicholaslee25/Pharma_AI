## Active Ingredient
Pelargonium sidoides 1X

## Purpose / Uses
Shortens duration and reduces severity of symptoms associated with the common cold and throat/nasal/bronchial irritations, including congestion, cough, hoarseness, and sore throat.

## Directions
Use at the first sign of symptoms and continue for an additional 48 hours after symptoms cease. Adults and children ≥12y: 1.5mL by mouth, 3 times daily.

## Brand-specific notes
- Labeled by manufacturer as "homeopathic" despite the underlying [[pelargonium]] ingredient page treating EPs 7630 pelargonium extract as a pharmacologically active standardized botanical, not a homeopathic dilution in the classical (ultra-dilute) sense. The "1X" notation (1:10 dilution under Homeopathic Pharmacopoeia convention) is a real but non-extreme dilution — parallel to Cold-EEZE's "2X" zinc gluconate labeling precedent already noted on the zinc ingredient page.
- No independent confirmation was found in this pass of how many mg of standardized EPs 7630 material a 1.5mL "1X" dose actually delivers, so it cannot be directly matched to the 30/60/90 mg/day doses tested in the Matthys 2010 RCT cited on the ingredient page.
