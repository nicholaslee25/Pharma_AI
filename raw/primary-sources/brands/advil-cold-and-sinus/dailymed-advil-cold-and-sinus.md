## Active Ingredients
Ibuprofen 200mg (NSAID, pain reliever/fever reducer) + Pseudoephedrine HCl 30mg (nasal decongestant)

## Purpose
NSAID pain reliever/fever reducer + nasal decongestant

## Uses
Temporarily relieves headache, body aches, sinus pressure/congestion, fever associated with the common cold, flu, or sinusitis.

## Brand-specific notes
- A separate "Advil Allergy Sinus" SKU adds chlorpheniramine maleate (antihistamine) — a genuinely different 3-ingredient formulation, not confirmed to be the specific product tracked in this database's category listings.
- Uses pseudoephedrine (behind-the-counter in the US), not phenylephrine — a real practical distinction from many other modern OTC combination products.
