# DHS Zinc Shampoo — DailyMed Summary

Manufacturer: Person and Covey
Pulled 2026-08-27

**Active Ingredient:** Zinc Pyrithione 2%
**Purpose/Uses:** Control of dandruff and seborrheic dermatitis; helps prevent recurrence of scalp itching and flaking associated with seborrheic dermatitis and dandruff.
**Directions:** Wet hair thoroughly; apply a liberal amount and massage into a rich lather; allow lather to remain on scalp for several minutes; rinse hair well and repeat application; for best results use at least twice a week or as directed by a physician.
**Warnings:** For external use only. Do not get into eyes; if contact occurs, rinse eyes thoroughly with water. If swallowed, get medical help or contact Poison Control Center right away.
**NDC Codes:** 0096-0811-08, 0096-0811-16, 0096-0811-22

## Note
2% concentration is double the 1% confirmed for Head & Shoulders and Dove DermaCare Scalp. See wiki/projects/pharmacy/ingredients/pyrithione-zinc.md and drugs/DHS Zinc Shampoo.md.
