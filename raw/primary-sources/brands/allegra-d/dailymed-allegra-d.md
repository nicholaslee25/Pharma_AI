## Active Ingredients
Fexofenadine HCl 180mg (immediate-release) + Pseudoephedrine HCl 240mg (extended-release), once-daily tablet

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one tablet once daily with water. Do not take with fruit juice.

## Brand-specific notes
Higher pseudoephedrine total dose (240mg) than the 12-hour combination products (Zyrtec-D, Mucinex D, both 120mg) reflects the once-daily (vs. twice-daily) dosing design, not an inconsistency in pseudoephedrine dosing convention.
