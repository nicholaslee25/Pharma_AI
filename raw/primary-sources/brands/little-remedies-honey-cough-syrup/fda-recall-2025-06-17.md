# FDA Recall Notice — Little Remedies Honey Cough Syrup

**Source:** FDA.gov Recalls, Market Withdrawals & Safety Alerts — "Medtech Products Inc. Issues Nationwide Recall of Little Remedies® Honey Cough Syrup Due to Microbial Contamination" (compiled from search-result summaries and secondary reporting: Food Safety News, CBS News, KIRO 7, NBC Connecticut, TopClassActions; original FDA URL returned 404 on direct fetch in this research pass)

## Key facts
- **Recall date:** 2025-06-17
- **Manufacturer:** Medtech Products Inc.
- **Product:** Little Remedies® Honey Cough Syrup, UPC 7-56184-10737-9
- **Reason:** Presence of *Bacillus cereus* and loss of shelf-stability
- **Lots affected:** 0039, 0545, 0640, 0450, 1198
- **Expiration dates of affected lots:** November 2025 – December 2026
- **Distribution:** Nationwide, retailers and online, December 14, 2022 through June 4, 2025
- **Health risk:** *Bacillus cereus* can cause nausea, vomiting, and stomach cramps 1–6 hours after consumption, or stomach cramps and diarrhea 8–16 hours after consumption. High-level exposure carries a rare but real risk of death, particularly in vulnerable individuals.
- **Adverse events reported at time of recall:** None reported as serious, per FDA/manufacturer communication.
- **Consumer guidance:** Stop using recalled product immediately; contact a doctor if symptoms occur.
