# ASPERCREME PAIN RELIEVING - DailyMed Drug Facts

## Active Ingredient
Trolamine salicylate 10%

## Purpose
Topical analgesic

## Uses
Temporarily relieves minor pain from arthritis, backache, muscle strains, sprains, bruises, and cramps.

## Warnings
External use only. Users with sensitivity to aspirin or salicylates should consult a doctor beforehand. Apply as directed without tight bandaging or heat application; avoid eyes and mucous membranes; do not apply to wounds or damaged skin. Do not combine with other topical pain relievers. Discontinue and seek medical advice if the condition deteriorates, symptoms persist beyond seven days, redness appears, or irritation develops. Pregnant or nursing individuals should consult healthcare professionals first. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if ingested.

## Directions
Adults and children over 12 may apply generously to affected areas, massaging thoroughly until absorbed. Repeat application permitted up to 3-4 times daily. Children 12 and younger require doctor approval before use.
