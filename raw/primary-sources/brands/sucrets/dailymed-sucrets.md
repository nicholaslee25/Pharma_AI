## Active Ingredients — varies by SKU
- Standard Sucrets: Dyclonine HCl + menthol + pectin
- Sucrets Complete Vapor Cherry: Dyclonine HCl + menthol (some sources also note vitamin C/zinc in a "Complete" line)
- Sucrets Sore Throat Wild Cherry: Dyclonine HCl only

## Purpose
Oral anesthetic (dyclonine — a local anesthetic distinct from benzocaine/lidocaine); menthol as a mild cough suppressant/soothing agent; pectin as a demulcent for dry mouth/throat.

## Uses
Temporary relief of sore throat pain, minor mouth/throat irritation.

## Warnings
Not independently extracted in full in this pass — general local-anesthetic lozenge cautions apply (do not exceed labeled dose; consult a doctor for persistent symptoms >2 days or if accompanied by fever/rash).

## Directions
Not independently extracted per-SKU in this pass — standard OTC lozenge dosing convention (dissolve slowly in mouth, repeat per label interval, age-based restrictions).

## Brand-specific notes
- Dyclonine has no dedicated ingredient page in this database — a genuine research gap distinct from the well-covered benzocaine (Orajel, Cepacol) and lidocaine (Aspercreme, Salonpas) anesthetic classes already on [[topical-anesthetics]].
- Formulation varies meaningfully by SKU (some add menthol/pectin, some add vitamin C/zinc per manufacturer marketing) — not all confirmed against individual labels in this pass.
