## Drug Facts
**Active Ingredient:** Hydrocortisone acetate USP 1.0% w/w (10 mg per 1 g)
**Purpose:** Anti-itch
**Uses:** Temporary relief of itching from minor skin irritation, inflammation, and rashes caused by atopic eczema, contact dermatitis (soaps/detergents/cosmetics), seborrheic dermatitis, and psoriasis. Other uses require doctor supervision.
**Warnings:** External use only. Not for diaper rash treatment. Avoid in children under 2 without doctor supervision. Avoid eye contact. Do not combine with other hydrocortisone products without medical guidance. Discontinue if condition worsens or symptoms persist beyond 7 days.
**Directions:** Adults/children 2+: apply to affected area not more than 3-4 times daily. Children under 2: consult a doctor.
**Inactive Ingredients:** Capric triglyceride, caprylic, caprylyl glycol, ceteareth-20, chamomile recutita flower extract, citric acid, glycerin, glyceryl stearate, palmitic acid, petrolatum, phenoxyethanol, stearic acid, purified water.

## Ester-form note
This is hydrocortisone ACETATE, not free hydrocortisone (the form used in Cortizone-10/Vanicream HC per ingredients/hydrocortisone.md). Standard clinical assumption treats the two as pharmacologically comparable at matched label concentrations, but no ester-specific comparative trial was located in this pass.
