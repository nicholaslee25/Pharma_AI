## Active Ingredients
Guaifenesin 1200mg (expectorant) + Pseudoephedrine HCl 120mg (nasal decongestant), extended-release tablet

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: one tablet every 12 hours, max 2 tablets/24h. Not for children <12y.

## Warnings
Do not use with a prescription MAOI or within 2 weeks of stopping one. If pregnant/breastfeeding, ask a health professional. Overdose: get medical help or contact Poison Control immediately.
