## 2008 FDA/CHPA Pediatric Cough-Cold Safety History
January 2008: FDA nationwide Public Health Advisory recommending against OTC cough/cold product use in children under 2, citing reports of convulsions, rapid heart rate, and death. A 2007 FDA advisory committee review surfaced many reports of harm/death. November 18, 2008: CHPA Board of Directors formally adopted voluntary industry-wide labeling change — OTC oral pediatric cough/cold medicine labels modified to state "do not use" in children under 4 years of age. FDA does not typically request removal of previously-labeled product from shelves during a voluntary label change.
