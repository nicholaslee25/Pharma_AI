# AmLactin Foot Repair — Research Summary

Sources: amlactin.com product page, drugs.com, PubMed search results, pulled 2026-08-27

## Manufacturer marketing (amlactin.com)
"15% Lactic Acid (AHA)" — chemical exfoliant. No formal Drug Facts label box found on product page. Ingredients (per Amazon/skinsort listings): Water, Ammonium Lactate, Potassium Lactate, Sodium Lactate, Glycerin, Propylene Glycol, Cetearyl Alcohol, Mineral Oil, Petrolatum, Stearyl Alcohol, Polysorbate 60, Steareth-21, Steareth-2, Sodium Benzoate, Disodium EDTA, Xanthan Gum. Directions: apply 2x daily. Warnings: external use only; AHA sunburn alert (increased sun sensitivity, use sunscreen during and 1 week after); mild stinging/irritation possible on sensitive/broken skin.

## drugs.com indexing
drugs.com treats "AmLactin Foot Cream Therapy" as ammonium lactate topical medication with standard drug-style allergy warnings (hives, difficulty breathing, facial/throat swelling = emergency; contraindicated if allergic to ammonium lactate/glycerin/mineral oil/propylene glycol/parabens).

## No DailyMed/NDC record independently located for this specific SKU in this pass.

## Ammonium lactate 12% trial evidence (general chemistry class, not confirmed as this exact SKU/concentration)
- Middleton & Roberts 1978 (J Am Acad Dermatol) — ammonium lactate 12% lotion vs 5% lactic acid lotion, xerosis: 12% AL significantly more effective during treatment and regression periods.
- Clinical evaluation of 40% urea vs 12% ammonium lactate in xerosis (PMID 11978141) — comparative trial.
- Double-blind RCT: pure lanolin vs ammonium lactate 12% cream for moderate-to-severe FOOT xerosis specifically (PMID 12553635) — most directly relevant trial to this brand's foot-specific indication, though not confirmed to be this exact product.
- Lactate 12% lotion vs vehicle vs petrolatum cream for ichthyosis (PMID 3543073).
- FDA package insert (Ammonium Lactate Cream 12%, Bryant Ranch Prepack) — indicated for ichthyosis vulgaris and xerosis; adverse reactions (rash/erythema/irritation, burning/stinging) in ~10-15% of ichthyosis-trial patients; established safe/effective down to age 2.

## Discrepancy
"15% lactic acid" (free acid, marketing framing) vs. "ammonium lactate 12%" (neutralized salt, the FDA-precedented/trial-tested concentration) — not confirmed equivalent in this pass. Also unclear whether this SKU carries the same FDA-monograph drug status as AmLactin's classic ammonium-lactate-12% lotion/cream line.
