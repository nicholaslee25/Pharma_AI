# Parodontax — toothpaste (stannous fluoride) vs. mouthwash (CPC) discrepancy

**Retrieved:** 2026-08-26

## Parodontax toothpaste line (confirmed stannous fluoride)
**Sources:** https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0f07aa1a-97be-44af-92d8-a10777e24e0a ; parodontax.com/gum-health/the-parodontax-difference/what-is-stannous-fluoride
Multiple sub-lines (Active Gum Repair Whitening, Active Gum Repair Breath Freshener, Complete Protection) all confirmed stannous fluoride paste. Manufacturer states 0.454% stannous fluoride, 1100ppm fluoride.

## Parodontax Active Gum Health Mouthwash (confirmed CPC, NOT stannous fluoride)
**Active Ingredient:** Cetylpyridinium Chloride (CPC) 0.07% w/w.

## Discrepancy
The brand's public identity and marketing center almost entirely on stannous fluoride (toothpaste), but the mouthwash under the same "gum health" family uses a different active ingredient (CPC) entirely.
