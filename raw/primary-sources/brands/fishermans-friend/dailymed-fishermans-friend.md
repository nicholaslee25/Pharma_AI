## Active Ingredient — varies by SKU
Menthol: Original Extra Strong 10mg/lozenge, Sugar Free Cherry 6.9mg/lozenge

## Uses
Temporarily relieves occasional minor sore throat and cough due to minor throat/bronchial irritation from a cold or inhaled irritants.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥2y: 1 lozenge dissolved slowly, repeat every 2 hours as needed. Children <2y: ask a doctor.

## Brand-specific notes
Highest confirmed menthol dose (10mg) among brands checked in this category — genuine basis for "extra strong" branding.
