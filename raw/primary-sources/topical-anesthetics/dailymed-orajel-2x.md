## Active Ingredients
Benzocaine 20%; Menthol 0.26%

## Purpose
Oral pain reliever

## Uses
Temporary relief of pain due to toothaches.

## Warnings
**Methemoglobinemia warning (verbatim intent):** "Use of this product may cause methemoglobinemia, a serious condition that must be treated promptly because it reduces the amount of oxygen carried in blood." Stop use and seek immediate medical attention for: pale/gray/blue-colored skin (cyanosis), rapid heart rate, shortness of breath, dizziness/lightheadedness, fatigue/lack of energy.
**Allergy alert:** do not use if history of allergy to local anesthetics (procaine, butacaine, benzocaine, other "-caine" anesthetics).
**Do not use:** more than directed; for more than 7 days unless directed by a dentist/doctor; for teething; in children under 2 years.

## Directions
Adults and children 2+: apply to affected area up to 4 times daily or as directed by a dentist/doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Ammonium glycyrrhizate, flavor, PEG-8, PEG-75, sodium saccharin, sorbic acid.
