## Active Ingredient
Terbinafine hydrochloride 1%

## Purpose
Antifungal

## Uses
Athlete's foot, jock itch, ringworm; relieves itching, burning, cracking, scaling accompanying these conditions.

## Warnings
For external use only. Do not apply to nails, scalp, mouth, eyes, or for vaginal yeast infections. Rinse thoroughly with water if eye contact occurs. Discontinue if excessive irritation develops. Keep away from children; contact Poison Control if ingested. Children <12y require doctor consultation.

## Directions
Wash affected area first; wear ventilated shoes; wash hands after application.
- **Athlete's foot, between toes:** twice daily (morning/night) for **1 week**
- **Athlete's foot, bottom/sides of foot:** twice daily for **2 weeks**
- **Jock itch and ringworm:** once daily for **1 week**

(Notably shorter treatment durations than clotrimazole 1% — see [[clotrimazole]] raw files, which specify 2–4 weeks for the equivalent indications.)

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzyl alcohol, cetyl alcohol, cetyl palmitate, isopropyl myristate, polysorbate 60, purified water, sodium hydroxide, sorbitan monostearate, stearyl alcohol.
