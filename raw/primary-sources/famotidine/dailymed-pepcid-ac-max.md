## Active Ingredient
Famotidine 20 mg per tablet

## Purpose
Acid reducer

## Uses
Relieves heartburn associated with acid indigestion and sour stomach; prevents heartburn from eating/drinking certain foods/beverages.

## Warnings

**Allergy alert:** do not use if allergic to famotidine or other acid reducers.

**Do not use if:** trouble/pain swallowing, vomiting blood, or bloody/black stools (signs of a serious condition needing medical attention). Do not combine with other acid reducers.

**Ask a doctor before use if:** heartburn lasting >3 months; heartburn with lightheadedness/sweating/dizziness; chest/shoulder pain with associated symptoms; frequent chest pain; frequent wheezing with heartburn; unexplained weight loss; nausea/vomiting; stomach pain; kidney disease.

**Stop use and ask a doctor if:** heartburn continues/worsens, or product needed for more than 14 days.

**Pregnancy/breastfeeding:** ask a health professional before use.

## Directions
Adults/children ≥12y: swallow 1 tablet with water, do not chew. For prevention: 1 tablet 10–60 minutes before eating. Max 2 tablets/24h. Children <12y: ask a doctor.

## Inactive Ingredients
Carnauba wax, hydroxypropyl cellulose, hypromellose, magnesium stearate, microcrystalline cellulose, pregelatinized starch, talc, titanium dioxide.
