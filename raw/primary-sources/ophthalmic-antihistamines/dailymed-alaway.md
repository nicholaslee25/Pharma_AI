# Alaway (Ketotifen Fumarate) — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=e4c310b5-1dba-4ddc-bc90-b27ebc765871
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
Ketotifen fumarate 0.25 mg/mL (=0.025%)

## Indication
OTC ophthalmic antihistamine — temporary relief of itchy eyes due to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander

## Directions
Adults and children 3+: 1 drop per affected eye twice daily, every 8-12h, no more than twice/day

## Inactive Ingredients
Benzalkonium chloride, glycerin, HCl and/or NaOH, water

## FDA History
First approved 2006-12-01, Bausch Health Companies
