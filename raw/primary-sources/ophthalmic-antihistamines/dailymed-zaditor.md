# Zaditor (Ketotifen Fumarate) — DailyMed FDA Label Extract

Source: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=ac66b1e4-c2b0-a4c3-09e3-ebd44a2f7c9f
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
Ketotifen fumarate ophthalmic solution 0.025%

## Indication
Temporary prevention of itchy eyes due to allergic conjunctivitis (pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander)

## Directions
Adults and children 3+: 1 drop in affected eye(s) twice daily, every 8-12h, no more than twice per day.

## Note
FDA approved OTC status for this ingredient/concentration. Marketed also as SYSTANE ZADITOR (Alcon) and generically.
