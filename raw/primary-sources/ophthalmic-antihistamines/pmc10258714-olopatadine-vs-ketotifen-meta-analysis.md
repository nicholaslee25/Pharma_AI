# Comparison of Olopatadine with Ketotifen for Allergic Conjunctivitis — Meta-Analysis

Source: PMC10258714 (2023), also ScienceDirect S1877032023004165
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Design
Systematic review/meta-analysis, 7 RCTs (databases: PubMed, Embase, Web of Science, EBSCO, Cochrane Library), olopatadine vs. ketotifen in allergic conjunctivitis patients.

## Results
- Olopatadine significantly reduced hyperemia (redness) vs. ketotifen: MD -0.77 (95% CI -1.24 to -0.30, p=0.001)
- No significant difference between the two drugs for itching, tearing, or papillae
- Both classified as "new generation multiple-action topical antiallergic agents," first-line allergic-conjunctivitis treatment
