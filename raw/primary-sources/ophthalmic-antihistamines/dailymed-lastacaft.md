# Lastacaft (Alcaftadine) — DailyMed FDA Label / OTC Switch Extract

Sources: DailyMed (setid a3002410-4bf1-4182-9d7c-8193eb7cd215), FDA OTC-switch approval letter (022134Orig1s008), Drugs.com approval history
Pulled: 2026-08-26

## Active Ingredient
Alcaftadine ophthalmic solution 0.25%

## Indication
Once-daily H1-antihistamine eye drop for itchy eyes due to pollen, ragweed, grass, animal hair/dander

## Regulatory History
Originally prescription-only; FDA approved full Rx-to-OTC switch December 2021. OTC labeling required in Drug Facts format (21 CFR 201.66). Marketed as "Original Prescription Strength," "Once Daily Relief."
