# Citation Index — Extracted from the ChatGPT Research Dossiers

**Purpose:** The two original ChatGPT-compiled dossiers (`otc-guide-master-dossier-volume-2.md` and `otc-evidence-corpus-chatgpt-continuation.md`) have been deleted — their AI-written interpretation/prose was redundant once real primary sourcing began (see the [[ibuprofen]] pilot). The only lasting value they had was pointing at real studies. This file preserves that pointer list so the primary-sourcing pass (StatPearls / FDA labels / Cochrane / PubMed, same process used for ibuprofen) has a checklist of what to go verify and pull directly, organized by ingredient/topic.

**Status of every citation below: unverified pointer, not yet confirmed against the actual source.** Treat a PMID/DOI here as "go check this," not as an established fact — the ChatGPT dossiers occasionally got citations wrong (one, PMID 42014704, looked suspicious enough to flag before this cleanup).

---
